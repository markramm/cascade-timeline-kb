Judge Lynn Adelman granted the United States' motion for a protective order. The City of Milwaukee had asked the court to compel White House conference-call agenda materials from January 2025 forward on facial coverings and identifying badges for federal agents. The judge wrote that the requests were "disproportionate to the requirements of this case." He would not compel production on RFPs 6-7. He also would not deem the City's disputed requests for admission admitted or compel further responses from the United States.

The order sets the schedule for the pending motions. The United States filed its motion for a preliminary injunction on July 24, 2026. It filed a motion for judgment on the pleadings on October 30, 2026. The judge ordered both briefed together. The United States must produce all unredacted discovery by November 17, 2026. The City's combined response is due December 8, 2026. The United States' combined reply is due December 29, 2026.

This is the federal suit that followed the standoff recorded in [[2026-07-17--doj-milwaukee-mask-ban-standoff-deadline-passes]].
