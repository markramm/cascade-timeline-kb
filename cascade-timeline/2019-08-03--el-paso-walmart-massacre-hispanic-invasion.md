On August 3, 2019, a gunman attacked a Walmart in El Paso, Texas. The Texas Tribune reported that 23 people were fatally wounded and another 22 were injured. Law enforcement said he drove nearly 10 hours from the Dallas suburb of Allen to El Paso. According to court records, he told police after the shooting that he was targeting Mexicans. The Tribune reported that most of the victims, though not all, were Mexican Americans or Mexicans, and called the attack the deadliest on Hispanic people in modern U.S. history.

He pleaded guilty in federal court in February 2023 to hate crime and weapons charges, and was sentenced in July 2023 to 90 consecutive life sentences on an indictment of 90 counts. He later pleaded guilty to capital murder in a Texas state court after local prosecutors took the death penalty off the table.

The Royal Commission of Inquiry into the Christchurch attack, in a report published in 2020, listed El Paso among four deadly attacks in 2019 that were associated with 8chan, and wrote that each of those attackers pre-announced their intentions on the platform's forums. Christchurch, Poway, El Paso and a mosque attack in Norway are the four it names.
