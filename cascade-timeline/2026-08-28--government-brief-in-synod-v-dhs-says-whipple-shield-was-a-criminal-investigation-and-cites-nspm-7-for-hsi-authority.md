Filed 08/28/26 in New England Synod v. DHS (D. Mass.). The defendants' brief states: "Project Whipple Shield’s purpose was to identify and disrupt individuals and networks who were engaging in acts of violence, destruction of property and intimidation of federal officers and employees—all Title 18 offenses." The brief also states: "In fact, Project Whipple Shield did not execute any immigration enforcement actions under Title 8."

On HSI's authority, the brief names "National Security Presidential Memorandum NSPM-7" and then states: "In sum, HSI has the authority to investigate suspected criminal activity under Title 18 of the U.S. Code."
