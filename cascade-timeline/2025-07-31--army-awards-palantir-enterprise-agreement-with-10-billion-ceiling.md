The Army's release says it "awarded Palantir a new Enterprise Agreement", with purchases "not to exceed the $10 billion cap". The release wrote: "This amount represents the maximum potential value of the contract, not any specific obligations or commitments." It also wrote that "the Army consolidates 75 contracts, comprised of 15 prime contracts and 60 related contracts". Breaking Defense wrote: "Palantir has been awarded up to $10 billion over 10 years", under "a new type of contract vehicle deemed an Enterprise Service Agreement".

## Correction

The earlier title named this a contract award of $10 billion. The $10 billion is a ceiling. The earlier text's remarks on significance and a remark by the President that it quoted are in the repository history; this pass did not re-read them. This pass located no agreement number. This agreement is separate from the Maven program.
