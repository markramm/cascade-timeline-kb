With a file-stamp of 05/06/2026, the Eleventh Circuit decided *Hernandez Alvarez v. Warden, Federal Detention Center Miami* and affirmed the grants of habeas relief. Judge Marcus wrote for a panel with Judges Rosenbaum and Lagoa; Judge Lagoa dissented. The court wrote: "We are unpersuaded by the Government’s re-interpretation of § 1225(b)(2)(A)." Noncitizens arrested in the interior were therefore eligible for bond hearings.

The opinion counts four other circuits that had already weighed in, "reaching well-reasoned yet distinctly contrary conclusions": the Second Circuit (Apr. 28, 2026, see [[2026-04-28--second-circuit-rules-ice-mandatory-detention-policy-illegal]]) against the government, the Fifth (*Buenrostro-Mendez*) and Eighth (*Avila*) for it, and the Seventh (*Castañon-Nava*). The Supreme Court later granted review in the Second Circuit case ([[2026-10-01--scotus-grants-review-rhoney-v-barbosa-da-cunha-mandatory-detention-no-bond]]).
