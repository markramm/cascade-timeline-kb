President Trump made these remarks at rallies, in the AP's account. On Camp Lejeune settlements in Greenville, North Carolina, he said: "if we don't win, there's no money." He added: "If we do win, you're getting it." Asked later at the White House, he said he "can't speak for another administration."

On storm recovery money in Gastonia, he said: "Now, if Michael loses, I'm not sending it," and he "immediately said he was kidding." On a year-round ethanol rule in Park City, Kansas, he spoke of Democratic candidate Adam Hamilton: "But if Adam Hamilton is in, I'm not doing it." No withholding of funds is reported; these are his statements.
