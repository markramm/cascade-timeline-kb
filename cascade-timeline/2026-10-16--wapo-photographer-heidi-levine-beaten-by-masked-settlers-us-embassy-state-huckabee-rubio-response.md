Sequence, with date kinds. Narrowing the task premise: the sources attribute this attack to masked Israeli settlers, with Israeli soldiers present, not to Israeli forces as perpetrators.

- 2026-10-16 (event date, per CBS page date): Levine, a Washington Post photographer, was attacked near Abu Falah while covering the olive harvest, she said. The Post said she "was knocked to the ground, repeatedly kicked and robbed of her cameras and phone". Her account is that it lasted more than ten minutes.
- Israeli statements, same day: the Prime Minister's Office said the facts "remain unclear and are under investigation"; police and the military said they opened investigations.
- Levine told NPR what colleagues relayed to her about the soldiers present: "from what everyone has told me, they were on the side of the settlers and didn't do anything to stop them". This is secondhand, not her own observation.
- US State Department spokesman, 2026-10-16 (statement date, as quoted by ABC): "We are greatly concerned when any American is harmed overseas and stand ready to provide all appropriate consular assistance". ABC described this as having stopped short of laying blame on Israeli settlers or any other party.
- US Embassy statement, same day (as quoted by CBS): "We condemn criminal violence by any party in the West Bank and call for investigations, arrests, and convictions, of those responsible".
- Saturday, the day after (per Al Jazeera, same-day report): Rubio said the State Department had inquired about the attack and that he expected to hear back from the embassy by Sunday. Huckabee on X: "We have called for a full investigation, arrest, and prosecution to the fullest extent of the law to whoever participated in this". Rubio told reporters: "We’ve asked the Israelis and continue to ask them to crack down on that stuff".
- 2026-10-19 (CBS page date; police announcement date): Israeli police announced the arrest of 10 people over recent settler attacks, covering the Levine attack and an attack on European diplomats. Huckabee on social media called it "a very positive development in finding & punishing those who severely attacked and beat Heidi Levine an unarmed journalist".

Not established: who the arrested are, what each is held for, or whether any charge follows. Huckabee's and Rubio's words were read through Al Jazeera and CBS, not the X post or a transcript.
