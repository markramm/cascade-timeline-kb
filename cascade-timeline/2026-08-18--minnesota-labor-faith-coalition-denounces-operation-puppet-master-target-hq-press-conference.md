A coalition of labor, community and faith organizations in Minnesota denounced Operation Puppet Master at a press conference outside Target's downtown Minneapolis headquarters, held on the Tuesday before Workday Magazine's August 20 report.

Workday summarizes the defense discovery motion in the Sant case as saying SEIU Local 26 is also named by DHS, and that Homeland Security Investigations subpoenaed financial records for several unions including SEIU locals in Minnesota and the Communication Workers of America. (The primary documents describe these as customs summonses; see [[2026-02-11--project-whipple-shield-hsi-financial-surveillance-minnesota-unions]].)

Marcia Howard, president of the Minneapolis Federation of Educators, spoke on the back-to-school Target boycott: "the only thing that this corporation needs to do is make good." The coalition asked Target to meet with it before early voting began.

Workday's article carries no statement from SEIU Local 26 or its president, Greg Nammacher.
