ICE's own 287(g) page, in a Wayback copy stamped as of March 20, 2026, said ICE had signed 1,547 Memorandums of Agreement for 287(g) programs covering 39 states and 2 U.S. Territories: JEM agreements with 155 law enforcement agencies, WSO agreements with 476 and TFM agreements with 916. The figure counts signed agreements, not departments or people.

In Idaho, House Bill 659 would require each law enforcement agency in the state apply for a 287(g) program. The Idaho House passed it 41-27-2 on 03/06, and the Legislature's page shows its last recorded action as the Senate's first reading and referral to State Affairs. The page records no Senate vote, no signature and no veto, so no Idaho law requiring 287(g) agreements is shown to have been enacted through this bill. For laws that did require sheriffs or jails to enter 287(g) agreements, see [[2026-05-19--tennessee-hb2219-287g-sheriff-mandate-signed-law]].
