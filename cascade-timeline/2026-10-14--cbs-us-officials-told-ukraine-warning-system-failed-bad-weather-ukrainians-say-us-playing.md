Sequence only. The CBS article is the primary for what CBS reported; every US-side statement in it comes from anonymous sources.

- Zelensky says Ukraine received no advance warning before the ballistic barrage "early Tuesday" (CBS wording). Other outlets (Kuwait Times, UNN) describe the same overnight attack as overnight Monday into Tuesday.
- Sources tell CBS that American officials said the launch-detection system failed due to "bad weather"; Ukrainian officials, anonymous, did not believe it and accused the US of "playing" with the system.
- Per CBS, the system appeared to be working again when another wave hit the next day (Wednesday, CBS wording).
- The "bad weather" explanation was carried a day earlier by Politico (via UNN, Politico itself not fetched).
- CBS reports no on-record response from the White House, Pentagon or ODNI on the warning allegations.
