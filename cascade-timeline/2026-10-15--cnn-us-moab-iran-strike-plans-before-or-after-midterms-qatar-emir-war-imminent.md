CNN reported, citing multiple US officials familiar with the matter, that the US military has developed new strike plans that include potentially using the so-called "mother of all bombs" (the MOAB) against Iranian energy infrastructure and nuclear sites should Trump order combat resumed either before or after the midterm elections. One source said the strike would come "prior to US retrograde in CENTCOM AOR (area of responsibility)", indicating the administration could strike, attempt to declare victory and quickly pull out. A second source said plans for striking nuclear sites including Pickaxe Mountain explicitly call for the MOAB. Trump told Newsmax "It's not nuclear at all, this is just an extremely large bomb." CNN also reported doubts over how effective the weapon would be. The planning claims are attributed to multiple US officials familiar with the matter.

Later reporting added context. CNN later reported that Trump had announced publicly he would not take new actions before the midterm elections, while several advisers privately did not know whether that was definitive, and that the US had moved all of its long-range bombers stationed in the United Kingdom back to American home bases earlier in the month. A Soufan Center IntelBrief reported that the Emir of Qatar told the Shura Council "War is imminent. We must be prepared for the worst possible scenarios," a reported 23 attacks on ships transiting the Strait since the start of October, and crude oil regaining the $100 per barrel level for West Texas Intermediate and nearly $110 for Brent. An IRGC spokesperson promised "new targets that have not yet been attacked". ## Update (2026-10-28, statement date): Sen. Murphy says he was briefed on the plan to resume the war and calls it "insane"

Sen. Chris Murphy said Wednesday at a Middle East Institute event that a closed briefing included a detailed discussion of what the plan would be if military operations resumed, and that "their plan is insane." He wrote on X: "Some of us have been briefed on the war plan. And while I cannot disclose any details, I can say this: the plan is insane." CT Insider reports that Murphy did not identify specific targets discussed in his briefing and said he was referring to open reporting about possible targets. MS NOW reports, citing three senior Gulf Arab officials granted anonymity, that the administration has briefed Gulf allies to prepare for a short, intense round of strikes as soon as Nov. 4.
