The Casey County Fiscal Court in Kentucky approved a 12-month moratorium on data centers by majority vote Wednesday at a special called meeting. Under the resolution, the construction, development and operation of data centers and data center energy systems in the county's unincorporated areas are prohibited pending review of the impact on water supply capacity, infrastructure and regulatory standards.
