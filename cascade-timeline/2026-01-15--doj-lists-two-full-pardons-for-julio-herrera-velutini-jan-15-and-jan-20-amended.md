DOJ's list of clemency grants has two entries for Julio Herrera Velutini, a party in 3:25-cr-297, listed under Puerto Rico. One is in the batch headed January 15, 2026 - 12 Pardons, with the offenses Conspiracy (two counts); federal program bribery (two counts); honest services wire fraud (two counts). The other is in the batch headed January 20, 2026 - 3 Pardons (Amended), with the offense Contribution by a foreign national. The warrant for each reads A FULL AND UNCONDITIONAL PARDON, and the warrant for the second names the case United States v. Julio Herrera Velutini, 3:25-cr-297 (SCC).

These are the dates of the list's batch headings, not a date read from the warrants. The documents read for this entry do not say who asked for either grant or why a second was issued, and the entry draws no conclusion on either.
