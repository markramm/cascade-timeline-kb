Syria's state-owned Syrian Petroleum Company signed a memorandum of understanding with Chevron International and Power International Holding for the development of the country's first offshore oil and gas field. The agreement aims to invest in exploration and drilling within Syrian territorial waters, strengthen strategic energy partnerships, and support development pathways.

This is a state counterparty to Power International Holding separate from the Ministry of Energy and the Civil Aviation Authority.
