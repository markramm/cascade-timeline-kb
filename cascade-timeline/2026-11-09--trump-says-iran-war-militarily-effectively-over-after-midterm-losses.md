The latest development came on November 9, when Trump declared US military action in Iran 'militarily, effectively over,' signalling that Washington was unlikely to resume large-scale military action against Tehran for now. Speaking at the White House after meeting Qatar's Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed, Trump said Iran had been 'decapitated' and that the US was maintaining the status quo, while warning that American forces remained 'locked and loaded' if circumstances changed, as Business Standard reported, citing the Associated Press.

The Republican Party lost control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate in the November 3 midterms, and Trump and Vice-President JD Vance acknowledged that public opposition to the prolonged Iran war and its impact on energy prices had contributed to the party's defeat.
