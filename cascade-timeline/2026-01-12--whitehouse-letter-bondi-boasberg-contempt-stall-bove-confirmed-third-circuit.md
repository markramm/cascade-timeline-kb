Sen. Sheldon Whitehouse sent a letter to Attorney General Pam Bondi asking for the result of the Department of Justice's disciplinary complaint against Chief Judge James Boasberg. Whitehouse's release recounts that Chief Judge Boasberg found probable cause that the Trump Administration had committed criminal contempt of court by willfully disobeying his court order to halt deportations. It states that two Trump-appointed judges stalled the contempt proceeding for over three months, just enough time for Senate Republicans to confirm Judge Emil Bove to the Third Circuit on a 50-49 vote. This is Whitehouse's account, in a press release.

## The New Jersey count of 56

Slate reported that the New Jersey U.S. attorney's office had violated at least 56 court orders since December alone. The Declaration of Jordan Fox, filed in the Kumar habeas case in New Jersey, reviewed approximately 547 immigration habeas matters and reported violations between December 5, 2025, to February 12, 2026. The declaration lists six missed answer deadlines, 12 missed bond hearing deadlines, 17 post-injunction transfers, one post-injunction removal, three late releases, three instances in which ICE imposed alternatives, 10 instances of incomplete evidentiary production and four miscellaneous instances, which the Office counted under a broad interpretation. Those categories add to 56. That is a Justice Department tally under its own broad interpretation, not a count by a court, and 12 of the 56 were missed bond hearing deadlines.
