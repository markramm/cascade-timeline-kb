Public Law 119-21 was approved July 4, 2025. Its section 50404, titled "Transformational Artificial Intelligence Models," states that $150,000,000 is appropriated, to remain available through September 30, 2026. The section directs the Secretary of Energy to mobilize National Laboratories to curate the Department of Energy's scientific data for use in artificial intelligence models. It also directs the Secretary to initiate seed efforts for self-improving artificial intelligence models for science and engineering. The section defines the American science cloud as a system of programs and infrastructures that uses cloud computing to support scientific research, data sharing, and computational analysis.

This is the statutory funding line that precedes the Genesis Mission. The executive order that launched the mission is recorded at [[2025-11-24--trump-executive-order-14363-launches-genesis-mission-doe-ai-science-platform]]. The section appropriates the $150,000,000 to carry out section 50404 as a whole; the statute text does not divide it between the data curation, the model seed efforts and the science cloud.
