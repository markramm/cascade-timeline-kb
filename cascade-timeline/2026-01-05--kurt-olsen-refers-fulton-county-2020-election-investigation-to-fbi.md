## What happened

Kurt Olsen referred a criminal investigation of Fulton County's 2020 election to the FBI. The Justice Department's court filing says he formally referred it no later than January 5, 2026, at 9:03 AM EST, the time a Special Attorney to the Attorney General received a copy of the referral. That is a received-by date; the referral document itself was not read.

## Same-day sequence

The same filing records that the motion to dismiss in the civil litigation was filed on January 5, 2026, at 8:36 PM EST. An FBI assessment was opened on January 6, 2026, and the full investigation was approved on January 14, 2026. The warrant application was submitted on January 27, 2026.

## Source for the referral's origin

The search warrant affidavit says that the investigation originated from a referral from Kurt Olsen, Presidentially appointed Director of Election Security and Integrity.
