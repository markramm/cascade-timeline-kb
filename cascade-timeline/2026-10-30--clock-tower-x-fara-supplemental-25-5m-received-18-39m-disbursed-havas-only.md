Clock Tower X LLC filed a Supplemental Statement, stamped Received by NSD/FARA Registration Unit 10/30/2026. Havas Media Network is the only foreign principal listed. Receipts total $25,500,000 and disbursements $18,388,988.90. The receipts appendix lists payments from Havas Media Germany GmbH for services, beginning with $3,000,000 and ending with $4,500,000. The statement describes the work as: "Registrant has provided strategic communications, planning, and media services in support of Havas' engagement by the State of Israel to develop and execute a nationwide campaign in the United States to combat anti-Semitism."

The disbursement appendix begins with Sparkfire Technologies (Direct Engagement, $375,000) and Portman Road Strategies (Media Placement, $675,000). See [[2026-05-18--clock-tower-x-fara-supplemental-15m-received-13m-disbursed-sparkfire-portman]] and [[2026-10-05--clock-tower-x-havas-invoices-45m-monthly-through-december-2026]].
