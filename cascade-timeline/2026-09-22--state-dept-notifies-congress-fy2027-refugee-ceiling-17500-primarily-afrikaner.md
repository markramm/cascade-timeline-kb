The State Department notified Congress on **September 22, 2026** that it intends to propose a Fiscal Year 2027 refugee admissions ceiling of **17,500**, with admissions "primarily" going to Afrikaners from South Africa, and an estimated resettlement cost of roughly **$500 million**. The notice, obtained and reported by the Associated Press, argues Afrikaners face uncompensated land seizures and employment/education discrimination — the same justification used for the FY2026 emergency expansion. A formal congressional consultation session followed on **September 23, 2026**; Senate Judiciary Democrats Dick Durbin and Alex Padilla and House Judiciary ranking member Jamie Raskin issued statements the next day condemning the plan. Durbin: "The Trump Administration's agenda is clear: prioritize white-only Afrikaners as refugees and disregard the millions of men, women, and children from around the world who are trying to escape horrific violence and persecution." Raskin: "the Administration has turned America's traditional refugee program into a whites-only airplane ride for South African Afrikaners."

**This is FY2026's emergency ceiling proposed as FY2027's starting ceiling.** FY2026 opened at 7,500 (Presidential Determination 2025-13, Sept 30, 2025, ordinary INA §207(a)) and only reached 17,500 in May 2026 via an emergency §207(b) determination (PD 2026-14) that found an "unforeseen emergency refugee situation" and reserved the entire 10,000-slot increase for Afrikaners from South Africa. The FY2027 notice proposes the same 17,500 figure as the year's ordinary opening ceiling — no new emergency finding required, because it would run through the routine annual consultation process rather than a mid-year emergency escalation. The headline number is unchanged from where FY2026 ended; what has changed is that reaching it no longer requires characterizing anything as an emergency.

**No Presidential Determination for FY2027 had issued as of September 28, 2026** (confirmed directly against the Federal Register — no FY2027 refugee-admissions presidential document exists yet). 17,500-primarily-Afrikaner is the administration's proposed position pending that determination, not yet binding.

**Continuity with the documented pattern.** [[2026-09-19--fy2026-refugee-admissions-9997-percent-afrikaner-eo-14204]] documented that FY2026 admissions ran 10,255-of-10,258 (99.97%) South African through July 31, 2026, against a program the Refugee Act of 1980 never repealed. Primary RPC data pulled through August 31, 2026 (cascade-research: [[rpc-state-gov-fy2026-refugee-arrivals-nationality-through-2026-08-31]]) shows the same pattern continuing: of 12,904 total FY2026 arrivals through August, exactly 3 were not South African. The FY2027 proposal is this same allocation mechanism requesting renewal as the default, not an exception.
