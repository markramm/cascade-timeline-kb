The Associated Press reported on September 22, 2026 that the Trump administration plans to limit refugee admissions in 2027 to 17,500 mostly white South Africans, as the administration told Congress. In a notice to Congress obtained by AP, the administration said the white South Africans faced seizures of their land and other forms of persecution and would be likely to assimilate when they come to America. The administration told Congress it intended to spend about $500 million to resettle the new arrivals. The notice itself has not been located; this entry rests on AP's account of it.

Raskin, Durbin and Padilla pressed DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin on the administration's refugee policies during the annual, statutorily required refugee consultation for Fiscal Year 2027, according to a House Judiciary Committee Democrats press release dated September 24, 2026. Durbin: "The Trump Administration’s agenda is clear: prioritize white-only Afrikaners as refugees and disregard the millions of men, women, and children from around the world who are trying to escape horrific violence and persecution". Raskin: "the Administration has turned America’s traditional refugee program into a whites-only airplane ride for South African Afrikaners into Trump’s America".

The FY2026 documents. Presidential Determination 2025-13 provides for admissions of up to 7,500 refugees in Fiscal Year 2026 and states that the numbers shall primarily be allocated among Afrikaners from South Africa pursuant to Executive Order 14204, and other victims of illegal or unjust discrimination in their respective homelands. Presidential Determination 2026-14, made under section 207(b), finds "an unforeseen emergency refugee situation" and states that an increase in the ceiling to 17,500 is warranted, with the additional admissions allocated among Afrikaners from South Africa.
