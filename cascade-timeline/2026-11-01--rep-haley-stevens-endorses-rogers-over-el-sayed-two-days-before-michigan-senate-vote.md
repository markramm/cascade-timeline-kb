Rep. Haley Stevens, the Michigan Democrat who lost the Senate primary to Abdul El-Sayed, endorsed Republican Mike Rogers on Nov. 1, 2026, attending Perfecting Church in Detroit with him. In a video on X she said she was "no longer welcome in his campaign," and added: "And that's why I am endorsing Mike Rogers for the United States Senate today." Bridge Michigan quotes her: "We can’t stand with hate."

MS NOW, citing a Democratic source speaking anonymously, reports El-Sayed's campaign asked her to stay away from its events after the dispute over a Rebellion PAC ad; see [[2026-10-30--stevens-conditions-unity-event-on-el-sayed-condemning-rebellion-pac-ad-el-sayed-statement-does-not-mention-it-campaign-asks-her-to-stay-away]].

El-Sayed said: "I actually don't think about Haley Stevens." Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer called it "a despicable betrayal." Her chief of staff said "many members of our team have resigned." Rogers praised the decision, and Trump praised Stevens' endorsement on Truth Social.

Early voting has been underway since Oct. 24, per Bridge Michigan.

## Update (2026-11-01, publication date of the post): A shout at the news conference

A post by Aaron Rupar with a video clip says a protester yelled "Haley Stevens is a traitor!" at the news conference and that Stevens then wrapped it up in a hurry. That is his description of the clip.

## Update (2026-11-01, statement date): Her staff, Sanders and Newsom

Haley Stevens' staff is right to resign in disgust." California Gov. Gavin Newsom: Stevens "went another 180 degrees overnight, so something was going on there," and "This may be curiously the October surprise of sorts."

## Update (2026-11-01, interview date): Dingell says she pulled the church invitation, not El-Sayed

Rep. Debbie Dingell helped organize the planned joint church appearance and says she asked Stevens not to attend, and that Stevens told her she would abide by that, the AP reports: "I'm the one who pulled it, because it had become way too distracting." She added: "The bishop was getting upset, because this is a real church service." Stevens's video gives a different account: El-Sayed "said I was no longer welcome in his campaign and that I should stay away." Fox News adds, as a separate step, that El-Sayed's campaign asked her to stay away from its remaining events.

## Update (2026-11-01, news conference date): What she and Rogers said at the news conference

From CNN's video of the news conference, https://youtu.be/b0vFVC1aa8I, about 0:30 to 7:04; hand update, because the sourcing gate can't read YouTube. The words were read from auto-captions and checked by ear by the editor.

## Update (2026-11-01, interview date): Rep. Stevens to Anderson Cooper: "this is just a weight off my shoulders"; Rep. Dingell calls it painful
