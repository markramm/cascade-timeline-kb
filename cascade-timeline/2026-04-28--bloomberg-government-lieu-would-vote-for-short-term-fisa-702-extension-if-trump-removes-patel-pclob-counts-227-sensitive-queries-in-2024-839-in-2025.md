**Read without a subscription.** The headline, byline, standfirst and opening paragraphs of the Bloomberg Government story. The standfirst reads: "A member of House Democratic leadership would vote for a short-term spy powers extension in exchange for President Donald Trump removing Kash Patel as FBI director, he told reporters Tuesday." The member is Rep. Ted Lieu, who is quoted: "Kash Patel is a major obstacle to getting to a FISA deal, and it’s because many members of Congress simply do not trust Kash Patel." The preview adds: "Democrats have expressed alarm over reports of alleged excessive drinking and investigating a reporter who wrote an unflattering article." The rest of the story was not read, and no copy of it was used.

**Where the sensitive-query figure comes from.** Privacy and Civil Liberties Oversight Board's staff count: sensitive queries by the FBI "have increased from 227 in 2024, to 839 in 2025." That is more than triple. A sensitive query is one using terms for "political, media, and religious organizations or individuals prominent in such organizations." The FBI's explanation, as the report gives it: "FBI reported to PCLOB that it had not formally surveyed the reason for this increase," and the FBI stated the increase was likely based on "significant operational need."
