DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin announced at a Dallas press conference on Oct. 29 that a Texas-wide ICE operation, Lone Star Mission, ran from October 19 to October 29 and produced 4,170 arrests. Mullin said: "Over 60% of those individuals had a criminal background." The National News Desk reports DHS did not release a full list of those arrested. These are the department's own figures, not independently verified. The release does not mention the election.
