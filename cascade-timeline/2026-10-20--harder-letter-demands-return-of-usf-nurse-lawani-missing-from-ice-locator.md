On Oct. 20, Rep. Josh Harder sent a letter to Department of Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin, demanding the immediate return of Lodi resident Dr. Ann Lawani, a University of San Francisco nursing professor detained in September and transferred thousands of miles away to Louisiana, KQED reported. Harder said her whereabouts are not reflected in ICE's online detainee locator system, leaving her family and attorney struggling to locate her. Her brother said: "My family and I want to see my sister returned home safely and as soon as possible."

The locator policy is recorded at [[2026-09-15--ice-removes-final-order-detainees-from-public-locator-including-guantanamo-eight]]; legal challenges to it at [[2026-10-28--legal-groups-sue-dhs-boston-ice-locator-final-removal-orders]].
