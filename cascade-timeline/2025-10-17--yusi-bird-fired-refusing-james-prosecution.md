Elizabeth Yusi, the top criminal prosecutor in the Norfolk office of the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Virginia, and her deputy Kristin Bird were informed of their removals on Friday, ABC News reported.

**Order of events.** A grand jury on Oct. 9 charged Letitia James with two counts of bank fraud and false statements to a financial institution (ABC). CBS reported that Halligan secured the indictment from a grand jury in Alexandria. The firings came after the indictment, not before it.

**Reasons, by whoever gave them.** ABC: sources familiar with the matter said their firings were "tied to their resistance to bringing charges against James". CNN, a week later: Yusi was dismissed "at least in part because of the mishandling of the personal information", people familiar with her termination said; that reason comes from unnamed people, not an on-record Justice Department statement, and the department declined to comment. Yusi's attorney, Margaret Donovan, told CNN the former prosecutor "has no record of any such email" and "has never used her personal email account for any portion of any investigation". CNN also reported that Yusi authored an internal memo explaining why prosecutors believed there was not enough evidence to charge James, and that sources told CNN the memo also contributed to her firing.

**What happened to the case.** On Nov. 24, 2025, Judge Cameron McGowan Currie determined that Lindsey Halligan's appointment as U.S. Attorney was illegal and wrote that "All actions flowing from Ms. Halligan’s defective appointment were unlawful exercises of executive power and are hereby set aside"; she dismissed the case without prejudice. That ruling rests on the appointment, not on the evidence. In a story published Dec. 4, 2025 the Associated Press reported that grand jurors rejected prosecutors' request to bring charges. Later developments are not in the sources read for this entry.

## Correction (2026-11-17)

Earlier entries (the old ids are in the frontmatter) carried later dates and put the indictment after the firings. The indictment, on Oct. 9, came before them.
