The Washington Post reported on November 7, 2026, as relayed by the Daily Beast, that Laura Loomer was paid $60,000 to post political messages on her social media channel, citing financial records showing four payments totaling $60,000 made to Loomer's King Breaker Media company. The posts went to her X account, where she has 1.9 million followers. Other sources told the Post she charged between $3,000 and $5,000 for an X post. Alex Bruesewitz said he paid Loomer through his consulting firm, X Strategies, and that he decided to speak out because he believed the practice had gone too far. Loomer, in a statement to the Post, described her work as journalism and activism and denied that she was paid to post on social media.

The account is the Post's, read here only through the Daily Beast's coverage; the underlying records were not reviewed. It fits the paid-influencer pattern in [[2026-08-30--nyt-campaigns-paying-political-influencers-steyer-porter-sideshift-majority-forward-no-federal-disclosure-rule]].
