The Trump Organization received payments from foreign governments including China, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, UAE, and Qatar during Trump's presidency, violating the Constitution's Foreign Emoluments Clause. Saudi officials paid Trump properties, as Washington Post reporting documented. T-Mobile executives stayed at Trump's hotel for over 52 nights while seeking merger approval. The payments included rent from the Chinese state-owned Industrial and Commercial Bank of China for Trump Tower space. Trump refused to divest his businesses or place them in a blind trust, instead having his sons run operations while he retained ownership. Federal judges found Trump violated the Emoluments Clause but the Supreme Court dismissed cases as moot after his term ended.
