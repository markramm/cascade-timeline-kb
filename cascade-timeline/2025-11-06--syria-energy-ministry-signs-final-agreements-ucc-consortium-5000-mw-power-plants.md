SANA reports that Syria's Ministry of Energy signed final agreements with the consortium led by UCC Holding to build four new power plants with a total capacity of 5,000 megawatts, using natural gas and solar energy. The Ministry said the agreement follows up on the memoranda of understanding signed in May 2025.

The National, citing Ministry officials, describes a $7 billion power generation project for eight new plants.
