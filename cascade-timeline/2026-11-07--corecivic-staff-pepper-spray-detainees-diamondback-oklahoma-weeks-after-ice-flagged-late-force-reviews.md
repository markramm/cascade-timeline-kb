Staff at the Diamondback Correctional Facility in Watonga, Oklahoma, used pepper spray on a group of detainees on Nov. 7, 2026, CoreCivic, the company that runs it, told The Frontier. CoreCivic said staff worked to de-escalate verbally and, when it became unsafe, "took action to restore order." In a separate incident on Nov. 5, several detainees reported breathing difficulties after being moved into a housing pod with residual pepper spray from a staff training exercise; one person was taken to a local hospital. ICE said the unit "was ventilated and decontaminated before detainees returned."

ICE inspectors had visited in September and found the facility was not filing use-of-force reviews on time: of the 18 use-of-force files they reviewed, five reports were filed between seven and nine working days after the incidents. ICE national detention standards require them within five working days.
