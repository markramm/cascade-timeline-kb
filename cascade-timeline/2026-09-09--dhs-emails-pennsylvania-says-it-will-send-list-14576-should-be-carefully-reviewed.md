DHS answered Pennsylvania's questions by email on September 9, 2026 (the email's date, as reported by Votebeat and Spotlight PA on Sept. 17, 2026). The report says "In the email, DHS said it would send Pennsylvania officials a list of the potential noncitizens it identified." and quotes it: "Each one of the 14,576 individuals identified should be carefully reviewed by your state to determine eligibility for voting in a federal election."

The same report says "As of Wednesday, the Department of State has not yet received the list." It adds that "It’s unclear whether the department has sent Pennsylvania the list".
