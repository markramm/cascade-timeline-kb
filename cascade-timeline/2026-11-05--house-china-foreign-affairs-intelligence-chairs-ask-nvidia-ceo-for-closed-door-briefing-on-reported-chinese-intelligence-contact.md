On November 5, 2026, the chairs of the House Select Committee on China, the House Foreign Affairs Committee and the House Intelligence Committee sent a letter to Nvidia's CEO "requesting his participation in a joint, closed-door briefing," according to the Select Committee's press release.

The release says the letter concerns the CEO's interactions with an executive of a Nvidia customer, described as "a publicly reported Chinese intelligence asset," and the company's security protocols against foreign-adversary espionage.

The chairmen wrote: "It is vital that Congress determines the extent to which U.S. national security has been placed at risk."

The letter asked for the briefing on November 18, 2026. Under the release, the CEO "must accept the invitation or provide alternative availability by November 11, 2026."

The release does not mention the Department of Energy, the national laboratories or the Genesis Mission. The board's response is in [[2026-11-08--nvidia-board-opens-investigation-after-reports-ceo-huang-targeted-by-chinese-intelligence-affair]] and the resignation in [[2026-11-12--nvidia-announces-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-cfo-colette-kress-interim-ceo]].
