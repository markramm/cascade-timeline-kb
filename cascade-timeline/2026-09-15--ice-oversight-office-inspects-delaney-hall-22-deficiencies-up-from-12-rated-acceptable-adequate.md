ICE's Office of Detention Oversight, under its Office of Professional Responsibility, inspected the Delaney Hall detention center in Newark on Sept. 15 to 17 and found 22 deficiencies, up from 12 during an inspection in late August of last year, according to Gothamist. The inspectors rated the facility “acceptable/adequate.” The most deficiencies in any one category, six, were in prevention and investigation of sexual assault and abuse. The inspection found about 820 people detained on an average day, and in four of the 25 cases reviewed, investigators found medications were not distributed properly. Spokespeople for ICE and the GEO Group could not be reached for comment.
