The New York Attorney General's office says Letitia James filed a lawsuit against the owners and operators of a former Holiday Inn in downtown Rochester, its renovation contractor, and IHG Hotels & Resorts. The release says the suit alleges they exploited immigrant workers through forced labor, wage theft and threats of deportation. The complaint is NYSCEF DOC. NO. 1 in the Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Monroe.

The release says the Attorney General's investigation found that workers were forced to work up to 112 hours a week. The release says that when workers demanded their wages, they were met with threats of deportation, eviction or arrest. The release says the suit alleges IHG violated the federal Trafficking Victims Protection Reauthorization Act by knowingly benefiting from forced labor. The Attorney General is seeking unpaid wages, restitution, damages and penalties for the workers, according to the release.

Documented NY reports an IHG spokesperson's statement that the company denies any knowledge of the alleged misconduct.
