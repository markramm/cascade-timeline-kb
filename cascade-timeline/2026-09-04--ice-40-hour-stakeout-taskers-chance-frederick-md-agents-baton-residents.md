Federal immigration agents began staking out the **Taskers Chance** neighborhood of Frederick, Maryland at **6 a.m. Thursday, September 3, 2026**, and remained for roughly **40 hours**, through Friday night. Around **9:30 p.m. Friday, September 4**, federal vehicles lined **Stoneybrook Court** with flashing lights, agents **drove onto sidewalks** where observers stood chanting, and — on video — **deployed batons** against residents who had linked arms in front of the targeted home and **dragged** those sitting before it.

**No arrests were made.** Not of the protesters, and the reporting does not establish that anyone was taken from the house either.

Three things give this its shape.

**First, the duration.** Forty hours is not a raid. A raid is an entry; this was a **presence** — two nights of federal vehicles parked in a residential cul-de-sac while people decided whether it was safe to leave for work. Whatever its investigative purpose, the operation's effect on everyone who was not its target was continuous and unavoidable, and it required no contact with them at all.

**Second, DHS declined to say what it was doing.** Asked for specifics, the department said only that "**it would not disclose or discuss information related to potential investigations.**" A 40-hour occupation of a residential street is a public act; the explanation for it is being withheld as an investigative matter. The asymmetry is the point — the neighborhood experienced the operation in full and is entitled to none of its reasoning.

**Third, the city's own officials are the witnesses.** City Council President **César Díaz** called the scene "**horrific**" and said of the vehicles on the sidewalk: "**People had to move or risk getting run over.**" Council Vice President **Katie Nash**, who was worried things would turn dangerous, stated that "**none of the observers was violent or throwing things toward the federal agents.**" A city spokesperson, **Jennifer Gerlock**, said local officers "**responded to maintain public safety and de-escalate**" — meaning municipal police were deployed to manage the consequences of a federal operation the city did not authorize and was not told about.

That last detail is the structural one. The federal government can conduct an operation of this length inside a city, decline to explain it, and leave the city's own police to absorb the confrontation it produces. Local government bears the cost and holds none of the information.
