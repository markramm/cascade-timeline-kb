At a news conference on July 17, DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin said that if election officials, "once we gave them the information they need to secure their elections, and they chose not to," they could "be held accountable by fines, by penalties, and even depending on how far it goes, prison time." The remark is conditional, and it names no statute and no official.
