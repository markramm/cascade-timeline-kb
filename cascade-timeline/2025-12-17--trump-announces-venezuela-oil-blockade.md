President Trump said on Tuesday, December 16, 2025 that he was ordering a blockade of all sanctioned oil tankers going into and out of Venezuela. In the same Truth Social post he said "the Venezuelan Regime has been designated a FOREIGN TERRORIST ORGANIZATION".

## What the record shows about the designation

The State Department designated Cartel de los Soles, not the Venezuelan government. Secretary Rubio's notice is dated November 16, 2025, was published in the Federal Register on Monday, November 24, 2025, and says the designation goes into effect upon publication. Federal Register notices from the State Department between November 2025 and January 2026 designate other organizations; none of them names the Venezuelan government. CNBC reports, citing the State Department, that an FTO designation makes it illegal for any U.S. citizen to knowingly provide material support or resources to the organization.

## Legal characterization, attributed

Just Security's analysis says that as a matter of law peacetime blockades qualify as a use of force even before force is used against ships attempting to breach them. That is the analysis of its authors, not a finding by a court or the UN.
