DOE's Oak Ridge release says that, with more than $1 billion in public-private investment, DOE, together with AMD and HPE, is delivering new AI capacity in record time.

DOE describes a new public-private partnership model that works "by allowing for co-investments from both the DOE and private partners".

DOE says the second system, Discovery, comes "As part of the traditional procurement model" and will arrive in 2028.

Oak Ridge says the AMD-led Lux system will leverage the Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) as part of the Lux AI Cluster.

Neither release gives the dollar split between DOE and the companies. See [[genesis-lab-ai-supercomputers-ownership]].
