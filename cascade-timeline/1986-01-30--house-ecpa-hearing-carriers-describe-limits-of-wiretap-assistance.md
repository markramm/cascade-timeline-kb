- On Jan. 30, 1986, a House Judiciary subcommittee heard carrier testimony on the bill that became the Electronic Communications Privacy Act. AT&T's witness was Neal Amick, "Division Manager for Corporate Security at American Telephone and Telegraph Company." Southwestern Bell's attorney, John Kelly, said that as modified by a proposed revision, Southwestern Bell Corp. "supports the passage" of the bill.
- Amick told the panel that when an officer determines an emergency exists, "a carrier is required to act at its peril," and asked for a good-faith defense. The carrier testimony sought legal cover for assistance already being given, not an end to it.
- AT&T's written analysis, which Amick calls "our October 25, 1985 comments," states that the technical assistance AT&T may be required to render under a wiretap order "is limited to advice and does not include any participation" beyond a Central Office cross-connection in effecting the interception.
- The appendix also carries comments from H. W. William Caming, whose biographical note begins "Since 1965, Mr. Caming has been the senior" and is partly illegible in the scan; he writes of "my testimony in behalf of the Bell System." He describes Bell System practice as "not permitting law enforcement authorities to enter operating premises" for wiretapping, and says pen-register cooperation took the form of cable-and-pair data on the target line plus "a leased line channel" to the law-enforcement listening post. He is a former carrier lawyer writing in his own name, so treat this as a recollection of Bell System practice, not carrier testimony.
- At the Mar. 5, 1986 session, Justice Department official James Knapp said the judge should have the power to "direct the cooperation of a carrier," with civil immunity for the carrier.

## Why it matters

Read together, these passages describe an arms-length practice: the carrier identified the line and made the cross-connection, law enforcement ran its own listening post, and the carrier's stated worry was liability. The 1992 digital-telephony push ([[1992-03-18--barr-sessions-meeting-carrier-executives-fbi-digital-telephony]]) set out to change that.
