On September 25, 2025, GSA announced a OneGov agreement with xAI making Grok AI models accessible to federal agencies for only $0.42 per organization. The offer is in place for 18 months and valid until March 2027. The agreement includes access to Grok 4 and Grok 4 Fast, and xAI committed dedicated engineers to help participating agencies adopt the tools.

The agreement is a price-and-access vehicle on GSA's schedule. It records what agencies may buy; it does not record any agency's order. GSA's own OneGov page lists the models as Grok 4 and Grok 4 Mini, so the model line-up differs between the release and the OneGov page.
