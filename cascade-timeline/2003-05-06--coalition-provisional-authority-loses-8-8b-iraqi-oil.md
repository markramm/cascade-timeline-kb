The Coalition Provisional Authority under Paul Bremer lost track of $8.8 billion in Iraqi oil revenues during the 14-month occupation period (May 2003-June 2004), representing one of the largest financial oversight failures in U.S. history. Nearly $12 billion in cash (363 tons) was shipped on C-130 cargo planes, including a December 2003 shipment of $1.5 billion that a Federal Reserve official described as the largest payout of U.S. currency in U.S. history. Special Inspector General Stuart Bowen found the CPA lacked adequate managerial, financial, and contractual controls for cash distribution, with billions handed to Iraqi ministries 'without assurance the money was properly used or accounted for.' Payments to ghost employees were identified at two ministries, and the Special Inspector General found that none of ten sampled disbursements of $120 million to $900 million carried the required supporting documentation, while two disbursements totaling $616 million had no disbursement voucher.
