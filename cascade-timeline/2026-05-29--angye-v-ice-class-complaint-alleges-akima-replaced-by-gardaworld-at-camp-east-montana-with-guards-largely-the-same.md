A detainees' class complaint over conditions at Camp East Montana says the guard contract moved from Akima to GardaWorld Federal Services LLC and that the guards stayed. It also says Amentum was already a subcontractor at the camp before it became the prime. The complaint is against ICE, DHS and the Department of War and their officials; the contractors are not defendants.

The complaint pleads the Akima termination on information and belief.

The date is the complaint's filing date.
