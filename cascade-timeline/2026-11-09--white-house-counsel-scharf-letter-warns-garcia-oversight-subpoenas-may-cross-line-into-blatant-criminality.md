---

CBS News reports the letter is dated Monday. It is signed by White House counsel Will Scharf and addressed to Rep. Robert Garcia, D-Calif., the incoming House Oversight chair. It is a different document from the preservation letters U.S. Attorney Aaron Reitz sent to Democratic senators ([[2026-11-15--reitz-letter-accuses-warren-of-wielding-gavel-like-tomahawk-demands-preservation-by-nov-23]]).

The letter says: "threats to executive branch officials designed to deter them from public service, or process designed merely to abuse or instill fear in the recipient may cross the line into blatant criminality." It adds: "And issuing unlawful subpoenas with the intention of depriving citizens of their constitutional rights and privileges may warrant further investigation." It closes: "The Administration will not be extorted and will not be cowed".

The letter came after Trump's Truth Social post in which he wrote: "By copy of this statement, I am ordering the Department of Justice to look into this criminal act, immediately!" ([[2026-11-07--trump-orders-doj-to-investigate-democrats-who-pledged-oversight-blanche-wont-rule-out-prosecuting-lawmakers]]).

Garcia said in a statement: "It appears Donald Trump has something to hide," according to CBS News. CBS News reported that Attorney General Todd Blanche told reporters Thursday that the department does not have any open criminal investigations into members of Congress.
