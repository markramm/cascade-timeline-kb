Federal Judge Mae D'Agostino has denied a group of sheriffs' request to block New York State's ban on agreements with Immigration & Customs Enforcement. In her ruling, Judge D'Agostino said the 15 sheriffs "have not established a likelihood that they have capacity to sue defendants for the alleged constitutional violations; their request for preliminary injunctive relief is denied." On Nov. 4, just one day after she won reelection, Attorney General Letitia James declared the sheriffs' attempt dead in the water on X. James said: "Local law enforcement should be focused on keeping New Yorkers safe, not carrying out the administration's cruel mass deportation agenda." The legislation was enacted on May 27 and prohibits local government and law enforcement from conducting civil immigration enforcement.

On Aug. 25, 15 New York sheriffs filed the lawsuit against Governor Kathy Hochul and Attorney General James; see [[2026-08-26--fifteen-ny-sheriffs-sue-fair-local-cops-local-crimes-act-287g-ban]].
