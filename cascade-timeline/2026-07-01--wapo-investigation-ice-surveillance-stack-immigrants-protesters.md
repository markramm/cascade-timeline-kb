A Washington Post interactive investigation documents ICE deploying biometric field scanners (NEC Mobile Fortify, used 100,000+ times per an Illinois lawsuit), Clearview AI facial recognition, cell-site simulators, commercial data-broker feeds, Palantir-integrated dossier systems, and drones against both undocumented immigrants and US-citizen protesters. ICE leadership has explicitly asserted authority to monitor anti-ICE protest networks. The piece is the first comprehensive documentation of tools originally justified for immigration enforcement migrating into domestic political surveillance — a convergence the corpus has previously tracked only through individual contracts.

This investigation is corroborated and extended by the later 404 Media leaked-document reporting confirming agency-wide access to the same tool inventory ([[2026-07-28--leaked-document-ice-cbp-surveillance-toolkit-clearview-vigilant-whooster]]), and sits alongside the Clearview contract ([[2026-06-20--ice-largest-clearview-ai-contract-dhs-deletes-oversight-policy]]) and Mobile Fortify local-police rollout ([[2026-06-18--dhs-mobile-fortify-facial-recognition-1300-local-police]]) stories already in the timeline.
