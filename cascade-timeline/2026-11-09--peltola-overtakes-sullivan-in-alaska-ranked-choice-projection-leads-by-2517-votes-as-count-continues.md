In an updated vote total published Monday by the Alaska Division of Elections, Democratic U.S. Senate candidate Mary Peltola led incumbent Republican U.S. Sen. Dan Sullivan by 2,517 votes out of 219,342 cast as of 4 p.m. The Alaska Beacon reports that its preliminary calculations had shown Sullivan leading once the state's ranked-choice runoff was counted, but that Peltola gained enough first-choice votes on Monday to outweigh his runoff advantage. She held 48.5% of first-choice votes.

The count was still moving. According to the Beacon, tens of thousands of absentee and early ballots were expected to be added on Tuesday, after officials cross-checked Election Day poll registers. The runoff projection is the Beacon's own calculation, not an official result.
