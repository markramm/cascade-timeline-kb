# ORR announces a $150M single-source award to Burke Law Group

On **2026-08-06**, at **91 FR 50848**, ORR published its intent to award a **single-source
cooperative agreement of up to $150,000,000** to **Burke Law Group, PLLC** of Houston —
"legal orientation, legal consultation, and **attorney-of-record representation**" for
unaccompanied children before EOIR and USCIS, plus discharge-related continuity planning.
Proposed performance period: **2026-08-15 → 2027-08-14**. Signed by **Dawnisha Helland**,
Assistant Principal Deputy Director of the UAC Bureau. The notice had been filed two days
earlier, **2026-08-04 at 4:15pm**.

## The authority paragraph

The notice states the services are required under 8 U.S.C. 1232(c)(5), 45 CFR 410.1309(a),
**"and the preliminary injunction in Community Legal Services In East Palo Alto, et. al. v.
HHS, et. al., No. 4:25-cv-02847 (N.D. Cal.)."**

ORR is naming, in its own Federal Register notice, the **federal injunction it is under** as
the reason it is buying these services. This is not discretionary program spending; it is
the government's chosen means of complying with a court order and the **anti-trafficking
statute** (TVPRA, 8 U.S.C. 1232(c)(4)–(c)(5)) that compels it to secure counsel for these
children. See [[clsepa-v-hhs-4-25-cv-02847-nd-cal-uac-counsel-injunction]].

## The recipient

**Burke Law Group** was founded in **February 2023** and describes itself as advising
"companies on complex environmental, regulatory, and enforcement matters." Founder
**Marcella Burke** is a Chambers-ranked **environmental** lawyer, **EPA Deputy General
Counsel** (2017–2018) and **Interior Deputy Solicitor for Energy and Natural Resources**
(2018–2020) in the first Trump administration, a **Don Willett** clerk, and a 2023
**Abbott** appointee to the Texas School Land Board. Her listed practice areas on the firm's
own site are administrative, commercial litigation, crisis management, energy, EHS, and
government affairs — **immigration is not among them**.

The roster also includes **Ilya Shapiro** as senior counsel and **Justin Shubow**, a former
Trump appointee to the Commission of Fine Arts. Of **fewer than 30 lawyers**, **two** listed
relevant immigration experience — and NOTUS reported those two profiles were **updated to
add "immigration and asylum" only after media coverage began**.

Against that bench: roughly **1,800 children in ORR shelters** and about **22,000 facing
deportation** while living with sponsors.

## Timing

Acacia Center for Justice's UAC legal-services bridge — funding a network of nearly **100
providers** — had **ended six days earlier, on 2026-07-31**. The notice describes the award
as "complementing existing ORR-funded legal services" and addressing "identified gaps."
The gap was the one ORR had just created.

The next day, **2026-08-07**, ORR signed a separate **non-competed** contract worth up to
**$244,034,658** with **Our Rescue** under FAR 6.302-2 urgency authority. See
[[2026-08-07--our-rescue-tim-ballard-244m-migrant-children-legal-contract]]. Two
single-source awards in four days, up to **$308M combined**, to two entities with no
relevant federal track record, replacing a defunded ~100-provider network.

**Burke withdrew itself from consideration five days later.** See
[[2026-08-17--burke-law-group-withdraws-150m-orr-single-source-uac-legal-award]].
