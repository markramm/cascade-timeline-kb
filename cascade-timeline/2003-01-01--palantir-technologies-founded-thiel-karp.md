Palantir was co-founded in 2003 by Peter Thiel, CNBC reported when the company filed to go public in 2020. The company's own filing, listed in the sources, gives the year and no month, so the date carries year precision.

Forbes reported in 2013 of Palantir's early team: "Initially they were bankrolled entirely by Thiel". Forbes quoted Thiel as having personally invested $40 million in Palantir by then. The CIA's venture arm is at [[2005-01-01--in-q-tel-cia-invests-palantir]]. Congress's bar on Total Information Awareness funding is at [[2003-09-24--congress-defunds-total-information-awareness-program]]; the filing dates the founding to a year only, so this entry does not place the founding before or after that vote.
