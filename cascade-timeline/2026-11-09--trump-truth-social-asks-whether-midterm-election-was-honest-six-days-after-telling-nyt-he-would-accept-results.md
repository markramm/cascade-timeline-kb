On Tuesday, Trump told The New York Times, "I am absolutely willing to accept those results," according to AFP. On Monday morning, he posted on Truth Social, "Does anybody really believe that this" and then the word Election in quotation marks, "was honest," according to The National News Desk. The post said early voting dumps "were so large that it was impossible to win, no matter how many people went to vote." It ended, "It’s all about the machines, which are far more important than the voter!"

According to Al Jazeera, he provided no evidence of election fraud or wrongdoing. Schumer responded that the president's "return to election denialism was inevitable." Democracy Docket described the early-voting remark as a reference to Senate races where Democrats won on the early vote.
