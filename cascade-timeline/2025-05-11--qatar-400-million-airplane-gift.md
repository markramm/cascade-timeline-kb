On May 11, 2025, NBC News reported that the Trump administration was preparing to accept a superluxury Boeing 747-8 jumbo jet from the royal family of Qatar as a gift, to be used by President Donald Trump as the new Air Force One until shortly before he leaves office, according to four sources. Two of the sources said ownership of the plane would be transferred to the Trump presidential library foundation once the president ends his second term. A White House official told NBC that the gift "will not be presented nor accepted this week while Trump is in Qatar," and Qatar's media attache said in a statement that the possible transfer was under review and "no decision has been made." Later, NPR reported that the United States had officially accepted the plane as a gift from the Qatari government, according to an Air Force spokesperson.

FactCheck.org put the plane's value at about $400 million if it were new, according to the New York Times; Rep. Jamie Raskin called it a "$300 million gift." Attorney General Pam Bondi served as a paid lobbyist to Qatar in 2019 and 2020 and, FactCheck.org wrote, had not publicly explained the legal path for accepting the gift. The Times reported that a person familiar with the matter said Bondi had personally signed a Justice Department memo blessing the plan as lawful, though it had been drafted and cleared by lawyers in the Office of Legal Counsel.
