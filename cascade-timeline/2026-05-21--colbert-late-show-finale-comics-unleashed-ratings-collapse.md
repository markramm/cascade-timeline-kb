**CBS cancelled The Late Show with Stephen Colbert in July 2025**, stating the decision was "purely financial" — the network characterized the timeslot as losing roughly **$40 million annually**. The show's **final episode aired Thursday, May 21, 2026**: an extended broadcast drawing **6.74 million viewers** (per TheWrap) — reported elsewhere as up to 9.77 million on an expanded Live+7 measurement window — making it the program's most-watched weeknight episode on record.

**CBS restructured the timeslot as a "time buy" arrangement with Byron Allen's Allen Media Group**, replacing the in-house production model. The network characterized this as flipping a "$40 million annual loss" into "$15 million in profit" — a claimed $55 million swing. Allen's replacement program, **"Comics Unleashed,"** debuted Friday, May 22, 2026 in the 11:35pm slot.

**The replacement's ratings collapsed relative to the finale it followed.** Reported declines range from **59% to 87%** depending on the outlet, comparison baseline (finale night vs. season average), and metric (total viewers vs. 18-49 demo) — TheWrap's headline figure is an 87% decline versus the Colbert finale specifically; TVInsider reports total-viewer figures around 878,000-995,000 for the Comics Unleashed debut against a Colbert 2026 Q1 season average of ~2.15-2.69 million. This entry does not adjudicate between the differing outlet figures beyond noting the range and the metric ambiguity; the directional finding — a large ratings collapse from Colbert-era levels — is consistent across all sources.

**Structural significance.** This is a distinct completion-event within the same CBS/Paramount-Skydance capture arc documented elsewhere in this KB (Black Thursday, Pelley firing, Weiss installation) — not a firing-for-content case like the 60 Minutes correspondents, but a cost-driven cancellation that nonetheless removed one of the most prominent Trump-critical voices in national broadcast television, replaced by an entertainment program with a fraction of the audience and none of the political commentary. The CBS-stated financial rationale is not independently audited in this entry; it is reported as the network's own claimed justification.
