OPR's executive summary wrote that the negotiations "culminated in the signing of the NPA on September 24, 2007". OPR wrote: "Although Acosta did not sign the NPA, he participated in its drafting and approved it, with knowledge of its terms".

OPR wrote: "The NPA required Epstein to make a binding recommendation that the state court sentence him to serve 18 months in the county jail followed by 12 months of community control". Epstein "actually served less than 13 months of incarceration". He pled guilty in state court on June 30, 2008.

OPR concluded that "Acosta’s decision to resolve the federal investigation through the NPA constitutes poor judgment" and did not find that it "resulted from improper factors". OPR wrote: "Victims were not informed of, or consulted about, a potential state resolution or the NPA prior to its signing". OPR concluded that Acosta "exercised poor judgment when he failed to make certain that the state intended to and would notify victims identified through the federal investigation about the state plea hearing".

## Correction

The earlier title and date placed the agreement's approval on June 30, 2008. That date is the day of the state pleas; the agreement was signed on September 24, 2007. The earlier text's other statements are in the repository history and were not re-read in this pass.
