The Department of Justice said it filed lawsuits on Wednesday against Maine, Washington, Oregon and Massachusetts challenging their policies denying confidential license plates to federal agents, which it called unconstitutional. The Department said each state refused to rescind its policy in response to a letter from the Department. In its press release, issued the next day, the Department said that, as alleged in the complaint, the policies threaten the operational effectiveness and safety of federal agents who have faced a wave of targeted harassment. The Department's statements are its own account of its case; no court ruling is recorded here.
