Newsweek reported, in a story published the same day (publication date), that Stephen Miller, whom it describes as an adviser to President Trump, said on Fox & Friends that an alternate slate of electors in the contested states is going to vote, with the results to go to Congress. Miller's stated purpose was to keep Trump's legal options open: if Trump won the court cases, the alternate slate could be directed to be certified.

Context: the remark is a contemporaneous, on-air statement of the alternate-electors plan by a person who now holds a senior White House role, which is why it is a row in the series on 2020 acts and 2026 offices.
