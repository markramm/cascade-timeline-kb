Scale AI announced that the Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAO) had expanded its enterprise agreement with the company, "increasing the total potential value from $100 million to $500 million." The instrument is a Production Other Transaction Authority (OTA) agreement, which Scale described at its award as a "five-year, $100 million ceiling enterprise agreement." The $500 million is a ceiling, Scale's "total potential value," and not an amount obligated or paid.

Scale says "Demand across the Department exceeded the original ceiling." Under the OTA a Department of War component can add its own Project Agreement "without requiring a new competitive solicitation." Washington Technology adds that Meta Platforms holds a 49% non-voting stake in the company.

## What the AI strategy memorandum says

The memorandum the earlier entry cited is the Secretary of War's "Artificial Intelligence Strategy for the Department of War." Its sentence on blockers reads: "We must eliminate blockers to data sharing, Authorizations to Operate (ATOs), test and evaluation and certification, contracting, hiring and talent management, and other policies that inhibit rapid experimentation and fielding." It creates a monthly Board "with authority to waive non-statutory requirements." The text of the memorandum contains no reference to privacy impact assessments.

## Correction (2026-11-17)

An earlier version of this entry quoted the memorandum as calling barriers to data sharing, authority to operate, test and evaluation and contracting operational risks, and described it as framing privacy impact assessment requirements as obstacles. The memorandum's text carries neither. The lines saying that OTA vehicles are exempt from privacy impact assessments, and that none had been filed, are removed. The earlier text also called the agreement no-bid and gave a CDAO announcement a day after Scale's own; the entry now describes the ceiling as a ceiling and rests the announcement on Scale's page. The sections on the officials around the contract, the Defense Llama assessment and the contract table are removed from this entry; the previous version is in the repository history.
