GEO Group announced the completed sale of its 1,280-bed Adelanto West ICE Processing Center, its 660-bed Adelanto East ICE Processing Center and its 704-bed Desert View Annex in Adelanto, California to the United States, through the Department of Homeland Security, for an aggregate gross sales price of $950 million. After taxes and fees, GEO anticipates receiving approximately $705 million in net proceeds.

GEO expects to continue providing support services under its existing ICE contract for the facilities, with a full term effective through December 19, 2034, inclusive of a five-year option period. The company says it remains engaged in an active process for the sale of multiple other company-owned facilities to ICE, subject to mutual agreement on price and GEO's continued management of those facilities under long-term support services contracts.
