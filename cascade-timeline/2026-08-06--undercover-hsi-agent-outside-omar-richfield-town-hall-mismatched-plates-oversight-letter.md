On the evening of Thursday, August 6, 2026, an undercover HSI agent was stationed outside Rep. Ilhan Omar's monthly town hall at the Richfield Community Center, in a vehicle with mismatched license plates. The agent claimed to have been conducting an undercover criminal operation with no prior coordination with local law enforcement, and Richfield police reported that they had received no advance communication of an undercover operation from HSI.

Omar sent HSI an oversight letter. She wrote that at worst the episode "emphasizes many Americans’ growing concern that the Trump administration uses ICE to surveil political opponents and may interfere with our democracy," and asked HSI to share details of any meetings or events she had hosted as a Member of Congress that HSI agents may have been assigned to observe.
