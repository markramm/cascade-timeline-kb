DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin's September 29, 2026 letter to Rep. Timothy M. Kennedy says ICE "plans to continue construction of a new detention facility at the Buffalo Service Processing Center." Asked what funds are being used and what the facility will cost, the response says funding from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act will be used and that the estimated cost of standing up and operating the facility in the first year is $8,779,275. Asked whether construction on the 100-bed facility has begun, it answers "No."

The letter encloses an ICE contract-approval memo dated June 15, 2026. The memo gives a total contract action value of $16,703,366.80, described as a two-year cost that includes contingencies; lists "Sole Source" as the competition status; names the vendor as Akima Global Services; and cites contract number 70CDCR25D00000011. It describes a six-month base period and three six-month operation periods, 24 months of support in all. The letter names Akima Infrastructure Protection as the contractor that staffs the Buffalo (Batavia) SPC, and USAspending lists AKIMA INFRASTRUCTURE PROTECTION LLC as the recipient of that contract number.

The letter says ICE currently plans to house female detainees in the temporary hard-sided facility, with men possible if the needs of the service dictate, and gives 653 detainees at the SPC as of July 8. It says twenty-eight percent of current detainees have criminal records. Investigative Post reported that Mullin's staffing list included 32 positions, 17 of them nurses, and that ICE has arrested nearly 6,300 migrants across upstate New York, with the latest data showing 75 percent of those arrested have no criminal history. Investigative Post also reported that the soft-sided facility is meant to house up to 100 migrant women.
