On 11/06/25 U.S. District Judge Sara Ellis issued a preliminary injunction limiting federal immigration agents' use of force during Operation Midway Blitz in the Chicago area, in *Chicago Headline Club v. Noem*, No. 1:25-cv-12173 (N.D. Ill.). She had held a hearing on November 5 and 6, 2025, and orally issued her ruling with findings. The written Preliminary Injunction Order (ECF 250, 8 pages) was filed the same day. A 16-page Opinion and Order (ECF 252), also filed the same day, certifies a class. The 233-page Opinion and Order with the findings was filed 11/20/25 (ECF 281).

## What the court found about Bovino

From the bench, Judge Ellis said Border Patrol Commander Gregory Bovino "admitted that he lied" about a rock hitting him before he deployed tear gas in Little Village (CNN's report quotes her as saying "Defendant Bovino admitted that he lied"). The written opinion of 11/20/25 finds that "the Court specifically finds his testimony not credible," that Bovino was "either providing 'cute' responses to Plaintiffs' counsel's questions or outright lying," and that "Bovino admitted in his deposition that he lied multiple times about the events that occurred in Little Village."

On the rock, the opinion records the sequence: Bovino and DHS had represented that a rock hit Bovino in the helmet before he threw tear gas; on the first day of his deposition he admitted he was not hit with a rock until after he had deployed tear gas; and at the final session on November 4, 2025 he admitted he was again "mistaken" and that no rock was thrown at him before the first canister.

On the tackle, the sources differ on place. The oral ruling, as WTTW reports it, was that Bovino lied about his conduct outside the Broadview ICE facility, where video shows that he "obviously attacks and tackles" a man, and that, in the judge's words, he "says that he never used force." The written opinion says that in another video shown to Bovino "he obviously tackles Scott Blackburn, one of Plaintiffs' declarants," and that Bovino denied using force against Blackburn and said force was used against him. The opinion also narrates Blackburn's account of an arrest on October 3 on Harvard Street, where the opinion elsewhere places the Broadview facility: Bovino grabbed Blackburn by his right arm and, with another agent, took him down. CBS Chicago's report has it in Brighton Park on Oct. 4. The opinion's own sentence does not give a date or place for the video, so linking it to the October 3 account is our reading of the two.

None of ECF 250, 252 or 281 uses the word perjury, and no court has made a perjury finding; the supported wording is that the judge found Bovino's testimony not credible and that he lied in his deposition.

## The ruling and the injunction

Ellis said, "I find the defendant's evidence simply not credible," and, of the force she had seen, "I would find the use of force shocks the conscience." On the government's argument that the injunction would do harm, she said, "It is difficult to conceive how an injunction requiring the government to comply with the Constitution could possibly be harmful."

ECF 250 enjoins federal agents in the district, among other things, from using riot control weapons on a class member unless objectively necessary to stop an immediate threat of physical harm to another person, and from using any riot control weapon without first giving at least two separate warnings. It enjoins dispersing, arresting, threatening or using force against a person agents know or reasonably should know is a Journalist, with stated exceptions. It requires agents' unique identifiers to be conspicuously displayed in two separate places, and requires agents equipped with body-worn cameras to activate them when engaged in enforcement activity.

## Response and what followed

A DHS spokesperson called the order "an extreme act by an activist judge that risks the lives and livelihoods of law enforcement officers" and DHS vowed to appeal. The Seventh Circuit's final judgment, filed 04/27/26 in No. 25-3023 (ECF 364), vacated the preliminary injunction and dismissed the appeal on the government's motion; see [[2026-03-05--seventh-circuit-vacates-ellis-injunction-chicago-headline-club]]. See also [[2025-10-23--bovino-little-village-tear-gas-tro-violation]] and [[2026-03-02--cbp-opr-compelled-declaration-bovino-ellis-credibility-findings]].
