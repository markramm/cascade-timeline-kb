At a midterm rally in Clarksville, Tennessee, Trump, speaking of Rep. Ilhan Omar, said: "She comes from Somalia... what they've done to Minnesota, these people from Somalia, what they've done to Minnesota should never be allowed. And let's see what happens. JD Vance and our people are looking at it very strongly." The next sentence moves to a Tennessee House candidate.

Binding note: The Guardian quotes "Let's see what happens," and that Vance "and our people are looking at it very strongly." The words match. In context the sentence follows remarks about Somalians and Minnesota generally, so "it" is not clearly an Omar investigation as opposed to the broader subject of Somali-Americans in Minnesota; the Guardian reads it as the former. This entry records the words and their placement, not which reading is right.
