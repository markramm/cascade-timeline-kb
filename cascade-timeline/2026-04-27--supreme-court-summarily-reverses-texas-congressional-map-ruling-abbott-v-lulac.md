On Monday, April 27, 2026, the Supreme Court disposed of the appeal in No. 25-845, Abbott, Governor of Texas, et al. v. LULAC, et al., by summary disposition. The Court's order list files the case under "APPEAL -- SUMMARY DISPOSITION."

The order reads: "For the reasons set forth in Abbott v. League of United Latin American Citizens, 607 U. S. ___ (2025), we reverse the District Court's judgment." The earlier opinion the order cites is the one in the same case that accompanied the stay of the district court's order; see [[2025-12-04--supreme-court-reinstates-texas-mid-decade-redistricting-map]].

Justices Sotomayor, Kagan and Jackson dissent "from the Court's summary reversal." The order list names the dissenters and gives no vote count, and the order carries no opinion of its own.

The Court's docket for No. 25-845 records that judgment issued on May 29 2026.
