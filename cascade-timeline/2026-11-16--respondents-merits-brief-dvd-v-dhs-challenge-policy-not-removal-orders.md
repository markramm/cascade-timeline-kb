The respondent class in Department of Homeland Security v. D.V.D. filed its merits brief at the Supreme Court, dated November 16, 2026. The brief states: "Respondents do not challenge their removal orders or the Executive’s authority to remove them to a third country." It aims at the policy memorandum and implementing guidance DHS issued for third-country removals.

The brief describes the policy as calling for "removal within 6 to 24 hours of notice of the third country" unless the person can immediately show they meet the burden for withholding or Convention Against Torture protection. The United States responded as follows when asked about removal to a third country at argument in Riley v. Bondi: "We would have to give the person notice of the third country and give them the opportunity to raise a reasonable fear of torture or persecution in that third country."

The Government's reply is due Tuesday, December 1, 2026, and argument is set for Wednesday, December 9, 2026.
