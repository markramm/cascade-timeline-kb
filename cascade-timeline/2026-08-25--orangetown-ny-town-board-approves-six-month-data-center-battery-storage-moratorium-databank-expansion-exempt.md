The Orangetown, New York Town Board approved a six-month moratorium on applications for new data centers and battery energy storage systems on Aug 25, 2026, per a Hudson Valley television report. The town supervisor said it does not apply to DataBank's proposed expansion, which is already in review with a full environmental impact study that could take six months to a year.
