The Justice Department unsealed an eight-count superseding indictment against three men. The Justice Department release says the indictment charges them with terrorism-related offenses in support of Kata'ib Hizballah and Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. The release says it includes a September 2026 plot at RAF Fairford in the United Kingdom, where American forces were stationed.

The release says al-Hassani orchestrated the Fairford plot. It says al-Hassani and Mohajeri were in Iran at the time. It says Al-Saadi was jailed in the Southern District of New York awaiting trial and tried to coordinate with them by coded messages passed through third parties.

According to the release, three vans were sent toward the base on Sept. 26, and at least two met a security roadblock. The release says UK authorities arrested the occupants on Sept. 27 and recovered three white vans with fuel-delivery logos. It says some vans were later found to contain barrels of fuel. The release adds that custom decals made the vans look like those of a fuel delivery business and a swimming pool supply business.

The indictment counts include conspiring to provide material support to Kata'ib Hizballah and to the IRGC, and conspiring to bomb a place of public use. These are allegations. The release states that a superseding indictment is merely an allegation.
