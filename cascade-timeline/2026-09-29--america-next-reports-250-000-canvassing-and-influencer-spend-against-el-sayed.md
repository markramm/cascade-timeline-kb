America Next Inc, a Baton Rouge, Louisiana filer that is not a registered committee, reported a $250,000 independent expenditure against Abdul El-Sayed in the Michigan Senate general. It filed on FEC Form 5 (the report for non-committee filers) and itemized the payment to Breakthrough LLC for "canvassing and influencer marketing."

- **Sept 29 (expenditure date):** $250,000 paid.
- **Oct 1 (FEC receipt date):** the Form 5 reached the Commission.

The Form 5 gives the expenditure date, not a dissemination date, so when the canvassing or influencer posts ran is not stated in the filing.
