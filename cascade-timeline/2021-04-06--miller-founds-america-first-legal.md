Stephen Miller launched America First Legal Foundation (AFL) on April 6, 2021, creating a conservative legal organization designed to serve as a right-wing counterweight to the ACLU and SPLC. Miller consulted Mark Meadows' Conservative Partnership Institute in setting up AFL, and Wikipedia lists former Trump administration officials including Gene Hamilton as vice president and former Acting Attorney General Matthew Whitaker as executive director. The organization positions itself as combating "anti-white bigotry" and opposing Biden administration policies on immigration, LGBTQ rights, and racial equity programs. AFL has taken on voting rights protections, diversity initiatives in corporations and universities, transgender rights, and immigration policies. AFL's litigation strategy targets sanctuary cities, challenges affirmative action programs, opposes COVID-19 public health measures in immigrant detention, and challenges protections for marginalized communities.
