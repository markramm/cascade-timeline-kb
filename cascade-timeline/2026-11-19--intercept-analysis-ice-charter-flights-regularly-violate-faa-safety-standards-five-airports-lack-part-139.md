The Intercept published an analysis on November 19 arguing that ICE flights regularly violate FAA safety standards. The authors write that there have been almost 20,000 individual flights chartered by ICE since Trump's second inauguration, and that they violate a federal standard that says a full plane must be evacuated in 90 seconds.

The piece also says at least five airports in the ICE flight network lack FAA Part 139 safety certification. See the related entries on ICE flights.
