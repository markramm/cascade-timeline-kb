A federal grand jury in Washington on Tuesday returned a "no true bill" on the Justice Department's bid to charge Cassidy Hutchinson with lying to Congress, Drop Site reports. It says the case was led by Harmeet Dhillon, head of the civil rights division.

## Update (2026-10-27, grand jury no-bill date, as reported): NYT account rests on people briefed; DOJ lawyer told a magistrate judge of the no-bill

The New York Times reported the no true bill on the day, citing people briefed on the matter, and said some Justice Department prosecutors had been deeply skeptical of the case. CNBC, citing The Washington Post, reported that a Justice Department lawyer told Magistrate Judge Zia Faruqui in open court that the grand jury had rejected a requested indictment, without naming Hutchinson. NBC News reported the refusal does not bar the department from trying to charge her again.
