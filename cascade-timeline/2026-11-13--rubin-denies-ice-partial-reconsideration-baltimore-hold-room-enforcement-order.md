Judge Julie R. Rubin signed a memorandum opinion and order on November 13, 2026 denying the Government's motion for partial reconsideration of the order enforcing the Baltimore Hold Room injunction. The motion asked her to revisit "the health screening and the notice of rights." The court held "no clear error resulting in manifest injustice." It conceded one misstatement about a detainee declarant's screening but called it "immaterial to, and had no bearing on, the court's adjudication," and said the record supported a finding that another declarant was "not screened until four days after his arrival." On the rest, it wrote that the motion "does not present evidence not previously available" and declined to explain its ruling again. The enforcement order at issue was issued September 28, 2026 after the oral ruling (ECF No. 231).

The denial leaves the enforcement findings on screening and notice of rights standing under the injunction entered in [[2026-03-06--rubin-certifies-class-enjoins-ice-baltimore-hold-rooms-dnn-v-liggins]]. The motion did not ask the court to revisit any other subject, and the opinion does not address occupancy.
