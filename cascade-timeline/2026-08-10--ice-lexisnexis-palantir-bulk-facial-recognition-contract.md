On Aug 10, 2026, ICE's Office of Acquisition Management posted a notice on SAM.gov that it intends to award a sole-source interim contract to LexisNexis Risk Solutions. The notice says ICE "provides notice of its intent to solicit and award a sole source interim contract," puts the estimated total contract value at $6.7 million, describes the anticipated performance as a seven-month base period and five one-month option periods, and states that Homeland Security Investigations requires continued access to LexID and Accurint Virtual Crime Center. It also says the notice "is not a request for proposals and is for informational purposes only." Biometric Update, which reported on the notice and an attached July performance work statement (PWS) on Aug 12, 2026, says the notice is not an executed contract or a competitive solicitation. The date is a notice-of-intent posting date, not an award date.

The Palantir and facial-recognition material comes from the PWS, not from the notice text. Biometric Update reports that the PWS requires connections to ICE applications "such as but not limited to" the Palantir platform and "advanced AI-driven facial recognition platforms," but also that the PWS does not identify the receiving Palantir application and that the sole-source notice leaves unclear whether the AI functions are part of the proposed bridge. SAM.gov's attachment list marks the PWS file as deleted on 2026-08-12, so the document could not be read directly for this entry. The widely repeated "82 billion" is LexisNexis's own description of the records behind its LexID product; Biometric Update says feeding data into Palantir is best understood as programmatic access and import, not necessarily transferring all 82 billion records. The original press report, published Aug 10, 2026, said that ICE plans to pay LexisNexis millions more for continued access to data it can feed into a Palantir platform.

Earlier versions of this entry tied the item to Palantir's ELITE system. That link is inference: the PWS "does not identify the receiving Palantir application."

## Update (2026-08-31, award signature date): Nearest award on USAspending: $2,935,308 to LexisNexis Risk Solutions

USAspending records a LexisNexis Risk Solutions award, signed on the date of this update, with a total obligation of $2,935,308. Its description reads "LAW ENFORCEMENT INVESTIGATIVE DATABASE SUBSCRIPTION". This is the nearest award to the notice; the link between them is inference.
