On Saturday night, May 20, 2017, at the California Democratic Party convention, Eric Bauman, the Los Angeles County Democratic chair, beat Kimberly Ellis for state party chair, 1,493 votes to 1,431 in a 62 vote margin per the Los Angeles Blade. AP reported that the result surprised and angered Ellis supporters, many of them "inspired by Bernie Sanders." Ellis did not concede.

## The concern and the party's response

Ellis supporters were "questioning whether all the votes came from credentialed party delegates" (Daily Kos, quoting press reports). Per AP, allies of Ellis "began verifying the count" at party headquarters on Tuesday. The sources read here do not say how the review ended.
