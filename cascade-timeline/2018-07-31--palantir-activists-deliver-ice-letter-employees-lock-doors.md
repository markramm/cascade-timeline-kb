On July 31, 2018, about 50 protesters gathered outside Palantir's Palo Alto headquarters to deliver a letter to CEO Alex Karp asking the company to cancel its ICE contract and calling on Palantir employees to demand the same. BuzzFeed News counted fourteen activist groups as signers of the letter and reported that no Palantir employees joined them. Palo Alto Online described a coalition of 15 groups. When Palantir declined to send anyone out to receive it, the protesters taped the letter to the door.
