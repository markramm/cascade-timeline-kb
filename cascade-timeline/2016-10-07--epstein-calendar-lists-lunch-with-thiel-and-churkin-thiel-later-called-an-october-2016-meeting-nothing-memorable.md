## What the records and reports show

**Scheduled.** An index of the DOJ Epstein release quotes an Epstein calendar page reading "12:30pm LUNCH w/Peter Thiel and Vitaly Churkin" on October 7, 2016 (document EFTA02041031), and quotes a note, cited to EFTA02040190, that a member of Epstein's staff was put "in charge of the important lunch today". Other calendar pages in the same set list both names as "TBD", but the excerpt does not date them, so they are not read here as the October 7 plan.

**One same-day email, not read in full.** The same index lists an email from Lesley Groff to Peter Thiel with the subject "Peter has walked in!!", dated 07 Oct 2016 at 16:41:59. Only the headers and the first words of the body are visible. The subject does not name Churkin or a lunch, and the email is not read here as proof of who attended.

**Reported attendance.** The Independent, reporting on a Wall Street Journal story, writes that Thiel commented on his October 2016 meeting with Epstein and Churkin, saying it featured "nothing memorable". The same article quotes Thiel on a separate point: "I was rather naive, and I didn't think enough about what Epstein's agenda might have been," said in an interview. The Independent does not say where or to whom Thiel spoke, and the "nothing memorable" wording is its paraphrase, not a full sentence from Thiel.

**The reporting's own caveat.** The Independent also writes that Epstein had at least eight scheduled meetings with Churkin and that "it could not be verified if every meeting on his calendar happened". The Daily Beast, reporting the same Wall Street Journal story, writes that Epstein's emails and schedules show he invited Thiel and Barrack to get-togethers with Vitaly Churkin, and that the purpose "remains unclear".

## What each kind of evidence supports

- **Scheduled:** the calendar entry and the staff note, both quoted by an index of the DOJ release.
- **Confirmed by the participants:** no document read in this pass is a confirmation from Thiel or Churkin.
- **Attended:** the one statement that bears on it is Thiel's reported remark about an October 2016 meeting, carried by the Independent. The documents read here do not show attendance on their own; the "Peter has walked in!!" subject line is suggestive and unread.
- **Not found in this pass:** the exhibits that earlier versions of this entry and its research notes cite for Thiel proposing the date, for Epstein inviting Ken Starr to lunch, and for a note that Thiel and Churkin would be coming. The pages read here do not carry them and the DOJ files were not reachable. They are not relied on here.
