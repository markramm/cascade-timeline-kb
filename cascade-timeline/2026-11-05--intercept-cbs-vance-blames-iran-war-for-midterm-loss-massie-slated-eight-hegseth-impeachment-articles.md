"The reason we lost is the war," Vice President JD Vance wrote on X, according to The Intercept. CBS News reports that Democrats have secured at least 54 Senate seats and estimates Democrats will hold 251 to 268 House seats. Asked about Vance's view that Republicans lost control of the House and Senate because of the conflict with Iran, Mr. Trump said it's "largely true" because the war drove up the price of gasoline.

Republican Rep. Thomas Massie of Kentucky, with support from Democrats, is slated to take aim at Hegseth as soon as next week with eight articles of impeachment for high crimes and misdemeanors. Massie told Roll Call just prior to Election Day, "I give it better than a 50 percent chance of passing." "Dead man walking," said one unnamed official quoted by The Intercept. House Speaker Mike Johnson told staffers he would not seek another term as GOP leader.
