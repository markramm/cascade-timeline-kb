The Hennepin County Attorney's Office posted a notice dated 11/2/2026 saying the Department of Justice again asked Hennepin County to approve DOJ staff entering and monitoring polling places, and the county denied the request. The notice says the Minnesota Attorney General's Office has stated plans to observe the DOJ monitoring teams. It expects the monitors to be DOJ staff in plainclothes.

The notice carries no statement from Attorney General Keith Ellison or Secretary of State Steve Simon. No Attorney General announcement was found. Election Day observations are not established here.
