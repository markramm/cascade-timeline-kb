CNN reported that the FBI is investigating an apparent breach of the National Crime Information Center, the nationwide database law enforcement uses to share information on warrants and background checks, according to two people briefed on the investigation. It is the second such breach in recent months, after the September ShinyHunters theft of employee data from the FBIJobs.gov site.

On Thursday, after bureau officials learned of the incident, FBI Director Kash Patel appeared on Fox News to tout the FBI's cybersecurity work: "We are staying ahead of the curve and making up for lost time."
