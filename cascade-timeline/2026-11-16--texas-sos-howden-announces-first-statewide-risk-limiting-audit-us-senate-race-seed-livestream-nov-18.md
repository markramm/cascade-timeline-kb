On November 16, 2026, Texas Secretary of State Robert S. Howden highlighted two statewide audits used to verify the results of the Nov. 3 election. The office says this will be the first year for statewide, risk-limiting audits, and that the race chosen for the audit is the U.S. Senate contest. Its release says the audits use "statistical methods and randomly selected paper ballots" to verify reported outcomes.

The release says the audit's 10-digit seed number will be generated on Wednesday, with the selection broadcast by livestream on Nov. 18 at 2:30 p.m. Central. It also says counties must conduct sample hand counts in counties where electronic voting equipment is used, in the races for U.S. Senate, Chief Justice of the Texas Supreme Court, and one race the county selects. The office adds that similar audits are conducted in 16 other states.

The audited Senate contest is the race covered in [[2026-11-03--ap-calls-texas-senate-race-for-talarico-over-paxton-first-democratic-senate-win-in-texas-since-1988]]. A Texas county party's pre-certification hand-count suit is in [[2026-10-27--potter-county-gop-sues-texas-hand-count-all-ballots-before-certification-ndtx]].

**Not established here:** any audit result, which does not exist yet; every statement above is the Secretary of State's own description of the process.
