The City of Minneapolis published an updated assessment of Operation Metro Surge. It estimates the operation cost the City, residents and businesses nearly $700 million in lost economic activity, and says the City itself spent nearly $10 million on staff payroll, police overtime and operational expenses. The assessment covers December 2025 through April 2026.

Mayor Jacob Frey said, "Throughout Operation Metro Surge, I saw two very different stories unfold in Minneapolis," one of disruption, fear and economic harm, the other of neighbors looking out for one another.

**Scope.** These are the city's own estimates. The release counts costs; it is not a review of MPD's or federal agents' conduct.
