During a gaggle outside the White House on Thursday, a reporter asked how Homan makes sure U.S. citizens and legal immigrants are protected from deportation operations. Homan answered: "Have U.S. citizens ever been shortly detained based on suspicion? I’m sure. I’m sure". He added that "you’ve got reasonable suspicion for a short detention" and that once agents determine the person is not the target, "you are released".

Mediaite's transcription has no "daily", no count and no "routine". On deportation he said "we don’t deport U.S. citizens". Related: [[2026-10-06--propublica-tally-506-us-citizens-detained-by-immigration-agents-since-january-2025]].
