Pope Leo XIV addressed participants in a dialogue between the Vatican and the scientific world on AI, organized jointly by the Pontifical Academy for Life and the Future of Life Institute. He applied his encyclical [[2026-05-25--pope-leo-xiv-magnifica-humanitas-encyclical-disarm-ai-concentration-tech-power]] to current systems.

"When a person is placed on a target list or entered into a border control database, there is a danger that he or she will be reduced to a mere data point," he said. Decisions on life, freedom and force, he said, "must always remain subject to effective, informed and responsible human oversight." He said artificial intelligence must be "disarmed," and encouraged "the States meeting in Geneva in November to take concrete steps toward banning lethal autonomous weapons."

He said "machines are tools, not persons," and that proposals for legal personhood risk "obscuring human responsibility." He said what is currently needed includes "effective international institutions."
