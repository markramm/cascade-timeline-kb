ICE announced on November 20, 2026 that on Nov. 17 a Nigerian national self-surrendered to Homeland Security Investigations and pleaded not guilty to a charge of voting by aliens. ICE says the case stems from an HSI Houston-FBI investigation into alleged noncitizen voting in the 2024 primary and general elections. The same release says ICE has arrested hundreds of aliens alleged to have voted in federal and state elections, and that some of those accused have already been charged and found guilty while others are awaiting legal proceedings.

Only ICE's release was read, not the court record.
