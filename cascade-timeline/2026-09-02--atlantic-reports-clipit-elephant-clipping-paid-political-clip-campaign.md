On 2026-09-02 (publication date) The Atlantic's Ali Breland published a report on "Elephant Clipping", a campaign promoted by a man who goes by Serviuos and run by his company, ClipIt, a marketplace that pays "clippers" to cut and post short videos. As quoted in an excerpt thread, "Clippers are paid a proportionate share of the campaign's monthly budget of $300,000" and "Every 1 million views yields a $300 payout." The article also names Enclave & Key, a Las Vegas marketing company, whose files appear to have been uploaded by three of its employees; its snippet says it is "not specified what role, if any" the firm plays.

Verification state: the Atlantic page is behind a Cloudflare challenge and was not read in full; quoted text comes from a forum excerpt (tier 3). Bonuses, exclusion lists and the "do not tell viewers how to vote" rule reported by a later Substack were not confirmed.
