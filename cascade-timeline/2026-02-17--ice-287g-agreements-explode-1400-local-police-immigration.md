ICE's own 287(g) page, in a Wayback copy stamped as of February 17, 2026 5:10 pm, said ICE had signed 1,427 Memorandums of Agreement for 287(g) programs covering 40 states. NPR reported the same day that, as of Feb. 13, ICE reported 1,412 active agreements across 40 states and territories, more than 1,130 of them signed in 2025 alone, and that in 2019, during Trump's first term, just 45 of these agreements were signed.

The counts measure different things and are not interchangeable. ICE's page counts signed agreements and NPR's 1,412 counts active ones, each as of its own date. NBC News reported an analysis of ICE data by FWD.US that counted agencies rather than agreements: 1,168 agencies as of Jan. 26, up from 135 during the Biden administration and 150 at the end of Trump's first term.

## Correction (2026-11-17)

This entry's earlier headline gave a rounded count and credited NPR and NBC News for its counts. The NPR link it carried matched no article and has been replaced with the story's address; NBC's figure counts agencies as of Jan. 26, not agreements.
