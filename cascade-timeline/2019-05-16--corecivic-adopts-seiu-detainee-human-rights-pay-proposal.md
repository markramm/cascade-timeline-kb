CoreCivic held its 2019 annual meeting on May 16, 2019. Its current report states that the company announced at the meeting that it "has decided to adopt the stockholder proposal submitted by SEIU", a proposal "to incorporate respect for inmate and detainee human rights into the Company’s incentive compensation arrangements for senior executives".

The SEC staff response dated March 15, 2019 had declined CoreCivic's request to exclude the proposal: "We are unable to concur in your view that the Company may exclude the Proposal".
