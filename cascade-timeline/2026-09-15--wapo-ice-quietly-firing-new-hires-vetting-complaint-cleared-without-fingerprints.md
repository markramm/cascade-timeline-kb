ICE has begun quietly firing some of the recruits it hired during the 2025 surge, according to three former DHS officials who spoke to the [Washington Post](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/09/15/ice-is-quietly-firing-new-hires-for-one-recruit-it-was-devastating/) on condition of anonymity.

Two former ICE deportation officers in Philadelphia told the Post they were hired without a job interview. A unit chief's August 2025 whistleblower complaint to the DHS inspector general, described by the Post, said applicants were cleared for duty "without fingerprints, identity verification, or credit checks."
