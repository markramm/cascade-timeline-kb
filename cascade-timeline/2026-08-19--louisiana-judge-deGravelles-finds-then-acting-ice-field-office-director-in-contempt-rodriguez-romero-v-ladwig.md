Judge John deGravelles of the Middle District of Louisiana (Baton Rouge) held a continued show cause hearing on August 19, 2026 in a habeas case, *Rodriguez Romero v. Ladwig*, No. 3:25-cv-01106-JWD-EWD. The court's own order of August 6 says the hearing was the one "during which the Court will issue its reasons for judgment on civil contempt." The finding itself is reported, not read: the written order is not in RECAP, and this entry rests for the finding on The Lens, which quotes the order: "The Court finds Respondents’ agent-then-Acting Field Office Director" in contempt "for violating the Court’s Orders." The Lens identifies the official as the then-acting ICE field office director in Baltimore and reports the court's remedy as the petitioner's attorney's fees and $180 for the time he lost in detention. The Lens also reports that counsel for ICE "acknowledged that ICE had violated those orders."

What the court's own orders show, read from RECAP: on July 28, 2026 the court granted the petitioner's second motion to enforce, recording that "Respondents’ counsel conceded that the Court’s orders have been violated," and ordered the respondents to release him and "not re-detain him without due process." The same order set a show cause hearing for August 5 as to "those specific decisionmakers who are responsible for this redetention," and required the person or persons who ordered the redetention to be available to testify. The August 6 order moved the ruling on civil contempt to August 19. On the finding itself the court is quoted as warning: "Should there be any further violations, additional and more serious sanctions will be issued by this court."

The finding is civil contempt, per the court's own scheduling order, against an official in an official capacity as the respondents' agent. The petitioner is a party named in the court's orders; this entry does not use his name.
