ICE's participating-agencies page counted 522 Task Force Model agencies as of September 25, 2025 (Internet Archive capture), and 1,972 as of October 28, 2026 (live page), within 2,725 Memorandums of Agreement. Both dates are ICE page stamps, not signing dates.

FWD.us estimates between 26,000 and 33,000 officers are deputized under the model, in a report published October 28, 2026.
