Chaofeng Ge was found dead from hanging in a shower stall at Moshannon Valley Processing Center on August 5, 2025, documents shared with Documented show, after his requests to be placed with Chinese-speaking detainees were not fulfilled. He had arrived with transfer paperwork showing previous suicide attempts. A psychological autopsy acknowledged that Ge "was socially isolated," and the ICE Office of Professional Responsibility investigation found staff did not perform timely or thorough rounds. A note Ge wrote recorded the response to a request to share a room with a Chinese person: "Not my duty."

Part of the pattern at [[2026-05-27--ap-investigation-ice-detention-suicides-record-fy-pace-corecivic-geo-facilities]]. The documents became public through Documented's reporting in November 2026, so the entry is dated to the death.

Later developments. The family's filed suit found is a Freedom of Information Act action by his brother, Yanfeng Ge, filed in the Southern District of New York in 2025, dismissed voluntarily without prejudice, and refiled in the Eastern District of New York in March 2026. In the Eastern District case ICE reported a third production of approximately 390 pages, said production was substantially complete apart from over 1,000 hours of video, and the parties agreed to confer to narrow it. No wrongful-death complaint appears in CourtListener. ICE has posted its Health Service Corps Mortality Review, released under FOIA, which finds that custody staff did not perform timely and thorough rounds and lists corrective actions including a lower-load-limit shower hook and cutdown tools carried by supervisors.
