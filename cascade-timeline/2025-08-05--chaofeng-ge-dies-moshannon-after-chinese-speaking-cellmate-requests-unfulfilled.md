Chaofeng Ge was found dead from hanging in a shower stall at Moshannon Valley Processing Center on August 5, 2025, documents shared with Documented show, after his requests to be placed with Chinese-speaking detainees were not fulfilled. He had arrived with transfer paperwork showing previous suicide attempts. A psychological autopsy acknowledged that Ge "was socially isolated," and the ICE Office of Professional Responsibility investigation found staff did not perform timely or thorough rounds. A note Ge wrote recorded the response to a request to share a room with a Chinese person: "Not my duty."
