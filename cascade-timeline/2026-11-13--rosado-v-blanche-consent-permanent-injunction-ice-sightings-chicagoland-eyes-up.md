On November 13, 2026, Judge Jorge L. Alonso entered an order, on the parties' joint motion, converting the preliminary injunction of May 5, 2026 into a permanent injunction and dismissing the plaintiffs' complaint with prejudice. This is a consent order, not a merits ruling: the court acted on the parties' joint motion, and its only stated basis is that it found the relief appropriate “for good cause shown.” The caption now names Attorney General Todd Blanche and Secretary of Homeland Security Markwayne Mullin as defendants in their official capacities.

The terms repeat the earlier order: defendants shall take “no actions, formal or informal, to demand or pressure” Facebook over the ICE Sightings – Chicagoland group, or Apple over the Eyes Up application, and may not coerce either company with sanctions for allowing or reinstating them. The order adds a notice duty by the seventh day, to the government's own officers and to Meta and Apple, and a written account of compliance to plaintiffs' counsel. Final judgment was directed.
