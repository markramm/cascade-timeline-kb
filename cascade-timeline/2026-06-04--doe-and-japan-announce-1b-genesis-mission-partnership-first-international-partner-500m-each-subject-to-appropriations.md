The Department of Energy announced a $1 billion strategic partnership with Japan's science and trade ministries on June 4, 2026. DOE calls Japan the first international partner in the Genesis Mission.

According to DOE, the plan is a combined $1 billion, with $500 million from each nation. DOE says the investment is subject to the availability of future appropriations.

DOE says the plan builds on a joint Statement of Intent signed in January 2026. DOE says the joint teams will unite twelve DOE National Laboratories, one DOE Office of Science User Facility and twelve Japanese research institutions.
