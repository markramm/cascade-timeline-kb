The Augusta Commission passed the city's 29-page data center ordinance on second reading Tuesday, according to WGAC. The vote was 6-5, per WRDW.

The ordinance keeps data centers out of residential and agricultural zones. Cooling systems must be closed-loop, and private wells cannot supply cooling, WGAC reports.

WRDW reported that the final draft sets a noise limit of 65 decibels during the day and 60 decibels at night, measured at the property line next door. WRDW reported that the draft sets a buffer of at least 500 feet from homes and farmland.

The QTS facility going up off Gordon Highway near Haynes Station falls outside these rules, WGAC reports. WRDW reported that the project received its zoning approvals before the moratorium on new applications took effect.
