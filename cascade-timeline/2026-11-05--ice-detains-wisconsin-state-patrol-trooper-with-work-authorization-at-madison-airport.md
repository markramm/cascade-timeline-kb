Carel Aaron Van Der Merwe, an asylum-seeker from South Africa who has a valid federal work authorization, was detained by ICE as he waited to board a domestic flight, according to a statement from his attorneys' law office reported by NBC News. ICE arrested him during a targeted enforcement operation at the Dane County Regional Airport in Madison, Wisconsin, on Thursday, DHS said; WMTV reports DHS confirmed the Nov. 5 arrest. His attorneys said he tried to explain that he had a valid work permit and an asylum application, but the ICE officers "said they did not care, placed him in handcuffs and shackles, and transported him to immigration detention." He has been a Wisconsin state patrol trooper for nearly a year, his attorneys said.

DHS responded: "To be clear: work authorization or a pending application do not confer legal status in the U.S." Its spokesperson said he entered the country in March 2019 with legal permission to stay until September 2019 and overstayed. His attorneys said he came to the country legally as a child and is included on his father's asylum application. WMTV later reported his attorney said he has been released from federal custody; he will remain under federal supervision, and his immigration proceedings will continue.
