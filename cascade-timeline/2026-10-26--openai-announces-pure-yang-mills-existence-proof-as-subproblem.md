OpenAI posted on October 26, 2026 that it is announcing three new discoveries in mathematics, computer science, and physics. OpenAI says its physics work includes, as a subproblem, a proof of the existence of pure Yang-Mills theory. OpenAI says that subproblem is part of the Quantum Yang-Mills Theory Clay Mathematics Institute Millennium Prize problem.

A statement by the shepherding committee for the result, dated October 2026, says confinement and the mass gap remain very important open challenges. The committee says it plans to release the full 1147-page proofs with its assessment once it is satisfied with their correctness.
