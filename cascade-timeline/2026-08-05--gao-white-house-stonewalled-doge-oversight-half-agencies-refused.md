A GAO report (GAO-26-108403) released August 5, 2026 found the White House and federal agencies refused to fully cooperate with oversight of DOGE operations: fewer than half of agencies responded to GAO's personnel-document requests, ten missed the response deadline entirely, and six claimed to have no matching records. The Executive Office of the President stonewalled every request for DOGE financial-disclosure and ethics-training records — a complete accountability blackout at the top of the initiative. GAO further found the U.S. DOGE Service can legally continue operating past DOGE's formal July 4 shutdown, meaning the structural changes DOGE made to the federal workforce have no accountability mechanism attached even in retrospect.

This completes a zero-accountability stack this corpus has tracked forming piece by piece: the elimination of independent agency-head removal protections in Humphrey's Executor ([[2026-06-29--scotus-trump-v-slaughter-overturns-humphreys-executor]]), the DOGE workforce cuts themselves running to hundreds of thousands of eliminated positions with no public personnel record ([[2026-03-04--doge-federal-workforce-386000-jobs-eliminated-opm-data]]), and now GAO's own inability to determine who worked for DOGE or what ethics obligations they were subject to. The absence of a penalty mechanism for agencies that simply do not respond to a GAO document request is itself the structural finding.
