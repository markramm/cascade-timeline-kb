Prince William County Registrar Eric Olsen declined, in an Oct. 26 email to the Department of Justice, to let federal monitors enter the county's polling places, citing the Virginia code that lets a general registrar approve or refuse requests for neutral observers, the Prince William Times reported. The email, as the paper quotes it, reads: "For this election, I am not authorizing any additional observers within the polling places in Prince William County, including Department of Justice observers."

DOJ replied that it would monitor from outside, "regardless of whether you are letting us into the polling places," and told the paper that "State or local approval is not required for Department monitoring." Olsen said monitors who come must stay outside a 40-foot boundary from the entrance.
