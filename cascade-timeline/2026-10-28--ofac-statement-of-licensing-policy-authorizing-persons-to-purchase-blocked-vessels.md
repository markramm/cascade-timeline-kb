OFAC's Russia-sanctions page lists a Statement of Licensing Policy on Authorizing Persons to Purchase Blocked Vessels dated October 28, 2026. PortNews reports it is licensing-policy guidance rather than a general licence, so buyers would apply for specific licenses. PortNews reads it as formalising an approval route outlined earlier by Energy Secretary Chris Wright.

The statement's text is unread: the document link on OFAC's page returned an error to curl. Eligibility criteria, covered programs, and whether vessels are delisted or only licensed for sale are unknown. OFAC's own page does not mention Wright; the link is PortNews's reading.
