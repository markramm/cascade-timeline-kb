City commissioners in Alachua voted 5-0 on the second reading of the city's one-year data center moratorium (WCJB, Oct. 27). The earlier first reading passed 4 to 1 (WCJB).

Correction to the dispatch wording: this is the City of Alachua commission, not the Alachua County Commission.

Stage: second reading. Agenda, minutes and ordinance text were not retrieved.

Background: the planning director said no data center project had been proposed in the city and that a property marketed as a possible site lies in unincorporated county land (Alachua County Today).
