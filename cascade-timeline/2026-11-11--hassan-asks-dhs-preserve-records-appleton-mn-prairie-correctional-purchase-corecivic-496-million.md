Sen. Maggie Hassan, D-N.H., a member of the Senate Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, wrote in a letter to Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin on Wednesday, according to Minnesota Reformer. She asked the Trump administration to preserve all records related to the federal government’s recent purchase of the Prairie Correctional Facility in Appleton.

Minnesota Reformer reports that CoreCivic, a private prison company, was paid $496 million for the 1,600-bed facility after offering it to the state of Minnesota in May 2025 for $75 million. The Reformer also says the Appleton immigrant detention center accepted its first detainees this week.
