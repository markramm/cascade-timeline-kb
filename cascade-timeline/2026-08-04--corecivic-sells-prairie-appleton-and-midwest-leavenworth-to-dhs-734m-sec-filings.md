On August 4, 2026, CoreCivic sold two facilities to the United States, through the Department of Homeland Security, in transactions completed the day the agreements were signed. CoreCivic's Form 8-K gives the price of the 1,600-bed Prairie Correctional Facility in Appleton, Minnesota as approximately $495.6 million, subject to adjustments, and the 1,033-bed Midwest Regional Reception Center in Leavenworth, Kansas as approximately $238.4 million. The company's quarterly report puts the aggregate at $734.0 million. The 8-K says the Prairie facility has been idle since 2010.

The same quarterly report lists Prairie's net carrying value at June 30, 2026 as $26,683 thousand (about $26.7 million), on the idle-facilities table. A carrying value is an accounting figure, not an appraisal or a market price, and neither filing says how the $495.6 million was set. CoreCivic expects an aggregate gain of about $600.0 million from the two sales after transaction costs.

The sale sits beside a separate ICE management contract for the same building. Per the 10-Q, CoreCivic was awarded it on August 3, 2026, expects about $75.0 million in annual revenue once the facility is fully activated, and the Prairie management contract expires in August 2031. That is revenue to CoreCivic as operator; it is a different figure from the $75M price CoreCivic reportedly offered the state in 2025.

This entry binds the purchase price to CoreCivic's own filings. The earlier $75M offer to Minnesota, the $495,639,588 figure and the question of who set the price are reported by KARE 11 and carried in the contract entry's October 29 update ([[2026-08-04--corecivic-536m-contract-reopen-appleton-mn-prairie-correctional]]). The structure repeats the July 2 sale of Otay Mesa and California City ([[2026-07-02--dhs-buys-corecivic-otay-mesa-california-city-1-5b-oversight-shield]]).
