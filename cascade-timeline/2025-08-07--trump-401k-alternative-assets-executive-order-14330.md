On August 7, 2025, Trump signed Executive Order 14330, "Democratizing Access to Alternative Assets for 401(k) Investors". The order defines "alternative assets" to include private market investments, real estate, commodities, infrastructure financing, lifetime income strategies and "holdings in actively managed investment vehicles that are investing in digital assets". It directs the Secretary of Labor to reexamine, within 180 days, the Department of Labor's guidance on a fiduciary's duties under ERISA when a plan offers an asset allocation fund that includes alternative assets, and to consider whether to rescind the Department's December 21, 2021 Supplemental Private Equity Statement.

The order directs agencies; it is not itself a rule. Any change to the Labor Department's fiduciary guidance or regulations would come from the Department's later action.

Significance: The order moves retirement-plan investment rules toward private-market and crypto-linked holdings, which the crypto and private equity industries had sought. Critics' concern, as the press coverage in the sources frames it, is that ordinary savers take on illiquid and volatile assets.

## Correction (2026-11-16)

This entry carried a later date, taken from the coverage. The signing date is August 7, 2025, per the White House text of the order, and the entry was renamed from its earlier id (kept in `previous_ids`). The earlier text also paraphrased the order as requiring crypto guidance on equal footing with traditional assets and gave a dollar total for retirement savings. Neither is in the order's text and no source for the total was read, so both are removed. The order names digital assets only inside its definition of alternative assets.
