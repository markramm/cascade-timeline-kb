BNATCS is the Federal Aviation Administration's Brand New Air Traffic Control System. The letter is dated September 14, 2026.

FAA has not developed a comprehensive and well-documented lifecycle cost estimate for BNATCS. FAA officials acknowledged that the estimate does not include government costs, most of the operations costs for phase 1, or any of the costs for phase 2. GAO wrote that FAA's $10.6 billion estimate for phase 1 systems modernization underestimates the full costs.

FAA has not developed an integrated master schedule for phase 1. Instead, FAA has 11,389 individual project schedules that are not integrated.

In July 2025, Congress appropriated $12.5 billion to support BNATCS. Phase 1 is to be fully completed by December 2028. FAA stated that they will need approximately $10.2 billion for this phase (not including facilities).

FAA officials said they have plans to implement an artificial intelligence tool to automatically identify schedule conflicts. This tool is currently undergoing testing, and the business rules for using the tool and the new process have not been established.

Transportation partially concurred with our two recommendations.
