President Trump said Thursday that he had pulled Vice President JD Vance back from potential peace talks with Iran. Trump wrote that "We don't need a deal with Iran," and that he had asked Vance to focus his "complete and total attention" on corruption and crime in Democrat-run cities and states. The Boston Globe reports that Trump had assigned Vance to lead negotiations this year, and that Vance had recently contacted a top Iranian political and military official to propose a renewal of comprehensive peace negotiations. The Globe also reports that the pivot follows Vance publicly blaming the war for the Republican Party's devastating midterm election losses.
