AMD announced that Lux is open to scientific users at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. AMD says it ranked No. 14 on the TOP500 list.

Oak Ridge says approximately half of Lux's annual 3.5 million node-hours will support the Genesis Mission, while the remaining half will be available to AMD.

Oak Ridge's Lux page says OLCF operates Lux on behalf of DOE. It says the other half are "reserved for paid access by public and private sector partners".

The page sets a floor. A minimum 175K node-hours per six-month commitment is required.

Oak Ridge's releases do not state who holds title to the hardware or what AMD and DOE each paid. See [[genesis-lab-ai-supercomputers-ownership]] and the October 2025 announcement, [[2025-10-27--doe-announces-oak-ridge-lux-ai-cluster-with-amd-and-hpe-under-new-public-private-model-over-1-billion-no-split-disclosed]].
