## Opening

The Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission approved the NIPSCO special contract with Amazon and the NIPSCO-GenCo power purchase agreement in an order approved June 17, 2026 (order date). The petition was filed November 7, 2025 (filing date). A partial settlement among Joint Petitioners, the OUCC and the NIPSCO Industrial Group was adopted; Citizens Action Coalition opposed it. Pricing and other negotiated commercial terms stay confidential.

## What the order records

- Dates by kind: petition filed November 7, 2025; docket entry of November 18, 2025 giving preliminary confidential treatment; CAC opposition testimony filed March 18, 2026; evidentiary hearing April 7, 2026; order approved June 17, 2026. The GenCo jurisdiction order, approved September 24, 2025, was issued in Cause No. 46183, a separate proceeding.
- Amazon's load ramps to up to 2,400 MW; GenCo resources are described as 3,000 MW, with 400 MW of BESS. The order text gives no gas/storage split. Utility Dive, not the order, describes two 1.3-GW gas-fired power plants.
- The Commission's standard was incremental cost plus some contribution to fixed costs, with $1.1 billion in contributions anticipated.
- The Commission relied on terms it holds confidential in rejecting CAC's cost-shift concerns.
- The order says the settlement is not precedent and calls the GenCo structure one possible solution. The vote line records Zay, Deig and Ziegner concurring, Veleta concurring with opinion, and Swinger not participating.

## Test of the novelty framing

A statement that no other Indiana utility had proposed a similar arrangement appears in the earlier cause, attributed to a CAC witness. It is an intervenor's testimony as summarized by the Commission, not a Commission finding.
