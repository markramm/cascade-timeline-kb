Durham County's Board of County Commissioners approved a data-center moratorium at its August 24, 2026 meeting, per the county's own account. The News & Observer reported a 4-1 vote on a nine-month pause, with Commissioner Nida Allam the single opposing vote. The county says the moratorium bars new data centers and expansions of existing ones beyond 100,000 total square feet, and the Haw River Assembly reports it runs through May 2027.

The vote followed a public hearing with 63 residents signed up to speak. The exemption for smaller facilities is the contested design choice, and it is the mechanism the August wave synthesis flagged as a grandfather-style channel ([[synthesis-august-datacenter-moratorium-wave-2026-08-27]]).
