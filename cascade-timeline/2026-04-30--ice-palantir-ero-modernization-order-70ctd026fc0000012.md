USAspending lists order 70CTD026FC0000012 to Palantir Technologies Inc. as signed on 04/30/2026 (month/day/year). Its performance period runs from 05/05/2026 to 05/04/2027; the award's "start date" field is that performance start, not the signing date. Total obligation on the award is $86,271,599.3.

The description reads "THIS ORDER PROVIDES ENFORCEMENT REMOVAL OPERATIONS MODERNIZATION AND OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY FOR THE ENFORCEMENT CASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM ON THE CASE MANAGEMENT AND ANALYTICS PLATFORM IN SUPPORT OF THE U.S. IMMIGRATIONS CUSTOMS ENFORCEMENT MISSION." The competition fields read "FULL AND OPEN COMPETITION" under a multiple-award fair-opportunity procedure.

This is the Enforcement and Removal Operations order placed under the DHS-wide Palantir blanket purchase agreement audited in [[2026-05-13--dhs-zero-privacy-impact-assessments-palantir-1-billion-no-bid]]; the investigations-side order is [[2026-06-24--ice-palantir-hsi-case-management-order-70ctd026fc0000018]].

Obligation is money committed on this order, not the blanket agreement's ceiling, and not a payment. The description names the Enforcement Case Management System on the Case Management and Analytics Platform. It names no product brand, so this entry does not tie the order to ELITE or to ImmigrationOS.
