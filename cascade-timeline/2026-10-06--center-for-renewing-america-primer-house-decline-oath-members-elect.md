Published October 6, 2026 by the Center for Renewing America, which its About page says was "launched in 2021 by Russ Vought". The document, by Benjamin Osborne, says: "This primer addresses that question."

The remedy it proposes: the House should "decline to administer the oath" to a member-elect when the record shows a commitment to "destroying the constitutional order."

The procedure: "Raise the Article VI objection when the oath is called." The new House first elects its Speaker. The record, it says, should be complete before the new Congress convenes on January 3, 2027.

Whom it names: "DSA and other Communist, Marxist, Muslim, or other anticonstitutional movements." It also says Congress "cannot exclude someone because he or she is Muslim."

## Update (2026-10-06, publication date): Further wording in the primer: "CRA welcomes that litigation."

The primer says its author is describing "what the Center for Renewing America (CRA) calls the Red-Green Alliance". It also says: "CRA welcomes that litigation."

## Update (2026-11-02, publication date): Wired: another CRA publication names Mamdani and El-Sayed in its alliance

Wired reported on Nov 2, 2026 that the CRA post argues winning candidates from a purported "Red-Green Alliance" can and should be kept from being seated. According to Wired, another CRA publication from earlier this year says that alliance includes the "Squad" and politicians like "New York mayor Zohran Mamdani and Michigan senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed." The CRA "did not respond to a request for comment."

## Update (2026-10-29, publication date): Fox News: Reps. Self and Burchett float the oath maneuver; Speaker Johnson says "We are going to follow the law."

Fox News Digital reported that Rep. Keith Self, R-Texas, said he could invoke the maneuver. Self told it: "Anyone who has personally embraced the destruction of the Constitution has no business being sworn in to uphold it" The same report says Rep. Tim Burchett, R-Tenn., told the New York Post he would use the maneuver against Darializa Avila Chevalier.

Asked by CNN's Manu Raju whether he would vow to seat every Democrat who wins on Jan. 3, Speaker Mike Johnson replied: "Of course, this is a nonsense narrative" and then "We are going to follow the law." He added: "We'll administer the oath to everybody"
