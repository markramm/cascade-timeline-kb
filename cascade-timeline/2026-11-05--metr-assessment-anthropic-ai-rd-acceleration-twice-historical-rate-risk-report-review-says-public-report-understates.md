METR published an assessment of Anthropic, following a high-access evaluation at Anthropic from August 12 to October 29, 2026. METR says most of its staff on the project believe Anthropic is likely improving the capabilities of its models on well-specified tasks at around twice the rate it did between March 2024 and October 2025, an estimate METR gives with high uncertainty (it says the true figure could be closer to 1.3x or 3x). In a separate review of the "Risks from automated R&D" section of Anthropic's August 2026 Risk Report, METR says it agrees with Anthropic that catastrophic risks from July 15th models were low, and that it believes the public report "overall understates the level of acceleration that was occurring."

METR says Anthropic redacted a majority of the evidence it found most informative, and quotes Anthropic describing the redactions as "aimed at removing confidential information that could accelerate other AI developers." The Associated Press describes METR as a nonprofit and reports that Anthropic co-founder Sam McCandlish responded in a post on X, writing that many of METR's conclusions match his own.

**Not established here:** the redacted evidence behind METR's conclusions.
