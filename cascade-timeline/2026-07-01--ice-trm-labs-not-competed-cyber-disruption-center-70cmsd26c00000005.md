USAspending lists contract 70CMSD26C00000005 to TRM Labs, Inc. as signed on 07/01/2026 (month/day/year). Its performance period runs from 07/01/2026 to 08/29/2027. Total obligation on the award is $94,655,840.

The description reads "THE PURPOSE OF THIS CONTRACT IS TO SUPPORT THE DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY, HOMELAND SECURITY INVESTIGATIONS, HOMELAND SECURITY TASK FORCE NATIONAL COORDINATION CENTER CYBER DISRUPTION CENTER THROUGH THE PROVISION OF ANALYTICAL SUPPORT SERVICES." The competition field reads "NOT COMPETED".

The product-service code on the award is a services code, and the description is of analytical support services, so this is labor and analysis at the Cyber Disruption Center and not a licence purchase. The cascade-research comparison (a vendor-keyword pull) put TRM's ICE obligations in the prior fiscal years at a small fraction of this figure. Nothing in the award names the tools or data the analysts use.
