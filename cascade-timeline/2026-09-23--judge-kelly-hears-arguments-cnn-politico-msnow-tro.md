On **September 23, 2026**, Judge Timothy J. Kelly held a remote videoconference hearing at 3:30 p.m. on plaintiffs' TRO motion in *Cable News Network, Inc. v. Trump*, 1:26-cv-03287 (D.D.C.). Per the docket, oral argument was heard and the motion "taken under advisement" (Court Reporter: Tammy Nestor); a full transcript (pages 1-39) was later filed September 25. CNBC reported the hearing lasted about 46 minutes and that Kelly ended it without an immediate ruling, telling counsel: "I'll consider what you told me today. I'll try and get you an answer as soon as I can."

At the hearing, plaintiffs' lead counsel **Theodore J. Boutrous Jr.** argued "This is a clear retaliation case." The Justice Department defended the ban on the national-security rationale first raised in its September 22 filing (see [[2026-09-22--doj-cites-national-security-cnn-politico-msnow-ban]]).

Earlier the same day, the **Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press**, joined by the **White House Correspondents' Association** and 49 other media organizations, filed an unopposed motion for leave to file an amicus brief (docket entries 17-19), later granted by minute order on September 24. Plaintiffs also filed three additional supplemental declarations (Boutrous, Sudeep Reddy, David Vigilante — docket entries 20-22) and defendants filed a further supplemental declaration with exhibits (docket entry 23) the same day.

Judge Kelly ruled that night, into the early hours of September 24 — see [[2026-09-24--judge-kelly-grants-tro-cnn-politico-msnow-press-passes]].

**Sourcing note.** Hearing timing, format, and the "taken under advisement" disposition are from the CourtListener/RECAP docket; the 46-minute duration and direct quotations are from CNBC's report, filed the same day.
