The Garfield Township, Michigan Board unanimously approved a temporary moratorium on data centers and cryptocurrency mining facilities on Tuesday for a year, with an option to extend for six more months. The township routed it through a zoning-ordinance amendment because, planning director John Sych said, moratoriums aren't included in the state law that regulates local zoning.

The ban likely stalls a small-scale proposal by a developer for a building on Cass Road. See also [[synthesis-august-datacenter-moratorium-wave-2026-08-27]].
