Minnesota's Office of the Legislative Auditor issued its final report on the state's voter registration system on July 8, 2026. The Secretary of State's office, in its own release, says the report found that the office met its responsibility for the statewide registration system and for guidance to local election officials, and that every 2018 recommendation the auditor re-evaluated had been implemented.

The findings above are the Secretary of State's description of the report. **The auditor's report itself was not read.**
