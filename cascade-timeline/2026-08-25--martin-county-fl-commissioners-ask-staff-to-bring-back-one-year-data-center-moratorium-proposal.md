At its Tuesday meeting the Martin County, Florida Board of County Commissioners voted by majority to have staff bring back a proposal for a possible one-year pause on large-scale data-center development, per a Treasure Coast television report. The item was not originally scheduled. The county attorney said the state laws create a legal gray area, and no moratorium was yet in effect.

This is a procedural step, not an adopted instrument. See also [[synthesis-august-datacenter-moratorium-wave-2026-08-27]].
