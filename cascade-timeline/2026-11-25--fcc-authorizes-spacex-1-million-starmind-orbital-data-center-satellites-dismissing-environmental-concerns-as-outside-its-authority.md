According to the AP, the Trump administration on Wednesday gave SpaceX the green light to deploy 1 million satellites that it said would create data centers in space. The AP reported that the FCC described SpaceX's one million "orbital compute modules" as forming a data center constellation called "Starmind".

The AP reported that the FCC's authorization order says the company must deploy at least "one authorized space station" by 2033 and then gradually add more through 2040. The FCC acknowledged it had received a "substantial number" of public comments, including petitions to deny SpaceX's request. The AP reported that the FCC said it dismissed topics that it did not have authority over, such as environmental concerns.

The American Astronomical Society, Viasat and Amazon-owned Kuiper Systems were among those who called on the regulator to deny SpaceX's proposed data centers in space, according to the AP.
