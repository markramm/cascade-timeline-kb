Cook's 'charm offensive' through multiple dinners and meetings with Trump 2018-2019 secured Apple exemptions from China tariffs on iPhones and core products. Cook dined at Trump Tower, Bedminster golf course, attended White House state dinner, gave Trump Mac Pro plant tour. USTR granted Apple 10 of its 15 tariff-exclusion requests for Mac Pro components, reported September 20-21, 2019 (9to5Mac, Engadget); five were later denied (October 2019).
