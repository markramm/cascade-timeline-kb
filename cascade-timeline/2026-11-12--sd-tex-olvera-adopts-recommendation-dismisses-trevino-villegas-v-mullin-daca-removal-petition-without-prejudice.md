Judge Rolando Olvera adopted the magistrate judge's recommendation and dismissed the habeas and mandamus petition of Jessica Trevino Villegas, who was removed to Mexico, without prejudice, and ordered the case closed. The recommendation says an alien deported under a final removal order is not "in custody" for the purpose of a habeas petition, and that 8 U.S.C § 1252(g) precludes a district court from exercising jurisdiction over a claim seeking to command the Government to adjust immigration status. The recommendation also records her allegation that she "remains in valid DACA status" and says its facts come from her petition. The court made no finding on that status.
