- 2024-08 (award date per Bloomberg): Pentagon contract with SpaceX extends Starshield access to about 2,500 Starlink terminals already in Ukraine, in addition to 500 previously connected.
- 2024-12-06 (Bloomberg publication date; disclosure date): Space Systems Command's Commercial Satellite Office states 3,000 terminals are served under "the two contracts," both "into 2025," for "Internet connectivity in Ukraine."
