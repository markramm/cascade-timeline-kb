On September 17, 2026, faith leaders at the Minnesota State Capitol in St. Paul said they will use lessons from Operation Metro Surge to defend Minnesota's election integrity. JaNaé Bates Imari, co-executive director of ISAIAH, said "this is not a normal election." The Minnesota Reformer report places the airport demonstration, which led to the arrest of about 100 clergy members, among last winter's protest actions. The faith leaders said they will train congregations on how to build rapid response networks in their communities and hold election night vigils.
