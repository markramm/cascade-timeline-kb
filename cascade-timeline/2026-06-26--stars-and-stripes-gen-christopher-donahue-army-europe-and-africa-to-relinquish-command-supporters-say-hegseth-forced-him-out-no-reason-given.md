Stars and Stripes reported on June 26, 2026 that Gen. Christopher Donahue, commander of U.S. Army Europe and Africa, was to relinquish command ahead of his retirement later this summer.

According to Stars and Stripes, supporters say Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth is forcing him out. According to the paper, Hegseth has not publicly explained the move.

According to Stars and Stripes, the Defense Department declined to comment on the circumstances and referred questions to the Army. The paper reports the Army issued a brief statement thanking Donahue for his service.
