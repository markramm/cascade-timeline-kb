The Federalist Society's own history has it founded in 1982 by students: Steven Calabresi (then at Yale), Lee Liberman Otis and David McIntosh (then at the University of Chicago), who partnered with Spencer Abraham (Harvard) to launch a first student conference at Yale that year. In April 1982, the Federalist Society held its first event. Philanthropy Roundtable puts the Institute for Educational Affairs, backed by the Olin, Earhart, JM, Scaife and Smith Richardson foundations, behind a $15,000 grant in 1982 for the conference.
