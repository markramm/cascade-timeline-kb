FBI Director Kash Patel announced the arrest of another suspected co-conspirator of the ShinyHunters hacking group, which the bureau holds responsible for the breach of its FBIJobs.gov site. The breach, first discovered in late September, obtained thousands of personnel records. According to sources who spoke to ABC News, a late-September notice told employees the group had obtained names, addresses, contact information, Social Security numbers, dates of birth and emergency contacts, and the bureau is operating as if every FBI employee's personal information was compromised.

The FBI's cyber assistant director said its review found the incident happened "after a contractor failed to implement a security patch explicitly issued to secure the platform," and that the bureau removed the contractor.
