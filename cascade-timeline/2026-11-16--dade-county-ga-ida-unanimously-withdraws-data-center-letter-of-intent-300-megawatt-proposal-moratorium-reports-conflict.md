The Dade County, Georgia Industrial Development Authority has unanimously withdrawn a letter of intent connected to a proposed data center in the county's Industrial Park, WTVC reported on November 16, 2026. The letter involved the potential sale of about 200 acres of IDA-owned property. The IDA also canceled a public informational meeting that had been scheduled for Nov. 23rd. WTVC reported that the data center under consideration could require about 300 megawatts of power.

Baxtel reports that Dade County commissioners in Georgia approved a 90-day moratorium on new data center development after an Atlanta company explored a 300-megawatt project there. This entry does not settle whether commissioners have voted; the withdrawal of the letter is the action it records.
