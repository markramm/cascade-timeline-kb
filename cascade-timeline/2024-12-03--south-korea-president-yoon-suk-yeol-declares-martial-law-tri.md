On December 3, 2024, according to CSIS, President Yoon Suk Yeol "declared emergency martial law in an unannounced nationally televised address at 10:30 p.m. (KST)". The decree took effect "effective as of 11:00 p.m. (KST)". The same account has the Assembly's response: "as of 1:00 a.m. (KST), the National Assembly passed a resolution demanding the lifting of martial law, with all 190 members present out of 300 voting in favor." Yoon lifted the declaration at 4:30 a.m. on December 4 in the Assembly's favor.
