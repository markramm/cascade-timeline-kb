On Saturday, Reps. Ilhan Omar, Angie Craig and Kelly Morrison arrived for an oversight visit at the ICE facility in the Whipple Federal Building. Omar told reporters that shortly after they were let in, officials said they were "rescinding the invitation to come in and declining any further access from the building." Craig said they were told that because the facility was funded by the reconciliation act rather than the annual appropriations bill, access could be denied.

DHS spokesperson Tricia McLaughlin told CBS News Minnesota that existing court orders and policies "mandate that members of Congress must notify ICE at least seven days in advance of Congressional visits." Omar and Craig later returned with notice: [[2026-02-20--omar-craig-whipple-oversight-visit-cells-empty-after-eight-days-notice]].
