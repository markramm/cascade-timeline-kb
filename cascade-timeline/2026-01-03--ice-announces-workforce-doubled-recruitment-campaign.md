DHS announced on January 3, 2026 that ICE had hired more than 12,000 officers and agents in less than a year. DHS said the agency received more than 220,000 applications, passed its original hiring target of 10,000, and more than doubled its officers and agents from 10,000 to 22,000.

**What DHS said:**
- The release credits the Big Beautiful Bill for 12,000 additional officers and agents, a 120% increase in the workforce, per Assistant Secretary Tricia McLaughlin.
- DHS said the recruitment initiative used data driven outreach efforts.
- DHS gave an earlier count of more than 150,000 applications on September 16, 2025; the January 3, 2026 figure is the later one.

**What CNN reported about the campaign:**
- DHS officials saw an opportunity for a "Call of Duty"-style recruiting video with helmet-camera images and dramatic music.
- CNN reported ICE had relaxed standards, waiving age requirements and offering a $50,000 signing bonus.
