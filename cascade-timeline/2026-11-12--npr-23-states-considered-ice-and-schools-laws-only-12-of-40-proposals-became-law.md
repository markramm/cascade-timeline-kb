An NPR analysis published November 12, 2026 found that at least 23 states have considered new legislative proposals governing federal immigration officers and K-12 public schools. Of the 40 proposals NPR reviewed, 35 out of 40 sought to limit federal officers' access to K-12 campuses and students; only one proposal, in Mississippi, sought to ensure that schools cooperate with ICE. Only 12 had become laws as of early this school year.

NPR ties the state activity to the administration's rescission, at the beginning of President Trump's second term, of a policy that protected schools from immigration enforcement ([[2025-01-21--dhs-ends-sensitive-locations-policy-churches-schools]]). DHS has said that ICE does not raid or target schools. NPR cites MPR News's January report that armed U.S. Border Patrol officers came onto school property during dismissal at Roosevelt High School in Minneapolis ([[2026-01-07--cbp-pepper-sprays-students-roosevelt-high-school-minneapolis]]).
