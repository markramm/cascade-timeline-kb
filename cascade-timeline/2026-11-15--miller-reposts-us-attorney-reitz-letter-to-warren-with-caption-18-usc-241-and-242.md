On November 15, 2026, White House chief of staff Stephen Miller reposted on X a letter from the U.S. attorney for the Southern District of Texas to Sen. Elizabeth Warren. His caption read "18 USC 241 and 242." The Daily Beast reported that Miller reposted the letter just minutes after it was published.

The letter itself is the U.S. attorney's, and is held in its own entry. The Daily Beast notes the letter did not explicitly state that Warren is under investigation.
