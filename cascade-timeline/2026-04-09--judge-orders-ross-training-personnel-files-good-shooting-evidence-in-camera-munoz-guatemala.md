In the federal case of Roberto Carlos Muñoz-Guatemala, convicted in December 2025 of assaulting ICE officer Jonathan Ross during a June 17, 2025 arrest in Bloomington, Judge Jeffrey M. Bryan partly granted the defendant's post-trial discovery motion. Four weeks after the verdict, on January 7, 2026, Ross shot and killed Renee Good in Minneapolis. The order required the government to give the magistrate judge, by May 1, 2026 and for in-camera review, Ross's complete training and personnel files for his whole federal career, the DHS, ICE and ERO use-of-force and officer-involved-shooting policies, Ross's statements before, during and after the Good shooting, vehicle and body-camera recordings, fitness-for-duty evaluations, and any reports on data from the cell phone he used that day. The court reasoned that Ross's conduct in the June encounter bears on "the nature and circumstances of the offense" at sentencing. The order makes no finding about Ross. The magistrate judge decides what, if anything, reaches the defense. Ross has not been charged in Good's death.
