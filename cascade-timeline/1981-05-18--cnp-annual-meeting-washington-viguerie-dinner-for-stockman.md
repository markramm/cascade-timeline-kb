The Council for National Policy set its 1981 annual meeting for May 18-19 in Washington, D.C., in a call letter to its Board of Governors dated April 29, 1981. The agenda put a dinner party at Richard Viguerie's home in honor of OMB Director David Stockman at 7:30 p.m. on Monday, May 18, with business sessions and a banquet on Tuesday, May 19. The Council already existed: a White House briefing in the same Reagan Library file has it formed in April 1981. The New York Times covered the meeting: [[1981-05-20--nyt-reports-council-for-national-policy-convened-lahaye-president]].

## Correction (2026-11-16)

This entry was titled and written as the Council's founding, with Phyllis Schlafly as a founding member. May 18 is not a founding date. The Council had been formed earlier (see [[1981-04-01--council-national-policy-founded-conservative-coordination]]), and May 18 is the opening day of its annual meeting. The entry now covers the meeting. The Schlafly founding-member account and the Wednesday-morning meetings at Viguerie's home came from secondary sources we did not re-check, and are cut.
