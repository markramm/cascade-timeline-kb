On November 12, 2026 the intervener defendants in DSCC v. Trump filed a notice that they appeal to the United States Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit from the district court's order granting the plaintiffs' renewed motions for a preliminary injunction against the Postal Service's mail-ballot rule. The district court's opinion had held that the plaintiffs are likely to succeed on their claim that the issuance of the rule was ultra vires.

The notice does not say whether the federal government appeals or seeks a stay.
