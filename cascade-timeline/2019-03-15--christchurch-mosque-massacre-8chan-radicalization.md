In March 2019, a gunman attacked two mosques in Christchurch, New Zealand. Fifty-one people died of gunshot wounds, and 40 more people suffered gunshot injuries, according to the Royal Commission of Inquiry into the attack. The Commission found that the attack was driven by an extreme right-wing Islamophobic ideology. The attacker has since been convicted of terrorism, the murder of 51 people and attempted murder of 40 people, and is serving a sentence of life imprisonment without parole.

Before the attack he posted an anonymous message to 8chan, an online board the Commission described as known to attract people with white supremacist and anti-immigrant views. At 1.33 pm he began to livestream to his Facebook page from a camera mounted on his helmet. The Commission found that the video went on to be shared by many others, multiplying its global reach.

The Commission also recorded that 8chan was associated in 2019 with four deadly terrorist attacks, among them Christchurch, and that each of those attackers pre-announced their intentions.
