On **June 11, 2026**, **Vance Boelter pleaded guilty in federal court** to all counts arising from
the June 14, 2025 attacks: the killings of **Minnesota House Speaker Emerita Melissa Hortman and her
husband Mark Hortman**, and the shootings of **state Senator John Hoffman and his wife Yvette
Hoffman**.

At the plea hearing, **Boelter's own attorney asked him whether he put the gun to Hortman's head and
pulled the trigger. He answered yes**, drawing cries from the courtroom (Minnesota Reformer).

Federal prosecutors sought an expedited sentencing hearing by the end of July, **noting the pending
state murder charges** — which is the fact that makes the later filing legible.

## Why this entry exists eleven weeks late

**The corpus tracked the 2025 attack and the data-broker evidence but never recorded the plea.** It
was found on 2026-08-30 only because a subsequent filing
([[2026-08-28--boelter-justifiable-taking-of-life-defense-state-murder-case]]) prompted a check of
what canon already held — and canon held **zero** mentions of a guilty plea.

**The gap matters because the two prosecutions are easy to conflate.** Without this entry, the
August filing reads as a defendant denying the killings. With it, the sequence is clear: **a federal
guilty plea in which he admitted the act, then a justification defense in a separate state case.**

## The data-broker evidence, which is why this case is in this corpus

Per FBI and court filings, Boelter's SUV contained a notebook listing **eleven data brokers and
people-search sites** — Spokeo, Intelius, and TruePeopleSearch among them — annotated with costs and
results. **He bought his targets' addresses.** See
[[harms-and-victims-data-colonialism-substrate-2026-06-11]] and
