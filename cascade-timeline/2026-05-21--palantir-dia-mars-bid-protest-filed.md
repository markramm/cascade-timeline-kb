Asia Business Daily, reporting Axios, wrote that Palantir's protest argued the Defense Intelligence Agency's approach "could waste taxpayer money and potentially violate relevant laws".

## Correction

The earlier title put Palantir's protest in the past tense as a filed bid protest. No forum is established by the sources read. The earlier body's closing paragraph listed other Palantir arrangements as contracts held, including an Army agreement it named the Maven enterprise agreement. That agreement is separate from the Maven program and its figure is a ceiling; see the entry for the Army Enterprise Agreement. The other listed ceilings and orders were not re-read in this pass and are withdrawn here. GAO's public docket pages, opened on the date of this correction, record later protests by Palantir USG, Inc. with the outcome Dismissed. Nothing here ties either docket to the May 2026 press accounts, and the research task's work log holds the docket details.
