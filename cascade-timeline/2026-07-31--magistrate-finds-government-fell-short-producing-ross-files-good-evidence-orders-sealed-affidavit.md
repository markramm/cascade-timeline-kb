Reviewing what the government had produced under Judge Jeffrey M. Bryan's discovery order in *United States v. Muñoz-Guatemala*, Magistrate Judge Shannon G. Elkins wrote that "production to this Court has fallen short of what was ordered" and that for many items the government said the materials did not exist or could not be obtained. She wrote that the court had received the training and personnel file of Jonathan Ross, the ICE officer who shot Renee Good, and that the government had "partially complied." Office of Professional Responsibility records in the file referred to "originating documentation" that was neither attached nor produced. The court said it had been told that "no reports regarding Jonathan Ross's cell phone were completed," and it raised concern about the lack of recordings from DHS, ICE, ERO and Ross. It ordered the government to file a sealed affidavit by August 14, 2026, covering each of the eleven ordered categories. The OPR records' content is not public, and the order makes no finding about Ross. Ross has not been charged in Good's death.
