On Monday, Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton's office filed a notice of nonsuit without prejudice in the Collin County lawsuit against the Council on American-Islamic Relations (CAIR), according to court records cited by Houston Public Media. Paxton filed the suit in February. Per the Texas Tribune's account of the filing, it asked a judge to declare CAIR a foreign terrorist organization and transnational criminal organization.

Gov. Greg Abbott designated CAIR as a foreign terrorist and transnational criminal organization, according to Houston Public Media. Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa told the Washington Post she would remove that designation when she assumes office, the same report says.
