**Miles Taylor**, former acting DHS chief of staff, argued on **September 14, 2026** that the administration is converting **SAVE** — the Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements system, built to check immigration status for benefit programs — into the foundation of a federal voter database that would centralize eligibility determinations the states have always made. His structural claim: a single centralized system is far easier to manipulate than fifty decentralized state-run ones, and the decentralization that frustrates election conspiracy theories is the same decentralization that frustrates election capture.

**The Texas case is the documented test, and it verified better than the ledger claimed.** Texas flagged **2,724 registered voters** as potential noncitizens using SAVE and began removals. **Independent tier-1 reporting from Votebeat and the Texas Tribune (both 2026-07-30) confirms** that state elections director **Christina Adkins** told county officials Texas had reviewed DPS records establishing the citizenship of some of those flagged — hundreds had registered *through* DPS, which requires documentary proof of citizenship and keeps copies on file. The state could not say how many of the 2,724 fell into that category. So the federal database flagged as suspect a set of people whose citizenship the state already held documents proving.

**That is the mechanism, independent of anyone's motive.** A verification tool built for benefits eligibility, pointed at voter rolls, produces false positives against citizens whose proof is already in a state filing cabinet — and the burden of the error falls on the voter. Courts have twice found against the program's methods: Judge Sooknanan blocked SAVE-based purges as unlawful ([[2026-06-22--sooknanan-blocks-save-voter-purge-database-unlawful]]), and the Sixth Circuit handed DOJ its first appellate loss on voter rolls ([[2026-06-24--sixth-circuit-doj-first-appellate-loss-voter-rolls-michigan]]). The purge blitz across Ohio, Texas and Idaho ahead of the midterms is already in canon ([[2026-05-04--dhs-save-voter-roll-purge-blitz-ohio-texas-idaho-2026-midterms]]).
