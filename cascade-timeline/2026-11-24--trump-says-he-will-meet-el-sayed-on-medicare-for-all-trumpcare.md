President Donald Trump said Tuesday that he plans to meet Michigan Sen.-elect Abdul El-Sayed to discuss El-Sayed's "Medicare for All" proposal, which El-Sayed has pitched to rebrand as "Trumpcare." "He's a smart guy," Trump told the New York Post, adding, "I know I'll be meeting with him." Trump had reposted a screenshot of El-Sayed's gold "Trumpcare" card post on Saturday. He paired the opening with a warning to the incoming Democratic majorities: "I’m just going to veto everything there is," and then expected them to negotiate.

The turn follows a campaign in which Trump called El-Sayed "Mohammed," falsely declared him a "jihadist," and told a Florida rally to "send him back to where he came from." Vice President JD Vance's office has begun reaching out to the incoming senator. El-Sayed said on ABC's "This Week" that he would meet anyone who can advance his goals to "get money out of politics, put money in pockets, pass Medicare for all."
