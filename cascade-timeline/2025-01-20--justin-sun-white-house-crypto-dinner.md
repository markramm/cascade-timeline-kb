Chinese crypto billionaire Justin Sun invested $75 million in Trump's World Liberty Financial (75% of proceeds go to Trump family as fees, netting them ~$56 million) and over $20 million in $TRUMP memecoin, totaling $93+ million in Trump crypto ventures. Sun attended a May 2025 black-tie gala at Trump's Virginia golf club where he received a $100,000 Trump Golden Tourbillon watch. The SEC paused its 2023 fraud charges against Sun after his investment announcement.

venue, and the headline event are not the same occurrence, and nothing in the body supports a

**What is NOT supported**: the January 20 date, the White House venue, and the "first White House
crypto dinner guest" characterization. **Do not cite any of the three.**

well-sourced; what failed is the *frame built around them*. "First White House crypto dinner
guest, January 20" is a far stronger and more publishable claim than "attended a May gala at a
golf club" — and it is the version a drafter would reach for. An entry can be factually stocked
and still assert an event that did not happen as described.

**Not deleted, deliberately.** The financial findings are the substance and they hold. The repair
is to re-date the entry to the documented May 2025 gala, correct the venue, and drop the White
