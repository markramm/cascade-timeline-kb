Elon Musk's buyer entity completed the Twitter acquisition on October 27, 2022. In an SEC ownership filing, Prince Alwaleed bin Talal reported that he, directly and indirectly, put 34,948,975 shares of Twitter stock into the buyer in exchange for shares of the buyer, and that he and Kingdom Holding Company were then the second largest investor in it. The same filing says every other Twitter share converted to $54.20 in cash; his stake was exchanged, not paid for with new money. A subsidiary of QIA contributed $375,000,000 to the buyer in exchange for shares.

Context: the Saudi holder is Alwaleed and his Kingdom Holding Company, which the filing names; it does not name the Saudi state or its Public Investment Fund. A rollover leaves a foreign-linked investor with a stake in a platform without new cash going in, which is the form of foreign influence at issue here, and it differs from the sovereign cash from Qatar.

## Correction (2026-11-16)

This version replaces the earlier title's dollar total, which no close filing carries, and the earlier description of Alwaleed's stake as cash. Musk's May 2022 ownership filing lists approximately $7.139 billion in new financing commitments from co-investors. Most are cash. One, Alwaleed's, is a commitment of existing shares of Common Stock in lieu of merger consideration, and the close filing sizes it at 34,948,975 shares at the $54.20 deal price. That is a rollover of shares he already held, not new money. The holder is Alwaleed with Kingdom Holding Company, not the Saudi government.
