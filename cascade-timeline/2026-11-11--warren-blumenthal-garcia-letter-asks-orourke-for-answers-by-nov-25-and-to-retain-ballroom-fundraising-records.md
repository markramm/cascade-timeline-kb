Sens. Elizabeth Warren and Richard Blumenthal and Rep. Robert Garcia wrote to Meredith O'Rourke, addressed as a board member of the National Park Foundation, to "request information concerning your role in soliciting and coordinating over $800 million in large corporate contributions" to projects including the White House ballroom and Freedom 250 celebrations. The letter is dated November 11, 2026. It asks for answers by November 25, 2026, and asks her to "retain all relevant documents, communications, and materials related to this request."
