NOTUS (republished by Spotlight PA) and Bloomberg Law reported in June 2026 on the Justice Department's election-protection work. NOTUS wrote that the department "has canceled election-integrity training sessions for prosecutors and FBI agents, deleted a 281-page guide to prosecuting election offenses, fired most of the lawyers in its Public Integrity Section and failed to replace the director of its Election Crimes Branch." Bloomberg Law wrote that "DOJ leaders have eliminated a centralized command post, discontinued mandatory election law training for prosecutors, and restricted access to threat briefings for state officials, said people briefed on the situation."

**The command center.** NOTUS wrote that the department had not taken the usual steps to establish its Election Day command center, according to "three sources with knowledge of the situation told NOTUS". Former DOJ attorneys, NOTUS wrote, described the center as "an intense, around-the-clock operation at FBI headquarters". On the department's response, NOTUS wrote that the DOJ "did not provide any answers before publication to detailed questions about the training cancellations and the election command center"; a spokesperson instead issued a statement of priorities on election integrity, voting fraud and civil rights. Earlier, ProPublica's April 2026 report said the cuts "have eliminated information channels that spotlighted threats as voting took place, including Election Day command posts run by the Justice Department and FBI." Its reply from the bureau: an FBI spokesperson said the agency "will still have capabilities to monitor the situation on the ground through designated election crimes coordinator experts in all its field offices." No official statement, record or court filing located so far says whether the headquarters center was set up.

**The training.** NOTUS wrote that "a fourth source with direct knowledge of the situation" said DOJ headquarters had canceled biannual training sessions scheduled for this year at the National Advocacy Center, and that internal notes cited changes in priorities. We located no official record of the cancellations.

This sits beside the earlier hollowing of the Public Integrity Section ([[2026-03-09--doj-abuse-power-public-integrity-section-gutted]], [[2025-02-01--doj-and-fbi-gut-public-corruption-enforcement-team]]) and the department's redirected attention toward voting-adjacent organizations ([[2026-06-11--fbi-raids-ohio-organizing-collaborative-probe-expands-america-votes]]).

## Update: field-office command posts on Election Day

CNN wrote after the election, "Throughout Tuesday, FBI field offices across the country operated command posts focused on monitoring any election issues that would require federal attention." That describes field offices, not the headquarters center, and leaves the June reporting about headquarters neither confirmed nor contradicted. CNN's separate line that "there were no actual significant threats, according to one senior law enforcement source" is where its anonymous sourcing appears.

## Corrections (2026-11-15)

- The earlier title and text stated as fact that training was canceled, that no FBI command center was stood up, and that no federal rapid-response mechanism existed. Those statements came from the June reporting. The title and text now attribute each claim to its outlet, and no official record was found.
- Added ProPublica's April report and the FBI spokesperson's reply.
- Added the CNN update on field-office command posts, leaving the headquarters question open.
- Removed unsourced background claims (a start year for the center and the "no federal body" conclusion).
