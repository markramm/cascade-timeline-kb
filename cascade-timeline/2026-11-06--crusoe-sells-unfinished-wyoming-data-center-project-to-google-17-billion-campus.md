Crusoe confirmed Friday that it has sold its unfinished data center project in southeastern Wyoming to Google, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/crusoe-sells-troubled-wyoming-data-center-project-to-google/). Google, which Crusoe had tried and failed to land as a customer for the project, is taking over after reportedly raising concerns about its cost and construction timeline under Crusoe’s management.

Google did not disclose what it paid, but announced plans to invest $17 billion to develop the campus. Crusoe had announced the project with Blackstone-backed infrastructure company Tallgrass, planning to scale it to 10 gigawatts of power capacity.
