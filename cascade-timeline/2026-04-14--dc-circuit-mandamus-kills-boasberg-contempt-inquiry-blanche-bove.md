A D.C. Circuit panel (Judges Rao, Walker and Childs) granted the petition for a writ of mandamus on April 14, 2026. Its order vacated Chief Judge James Boasberg's December 8, 2025 order and directed the district court "to terminate its criminal contempt proceedings." Judge Childs dissented. On June 22, 2026, the full court ordered that the case be reheard en banc and that the April 14 order be vacated, with the December 12, 2025 administrative stay in effect. The en banc court heard oral argument on Sep 29, 2026.

The panel opinion records that the government identified then-Secretary of Homeland Security Kristi Noem as the official responsible for the transfer decision. Judge Childs's dissent records that Emil Bove and Todd Blanche each stated in declarations that they provided privileged legal advice to Noem.
