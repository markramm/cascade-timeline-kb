The United States filed a notice of voluntary dismissal in United States v. California (C.D. Cal. 2:26-cv-12025) on November 6, 2026, before Judge David O. Carter. The notice says that California will have already commingled the challenged ballots with lawful ones by the time of the hearing the court set for November 13, 2026.

The suit had sought an order that California segregate mail ballots flagged as cast by likely noncitizens.
