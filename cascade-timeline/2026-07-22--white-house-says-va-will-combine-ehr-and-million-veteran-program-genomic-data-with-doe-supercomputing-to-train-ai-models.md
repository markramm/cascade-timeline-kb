The White House published a Genesis Mission release on July 22, 2026. The release lists the National Science and Technology Challenges.

According to the release, VA will "combine its vast electronic health records and genomic data from the Million Veteran Program with DOE’s supercomputing to train AI models".

The release says more than 15 federal agencies will contribute datasets and facilities to the challenges.

The release names no consent terms, no privacy notice and no non-federal access terms for the veterans' data. The research note [[genesis-mission-va-mvp-data-at-ornl]] holds the agreements, the notices and the participant statements.
