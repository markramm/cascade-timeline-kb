Benny Johnson told DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin that Vice President Vance had told him on the show Johnson believes Omar committed immigration fraud, and that Tom Homan had told him "live on stage at a Turning Point event" to "get ready, something's about to happen." Mullin answered: "Something's going to happen is this is an ongoing investigation. We know that she married her brother to try to bring him in the United States. We know he lives now in London... we feel that there may be more to this, the whole family migration, and possibly some illegal activity for them to come to the United States to begin with. But all of us... we're investigating."

He added: "If we actually move a case forward, it's not a political case. It's actual case because they did things wrong," and said that lying on an application has "no expiration... if we can prove it," then "we'll deport you."
