ICE announced on November 24, 2026 that an enhanced enforcement operation in Indiana resulted in 115 arrests over several weeks. ICE's own release says those arrested have criminal histories that include arrests for and convictions of offenses ranging from aggravated assault and drug trafficking to driving under the influence, traffic offenses, licensing violations and public disturbance, and that 18 had final orders of removal. The release does not say how many of the 115 had convictions rather than arrests alone, and gives no dates for the operation. "Federal immigration laws will be enforced in every American community, regardless of its size or location," said ICE Chicago Assistant Field Office Director Raymond Hernandez. The release credits Secretary Mullin and acting ICE Director David J. Venturella.

The figures are ICE's own and unverified. By the release's own count, most of those arrested had no final order of removal, and its list of criminal histories mixes arrests with convictions, so it does not support a count of people convicted of violent crimes. The release names individual arrestees; this entry does not.
