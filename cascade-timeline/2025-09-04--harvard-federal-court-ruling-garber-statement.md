On 2025-09-04, President Alan Garber issued a statement ("An Update on Our Litigation," published date) responding to a federal district court ruling that granted Harvard's motion for summary judgment, finding the Trump administration's freeze of University research funding unlawful. Garber's statement (verbatim, from the primary posting): "The ruling affirms Harvard's First Amendment and procedural rights, and validates our arguments in defense of the University's academic freedom, critical scientific research, and the core principles of American higher education." This is a single-institution litigation-outcome statement, not a joint-signature letter — it responds to a court ruling in Harvard's own suit against the federal government (the same funding-freeze dispute documented in [[2025-08-26--harvard-extortion-demand]]), not to a coordinated administration action against multiple universities.
