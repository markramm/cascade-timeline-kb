The Senate Appropriations Committee posted the text of its FY 2027 Energy and Water Development bill and an explanatory statement. The Committee's release page says it "today released the text of five additional FY 2027 funding bills".

The explanatory statement for the Energy and Water bill says: "The Committee is concerned that the Department is executing the Genesis Mission as an integrated Department-wide initiative without the necessary program planning, which could prevent the Genesis Mission from meeting its policy objectives".

The statement directs DOE to give the Committee "a briefing on Genesis Mission funding by program" not later than 180 days after enactment.

The briefing is to include "the security of existing data assets being used for Genesis".

This is committee text, not enacted law. The House counterpart is [[2026-05-22--house-appropriations-report-119-667-bars-funds-for-donated-genesis-ai-supercomputers-and-asks-doe-for-cost-and-integrator-reports]]. The other findings are in [[genesis-oversight-record]].
