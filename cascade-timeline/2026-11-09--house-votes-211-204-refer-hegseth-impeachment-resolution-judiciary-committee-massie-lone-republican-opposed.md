The House voted 211 to 204 to refer the resolution to the Judiciary Committee, effectively killing it. Massie was the lone Republican to vote with all Democrats against it. The impeachment resolution accused Hegseth of abusing his office by launching the Iran war without congressional approval, ignoring laws designed to minimize civilian casualties.

Massie, a Kentucky Republican, said Monday he would not bring up another resolution to impeach Hegseth. Massie said Tuesday he planned to force a vote on a war powers resolution.
