President Trump signed Executive Order 14363, which launches the Genesis Mission, on November 24, 2025. The order says the Secretary of Energy may designate a senior political appointee to oversee day-to-day operations of the Mission. It gives the Secretary 60 days to submit a list of at least 20 science and technology challenges.

The order tells the Secretary to establish clear policies for ownership, licensing, trade-secret protections, and commercialization of intellectual property developed under the Mission. It sets no deadline for those policies.

The order asks for an initial operating capability within 270 days, subject to available appropriations.
