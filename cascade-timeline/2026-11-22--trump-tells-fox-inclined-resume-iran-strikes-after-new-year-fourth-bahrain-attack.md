In an interview with Fox News on Sunday, Trump said he was inclined to resume strikes on Iran after the new year if no deal was reached, Iran International reports, and warned that Iranian ships trying to run the US naval blockade would be sunk. He called Iran's negotiators "professional tap dancers" who talked constantly but said nothing of substance. Asked about Iranian state media reports that Iran might test the blockade, Trump said: "I hope they do, they'll all be at the bottom of the sea like the rest of the navy." He also said: "I'm going to make a deal that is going to be permanent."

The remarks came as Iran made what CBS News described as the fourth attempted attack by Iran in Bahrain in roughly a week. Bahrain's interior ministry said Iran had attacked a civilian industrial facility, sparking a fire and causing material damage, though no injuries were reported. The State Department urged Americans in Bahrain to practice "heightened vigilance." The United States has two aircraft carrier strike groups deployed to the Middle East, Iran International reports.
