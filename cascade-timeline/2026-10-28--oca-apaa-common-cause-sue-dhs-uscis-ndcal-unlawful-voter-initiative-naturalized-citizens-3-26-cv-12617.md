On 10/28/26 (filing date), OCA-Asian Pacific American Advocates, OCA-San Mateo, OCA-Sacramento and Common Cause filed a complaint for declaratory and injunctive relief in the Northern District of California (3:26-cv-12617) against the Department of Homeland Security, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services and their top officials.

The complaint says the "Unlawful Voter Initiative," which USCIS adopted in August 2026, "systematically targets thousands of naturalized and derivative United States citizens" for voter eligibility investigations and tracking by the federal government. It alleges USCIS has reassigned hundreds of agents to investigate individuals flagged by DHS as potential noncitizen voters, and that agents who suspect noncitizen voting are told to create a TECS record for the individual. It says the agency describes its process as "supplemental magic." It also alleges that the federal government presented Washington with a list of 405 individuals it believes to be noncitizen voters.

The Initiative surfaced earlier in a whistleblower disclosure ([[2026-09-14--whistleblower-disclosure-uscis-fdns-unlawful-voter-initiative-state-voter-sites-12-minute-quota]]) and in discovery in a separate suit ([[2026-10-08--naacp-v-dhs-plaintiffs-seek-expedited-discovery-on-unlawful-voter-initiative-court-sets-response-dates]]). The federal government's use of the DHS list in California is in [[2026-10-19--doj-seeks-tro-us-v-california-sworn-dhs-declarations-describe-how-20000-voter-list-was-built]].

## Update (2026-10-30, order date): Judge handling California Nurses Association v. Mullin takes the OCA case; SEIU suit is not related

In a related case order dated October 30, 2026, Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín, assigned to California Nurses Association v. Mullin, 26-cv-10680-AMO, listed 26-cv-12617 under Related and 26-cv-12800, the SEIU suit against the Justice Department, under Not Related. Her order says related cases "shall be reassigned to me".
