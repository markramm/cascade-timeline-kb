Deputy White House Chief of Staff Stephen Miller and DHS Secretary Kristi Noem demanded in a meeting with ICE officials that ICE arrest 3,000 immigrants each day, approximately triple the number of daily arrests ICE agents were making at the beginning of the administration (Axios, as relayed by The Independent via Yahoo News). The meeting was on May 21 at ICE headquarters in Washington, and the report was published May 28. The meeting was, in the Guardian's words, first reported by Axios and confirmed by the Guardian, and the Guardian described the target as tripling arrest figures from earlier this year. Latin Times, relaying the same Axios report, wrote that the figure is triple the daily average from the early months of Trump's first term, wording that differs from the Independent's and the Guardian's.
