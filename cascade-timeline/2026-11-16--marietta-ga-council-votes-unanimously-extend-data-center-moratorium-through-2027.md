Fox Atlanta, citing the Marietta Daily Journal, reported on Nov. 16 that the Marietta City Council voted unanimously to extend its pause on new data center applications through Dec. 31, 2027. The one-year moratorium takes effect Jan. 1, immediately after the current six-month pause expires. The council reversed an earlier proposal to lift the restrictions. City leaders said the extension will give them more time to hear from residents, address community trust concerns and evaluate local utility limits before deciding how to regulate data center development.
