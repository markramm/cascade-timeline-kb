The Supreme Court heard argument in Trump v. Cook on the date of this entry. ABC News described the case as "in a very preliminary posture", focused on Trump's request to stay a lower court order that Cook remain on the job.

Justice Brett Kavanaugh told Solicitor General John Sauer, in CNN's account: "Your position that there’s no judicial review, no process required, no remedy available, very low bar for ‘cause’ – that the president alone determines – I mean that would weaken, if not shatter, the independence of the Federal Reserve." Chief Justice John Roberts asked: "If you’re correct that courts do not have authority to reinstate a removed officer, why are we wasting our time wondering if there’s cause or not?" Justice Samuel Alito noted, in ABC's account: "No court has ever explored those facts." Sauer answered the question of process: "It's our position that adequate process was already provided."

The argument was on an application to stay, not a decision on the merits. The Court's later order is in a separate entry.
