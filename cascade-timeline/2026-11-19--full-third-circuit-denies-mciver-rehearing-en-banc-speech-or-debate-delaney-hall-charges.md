The full U.S. Third Circuit denied Rep. LaMonica McIver's petition for rehearing, keeping in place an August appellate ruling that the Speech and Debate Clause did not shield her from at least two of the three charges against her. Only three of the 13 judges who participated in the vote moved to grant the petition. Judge Cindy Chung wrote in the court's one-paragraph ruling, "The petition for rehearing by the panel and the Court en banc, is denied." McIver is accused of forcibly impeding and interfering with federal officers during a scuffle outside the Delaney Hall immigrant detention facility in Newark in May 2025. In a statement Thursday she repeated her claim that the charges were politically motivated. Judge Emil Bove did not participate in the ruling.

The New Jersey Monitor, the only source read, does not give the date of the order, so this entry is dated by the article's publication date. The order and the August panel opinion were not read.
