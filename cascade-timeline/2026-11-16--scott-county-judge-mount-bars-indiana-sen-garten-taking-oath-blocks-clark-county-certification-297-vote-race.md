On Monday, a Scott County Circuit Court judge, Jason Mount, issued an order blocking Republican state Sen. Chris Garten from taking the oath of office for a new term ahead of an expected recount of his tight race for reelection, according to the Indiana Capital Chronicle. The order reads: "Chris Garten is restrained from presenting himself to take the oath of office as, or to be seated as, the Senator from District 45." The judge's order also blocks the Clark County Election Board from certifying the election results until he decides whether to grant an injunction. A court hearing on the request for a preliminary injunction is set for Wednesday afternoon in Jeffersonville.

The restraining order was sought by the Clark County Democratic chairman, who argued that voting equipment problems on Election Day in Jeffersonville leave questions about the accuracy of the reported election results. The tallies show Garten ahead by 297 votes. Garten last week called the claims from Democrats "political theater." Democrats sought the order ahead of the Legislature's Organization Day on Tuesday, when the Senate's majority Republicans planned to install him in the chamber's top position of president pro tem.
