A joint investigation by The Nineteenth News and Uncloseted Media reports that Meta's Facebook Content Monetization program pays at least 29 accounts with histories of anti-LGBTQ+ rhetoric, including Libs of TikTok and Tucker Carlson. It reports at least 8 official Republican Party pages enrolled despite a policy barring political and government entities. Meta did not respond to requests for comment, it reports.
