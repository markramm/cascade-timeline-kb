GD Culture Group Limited (Nasdaq: GDC), a Nevada corporation, closed its acquisition of Pallas Capital Holding Ltd, "a British Virgin Islands company," in exchange for 39,189,344 newly issued GDC shares, equal to 233.33% of the shares outstanding before the deal. The annual report says Pallas holds 7,500 Bitcoin, "valued at approximately $842 million at the time of acquisition."

## Why it is a related-party deal

The Share Exchange Agreement names a group of sellers that includes Yan Wang, Qing Wang, Wealthy Concord Limited and East Valley Technology Limited. The filing identifies Yan Wang and Qing Wang, who beneficially owned 4.12% and 7.28% of GDC, as directors of the target with voting and dispositive power over its shares, and says the Transaction "constitutes a related party transaction for the Company pursuant to Item 404 of Regulation S-K." The annual report puts the director-shareholders' combined beneficial ownership at 12.86%.

A later beneficial-ownership filing reports that East Valley Technology Limited holds 2,782,443 GDC shares of record and that Cui Runan is its sole director and sole owner; it reports the same holding for Wealthy Concord Limited, whose reporting person is Zhang Binyang.

## Process

GDC's Audit Committee, composed entirely of independent directors, reviewed and unanimously approved the terms, and the Board obtained "a third-party fairness opinion."

## Relation to the May 2025 equity line

The May 2025 Common Stock Purchase Agreement (a put right held by GDC, with proceeds "if any" for Bitcoin and OFFICIAL TRUMP) is a separate transaction with an unnamed investor. See [[2025-05-13--tiny-company-with-china-ties-announces-big-purchase-of-trump]].

## Later developments

The same filing reports a proposal delivered on May 1, 2026 to acquire the shares the filers do not already own for US$10.75 per share in cash, which the filing calls "preliminary and non-binding."
