Seven people died in ICE custody in December 2025, four of them within a four-day span, making December the deadliest month for detainees of the second Trump term, NOTUS reported. ICE's published death list dates the four deaths as December 12, 14, 14 and 15. NOTUS wrote that the year had the most deaths in immigrant detention since 2004, and that ICE was ending it with a record of more than 68,000 people in detention.

Among the dead: Jean Wilson Brutus, 41, a Haitian man. ICE said he entered its custody on December 11, experienced a medical emergency at Delaney Hall in Newark, and was pronounced dead at University Hospital on December 12, from suspected natural causes. Fouad Saeed Abdulkadir, 46, an Eritrean citizen, died on December 14 at Moshannon Valley Processing Center in Philipsburg, Pennsylvania, after 215 days in ICE custody; the Inquirer described him as an Ohio Islamic leader, citing a funeral fundraiser. Nenko Stanev Gantchev, 56, a Bulgarian citizen, died on December 15 at North Lake Processing Center in Baldwin, Michigan. Dalvin Francisco Rodriguez, 39, of Nicaragua, was found without a pulse at a Pennsylvania detention center on December 4 and was pronounced dead 10 days later, according to NOTUS.

A researcher who tracks ICE data wrote in late December that ICE is required to report deaths in custody within 30 days on its Detainee Death Reporting page, but that the page had not been updated since October, so none of the December deaths appeared there. The same post said people with no criminal histories made up 35% of all people held, and that the coincidence of record detention and seven deaths raised the question of "whether the detention system is growing faster than it can responsibly manage."
