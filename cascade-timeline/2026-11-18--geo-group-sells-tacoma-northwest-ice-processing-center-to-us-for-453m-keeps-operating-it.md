The GEO Group sold the Northwest ICE Processing Center in Tacoma, Washington, to the U.S. government for $453 million. GEO said it expects approximately $340 million in net proceeds and plans to use money from the sale, with operating cash flow, to buy back $500 million of its own stock. GEO keeps running the facility under a new 10-year support-services contract through Oct. 27, 2036, and is negotiating to sell up to three more of its facilities to ICE before the end of 2026, as long as both sides agree on a price and GEO continues managing them.

Rep. Pramila Jayapal said the city of Tacoma assessed the property at just under $72.6 million, less than one-sixth of the price, and said that in Congress "we intend to investigate every one of these sales." Rep. Emily Randall called the sale a "taxpayer-funded giveaway to shareholders."

The October sale of GEO's three Adelanto facilities is at [[2026-10-05--geo-group-completes-950m-sale-of-three-adelanto-ice-facilities-to-dhs-keeps-operating-them]].

## Update (2026-11-18, release date): GEO release and USAspending record

GEO's own release calls the Northwest ICE Processing Center a 1,635-bed facility. GEO's release says there is "no definitive agreement in place" for any additional transactions. GEO says its negotiations could result in the sale of "up to three additional GEO-owned facilities" before the end of 2026. USAspending lists a total obligation of $24,256,301 on the Tacoma task order.
