ICE detained more than 46,000 people in July 2026 — the highest single-month total of the Trump second term — as Enforcement and Removal Operations widened its target set across visa overstays and illegal entry. ICE in-custody deaths reached at least 24 for 2026, including 41-year-old Salvadoran national Edwin Lopez-Cornejo, who died at GEO Group's Delaney Hall facility in Newark, NJ on August 1. The 2026 pace is tracking to exceed 2025, already the deadliest year on record for ICE custody.

July's 46,000 figure surpasses June's then-record 43,000 monthly arrests documented in [[2026-07-29--ice-100k-bed-push-june-arrests-record-43000]] and extends the near-600,000-cumulative-deportation trajectory reported in [[2026-07-23--ice-nears-600k-deportations-july-arrest-record]]. Lopez-Cornejo's death is the second at Delaney Hall in 2026 ([[2026-08-01--edwin-lopez-cornejo-dies-delaney-hall-second-death-geo-1-2b-contract]]), a facility with an established pattern of health and oversight failures ([[2026-06-02--nj-sues-geo-group-delaney-hall-health-inspector-access]]). The rising-arrest/rising-death correlation matches the doubled death-rate finding in [[2026-06-17--reuters-ice-detention-death-rate-doubles-50-deaths-overcrowding-medical-failure]].

## Population, composition, and a live arrest-count discrepancy (added 2026-08-27)

Two measures beyond the monthly arrest figure. ICE held **65,765 people in detention as of July 11, 2026**, against an all-time peak of roughly **73,400 in mid-January**; TRAC puts the share with **no criminal conviction at 70.6 percent**.

The arrest count itself is contested. Austin Kocher's analysis of Deportation Data Project records puts July arrests at **51,000**, against the **46,000** ICE reported officially — a gap this entry records rather than resolves. It may be definitional (arrests versus book-ins) or a disclosure discrepancy; it should be settled by parsing the primary release rather than by citing either total. The discrepancy, and the reason for it, is arguably more informative than either number.
