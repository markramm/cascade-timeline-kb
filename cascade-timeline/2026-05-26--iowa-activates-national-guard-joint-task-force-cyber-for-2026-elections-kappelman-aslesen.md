At the Iowa Secretary of State's election-security briefing on Tuesday, May 26, Guard officer Capt. Benjamin Aslesen said, per the Secretary of State's release: "The Joint Task Force - Cyber stands ready to leverage its expertise to safeguard the electoral process." The same statement dates the collaboration to 2017. KCRG quoted Guard officer Brig. Gen. Mark Kappelman: "we have activated Joint Task Force Cyber", and said the team will "monitor and defend critical election networks".
