The town of Marana approved a business license for Management & Training Corporation, or MTC, to run an immigration detention center in a former state prison. Arizona Luminaria reports the license, issued on Nov. 18, lifts a final bureaucratic roadblock before the center could become operational. The federal government has said it aims to nearly triple the Marana ICE detention capacity.

The approval follows the town's review recorded in [[2026-10-28--marana-az-asks-mtc-to-explain-prea-audit-gap-in-ice-camp-license-review]] and the contract in [[2026-09-29--mtc-signs-355m-ice-contract-marana-775-bed-detention-facility]]. MTC said that of the three allegations identified in the article, only one was mistakenly omitted from the information provided to the auditor. In an email shared at the end of September, the company said it aimed to reopen the former prison as an immigration detention center in the next 60 days.
