The Immigration and Nationality Act's section 287(g), the provision that lets federal immigration authorities enter agreements with state and local law enforcement, was added by Congress in the Illegal Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996. The U.S. Code's amendment note records that section 133 of that act added subsection (g). The Code's source credit dates the act Sept. 30, 1996.

ICE's own fact sheet says the 1996 act added Section 287(g) to the Immigration and Nationality Act, and that the provision authorizes agreements with state and local law enforcement agencies under which designated officers perform immigration law enforcement functions. That fact sheet's roster of participating entities, archived in January 2009, has as its earliest dated row a Florida Department of Law Enforcement task force officer agreement dated 07/02/2002. NPR reported in February 2026 that the program was established in 1996.

This entry records no event in 1997: the provision came from the 1996 act, and no 1997 start or effective date for the program appears in these sources.
