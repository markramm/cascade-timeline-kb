KPBS reports that, over the last few months, federal agents have dumped people who judges have ordered released from the Otay Mesa Detention Center onto the streets of San Ysidro late at night. Oftentimes they are left without charged phones, money, food, or any help getting home, according to human rights volunteers trying to help this vulnerable population. One released man said that, despite a federal judge's order for release, he and other detainees were handcuffed, loaded into a van and left at the San Ysidro Transit Center around 10 p.m.

The Pope Francis Center began hosting released detainees in October, and since then more than 100 people have slept in their building, which used to be a convent for the church. The account rests on volunteers and released detainees; the entry does not carry an ICE response.
