Stephen Miller and DHS Secretary Kristi Noem held a tense meeting at ICE headquarters demanding field offices achieve 3,000 daily arrests—triple the early administration numbers. Miller threatened to fire the bottom 10% of directors monthly if quotas weren't met. A week later, CBS News reported that Kenneth Genalo, a longtime ICE official, was retiring and Robert Hammer was reassigned, amid frustrations with arrest and deportation levels; CBS does not tie either move to quota performance.
