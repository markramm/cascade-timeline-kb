On Ash Wednesday, Feb. 18, 2026, Cardinal Blase Cupich celebrated an outdoor, bilingual Mass at Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish in Melrose Park, Illinois, hosted by the Coalition for Spiritual and Public Leadership (CSPL). The Chicago Catholic reported that more than 3,000 people attended, including relatives of people who have been detained and deported, and that a candlelight procession through the surrounding streets followed.

The Mass was not held at Broadview. Earlier that day, at noon, CSPL learned that DHS would admit representatives to the Broadview ICE facility at 3 p.m. to offer Communion, ashes and pastoral care, in compliance with Judge Gettleman's Feb. 12 order (see [[2026-02-12--gettleman-orders-ice-to-admit-clergy-to-broadview-on-ash-wednesday]] and [[2026-02-18--clergy-enter-broadview-ice-facility-ash-wednesday-communion-ashes]]). Three ministers, among them the parish pastor Fr. Leandro Fossá, went in.

Date note: the Chicago Catholic's photo captions give the year as 2025; the story text, the date line and the Lent reference place the Mass on Feb. 18, 2026.
