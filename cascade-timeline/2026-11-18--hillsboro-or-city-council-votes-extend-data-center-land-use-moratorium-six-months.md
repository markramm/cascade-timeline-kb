KPTV reported on Nov. 18 that the Hillsboro City Council voted to adopt a six-month extension of a land use moratorium on data centers. The outcry comes after a 120-day moratorium, which was adopted in July, is set to expire at the end of the month. City leaders said they want to do more research on data centers and their impacts.
