Reporty Homeland Security, now Carbyne, was backed in 2015 through a partnership Barak formed, Sum (E.B.) 2015. Where the sources differ, each is given as its own account; none is averaged or chosen.

Calcalist reported that Barak "invested $1 million in the company in 2015" and that Epstein "is another partner at Sum". Citing Kan, Calcalist reported that Epstein "had invested $1 million in Sum in 2016, through a British Virgin Islands-registered company named Southern Trust".

Haaretz, as relayed by JTA, reported that Epstein "financed much of an investment of several million dollars formally made by Barak". Barak told JTA "I saw the business opportunity and registered a partnership in my control in Israel". Buchris, a Carbyne director, told Reason "I don't know Epstein and haven't met him".

Carbyne's announcement of Barak's departure says "Effective May 1, Mr. Barak will be replaced by Founder and CEO Amir Elichai", that "all shares owned by Mr. Barak’s Sum (E.B) LLP are being moved to IBI" and that they "will be sold in the coming weeks to a new investor who has been identified". The page printing it has no year on it.
