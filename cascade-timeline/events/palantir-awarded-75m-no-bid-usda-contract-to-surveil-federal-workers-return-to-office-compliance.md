USDA signed an order with Palantir Technologies to design, configure, deploy and manage a tool to track USDA employees' return to the office, with insights into employee and facility mapping, facility utilization and office capacity. The order's ceiling is $13,304,802.99; $8,608,990.17 has been obligated. USAspending codes the competition as full and open, under multiple-award fair opportunity, with one offer received.

The same company that built ICE's deportation targeting system now supplies the tool that tracks whether federal employees are at their desks.
