## Opening

This closes primary-source gaps in [[2026-06-18--ferc-section-206-show-cause-orders-large-load-interconnection-rm26-4-000-still-open]], which was built on trade press. FERC's own records now give the docket numbers and the next step.

## What the primary record shows (fetched 2026-10-20)

- **RM26-4-000 (the rulemaking):** the eLibrary docket sheet holds 447 entries. Its last entries are comments and motions by outside filers; the newest is dated October 8, 2026 (an individual's supplemental comments). No Commission-issued entry appears after the April 2026 intent-to-act order in the entries read (pages 4-5). No NOPR or final rule has issued. FERC's August 14, 2026 Unified Agenda still lists the next action as "Undetermined, 11/00/26" (see the June 18 entry).
- **The Section 206 proceedings:** the June 18, 2026 order (195 FERC ¶ 61,211) instituted the PJM proceeding, **EL26-67-000**; the Federal Register notice of institution published June 24, 2026. FERC's own October 20, 2026 Federal Register notices of a joint FERC-NRC meeting and a reliability technical conference list the companion dockets: SPP **EL26-68-000**, MISO **EL26-70-000** (and -001), CAISO **EL26-71-000** (and -001), PJM EL26-67-000 and -001.
- **Delay:** on July 28, 2026 PJM and the Indicated PJM Transmission Owners each moved to hold EL26-67 in abeyance for 90 days (Silver Run followed August 3). **On August 14, 2026 the Commission issued a letter order granting the abeyance** (accession 20260814-3059). Ninety days from August 14 is about **November 12, 2026**. An errata to the June 18 order was issued August 13. A rehearing request (EL26-67-001) was deemed denied by operation of law on August 20 and then dismissed by order on September 21, 2026.
- Reading: the June 18 show-cause orders did not produce a uniform rule and the PJM leg is now paused until roughly mid-November, the same month FERC's agenda gives for the rulemaking's next action. This matches the earlier entry's fragmentation finding; the new fact is that FERC itself paused the PJM proceeding.

## Not verified

Whether the abeyance covers the SPP, MISO and CAISO dockets was not checked; the June entry's report of an all-RTO extension request is a trade-press item. Commissioner statements or dissents on the June 18 order were not read (order PDF, accession 20260618-3105, not fetched). Whether the abeyance is extended past November is unknown.
