ICE's detention and removals office signed a delivery order to GardaWorld Federal Services LLC on Aug. 27 to renovate an ICE-owned structure in San Antonio into a processing and detention facility. Project Saltbox reported the award as $17.3 million. USAspending lists award 70CDCR26FR0000103 at $17,333,698.5 obligated, with a potential value of $1,008,135,768.12. The award description reads: "THIS TASK ORDER IS TO PROCURE THE RENOVATION OF EXISTING, ICE-OWNED PERMANENT STRUCTURE IN SAN ANTONIO, TX TO SERVE AS A PROCESSING AND DETENTION FACILITY AND PROVIDE ALL NECESSARY WRAPAROUND SERVICES FOR OPERATION OF THE FACILITY."

The order sits under Navy contract N0002325D0032. It is coded "FULL AND OPEN COMPETITION" and "SUBJECT TO MULTIPLE AWARD FAIR OPPORTUNITY", with 8 offers received; its solicitation identifier is blank and its FedBizOpps field reads "NOT APPLICABLE".

Saltbox reports that Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin told a House Appropriations subcommittee on June 25 that the department was no longer using the Navy program for detention services, and that the San Antonio requirement was solicited Dec. 23, 2025, before that retreat. No transcript of that testimony has been read; the words are Saltbox's account.

KSAT, republishing a San Antonio Business Journal report, said ICE awarded a $17.3 million contract on Aug. 27 for the redevelopment of an East Side warehouse into a detention center, "federal contract records show." The warehouse, Oakmont 410, is at 542 Southeast Loop 410. KSAT also reported, citing the Business Journal, that ICE acquired the warehouse in February for $66.1 million; that price is the outlet's report, and no deed or federal record read states it.

GardaWorld Federal Services LLC also holds the Surprise, Arizona order, 70CDCR26FR0000043, which USAspending lists at $313,357,612.46 obligated and $704,091,073 potential.
