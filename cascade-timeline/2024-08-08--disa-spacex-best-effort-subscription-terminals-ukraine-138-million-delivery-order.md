- 2024-08-08 (action date): the Defense Information Systems Agency (Telecommunications Division, Fort Meade) placed delivery order HC101324F0143 with Space Exploration Technologies Corp., described as "BEST EFFORT SUBSCRIPTION & TERMINALS - UKRAINE," under parent vehicle HC101323D9000. Base obligation $106,875,000; two funding-only modifications (2024-09-20, 2024-11-26) bring total obligation to $138,058,450; ceiling with options $537,733,000. Performance ends 2025-11-30, potential end 2026-04-08 (USAspending).
- What the record does not say: the description does not use the word Starshield, and it names no controlling office, tasking authority or end-user terms. The award month matches Bloomberg's August 2024 date for the Space Systems Command contract that gave about 2,500 Ukraine Starlink terminals Starshield access ([[2024-12-06--bloomberg-ssc-starshield-ukraine-contract-3000-terminals]]), but USAspending shows DISA, not Space Systems Command, as awarding office; whether they are the same award is not established by these records.
- Date kind: action date of the order, not the date it became public; the USAspending record is the first public trace found in this pass.
