This is a motion, not a ruling. There are stays of different kinds in play, and each was sought by a different side.

The September 17 preliminary injunction, won by the Plaintiff States, enjoins AAMVA from complying with the Federal Defendants' Data Demand and the Federal Defendants and other federal agencies from imposing threatened sanctions for AAMVA's non-compliance with it. The district court acted on the federal defendants' request to stay that injunction pending appeal, and on a separate request by the American Federation of Teachers under Section 705 of the Administrative Procedure Act, in separate orders dated October 29, 2026. One order said the federal defendants' Motion to Stay "be, and the same hereby is, DENIED." The second granted AFT's motion, and the Agency Defendants' Data Demand to AAMVA was "STAYED pending further order of the Court" (the earlier entry [[2026-10-08--doj-appeals-cdlis-preliminary-injunction-to-fourth-circuit]] records both orders and the appeal of the second).

The new request is the federal defendants'. On 11/05/2026 the Department of Transportation and the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration moved the Fourth Circuit, in Nos. 26-2491 (L), 26-2595, for a stay pending appeal. The motion's text is not in RECAP; its addendum is, and it reproduces both orders under the headings "Order Denying Stay of Preliminary Injunction (Oct. 29, 2026)" and "Order Granting 5 U.S.C. § 705 Stay (Oct. 29, 2026)". AFT reads the motion as seeking relief from both: FMCSA "has not shown that it is entitled to a stay of either the preliminary injunction or the section 705 stay".

The motion also overlaps a request still before the district court. AFT writes that "FMCSA moved to stay the section 705 order on November 3" and then "came to this Court less than 48 hours later", before the district court had ruled. AFT's opposition is dated November 11, 2026; the States' opposition was filed November 12, 2026 and closes: "The stay motion should be denied".

As of the newest RECAP docket entry read (the States' opposition), no Fourth Circuit order on the motion and no administrative stay appears. RECAP holds only what has been pulled from PACER, so a later filing may exist that has not been copied.
