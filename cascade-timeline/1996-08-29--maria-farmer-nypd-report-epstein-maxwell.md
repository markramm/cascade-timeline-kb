Artist Maria Farmer reports the sexual assault by Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell at Les Wexner's Ohio estate, the theft of nude photographs of her 16- and 12-year-old sisters, and threats made against her, to the New York Police Department. This is a **distinct document from the FBI complaint** Farmer filed five days later on September 3, 1996 (see [[1996-09-03--maria-farmer-fbi-complaint-epstein-maxwell]]) — NYPD advised her to also call federal law enforcement, and her lawsuit against the government cites a copy of the NYPD police report bearing this date. USA Today's December 2025 reporting, drawing on Farmer's civil lawsuit and the DOJ's Dec. 19, 2025 Epstein-files release, is the source establishing both dates; no earlier reporting on Farmer's case (2019 Daily Beast, Business Insider, Guardian coverage) specified a precise date for either report.

This entry corrects a prior cascade-timeline entry dated 1996-08-26, which conflated the NYPD and FBI reports under an unsourced date matching neither document.
