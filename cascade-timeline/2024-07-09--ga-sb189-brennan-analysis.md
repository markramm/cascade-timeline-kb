The Brennan Center warned that Georgia's SB 189, effective July 1, 2024, dramatically eases third-party challenges to voter eligibility, enabling mass challenges based on unreliable data. The law requires local boards to hold hearings when there's "probable cause," specifically including known unreliable information sources. In early July 2024, the Brennan Center reports, one individual submitted two lists comprising around 34,000 voter challenges that alleged only that these people may have a new address; Brennan does not name the county. On July 1 the Bibb County party chair challenged 243 voters and the local board put 45 of them into "challenged status." Separately, and not an SB 189 figure, ProPublica found that about 89,000 of 100,000 challenges since SB 202 became law in 2021 were submitted by six right-wing activists, in data from 30 of Georgia's 159 counties. The law prevents challenges within 45 days of elections from being heard until after certification.
