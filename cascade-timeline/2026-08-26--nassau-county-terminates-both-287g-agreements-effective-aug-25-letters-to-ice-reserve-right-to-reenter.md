**What happened.** Nassau County has terminated its formal cooperation agreements with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement under a new state law, County Executive Bruce Blakeman announced on Wednesday, Aug.26. Letters to the acting director of ICE show that both the Nassau County Police Department and the Nassau County Sheriff's Office terminated their 287(g) agreements with ICE effective Aug. 25.

**The reservation.** The letters, signed by County Attorney Thomas Adams, said the agencies opposed the "forced termination" and reserved the right to reenter agreements with ICE if the state law is determined to be unlawful. That makes the termination a conditional one, tied to the outcome of the court challenges to the state ban.

**Rensselaer.** A later WAMC account says New York State has since sued Rensselaer County and is asking a state judge to terminate the county's 287(g) agreement.
