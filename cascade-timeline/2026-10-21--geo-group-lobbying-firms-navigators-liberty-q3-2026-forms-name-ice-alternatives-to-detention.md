- 10/21/2026 (signature date on the filing, LDA database): Navigators Global LLC, an outside firm, filed its third-quarter 2026 lobbying form for The GEO Group, Inc. Its issue text reads: "Issues related to alternatives to detention within ICE and other Appropriations considerations for FY27, including Homeland Security Appropriations legislation (H.R.9310)."
- 10/23/2026 (signature date): Liberty Government Affairs, a second firm working under Navigators on the same client, filed its own third-quarter form with the issue text: "Issues related to alternatives to detention within ICE and other Appropriations considerations for FY25 and FY26."
