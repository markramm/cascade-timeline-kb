Deputy Secretary of Defense Steve Feinberg's memorandum designating Palantir's Maven Smart System a Pentagon program of record is dated March 9, per DefenseScoop. Reuters reported it on March 20, from a letter it reviewed that had not been previously reported.

## What the memo directs

Reuters reported that the memo ordered oversight of Maven moved from the National Geospatial Intelligence Agency to the Chief Digital Artificial Intelligence Office within 30 days, and that future contracting with Palantir will be handled by the Army. The decision is expected to take effect by the end of the fiscal year in September. Feinberg wrote of making AI-enabled decision-making "the cornerstone of our strategy".

## The Claude complication

Reuters noted that one potential complication in deeper Maven adoption is the software's use of the Anthropic-made Claude AI tool, as it had previously reported, while the Pentagon had recently deemed Anthropic a supply chain risk.
