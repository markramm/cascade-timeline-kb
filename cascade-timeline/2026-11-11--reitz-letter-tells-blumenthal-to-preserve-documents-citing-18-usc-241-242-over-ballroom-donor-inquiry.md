Aaron Reitz, U.S. attorney for the Southern District of Texas, sent Sen. Richard Blumenthal, D-Conn., a letter on Wednesday directing him to preserve documents. Democracy Docket quotes the letter as giving "notice of your obligation to preserve all documents, communications, and other electronically stored information relevant to your intent to deprive citizens of their core constitutional rights in violation of 18 USC statutes 241 and 242." It sets a Nov. 18 deadline for Blumenthal to respond. Democracy Docket writes that Blumenthal spearheaded an investigation into donors to Trump’s ballroom; the letter, as quoted, refers to his "efforts to suppress associational activities for philanthropic projects throughout the nation’s capital." Blumenthal answered on social media that "the implied threat of an investigation is baseless."

Reitz also sent letters to Sen. Elissa Slotkin, D-Mich. ([[2026-11-12--reitz-letter-tells-slotkin-to-preserve-documents-over-go-hard-on-accountability-comments]]) and Sen. Elizabeth Warren, D-Mass. ([[2026-11-15--reitz-letter-accuses-warren-of-wielding-gavel-like-tomahawk-demands-preservation-by-nov-23]]). Democracy Docket reports that legal experts say Reitz's letters make specious legal arguments. The letter itself was not obtained here; the text above is as quoted by Democracy Docket.
