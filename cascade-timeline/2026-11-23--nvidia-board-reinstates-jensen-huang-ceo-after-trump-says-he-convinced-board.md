On November 23, 2026, Nvidia announced that its board had voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president and chief executive, saying the board received input from senior levels of the United States government and was confident his reinstatement poses no national security risk. Speaking to CNBC that day, President Trump said of the board, "I spoke to them and I convinced them," and, of Huang, "We gave him assurance, at least with everything we know, and we know a lot." Nvidia shares closed up 31.48 per cent on November 23, according to the International Business Times. The Independent reported that the president holds between $13 million and $62 million in Nvidia stock, according to financial records.

## Update (2026-11-23, market close): Nvidia's record close and options volume; Schumer's reaction

CNN Business reported that Nvidia shares ended the day up 31.48%, their best day since 2003. CNBC reported that more than 11 million options contracts traded on Nvidia stock Monday, the most active single day of trading for the stock's options in history. The founder of the options-analytics firm Volland told CNBC that traders "were all repricing, which created record volume, but did not create any obvious one-sidedness."

Senator Chuck Schumer wrote on X, as Axios reported, that "Trump is putting his own financial interests and the needs of his billionaire buddies before our national security."
