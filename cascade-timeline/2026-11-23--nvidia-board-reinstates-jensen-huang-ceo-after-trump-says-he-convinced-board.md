On November 23, 2026, Nvidia announced that its board had voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president and chief executive, saying the board received input from senior levels of the United States government and was confident his reinstatement poses no national security risk. Speaking to CNBC that day, President Trump said of the board, "I spoke to them and I convinced them," and, of Huang, "We gave him assurance, at least with everything we know, and we know a lot." Nvidia shares closed up 31% after the announcement. The Independent reported that the president holds between $13 million and $62 million in Nvidia stock, according to financial records.
