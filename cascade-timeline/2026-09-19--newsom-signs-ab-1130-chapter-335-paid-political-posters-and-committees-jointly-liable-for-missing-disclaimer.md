AB 1130, authored by Berman and Blanca Rubio, was approved by the Governor and chaptered on September 19, 2026 as Chapter 335. It amends one section of the Government Code that already required a person a committee pays to post political content online to disclose the payment. The Assembly concurred in the Senate amendments with Ayes 79.

The change is in who answers for a missing disclaimer. The Legislative Counsel's digest says existing law exempted the poster from administrative, civil and criminal penalties and left the Fair Political Practices Commission only an injunction. The chaptered text now provides that when a paid poster omits the disclaimer, "that person and the committee shall be jointly and severally liable for any civil or administrative penalties incurred for the violation." The disclaimer duty is unchanged: the person must "concurrently include a disclaimer with that content stating that the person was paid by the committee in connection with the posting." The committee must also mark the payment on its campaign report with "a statement that the payment was provided for a paid third-party post."

The bill text contains neither a dollar penalty (the per-violation ceiling reported in press coverage comes from the general FPPC penalty section, not from this bill) nor an operative-date clause. Payments the law addresses are documented in [[2026-08-30--nyt-campaigns-paying-political-influencers-steyer-porter-sideshift-majority-forward-no-federal-disclosure-rule]].
