U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani (D. Mass.) granted the plaintiff organizations' renewed motion for a preliminary injunction in *League of Women Voters of Massachusetts v. Trump* on August 11, 2026. Her order (Doc. 183) enjoins the Postal Service defendants from implementing, giving effect to or enforcing Section 3 of Executive Order 14399 for the November 3, 2026 or any earlier federal election. It is the one injunction entered in that case in this window.

It follows her June 25 judgment in the related *California v. Trump*, which enjoined Sections 2 and 3 only as to the twenty-three plaintiff States and D.C. ([[2026-06-25--talwani-blocks-trump-mail-voting-eo-23-states-dc]]). The Supreme Court later stayed that California injunction ([[2026-08-28--scotus-lifts-talwani-block-mail-voting-order-usps-rule-effective-nc-first-to-comply]]). Earlier entries in the sequence: [[2026-07-28--trump-races-scotus-shadow-docket-mail-ballot-restrictions]] and [[2026-08-03--scotus-sets-response-deadline-trump-mail-ballot-emergency-appeal]].
