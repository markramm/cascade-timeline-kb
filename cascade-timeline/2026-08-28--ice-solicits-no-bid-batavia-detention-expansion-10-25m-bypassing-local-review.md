## Opening

**Reported 2026-08-28: ICE has solicited a no-bid expansion of the Buffalo Federal Detention Facility
in Batavia, New York, valued between $10 million and $25 million**, in a manner the reporting
describes as bypassing local review.

## STATUS IS `reported`, NOT `confirmed` — and that is deliberate

**Both sources are tier 2 and neither is a primary procurement record.** The second is referenced by
the first rather than captured directly. **No SAM.gov solicitation number, no USAspending award, and
no contracting-officer classification has been obtained.**

**Every figure here is unverified and must not be repeated as established:**
- **"$10–25 million" is a RANGE from secondary reporting.** It is almost certainly a solicitation
  ceiling or an estimated value, **not an obligation.** A ceiling reported as spending is this
  corpus's single most repeated error.
- **"No-bid" is a characterization.** The procurement record uses specific terms — sole-source,
  limited competition, an exception under FAR 6.302 — and **which one applies is the finding.**
- **"Bypassing local review"** needs the mechanism named: is the facility federally owned (in which
  case local land-use review may simply not apply), or is review being avoided? **Those are very
  different stories** and the corpus has documented both.

## Why This Event Matters — with a caution

**Batavia is already in this corpus**: [[2026-07-09--ice-batavia-hard-sided-expansion-after-new-york-jail]]
documents a hard-sided expansion at the same facility in July. **This is a SECOND reported expansion
action at the same site seven weeks later — a sequence, not a duplicate.** Confirm that reading
against the procurement record before drafting; **two reports of an expansion are not necessarily two
expansions.**

**New York is also the state where this corpus tracks an active 287(g) termination fight** — the
August 25 compliance deadline, the AG's subpoenas to four counties, and fifteen sheriffs suing via
FAIR. **A federal detention expansion in the same state and period is adjacent, not connected. Do
not assert a relationship the record does not establish.**
