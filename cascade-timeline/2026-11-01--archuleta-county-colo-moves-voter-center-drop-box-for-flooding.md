The Colorado Secretary of State said that because of "severe continuing rain and flood risk in Pagosa Springs," the Archuleta County clerk "has implemented changes to its voting locations." Voters affected by the floods who have not voted "can visit any voter center across the state to request and receive a replacement ballot." Outside the home county, that means "a replacement ballot with only statewide races."
