On **September 26, 2026**, the White House barred **CNN** from its customary seat on Air Force One — the White House press pool's television-network slot — for President Trump's trip to a college football game in Knoxville, Tennessee, replacing CNN with the conservative outlet **Real America's Voice News**, which is not a member of the five-network broadcast pool. This came two days after Judge Kelly's TRO ordered White House hard passes restored to CNN, Politico, and MS NOW (see [[2026-09-24--judge-kelly-grants-tro-cnn-politico-msnow-press-passes]]).

In a show of solidarity, the other four broadcast pool members — **Fox News, ABC, CBS, and NBC** — declined to participate in the pool until CNN's access was restored, per Al Jazeera/AP.

**Legal ambiguity plaintiffs are now testing.** Al Jazeera reported that Judge Kelly's TRO "addressed press passes to the White House" but "did not directly mention pool duties or Air Force One." Plaintiffs' counsel **Theodore J. Boutrous Jr.** said on CNN's *The Lead with Jake Tapper* that same day that plaintiffs believe the ruling also covers pool access — signaling this is likely to become a live dispute (potentially feeding the pending non-compliance/emergency-hearing track, or the forthcoming preliminary-injunction briefing) over whether pool exclusion is a distinct restriction outside the TRO's literal scope or a continuation of the enjoined conduct.

**Sourcing note.** Originally single-sourced to Al Jazeera/AP; independently corroborated 2026-09-29 against CNN's own reporting, the Washington Post, and NPR — all confirm the exclusion, the Real America's Voice News replacement, and the Fox/ABC/CBS/NBC boycott.
