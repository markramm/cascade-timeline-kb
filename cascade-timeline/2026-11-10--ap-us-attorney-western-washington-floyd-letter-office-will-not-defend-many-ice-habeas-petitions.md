Acting U.S. Attorney Charles Floyd said in a letter to U.S. District Judge David Estudillo that "there will be many habeas cases in which the USAO will no longer assume responsibility for providing a defense on behalf of the respondents." He didn't specify how many cases could be affected. The newsletter Eyes On Ice dates the memorandum October 26, 2026 and describes a rule under which, if no Notice of Appearance is filed within seven court days of a standard scheduling order, the office will not file a response or defend the detention.

This is a different matter from the OneAmerica Votes v. Trump entries that name Floyd.
