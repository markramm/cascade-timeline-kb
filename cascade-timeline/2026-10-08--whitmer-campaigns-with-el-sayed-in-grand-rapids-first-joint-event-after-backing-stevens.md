The Detroit News reported that Gov. Gretchen Whitmer campaigned with El-Sayed at a Grand Rapids restaurant on Thursday, Oct. 8. She "endorsed El-Sayed's opponent in the Democratic Senate primary, U.S. Rep. Haley Stevens," and said at the stop: "Who is the one person talking about affordable health care? Dr. Abdul El-Sayed."
