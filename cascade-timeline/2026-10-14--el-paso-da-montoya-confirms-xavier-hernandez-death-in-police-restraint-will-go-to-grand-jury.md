El Paso District Attorney James Montoya confirmed that the case of Xavier Hernandez will go before a grand jury, according to KVIA. Hernandez died on July 13, 2025, after an encounter with El Paso police. The El Paso County Medical Examiner classified the death as homicide by asphyxia due to chest compression during law enforcement restraint. Police said a taser deployment was ineffective and that an off-duty officer and a witness helped restrain him. The case is an El Paso Police Department restraint death, not an immigration-enforcement case. KVIA reported no grand jury date, and no charges have been reported. Supporters of the family and the Texas LULAC planned a rally at the state capitol on October 18, calling for police reform and greater oversight.
