The Justice Department press release is dated Thursday, April 30, 2026. It says the Task Force published a report under Executive Order 14202 and that "The Acting Attorney General serves as Chair of the Task Force." The release quotes "Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche, Chair of the Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias."

The release describes a 200-page report with "over 1100 footnotes and over 300 pages of exhibits", and says the Justice Department "met with and received information from over 100 stakeholders and victims." The report PDF is headed "The 2026 Report by the Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias" and dated April 30, 2026.
