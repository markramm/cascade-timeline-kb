**Dates, typed.** Fieldwork: interviews were conducted online between August 20 and September 7, 2026. PRRI release: October 20, 2026. Democracy Docket's write-up, the secondary coverage that prompted this entry, carries the same October 20, 2026 date. The entry date is the release date, not the fieldwork date.

**The finding.** PRRI reports that 35% of Americans agree with the statement "if there are serious concerns about voter fraud in a local voting location, the president should order federal agents to seize the ballots and take over the vote counting," and 59% disagree. Among Republicans 58% agree.

**What "agree" is.** In PRRI's topline the agree figure is the sum of "completely agree" and "mostly agree"; it is a combined figure, not a single response box.

**What the item does not say.** It asks about "the president" generically and does not name Trump. The secondary write-up's phrasing, that respondents support Trump doing this, goes beyond the item.

**Method.** PRRI designed and conducted the survey, online, on Ipsos's KnowledgePanel with an opt-in supplement for small states: 5,200 adults on the panel plus an opt-in supplement. The margin of error for the national survey is +/- 1.6 percentage points including the design effect.

An opinion measure of a hypothetical, not an event: it records agreement with a statement, not any seizure order.
