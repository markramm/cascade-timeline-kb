DHS OIG's report on Krome North Service Processing Center is dated **September 11, 2026**. The inspection it describes was conducted **May 28 to 29, 2025**.

On the first day, there were **1,102** detainees at a facility with a maximum capacity of **882**; OIG put the overage at **25 percent**. That produced overcrowded intake hold rooms and housing units and detainees held in intake for more than **12 hours**. OIG also found that Krome staff did not "provide the minimum required number of operational toilets, sinks, and showers," among other noncompliance with detention standards. OIG made **nine** recommendations, and ICE concurred with **all 9**.

Krome was previously inspected by OIG in June 2023 (OIG-24-21).
