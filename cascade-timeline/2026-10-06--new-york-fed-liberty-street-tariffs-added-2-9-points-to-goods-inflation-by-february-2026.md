New York Fed economists Mary Amiti, Sebastian Heise and David E. Weinstein estimate that by February 2026 tariffs had contributed 2.9 percentage points to goods price inflation, and that nearly 90 percent of the 2025 tariffs were passed through to U.S. import prices. The estimate covers sixty-seven non-oil goods categories and excludes services.

The authors say the comparisons cannot establish how much of broader price movements was itself caused by tariffs. Related: [[2025-04-02--liberation-day-tariffs-consumer-price-shock]] and [[2026-02-20--scotus-strikes-ieepa-tariffs-6-3-historic-ruling]].
