Skydance and Paramount officially closed their $8 billion merger on Thursday. The new company will trade under the "PSKY" ticker, and David Ellison is Chairman and CEO of Paramount, a Skydance Corporation.

The FCC's approval came on July 24. Paramount agreed to pay a $16 million settlement to the president in early July. Skydance management had assured regulators that it would hire an ombudsman to review any complaints about fairness.
