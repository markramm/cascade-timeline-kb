The FBI complaint filed by artist Maria Farmer against Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell — describing sexual assault at Les Wexner's Ohio estate, Epstein's theft of nude photographs of her 16- and 12-year-old sisters, her belief he was selling the photos to "potential buyers," his request that she "take pictures of young girls at swimming pools," and his threat to burn her house down if she told anyone — is stamped September 3, 1996. The document was released, heavily redacted, as part of the Justice Department's Dec. 19, 2025 Epstein-files release; it is the specific document USA Today reported on Dec. 21, 2025, and the one Rep. Robert Garcia cited when he asked the DOJ Inspector General on Dec. 23, 2025 to investigate the FBI's decade of inaction. The FBI took no action on the complaint for roughly a decade, until the 2005 Palm Beach Police investigation eventually surfaced Epstein's abuse independently.

This document is distinct from — and filed five days after — Farmer's Aug. 29, 1996 report to the New York Police Department (see [[1996-08-29--maria-farmer-nypd-report-epstein-maxwell]]). NYPD had advised her to also contact federal law enforcement.
