Lee County, Georgia commissioners extended the county's data center moratorium a second time. The Lee County Board of Commissioners met Tuesday, Nov. 10 to consider a resolution to extend the moratorium on data centers in unincorporated areas of the county to June 30, 2027, and the extension was approved at Tuesday night's meeting. The board had adopted Resolution No. 2025-20 on the 9th day of December, 2025, then Resolution 2026-12 on June 9, 2026, which extended the termination date from July 30, 2026, to December 31, 2026. The new resolution directs county staff to continue drafting an ordinance regulating data center location. If the county adopts an ordinance before then, the moratorium will end automatically.
