**Correction: this entry's original premise is not supported and is withdrawn (status: disputed).** The original text had Texas, Missouri, North Carolina, Ohio, Florida and Tennessee each scheduling a special session for August of this year. None of its three sources says that: the Brennan Center piece is dated April 30, 2026 and the KQED episode May 5, 2026 (both about the Supreme Court ruling in *Louisiana v. Callais*), and The Fulcrum piece describes early-2026 redistricting fights, before that ruling. The source dates on the original were wrong. The legislatures' own records show no August redistricting session this year in the six states; each had already moved earlier. What each date is:

| State | What the record shows | Kind of date |
|---|---|---|
| Texas | the redistricting bill, filed 08/15/2025, signed 08/29/2025 ([Texas Legislature Online](https://capitol.texas.gov/BillLookup/History.aspx?LegSess=892&Bill=HB4)) | filing and enactment, August **2025** |
| Missouri | Governor convened the special session August 29 and the General Assembly passed the bill on September 12([governor's office](https://governor.mo.gov/press-releases/archive/governor-kehoe-signs-missouri-first-map-law)) | session call and passage, **2025** |
| North Carolina | Session law chaptered 10/22/2025 ([ncleg.gov](https://www.ncleg.gov/BillLookUp/2025/S249)) | enactment, October **2025** |
| Ohio | Redistricting Commission approved the map October 31, 2025 ([Bricker Graydon](https://www.bricker.com/insights/publications/ohio-approves-new-congressional-map-what-it-means-for-2026-elections), secondary; the commission's own page could not be retrieved) | commission adoption, **2025** |
| Florida | the Special Session D bill, filed 4/27/2026, Senate passage 4/29/2026 ([Florida Senate](https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026D/1D)) | filing and passage, April 2026 |
| Tennessee | The bill passed the Senate on 05/07/2026 and was signed by the Governor 06/03/2026 ([General Assembly](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default.aspx?BillNumber=HB7003&GA=114)) | passage May 2026, signature June 2026 |

The likely source of the error is two real August sessions, Texas's second called session and Missouri's special session, both in **2025**, which the entry placed in 2026 and extended to four states that did not hold one. The 2025 sessions are covered in [[2025-07-25--texas-passes-redistricting-without-democrats]] and [[2025-09-08--missouri-gop-mid-decade-gerrymander]]; the *Callais* ruling is in [[2026-04-29--scotus-callais-guts-section-2-vra-reworks-gingles-standard]]. The state-by-state position as of this date is in [[2026-mid-decade-redistricting-state-by-state-status-as-of-2026-10-15]].

What survives from the original is the Callais ruling itself ([KQED](https://www.kqed.org/news/12082390/what-the-supreme-courts-voting-rights-act-ruling-means-for-the-2026-elections)); Florida and Tennessee moved after it, on the dates above. The original's inference of coordination from a single month had no basis in these dates and is withdrawn. The Texas litigation sequence the original linked (the December 2025 stay and the later summary reversal) is unchanged: [[2025-12-04--supreme-court-reinstates-texas-mid-decade-redistricting-map]], [[2026-04-27--supreme-court-summarily-reverses-texas-congressional-map-ruling-abbott-v-lulac]].
