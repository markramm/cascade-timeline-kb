Eyes on ICE, in a synthesis of a Marshall Project and The 19th investigation dated Oct. 14, 2026, reports that ICE recorded 18 miscarriages in detention during the first 9 months of 2025 while booking nearly 500 pregnant or nursing women, and that ICE quietly stopped compiling or releasing data on in-custody miscarriages after October 3, 2025. ICE claims it has "no updated information" on the subject, per Eyes on ICE. DHS disputes the data-stoppage framing: NPR, relaying Guardian U.S. reporting, says the reporting found that ICE has lost track of the number of miscarriages happening in custody, and a DHS spokesperson responded: "ICE tracks miscarriage data and reports the information on a monthly basis."

The Eyes on ICE piece centers on the Bluebonnet Detention Center in Jones County, Texas, which it says is operated by the private prison conglomerate Management and Training Corporation (MTC) under an Intergovernmental Service Agreement with Jones County and overseen by the U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) Dallas Field Office, under ICE Contract 70CDCR19DIG000017. Citing records and testimony gathered by the Marshall Project and The 19th, it reports that poultry served to pregnant detainees at Bluebonnet was routinely pink, cold, and visibly undercooked.

Eyes on ICE also reports that, following 33 reported detainee deaths in 2025 and at least 29 additional deaths recorded by the National Immigration Project (NIPNLG) through October 19, 2026, the DHS Office of Inspector General was forced to announce a formal review of detainee deaths and use of force across ICE custody; the source does not give the date of that announcement.
