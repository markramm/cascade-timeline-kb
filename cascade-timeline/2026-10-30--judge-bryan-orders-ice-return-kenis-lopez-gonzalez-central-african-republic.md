U.S. District Judge Jeffrey M. Bryan ordered on Friday that ICE return Kenis Lopez Gonzalez to Minnesota from the Central African Republic, the Minnesota Reformer reports. An immigration judge had ruled that he could not be deported back to Nicaragua because he was at risk of being tortured there. Lopez said officers shot men on the bus with pepper balls and rubber bullets at close range, and that he was placed in a 'wrap' and carried onto the plane. One of his attorneys said: "ICE violated Mr. Lopez's constitutional rights, and hardly even denied it." The Reformer reports the government paid $85 million to the International Organization for Migration in the Central African Republic, and that a letter asked DHS to "refrain from publicly discussing our cooperation with CAR on this matter."
