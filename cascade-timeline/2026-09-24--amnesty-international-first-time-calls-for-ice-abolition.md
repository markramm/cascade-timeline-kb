Amnesty International called for the abolition of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement on September 24, 2026, in a new report. MPR News reported that it is the first time the organization has called to abolish ICE. The report, "We Had Whistles, They Had Guns": Human Rights Violations in U.S. Immigration Enforcement Operations and the Case to Abolish ICE, analyses four of the Trump administration’s large-scale, militarised immigration enforcement operations, in Chicago, Minneapolis-St. Paul, New Orleans and Washington, DC. Amnesty says it interviewed 154 people across the four locations from March to June 2026, and that three people were shot and killed by ICE and Border Patrol agents during those operations: Silverio Villegas González, Renee Good and Alex Pretti.

In its conclusion Amnesty "calls for ICE to be abolished". It assesses that the three killings "may amount to extrajudicial executions"; that is Amnesty's characterization under international human rights law, not a court finding. Amnesty says it offered ICE, DHS and the governments of Illinois, Louisiana, Minnesota and Washington, DC an opportunity to respond to its findings, and "did not receive a response from any of these actors".
