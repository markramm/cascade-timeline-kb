Edwin Giovanni Lopez-Cornejo, a 41-year-old man from El Salvador, died on August 1, 2026, in ICE custody at the Delaney Hall Detention Facility in Newark, New Jersey, after being denied his epilepsy and diabetes medication. He is the 23rd person to die in ICE custody in 2026 and the second detainee to die at Delaney Hall this year. The 1,000-bed facility operates under a 15-year, $1.2 billion contract between GEO Group and ICE. The death has triggered calls from advocates and elected officials for a federal investigation, and comes days after a House Judiciary Committee oversight hearing examined conditions at the facility. Tier-1 reporting from Documented NY and CNN confirms the death date, the medication-denial allegation, and the contract figure.

Lopez-Cornejo's death follows that of Jean Wilson Brutus, who died at Delaney Hall on December 12, 2025 — one day after intake — the facility's first fatality (see [[2026-06-02--nj-sues-geo-group-delaney-hall-health-inspector-access]]). Two deaths inside a single facility within roughly eight months, both preceded by documented conditions fights — the May 2026 hunger strike and protest crackdowns ([[2026-05-26--delaney-hall-newark-hunger-strike-geo-group-protests-pepper-ball-clashes-sen-andy-kim]], [[2026-05-30--delaney-hall-nj-state-police-mass-arrests-kettling-protest-crackdown]]) and New Jersey's lawsuit over health-inspector access ([[2026-06-02--nj-sues-geo-group-delaney-hall-health-inspector-access]]) — mark this as a systemic medical-neglect pattern rather than an isolated incident. The 23rd-death-of-the-year count extends a documented 2026 pace that HRW and Reuters have separately flagged as roughly double historical rates ([[2026-06-17--reuters-ice-detention-death-rate-doubles-50-deaths-overcrowding-medical-failure]], [[2026-06-25--hrw-documents-52-ice-custody-deaths-un-demands-investigation]]), following the 20th and 22nd deaths earlier in the summer ([[2026-06-26--felix-alcorta-rodriguez-twentieth-ice-custody-death-2026]], [[2026-07-16--arenas-silva-dies-ice-transport-irwin-county-22nd-death]]). GEO Group's Delaney Hall contract sits alongside its broader 2026 earnings expansion ([[2026-05-06--geo-group-q1-2026-results-raises-guidance]]) and its Adelanto facility's own death cluster in California ([[2026-03-30--adelanto-geo-fourth-death-california-record-seven-months]]), reinforcing that medical-neglect deaths are concentrated in the same small set of contractors expanding fastest under ICE's 2026 detention build-out.
