Thousands of counter-protesters gathered on Jan. 17 outside Minneapolis City Hall and stopped Jake Lang's anti-Islam rally before it began (Unicorn Riot). Lang had announced on X that Americans Against Islamification would hold a "CRUSADER MARCH" on "Little Somalia"; the plan was a Quran burning at 1 p.m. followed by a march on the Cedar-Riverside neighborhood, home to a large Somali population. Protesters chased him from City Hall through a hotel lobby. He later asked supporters on GiveSendGo to crowdfund $20,000 for medical expenses and bodyguards and raised roughly $15,000.

The rally fell inside Operation Metro Surge: DHS dates its launch to December 1, 2025, and border czar Tom Homan announced its conclusion in February ("I have proposed, and President Trump has concurred, that this surge operation conclude"). Lang's other Twin Cities appearances in that window were aimed at anti-ICE protesters and displays rather than at Muslims; they are logged in the research note, not bound here.

**Not established.** Any coordination between Lang and DHS. The overlap is one of time and place.
