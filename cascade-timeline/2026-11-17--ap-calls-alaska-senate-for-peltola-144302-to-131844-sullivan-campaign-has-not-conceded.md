The Associated Press called the Alaska Senate race for the Democratic candidate, Peltola, at 10:19 p.m. Eastern time on Tuesday, November 17, according to Newsweek. Peltola received 144,302 votes to Sen. Dan Sullivan's 131,844, according to the AP. She received 50.1 percent of first-choice votes, according to the AP.

The result remains unofficial, according to Newsweek. The state has targeted November 25 to certify the result.

Sullivan's campaign has not conceded. On Monday, the day before the call, his campaign said it would let the process run its course "until every legal vote is counted and certified," according to Newsweek.

Trump argued in a November 5 Truth Social post that the votes for Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. should be handed to the "REAL Dan Sullivan," according to Newsweek. These are Trump's statements. The Justice Department has appealed the dismissal of its certification suit to the 9th Circuit, according to Newsweek.
