GSA's Federal Acquisition Service recorded a $1,232,840 funding-only modification, PS0002, to its delivery order with Amazon Web Services, signed on this entry's date. Its description in the federal procurement record includes the words "INCLUDING TTS AMERICA.GOV".

The full description reads: "INCREMENTAL FUNDING FOR BASE PERIOD CLINS 0001, 0002, INCLUDING TTS AMERICA.GOV, ADD PWS ATTACHMENT FOR AMERICA.GOV TASK".

Counting all of the order's actions in the record, obligations total $8,221,003.30. The $462,791,242.03 figure on the order is its base-and-all-options value, a ceiling, and has not been obligated.

The public record does not name an AI model service. The "PWS attachment" the description mentions is not published there, so what the America.gov task requires of AWS is not established here, nor how much of the obligated money goes to it.

Date kind: signed date of the modification as recorded in FPDS.
