A transgender woman from Singapore was met by four men identifying as ICE agents at the Rochester airport around 12:30 a.m. on October 28, according to her account as reported by LGBTQ Nation and Eyes on ICE. She reports 18 hours in custody at Batavia and says the warrant she was shown didn't have a judicial signature. After a medical check by two female staff, she was released with an ankle monitor. ICE has not commented, Eyes on ICE reports. She is not named here.
