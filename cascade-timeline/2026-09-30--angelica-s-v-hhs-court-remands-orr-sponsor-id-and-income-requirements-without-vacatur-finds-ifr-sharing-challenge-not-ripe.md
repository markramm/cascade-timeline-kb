Judge Dabney L. Friedrich ruled on cross-motions for summary judgment in Angelica S. v. HHS on 09/30/26. The order granted the plaintiffs' motion in part and denied it in part.

The order remands the case to HHS without vacatur. The agency is to reconsider its sponsor information-collection requirements.

The opinion found that ORR did not obtain OMB approval before imposing its revised sponsor requirements. In the opinion's words: "Yet ORR did not get OMB approval for its revised Sponsor Application Packet before imposing the new requirements."

The case also challenged the interim final rule that removed the bar on ORR sharing sponsor immigration status with enforcement entities ([[2025-03-25--hhs-orr-ifr-removes-bar-on-sharing-sponsor-immigration-status-with-ice]]). The court held that this challenge is not ripe: "The challenge to the IFR is thus not ripe."

The court gave this reason. "The present record does not establish whether ORR will start sharing sponsors" immigration information with DHS, the opinion says.

The court did not rule on whether any sharing is lawful. It also did not rule on whether sharing is occurring.

On the earlier preliminary injunction: "It remains in effect."

Footnote to the reissued opinion: "This memorandum opinion includes corrections of clerical errors."
