Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on CNBC's Squawk Box, per Newsweek, that "Treasury, War, USTR, and State, they were in favor of putting Megaspeed on the entity list for months," and that "Commerce has the pen, Commerce BIS has the pen, and you will have to ask them about their internal processes" ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/scott-bessent-howard-lutnick-nvidia-scandal-12712195)).

Megaspeed is not on the Entity List. A Nvidia spokesperson is quoted, in a Financial Times report relayed by BigGo, saying "Megaspeed is not on the Entity List or a recipient of a Commerce Department advance notice letter." Our own search of the Federal Register for Megaspeed and for Swiftdata returned no documents, and the Federal Register's BIS rules since late 2025 include no listing of either. That search is fuzzy full-text, does not cover unpublished actions, and did not read the supplement's own text; the finding is bounded to those corpora.

Not established: why Commerce has not acted. The list of supporting agencies is one person's televised statement, and the BigGo summary of the FT names a different set.
