Public Citizen, The Alliance for Secure AI and other nonprofits sued on Nov. 12 in San Francisco Superior Court, according to Just Security. They are asking for an immediate injunction barring Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX Super Intelligence from building or deploying AI systems more capable than their current most advanced models.

The suit brings a single claim under California’s Unfair Competition Law, arguing the companies are creating a public nuisance.
