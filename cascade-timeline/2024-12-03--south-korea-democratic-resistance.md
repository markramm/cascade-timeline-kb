On December 3, 2024, according to CSIS,  President Yoon Suk Yeol "declared emergency martial law in an unannounced nationally televised address at 10:30 p.m. (KST)". It continues: "as of 1:00 a.m. (KST), the National Assembly passed a resolution demanding the lifting of martial law, with all 190 members present out of 300 voting in favor."

The constitutional rule, in CSIS's words: "when the National Assembly, with the consent of a majority of its members, requests the lifting of martial law, the president is required to comply." The resolution was a request that the president had to obey; it did not itself cancel the decree. Yoon lifted the declaration at 4:30 a.m. on December 4 "in accordance with the majority vote in the National Assembly."
