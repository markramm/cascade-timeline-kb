A third American aircraft carrier has arrived in the Middle East, a U.S. official said, and according to another official the USS Theodore Roosevelt arrived on November 15. The Navy previously had three carrier strike groups in the Middle East in April, for the first time since 2003. In addition to the three aircraft carriers, the flotilla in the Arabian Sea now includes 13 destroyers, two cruisers and a littoral combat ship, according to the Navy.

Lloyd's List assessments show traffic through the strait remains at less than a third of prewar levels. Mines have damaged six oil tankers since Sunday, according to the Revolutionary Guard's navy, and CENTCOM said it has redirected 150 merchant vessels since June under the blockade. Trump also declared that the U.S. no longer needs a deal with Tehran and told Vance, a chief negotiator in earlier ceasefire talks, to focus on the administration's domestic anti-fraud campaign. Vance had publicly broken with Trump over the conflict, posting that "the reason we lost is the war."
