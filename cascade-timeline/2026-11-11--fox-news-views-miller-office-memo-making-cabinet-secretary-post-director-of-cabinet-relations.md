Fox News Digital reported on November 11, 2026 that a memo from the office of White House chief of staff Stephen Miller circulated within the administration that week. The memo was sent by Miller's office after he took over the role on Monday, and Fox News Digital viewed it.

The memo says "The White House position of Cabinet Secretary will be changed to the role of Director of Cabinet Relations, focused on providing support and reinforcement of President Trump's tremendous Cabinet Secretaries." Fox reports, from a source familiar with the message, that Cabinet secretaries will have more control over their own messaging.
