On the afternoon of **August 18, 2026**, a man in a gray sweatsuit climbed the steps to an Eastern Airlines flight at Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport, bound first for Alexandria, Louisiana and then El Paso, Texas. He was chained at the ankles, wrists, and stomach, and he was the **5,000th immigrant detainee** flown out of Minnesota's flagship airport since November 2025. The count is not the government's. It belongs to **Nick Benson**, a Minneapolis resident who has stood on the top floor of an airport parking garage with two zoom-lens cameras since November, photographing and filming each flight — that afternoon, fourteen men and two women boarding one plane.

The methodological detail is what makes this a record rather than a tally: Benson tracks the flights in person **and cross-checks his counts against Department of Homeland Security data** to confirm their accuracy. Roughly 5,000 people in nine months implies a sustained rate near 550 per month from a single airport. ICE's air transport network moves detainees between facilities without contemporaneous public records, and the practical effect of each transfer is distance — from counsel, from family, from the immigration court where a case was filed. A civilian with a camera and a parking-garage vantage has produced the public series that the agency's own reporting does not provide.

This is the transport layer of a Minnesota enforcement picture the corpus has documented from several directions in the same period: HSI's undercover infiltration of anti-ICE community meetings under Operation Puppet Master ([[2026-08-13--operation-puppet-master-hsi-infiltrates-minnesota-anti-ice-community]]), the financial-surveillance operation against Minnesota unions ([[2026-02-15--project-whipple-shield-hsi-financial-surveillance-minnesota-unions]]), a county attorney charging an ICE agent with assault ([[2026-04-16--moriarty-charges-ice-agent-morgan-assault-minnesota]]), and a federal judge dismissing the DOJ's voter-roll suit as a fishing expedition ([[2026-08-17--minnesota-judge-dismisses-doj-voter-roll-suit-fishing-expedition-0-23]]). It also belongs beside the corpus's other documented ICE Air finding, the coordination with the Iranian government on deportation flights during the Twelve-Day War ([[2026-08-18--intercept-ice-coordinated-iran-deportation-flights-during-twelve-day-war]]) — dated the same day, and reached by the same route of outside documentation rather than agency disclosure.
