On March 24, 2026, the U.S. Senate voted 47 to 53 on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 116 from the Committee on Foreign Relations. The Senate's record gives the result as "Motion to Discharge Rejected". Vote Number: 69.

The measure's title is "A joint resolution to direct the removal of United States Armed Forces from hostilities within or against the Islamic Republic of Iran that have not been authorized by Congress".
