Civil rights attorneys from Illinois and Indiana filed a federal lawsuit Thursday over conditions at Miami Correctional Facility in Bunker Hill, Indiana, which DHS is expanding for ICE use by 1,000 beds in partnership with the state. Since 2025, at least two immigrants have died in custody there, the Sun-Times reported. ACLU of Illinois staff attorney Kathleen Hickey said: "People are suffering, people have died, and more people will suffer and die if those continue." DHS denied the allegations. The complaint itself was not reviewed for this entry.
