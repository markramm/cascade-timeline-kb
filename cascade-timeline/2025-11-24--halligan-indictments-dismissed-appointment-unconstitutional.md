On November 24, 2025, Senior United States District Judge Cameron McGowan Currie, sitting by designation in the Eastern District of Virginia, granted James Comey's motion to dismiss the indictment. Her order holds that the appointment of Lindsey Halligan as Interim U.S. Attorney "violated 28 U.S.C. § 546 and the Appointments Clause of the U.S. Constitution," and the indictment is dismissed without prejudice. The motion argued that Halligan was "the sole prosecutor who presented the case to the grand jury."
