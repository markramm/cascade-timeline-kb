Attorney General Pamela Bondi's memorandum is dated September 29, 2025 and is subject-lined "ENDING POLITICAL VIOLENCE AGAINST ICE." It is addressed to the heads of the FBI, ATF, the U.S. Marshals Service, DEA and the Executive Office for U.S. Attorneys.

It says: "All state and local law enforcement officers protecting ICE facilities and personnel are hereby added to a temporary ICE Protection Task Force alongside each of the Department's law enforcement components". It directs the U.S. Attorney's Offices in Oregon and Northern Illinois to "charge all such persons with the highest provable offense available under the law."

The charging priority reaches beyond people caught in the act: the Department "will arrest and prosecute to the fullest extent of the law every person who aids, abets, or conspires to commit these crimes, whether through funding, coordination, planning, or other means".

This memo is not the later implementing guidance recorded in the entry linked below.
