The Department of Energy announced new initiatives at its Science: A New Golden Age Summit on October 8, 2026.

DOE said it is identifying 190 priority dataset repositories aligned with the Genesis Mission's national challenges. DOE said the repositories will lay the initial foundation for government-wide data federation with the Genesis Mission Platform.

DOE said it is investing $30 million in National Laboratory-led robotics and autonomous-science testbeds.

DOE said its new awards bring the Genesis Mission's first-year portfolio to 297 projects.
