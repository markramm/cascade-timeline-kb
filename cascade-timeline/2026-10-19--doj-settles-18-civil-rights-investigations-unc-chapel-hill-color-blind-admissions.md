The Justice Department said Monday, that it reached a settlement with UNC-Chapel Hill that closes 18 civil rights investigations without financial penalty. As WRAL reports the Justice Department's description, UNC will adopt a "color-blind" admission system, hire a Title VI coordinator (with system-wide antisemitism training and a mask ban), and hire a "Merit & Civil Rights administrator" within the next 30 days. The settlement is in effect through the end of 2028. UNC said the settlement neither admitted wrongdoing or liability nor made a monetary settlement payment; Bloomberg Law, quoted by TaxProf Blog, describes the deal as without adverse findings or penalty.

Context: WRAL notes it is unclear who would define antisemitism for discipline purposes or how the mask ban would be enforced.
