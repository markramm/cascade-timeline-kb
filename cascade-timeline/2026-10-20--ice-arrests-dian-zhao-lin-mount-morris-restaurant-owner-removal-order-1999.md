ICE arrested Dian Zhao Lin, who has owned and operated the Mun Yuen Chinese restaurant in Mount Morris, New York for more than 20 years, on October 20, 2026. ICE says an immigration judge ordered him removed on November 29, 1999 and that the Board of Immigration Appeals denied his motion to reopen on July 26, 2023. His family says he has been working to get his green card for six years. He is held at the Batavia federal detention facility.

The community responded with a "Free Mr. Lin" protest on Saturday, Oct. 24, 2026 and a Change.com petition with more than 7,000 signatures. Gov. Kathy Hochul said she spoke with Secretary Mullin about his arrest and that of German Machado.
