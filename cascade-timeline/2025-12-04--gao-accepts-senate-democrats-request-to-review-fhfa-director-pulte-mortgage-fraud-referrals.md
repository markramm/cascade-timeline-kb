A GAO spokesperson told CBS News on the date of this entry: "I can confirm that GAO has accepted this request following our standard process."

The senators' letter asked GAO to look into "referrals of New York Attorney General Letitia James, U.S. Senator Adam Schiff, Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook, and Congressman Eric Swalwell to the U.S. Department of Justice for mortgage fraud." The senators said they want GAO to "determine whether the agency and its employees misused federal authority and resources." GAO has not answered that question in anything this entry holds.

The spokesperson described the first step: "The first thing GAO does as any work begins is to determine the full scope of what we will cover and the methodology to be used." No GAO report or finding is among the sources. CBS News: "Some Trump administration critics say Pulte is using his authority to target political opponents." NBC News: "All the officials Pulte has referred to the Justice Department have repeatedly denied wrongdoing." The FHFA declined to comment.
