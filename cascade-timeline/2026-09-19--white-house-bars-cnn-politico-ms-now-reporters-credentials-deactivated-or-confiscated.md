On Saturday, September 19, 2026 reporters from **CNN**, **Politico** and **MS NOW** were turned away at the White House after Trump announced the ban on Friday. Al Jazeera, with the Associated Press, reports they "have had their press credentials deactivated or confiscated." Which verb applies differs by reporter: Salon reports that CNN's Betsy Klein "reported that her credential had been deactivated, while Politico said Haslett's badge was confiscated." MS NOW's Akayla Gardner "passed through two security gates before discovering that her White House badge had been disabled."

Only one account names an agency: Politico said "Secret Service denied her entry to the complex and confiscated the pass that allows her access to the White House." The Gardner account says an officer, without naming an agency.

Asked about the timing, Trump said it was "really just cumulative stories over the past few years." Salon: "The president did not identify a specific report that prompted the decision."

Politico: "we will vigorously defend our First Amendment rights." MS NOW: "MS NOW intends to take any and all steps necessary to defend our First Amendment rights."
