Gov. Jared Polis signed an executive order that, per the Governor's Office, activates "unarmed members of the Colorado National Guard" to assist with election cybersecurity defense for the statewide election on June 30, 2026. The Governor's Office says the Guard has been activated for this each year since 2019. Polis "signed an executive order on Wednesday", KKCO reported in a story published Jun. 17, 2026.
