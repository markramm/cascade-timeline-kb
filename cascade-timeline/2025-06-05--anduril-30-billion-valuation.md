Anduril Industries raised $2.5 billion at a $30.5 billion valuation in a Series G round, with Founders Fund leading. Anduril chairman Trae Stephens, who is also a partner at Founders Fund, disclosed the round on Bloomberg Television on June 5, 2025, and said Founders Fund put in $1 billion, the largest check the firm has written.

## What the sources say

CNBC reports that the round "doubles Anduril's valuation from August" and was led by Peter Thiel's Founders Fund. TechCrunch reports that "Existing investors also piled in" according to an Anduril spokesperson, who said the round was over 8x times oversubscribed, and that the company raised after it "doubled its revenue in 2024 to about $1 billion". Neither names any other investor.

Stephens gave the reason as balance-sheet strength: it was "really important to shore up the balance sheet and make sure we have the ability to deploy capital into these manufacturing and production problem sets that we're working on".

## 1789 Capital

ABC News reports that 1789 Capital "invested $50 million into Anduril Industries as part of a larger $2.5 billion funding round last year", according to Anduril's founder. Our reading is that it is this round, because the amount matches.
