The Kentucky Lantern reports that the Mercer County Fiscal Court rejected zoning regulations on Monday that would have allowed the siting of large data centers on land adjacent to a Kentucky Utilities coal-fired power plant. The unanimous vote came at a special meeting and sends the proposed regulations back to a planning and zoning commission. The county had passed a one-year moratorium on data center construction in August that would have expired automatically had the fiscal court adopted the regulations.

County Judge-Executive Sarah Steele said "I am not completely anti-data center, but we have to get this right before we approve it" to more than 170 people at the meeting. Earlier instruments in this fight are in [[2026-08-17--mercer-county-ky-hyperscale-opposition-anthropic-tied-datacenter]].
