A Brown County judge on Thursday dismissed a lawsuit against Elon Musk alleging that payments he made to voters during the 2025 Wisconsin Supreme Court campaign amounted to bribery and an illegal lottery. In his ruling, Judge Marc Hammer said the plaintiffs, the Wisconsin Democracy Campaign and two voters, lacked standing because they could not prove a concrete injury to themselves, writing, "That is not a concrete enough injury to give rise to standing."

Wisconsin Public Radio calls it the second unsuccessful attempt at targeting Musk's involvement in the race. Earlier this year the Wisconsin Elections Commission referred criminal charges to a special prosecutor over Musk offering $1 million to two people in the days before the election, and the special prosecutor declined to pursue the recommended charges, saying he did not believe he could successfully get a conviction. The ruling itself was not read, and WPR does not say whether the plaintiffs will appeal.
