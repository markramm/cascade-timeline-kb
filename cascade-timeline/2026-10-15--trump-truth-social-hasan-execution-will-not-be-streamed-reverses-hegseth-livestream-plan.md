President Trump announced Thursday that Hasan's execution by firing squad will not be streamed, reversing the livestream plan Hegseth had announced the week before. Trump's post says: "He will die, as scheduled, by firing squad, but this event will not be streamed." In the post, as States Newsroom quotes it, Trump says streaming "might have been a strong deterrent," but that "many very good and well meaning people would rather not watch him die." He adds that the execution may be viewed in person by the immediate families of the victims.

Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell told Newsweek that the Secretary supports the President's decision and that Trump and Hegseth are "completely aligned." An ABC News/Washington Post/Ipsos poll found Eight in 10 Americans say the execution should not be livestreamed.

NBC's text does not say whether the public may attend. The post's own timestamp was not retrieved.
