In Service Employees International Union v. DOJ, the government "moved for an immediate administrative stay and a stay pending appeal of the district" court's restraining order. A three-judge panel order, stamped "NOV 2 2026," is attached to the full court's later order, with a dissent by Chief Judge Murguia. On Election Day the full court's order states: "A majority of the en banc court has voted to reinstate the three-judge panel’s" order, so the stay stands. This was the one ruling in the week that went for the government. The district order it stays is at [[2026-10-30--thompson-tro-bars-doj-dhs-demands-and-prosecution-threats-election-officials-seiu-v-doj-3-26-cv-12800]]; the plaintiffs later dismissed the case ([[2026-11-06--plaintiffs-voluntarily-dismiss-seiu-v-doj-ninth-circuit-stayed-restraining-order-on-doj-ballot-demands]].)
