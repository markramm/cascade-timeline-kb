The Justice Department announced charges against 16 individuals in connection with illegal voting and related election-fraud schemes. The national release says eight aliens residing in Texas face charges, while the Northern District of Texas U.S. Attorney's own release counts seven. The release text carries the Justice Department's own account; the charges are allegations.
