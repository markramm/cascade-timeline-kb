This entry sets out the Schedule F coordinated expenditure lines the NRSC and DSCC filed for the Michigan and Texas Senate races, each dated by its expenditure date.

**Michigan, NRSC for Rogers.** The NRSC's filings carry coordinated lines of $1,500,000 to Smart Media Group, $2,715,700 and $210,215 to Cross Roads Media LLC, then $2,837,922, $1,996,722, $2,073,783 and $2,107,371 to Cross Roads Media in the September report, and $1,010,301 to Cross Roads Media and $23,000 to Premiere Transportation in the pre-general report.

**Texas, NRSC for Paxton.** No NRSC Schedule F line in either filing carries a Texas Senate candidate, and the bulk file holds no NRSC coordinated line for Ken Paxton. The NRSC did give his campaign a $62,000 direct contribution, a separate transaction type.

**Michigan, DSCC for El-Sayed.** The September and pre-general filings carry many lines for El-Sayed, among them $1,000,000 payments to LC Media LLC. Every DSCC line in them shows the Democratic National Committee as the designating committee. The DSCC also gave El-Sayed's campaign a $62,000 contribution.

**Texas, DSCC for Talarico.** The pre-general filing carries $564,760 and $358,445.59 to Thematic Campaigns LLC, along with smaller lines.

**Scale against the old limits.** Before [[2026-06-30--scotus-nrsc-v-fec-strikes-party-coordinated-expenditure-limits-6-3]], the FEC's 2026 table set the Senate limit at $1,053,200 for Michigan and $3,148,400 for Texas. The only DSCC line for these races dated before the ruling was $4,000 for a different Michigan candidate, Haley Stevens.

Caveats. Totals are computed from the raw files and recorded in the research task rather than here. Super PAC spending is covered in separate entries.
