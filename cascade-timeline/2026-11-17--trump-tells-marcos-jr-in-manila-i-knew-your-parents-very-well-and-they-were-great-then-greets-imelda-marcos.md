AP reports he spoke before ASEAN and other Asian leaders over dinner that day and praised Marcos and his parents.

The Star quotes his opening remarks: "I knew your parents very well, and they were great." It then quotes him: "You have very good genetics, I can tell you that."

On Wednesday, Nov. 18, Trump visited Malacañang Palace. The Manila Times says he told Imelda Marcos "We love you!" It also says he said the Marcos family has "very good genetics."

The remarks name Marcos Sr. only as one of "your parents." Marcos Sr. was ousted in 1986, according to AP.

The Ninth Circuit, in the human rights case brought against his estate, describes the abuses as torture, summary execution and "disappearance" committed by the Philippine military and paramilitary forces under his command. It says 9,539 of the claims were found valid and awarded damages.

The official compilation of Trump's earlier White House remarks to Marcos Jr. has him calling the family "a great family, great family legacy, and highly respected in his country."

On the trade side, Reuters reports that the sides did not sign any agreement. Trump called it a trade deal on social media. Marcos' office said the countries "reached an understanding" on economic cooperation.
