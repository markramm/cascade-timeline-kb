The Financial Times reported on Sunday that Anthropic has struck a deal worth about $3.4 billion to secure gas-fired power generators for data centers. According to the report as relayed by CNBC, Anthropic bought commitments for 1.7 gigawatts of heavy-duty generators in late September from OnePWR Solutions, a San Antonio, Texas-based equipment supplier. The report adds, citing a person familiar with the matter, that Anthropic did not acquire OnePWR itself and that none of the supplier's employees transferred to Anthropic, but that the deal included engineering and construction capabilities needed to install the generators.

Reuters could not immediately verify the report, and Anthropic and OnePWR did not respond to requests for comment. Reuters reported in September that Anthropic expects to spend more than $500 billion over the next 10 years building AI infrastructure.

**Not established here:** where the generators will be installed.
