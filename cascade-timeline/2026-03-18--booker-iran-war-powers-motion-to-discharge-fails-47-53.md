On March 18, 2026, the U.S. Senate voted 47 to 53 on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 118, a joint resolution to direct the removal of U.S. armed forces from hostilities within or against Iran that have not been authorized by Congress. The Senate's record gives the result as "Motion to Discharge Rejected". It was Senate roll call vote 58.

One press page has it "sponsored by Sen. Booker (D-NJ) (S.J. Res. 118)". It is a separate vote from [[2026-03-04--senate-war-powers-iran-vote-defeated]].
