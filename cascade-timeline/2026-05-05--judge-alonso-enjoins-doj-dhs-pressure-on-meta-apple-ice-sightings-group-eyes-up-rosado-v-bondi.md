On May 5, 2026, Judge Jorge L. Alonso of the Northern District of Illinois granted a preliminary injunction in Rosado v. Bondi, No. 26 C 1532, brought by Rosado and Kreisau Group LLC. The order bars the defendants, their officers and anyone acting for them from taking “no actions, formal or informal, to demand or pressure” Facebook to suppress the ICE Sightings – Chicagoland group on Facebook, or Apple to suppress Kreisau Group's Eyes Up application, and from coercing either company with sanctions for allowing or reinstating them. It also directed defense counsel, by the seventh day, to send written notice of the order to Meta and Apple. The order rests on a memorandum opinion at R. 34, which this entry does not summarize; it records what the order itself bars.

The order answers pressure the government had already applied to platforms. Attorney General Bondi said in a comment to CBS News that DOJ had asked Apple to remove the ICEBlock app: “We reached out to Apple today demanding they remove the ICEBlock app from their App Store — and Apple did so.” The injunction names the Facebook group and Eyes Up; Bondi's statement concerned ICEBlock. The case ended with a consent permanent injunction: [[2026-11-13--rosado-v-blanche-consent-permanent-injunction-ice-sightings-chicagoland-eyes-up]].
