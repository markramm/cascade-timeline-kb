Skydance Corporation's SEC filing on its director appointments records that on October 4, 2026 its Board of Directors appointed Robert A. Kotick, former chief executive of Activision Blizzard, as a member of the Board effective at the closing of the Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition, and that on October 5, 2026 the Board appointed Laurene Powell Jobs, founder and president of Emerson Collective, effective at the closing. Kotick will serve on the Nominating and Governance Committee. The filing's biography of Powell Jobs says she is majority investor and Chair of the Board of The Atlantic.

**Why it belongs in the media-capture thread.** The state attorney-general consent decree in the Paramount-WBD case requires an Editorial Independence Board. In the text as filed, its members "shall be appointed by the Combined Entity's Board of Directors." These directors are therefore among those who will appoint the body meant to protect CNN and CBS News from owner interference. The decree's other terms are in [[2026-09-30--court-enters-paramount-wbd-state-ag-consent-decree-editorial-independence-board-colorado-washington-opt-out]], and the close itself in [[2026-10-06--paramount-skydance-closes-wbd-acquisition-cnn-cbs-news-under-one-owner-supersedes-pause]].

**What this does not show.** Nothing in the filing links either appointment to editorial decisions. The filing gives as qualifications experience running a public media company and investing in journalism and storytelling. Nothing read for this entry names the members of the independence board.
