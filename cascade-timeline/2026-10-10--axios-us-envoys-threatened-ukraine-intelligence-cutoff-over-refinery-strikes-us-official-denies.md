Sequence only, with date kinds.

- 2026-10-10 (reported date; Ukrainska Pravda 09:16, relaying Axios): a Ukrainian official told Axios that Witkoff and Kushner threatened, in talks with the Ukrainian team in Miami, that Trump could cut off Ukraine's access to US intelligence if Kyiv kept ignoring requests to stop refinery strikes. The talks "had been expected to last two days" and ended earlier than planned; Witkoff called them productive.
- Same report: a US official denied any threat was made. Web-search summaries of Axios add that the US official described the envoys as stressing the US is a dependable source of ammunition and intelligence; that wording was not read on a page and is not relied on.

The Axios original was not fetched; "Axios said" here rests on a Ukrainian outlet's relay. A single anonymous Ukrainian source vs. a US denial: the entry records that the threat was reported, not that it was made.
