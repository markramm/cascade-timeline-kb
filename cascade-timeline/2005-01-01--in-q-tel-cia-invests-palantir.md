In-Q-Tel, the CIA's venture arm, made two rounds of investment in Palantir totalling more than $2 million, Forbes reported in 2013, after Palantir was referred to it. Forbes gives no date for either round. It says that from 2005 to 2008 the CIA was Palantir's patron and only customer; the year on this entry is the start of that period and carries year precision, since no page read states the year of the investment. CNBC's report on Palantir's 2020 filing lists In-Q-Tel among its investors. For the company's founding see [[2003-01-01--palantir-technologies-founded-thiel-karp]].
