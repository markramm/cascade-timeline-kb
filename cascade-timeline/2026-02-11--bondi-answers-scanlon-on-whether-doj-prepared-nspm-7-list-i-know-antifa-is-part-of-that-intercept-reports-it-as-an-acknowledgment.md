The House Judiciary Committee's hearing "Oversight of the U.S. Department of Justice" was held on Wednesday, February 11, 2026, with the Attorney General as the only witness. The earlier version of this entry put the testimony on the day The Intercept published, one day later.

**The exchange, from the Rev transcript.** Rep. Mary Gay Scanlon's questioning put the word "list" on the record first. Her question said that "section three of that memo directed you" to submit a list, and that the December memo had the FBI compile one. She then asked: "Did you prepare the list?" Bondi answered: "I'm not going to answer yes or no" and added "I know Antifa is part of that." Scanlon asked whether Bondi would commit to giving the committee the list; Bondi said: "We will comply with the law in all matters." Asked again, she said: "I'm not going to commit to anything to you." Scanlon then summed up her reading in her own words, that "you have a secret list of people or groups" accused of domestic terrorism which Bondi would not share with Congress. In the transcript read, Bondi does not use the word list.

**What The Intercept made of it.** The outlet wrote that Bondi "for the first time acknowledged the existence of a secret list of domestic terrorist organizations," and quotes her as saying "I know antifa is part of that." Scanlon told the outlet that her aim was "to acknowledge that it existed." So "secret list" and "acknowledged" are the questioner's and the reporter's words. What Bondi said, on this transcript, is a refusal to answer yes or no together with a remark that antifa is part of "that."

**Not read.** The committee's video record, which would settle the transcript's inaudible passages and who was speaking when the transcript labels the speaker only by number. The Intercept names Scanlon as the questioner, and the transcript names her as the member recognized just before.
