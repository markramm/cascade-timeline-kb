ICE and CoreCivic signed a 5-year, $536 million contract to reopen the Prairie Correctional Facility in Appleton, Swift County, Minnesota — a private prison that has sat closed since 2010 — as the state's first dedicated immigrant detention center. The facility will hold 1,600 beds, with detainee intake beginning in Q4 2026 and full capacity reached by Q2 2027. The contract runs at approximately $75 million per year, roughly $67 per bed per day. Minnesota has until now had no dedicated ICE detention facility, instead shipping detainees out of state or housing them in local jails. As of the signing, no organized local-government opposition has been reported — this is a completed deal, not a fight in progress. Rep. Angie Craig has pressed the administration on the contract.

The Appleton contract extends the 2026 pattern of ICE reactivating shuttered or underused private-prison capacity at scale: GEO Group's $529M Hudson, Colorado Big Horn contract signed weeks earlier ([[2026-07-09--geo-group-529m-hudson-colorado-big-horn-detention-contract]]), which itself drew a zoning lawsuit ([[2026-07-23--weld-hudson-county-co-geo-big-horn-zoning-lawsuit]]), and Target Hospitality's Dilley restart ([[2025-03-05--target-hospitality-dilley-5-year-246m-contract-restart]]). It also lands amid a documented buildout that AP found had produced a record pace of in-custody suicides across CoreCivic and GEO facilities ([[2026-05-27--ap-investigation-ice-detention-suicides-record-fy-pace-corecivic-geo-facilities]]) and a broader 2026 expansion tracked at 152 new detention facilities across 39 states alongside 17 in-custody deaths as of that May count ([[2026-05-07--ice-custody-deaths-reach-17-152-new-detention-facilities-39-states-2026-record-pace]]). Swift County's rural courts, public defenders, and medical infrastructure have no prior track record handling a 1,600-bed detention population — the same capacity mismatch that preceded IGSA extension fights elsewhere, such as Torrance County, NM ([[2026-02-04--torrance-county-nm-corecivic-igsa-extension-12-hours-after-ban]]).
