Trump fired the Election Assistance Commission's two Democratic commissioners, Thomas Hicks and Benjamin Hovland, by email on July 9, 2026, ten days after the Supreme Court's June 29, 6-3 ruling in Trump v. Slaughter overturned Humphrey's Executor and eliminated statutory removal protections for independent agency commissioners. The EAC certifies voting machines and sets federal election administration standards; its dismemberment lands four months before the November midterms.

This is distinct from, and on the same day as, the reported July 9 firing of the EAC's top four staff ([[2026-07-09--trump-purges-election-assistance-commission-save-act-backdoor]], an entry marked disputed for want of a source) — that action would have removed the technical and operational expertise needed to certify voting systems; this action removes the commission's actual bipartisan leadership. Together the two firings, both dated July 9, leave the EAC without either its senior staff or its statutorily-independent commissioners, arriving in the same window as DOJ's criminal-prosecution threats against state election officials ([[2026-07-07--doj-threatens-criminal-prosecution-election-officials-50-states]]) and June's dismantling of DOJ's own election-crime oversight apparatus ([[2026-06-09--doj-dismantles-federal-election-oversight-before-midterms]]). The Humphrey's Executor ruling ([[2026-06-29--scotus-overturns-humphreys-executor-presidential-firing-power]]) is the legal instrument that made this specific firing possible — a ten-day turnaround from precedent to use.

**Update:** The third remaining commissioner, Republican Christy McCormick, was not fired: Votebeat reports she "was allowed to resign" the same day, leaving the EAC with zero commissioners and no quorum — meaning no official action is possible: no updating the national mail-in voter registration form, no certifying voting equipment, no distributing Help America Vote Act grants to states. Independently confirmed by CNN, NBC News, and Votebeat beyond the original two tier-1 sources. The agency's functional capacity, not just its bipartisan balance, is now zero months before the midterms.
