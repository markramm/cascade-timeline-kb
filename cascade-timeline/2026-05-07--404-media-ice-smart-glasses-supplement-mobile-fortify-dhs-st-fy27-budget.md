The reporting rests on a DHS official and a conference attendee, and the article is paywalled beyond its free excerpt.

The primary record is the DHS Science and Technology Directorate's FY2027 budget justification, a request and not an enacted appropriation. It lists smart glasses as one deliverable inside a single research project, not as a line item of its own, and the project sits in S&T's research portfolio rather than ICE's component budget. A DHS spokesperson told NewsNation that no federal funds had been committed.
