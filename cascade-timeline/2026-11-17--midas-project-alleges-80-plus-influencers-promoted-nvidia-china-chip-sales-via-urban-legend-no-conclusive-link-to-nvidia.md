The Midas Project, an AI-safety watchdog nonprofit, alleges in a report that over 80 right-wing influencers on X were likely paid, without clear disclosure, to promote pro-AI-industry articles, including support for Nvidia chip exports to China, according to Cybernews on November 17. Per the report, the campaigns were organized through Urban Legend, an influencer marketing company, and some campaigns shared connections to Innovation Council Action, a dark-money group linked to Trump AI adviser David Sacks, and to a group connected to the super PAC Leading the Future. The influencers who posted in support of Nvidia exports to China have 14 million followers. Cybernews reports that after the Midas Project contacted Urban Legend, the company tried to cover its tracks and a new redirect domain appeared; it also states there is no conclusive link between Nvidia and these campaigns.

The Midas Project report itself was not read for this entry; the account comes from one outlet, and no response from Urban Legend or the influencers was reviewed.
