In the District of Nevada, a criminal information filed 10/29/26 charges a Class A Misdemeanor over a vote cast on November 8, 2022. In the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, a government memorandum describes an October 28 change-of-plea hearing on a misdemeanor count over the 2024 general election, by a citizen of Mexico who is a lawful resident. The Nevada and Texas papers state charges only. Defendants are not named here.
