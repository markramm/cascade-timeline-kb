The Office of Refugee Resettlement published an interim final rule on March 25, 2025. This IFR is effective March 25, 2025.

The rule removes 45 CFR 410.1201(b), a provision added by the April 30, 2024 Foundational Rule. The 2024 text said: "ORR shall not share any immigration status information relating to potential sponsors with any law enforcement or immigration enforcement related entity at any time."

HHS stated its ground this way: the regulation is "not in accordance with law." HHS's own summary of the effect: "ORR removes the prohibition on sharing immigration status information relating to potential sponsors with law enforcement and immigration enforcement entities."

Comments on this IFR must be received on or before May 27, 2025.

**What the rule is and is not.** The text is about sponsor immigration status information. The Federal Register document, as read here, does not mention sponsor biometrics. The rule removes the bar. It does not say what ORR then sends ICE.
