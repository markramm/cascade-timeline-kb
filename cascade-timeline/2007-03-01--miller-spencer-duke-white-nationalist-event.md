While students at Duke University, Stephen Miller and white nationalist leader Richard Spencer collaborated on an immigration debate featuring Peter Brimelow, founder of VDARE, in late March 2007. Brimelow was set to debate Peter Laufer on Mexican immigration at an event Spencer later described as "pretty edgy" for 2007. An email from Spencer stated: "At 6:45 Stephen Miller and I will leave early to do more set-up," demonstrating their direct coordination. Spencer later revealed: "I knew him very well when I was at Duke... We worked on an immigration debate, which we brought Peter Brimelow to campus." Spencer also said, "Clearly I was influencing him," but added that he did not think he was decisive in Miller's life. In an October 2016 email to Mother Jones, Miller denied the relationship: "I have absolutely no relationship with Mr. Spencer. I completely repudiate his views, and his claims are 100 percent false." Spencer's 2007 email and his later account document that the two worked together on the debate.
