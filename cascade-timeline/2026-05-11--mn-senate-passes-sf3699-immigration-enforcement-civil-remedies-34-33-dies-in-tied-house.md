The Minnesota Senate passed the Omnibus Immigration Enforcement and Civil Remedies bill on a 34-33 party-line vote. The House, evenly split between the parties, took no action, and the session ended without any state limit on federal immigration agents reaching the governor. House DFL leader Zack Stephenson said House Republicans blocked Operation Metro Surge relief; Speaker Lisa Demuth, asked whether she had held the bills, answered that is not true.

The Revisor's status page records the bill's third reading as passed as amended and its House companion as never leaving committee. See the cascade-research note for the full bill ledger.
