NPR reported on June 3, 2025 that Elon Musk had called the Republican spending bill a disgusting abomination in a post on X, days after he left his role leading the Department of Government Efficiency.

On June 5, Trump spoke in the Oval Office and then posted on Truth Social. Al Jazeera quotes him saying he was very disappointed because Elon knew the inner workings of the bill better than almost anybody, and Deadline quotes his post proposing to terminate Musk's governmental subsidies and contracts. Musk answered on X. Deadline reports that he called Trump's post about asking him to leave an obvious lie, and Al Jazeera quotes him saying that without him Trump would have lost the election.

Musk then wrote on X that Trump is in the Epstein files. CNN reports that he provided no evidence for the claim. Deadline reports that Musk also endorsed a post calling for Trump's impeachment. CNN reports that Musk later deleted both posts, and that he later wrote that he regretted some of his posts about Trump and that they went too far.

Musk's spending in the 2024 election, as NBC News tallied it from campaign finance reports on December 5, 2024: more than $250 million overall to boost Trump. NBC separates it into $238 million that Musk donated to America PAC, a super PAC that reported spending $157 million supporting Trump, and more than $920,000 to one of Trump's joint fundraising committees, and more than $20 million to RBG PAC, another super PAC. NBC noted that Musk might have donated more, as committees were still filing. A super PAC is not the campaign, and no filing was pulled for this entry.

On July 1, NBC reports, Trump wrote on Truth Social that without subsidies Musk would probably have to close up shop and head back home to South Africa, and also said that DOGE is the monster that might have to go back and eat Elon.

## Correction (2026-11-16)

The earlier text said Musk contributed over 250 million to Trump's 2024 campaign. The figure is NBC's tally of his spending overall, and most of it went to a super PAC, so it is restated above with its base. The earlier text also said Trump told Musk to go back to where he came from at a rally in Michigan; no source read for this pass records that, and the July 1 statements above are what NBC reports. The earlier DOGE and Epstein lines had no date or source and are now bound to the sources above, with the later deletion. The title and id are renamed because the earlier ones put the impeachment suggestion as Musk's own act, where Deadline says he endorsed another user's post; the earlier id is kept in previous_ids.
