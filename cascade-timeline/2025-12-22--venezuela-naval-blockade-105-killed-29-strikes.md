On December 22, 2025 the US military struck a "low-profile vessel" in the eastern Pacific, killing one person (InSight Crime's timeline of the strikes). The strike is one of the boat strikes that began in early September; it is not part of the oil blockade ordered on December 16.

## The dead belong to the boat strikes, not the blockade

The blockade, as announced, covers sanctioned oil tankers going into and out of Venezuela. The people killed died in strikes on boats. Two outlets gave running counts of those strikes around this date, each a media tally of the administration's announcements:

- NBC News reported that the campaign had resulted in 28 boat strikes that had killed more than 100 people.
- Al Jazeera reported, the day after the December 22 strike, that the attacks had killed more than 100 people, in more than two dozen strikes.

The sources read do not name the dead. NBC also reported that Trump, asked in a phone interview whether war with Venezuela was possible, said "I don’t rule it out, no".

## Reaction

China called the US's latest moves a "serious violation of international law", and Russia's foreign ministry reaffirmed its full support for Venezuela's leadership, per Al Jazeera. Those are the two governments' statements, not findings.
