High Time Nebraska reported spending $50,000 on media placement and production supporting Nebraska Senate candidate MIKE MARVIN, disseminated 10/23/2026 ([[#c1]], [[#c2]], [[#c3]]). Cornhusker Choice reported $55,000 supporting ROBIN MRS. RICHARDS, disseminated the same day ([[#c5]], [[#c6]]).

**Dates, by kind.** FEC registration: High Time Nebraska October 16, 2026; Cornhusker Choice October 21, 2026 ([[#c7]], [[#c8]]). Pre-general reports include activity from the close of books on the committee's last report through 20 days before the election ([[#c16]]); for PACs the period ran 10/01/2026 - 10/14/2026 ([[#c9]]). The post-general report covers 10/15/2026 - 11/23/2026 and is due 12/03/2026 ([[#c10]]). Domain registration: hightimenebraska.com at 2026-10-14T22:19:34Z and cornhuskerchoice.com at 2026-10-14T22:19:36Z, seconds apart ([[#c11]], [[#c12]]). Each site carries its committee's "Paid for by" disclaimer ([[#c13]], [[#c14]]).

**Shared filing facts.** The committees paid different payees, Potomac Strategy Group and Maryland Avenue Media. On the filings, both payees give the address 1309 COFFEEN AVE STE 1200 SHERIDAN, Wyoming 82801 ([[#c4]], [[#c6]]). So does Relay Media Group, payee of another super PAC, Honest Choice Maine ([[#c15]]). The FEC committee master lists the same treasurer for High Time Nebraska, Cornhusker Choice and Honest Choice Maine.
