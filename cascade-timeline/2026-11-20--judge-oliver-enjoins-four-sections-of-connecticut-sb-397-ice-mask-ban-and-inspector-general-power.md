U.S. District Judge Vernon Oliver issued an injunction blocking four sections of Connecticut's S.B. 397 from being enforced while the Justice Department's lawsuit against the state proceeds, CT Mirror reported. Three of those sections sought to let Connecticut's Inspector General examine and criminally prosecute federal agents operating in the state; the fourth made it a misdemeanor for officers to wear a mask "while interacting with the public" on duty.

In his written order, Oliver said those portions likely violate the Supremacy Clause, writing that it ensures "federal officers are immune from state prosecution when acting within the scope of their federal authority." Attorney General William Tong's office said it was "reviewing the decision and evaluating next steps."
