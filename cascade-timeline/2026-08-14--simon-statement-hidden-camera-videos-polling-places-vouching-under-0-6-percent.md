On August 14, 2026, Secretary of State Steve Simon released a statement about hidden-camera videos recorded inside polling places during the primary. He does not name anyone. He says poll workers reported the visits and law enforcement is reviewing the situation.

The release also gives the state's account of vouching: under 0.6 percent of 2024 general-election votes used it, and about 71 percent of those voters were already registered and needed to update a name or address. These are the Secretary of State's own figures, not an independent count.

He says he has zero tolerance for lying, lawbreaking and disruptions at polling places.
