DHS paid $70 million in cash for a warehouse in Surprise, Arizona on Jan. 23, 2026, according to the document Bloomberg obtained, as KTAR reported. The seller is listed as RG Surprise AZ LLC, an entity connected to the New York-based Rockefeller Group, which Bisnow describes as a subsidiary of Japanese real estate giant Mitsubishi Estate Group. Rockefeller Group reportedly purchased the 25-acre site in April 2023, and construction of the 418,400-square-foot warehouse was completed in 2024. Bisnow, citing AZ Big Media, reported that Rockefeller paid $12M for the site in 2023; that figure is the land price, not the cost of the finished building.

An ICE spokesperson told the Phoenix Business Journal that the detention facility will hold about 1,500 beds. The Phoenix Fox station later reported that, after community members and the Surprise City Council asked the federal government to work with the city, DHS scaled down its original plan.

The city has not blocked the conversion. Surprise Mayor Kevin Sartor has said the city lacks legal grounds to stop the facility, and the City Council's May 5 letter to the Homeland Security Secretary asked DHS to follow Arizona laws, the city's zoning regulations and other local ordinances. The state has acted instead: Arizona Attorney General Kris Mayes filed a lawsuit in April claiming environmental reviews still needed to be completed, and Mayes announced on July 1 that a deal with DHS will, for now, prevent the building from being used as an immigration detention center.

The operating contract for the facility is carried in [[2026-03-06--ice-awards-gardaworld-313m-surprise-arizona-contract]]. The Tremont, Pennsylvania purchase is at [[2026-01-29--dhs-purchases-blue-owl-tremont-warehouse]].
