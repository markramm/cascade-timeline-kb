On **August 27, 2026**, ICE published a procurement notice — **Record F2026075113** — for
**Boston Dynamics SPOT** quadruped robots and accessories, budgeted at **$1 million to $2 million**,
citing **"situational awareness"** in high-risk environments. The notice targets a **September 4**
solicitation, an **FY2026 Q4** award, and completion by **April 24, 2027**, at **Fort Benning,
Georgia**.

## This is a solicitation, not an award — and the distinction is load-bearing

**No ICE contract for Boston Dynamics appears in USAspending.** A search across all award types
returns only older **DARPA and Navy** awards (`HR001110C0025`, `HR001112C0106`, `N6600103C8044`).
**That absence is expected and correct**: a procurement notice precedes an award, and there is
nothing yet to obligate.

**Do not report this as ICE having bought robots.** A budget range in a notice is a **ceiling on an
intention**, not money spent — the figure-type distinction this corpus has repeatedly had to correct
after the fact. **If the award lands, it will appear in USAspending with an obligated figure, and
that is the number to report.**
