The memorial service for Charlie Kirk, who had been assassinated on September 10, was held on September 21, 2025 at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona. Wikipedia lists Erika Kirk, Vice President JD Vance, Stephen Miller and Secretary of State Marco Rubio among those who spoke, with President Trump scheduled to speak last, and says that Elon Musk attended the service.

Fortune reports that before Trump took the stage, Musk went to where the president was sitting and sat down in an empty seat next to him, and that they were seen shaking hands and chatting. Afterward Musk posted a picture of them on X with the words For Charlie. Wikipedia describes it as their first public meeting since the beginning of their feud earlier in the year, and says that Kirk was a friend of both and had publicly called for their reconciliation.

Just the News reports that Trump downplayed speculation about a reunion on Monday. He told reporters that Musk came over and said hello, and that it had nothing to do with that.
