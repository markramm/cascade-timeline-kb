On August 13, 2025, the Civil Rights Division sent Minnesota's Secretary of State a reply to the state's July 25 response. The letter, as the state filed it in *United States v. Simon*, makes "a demand pursuant to 52 U.S.C. § 20703 for a complete copy of the state of Minnesota’s statewide VRL" and gives its purpose as ascertaining compliance with HAVA list-maintenance requirements. It asks for the list within seven days, by August 21, 2025, with the last four Social Security digits and the full driver's license number for each registrant. On August 21, 2025, Mr. Erickson again responded on behalf of Secretary Simon.
