NVIDIA announced, with Oracle and the Department of Energy, AI supercomputers for Argonne National Laboratory. The release is current as of October 28, 2025.

The release says the Solstice system will feature a record-breaking 100,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs.

It says the Equinox system will include 10,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs and is expected to be available in the first half of 2026.

The release says the Solstice and Equinox supercomputers will be located at Argonne National Laboratory. It says Solstice will be built with the DOE's new public-private partnership model, "including industry investments and use cases".

The release gives no dollar figure, no ownership statement and no share of computing time for the companies. See [[genesis-lab-ai-supercomputers-ownership]].
