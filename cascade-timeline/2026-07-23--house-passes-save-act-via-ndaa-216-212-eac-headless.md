The House passed the FY2027 National Defense Authorization Act (H.R. 8800) 216-212 on July 22, 2026 (Roll Call 278, U.S. House Clerk) with the SAVE America Act — documentary proof of citizenship to register for federal elections and photo ID to vote — attached; six Democrats (Cuellar, Davis, Golden, Gonzalez, Gray and Perez) voted yes. The 216-212 vote was on the whole defense bill, not a stand-alone SAVE vote. The bill moves to the Senate. The Election Assistance Commission has had no commissioners since July 9, when Trump fired its two Democratic commissioners and Republican Christy McCormick was allowed to resign ([[2026-07-09--trump-purges-election-assistance-commission-save-act-backdoor]], [[2026-07-10--trump-fires-eac-commissioners-hicks-hovland-after-humphreys-executor]]).

This is a distinct legislative stage from the June 30 House vote in which the House rejected its own rule for the defense bill, 198-224 ([[2026-06-30--save-act-dies-in-senate-ndaa-state-citizenship-proof-laws-spread]]) — the same must-pass-bill strategy revived and passed in the House three weeks later, now heading to a Senate where the defense bill itself failed cloture 50-46 on July 14 (the Senate has not voted on the SAVE attachment). The commission has no commissioners at the moment the House acts; this entry does not claim the bill assigns it implementation duties, which the fact-check could not confirm from the bill text.
