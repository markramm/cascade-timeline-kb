Sequence only, with date kinds.

- November 21, 2025 (the Committee's account of the D.C. records): the Committee's letter says the records show Graham Wilson "filed as founder" of a new domestic nonprofit corporation called Creator Collective. The D.C. open-data record for Creator Collective carries an effective date matching that day (a Domestic Nonprofit Corporation; registered agent Registered Agents, Inc.). The open-data layer does not list a founder or directors.
- December 16, 2025 (Committee's account): Sixteen Thirty Fund told the Committee Chorus is "not an independent legal entity."
- December 17, 2025 (D.C. trade-name record): "Chorus" is registered as a trade name under the Creator Collective file; the effective date in the layer matches that day.
- February 6, 2026 (Committee's account of an email to staff): Sixteen Thirty Fund said that as of January 1, 2026, "Chorus became inactive as a project of the Sixteen Thirty Fund."

The Committee finds the sequence "dubious at best" and says the episode "strongly suggests an intent to hide potentially responsive documents." Those are the Chairman's characterizations, not findings.
