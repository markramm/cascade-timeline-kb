Spotlight PA and Votebeat reported on Oct. 23, 2026 (publication date) that "As of today, DHS has not yet shared the list" of 14,576 potential noncitizen voters with Pennsylvania officials, and that the state had not spoken to DHS since a Sept. 8 email laying out DHS's method. This is the outlet's statement, not a state election-office release. Among the other states, no "likely noncitizen" list had reached Pennsylvania by that date, by this account.
