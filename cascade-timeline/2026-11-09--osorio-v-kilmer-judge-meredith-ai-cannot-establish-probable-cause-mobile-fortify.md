On November 9, 2026, in Osorio v. Kilmer, U.S. District Judge S. Chad Meredith ruled: "The Fourth Amendment to the United States Constitution does not allow a detective to delegate to AI the task of assessing probable cause." The ruling directly conflicts with ICE's Mobile Fortify app, which federal agents had run more than 100,000 times on American streets. ICE is pursuing more than $300 million in AI surveillance contracts under Trump, including a $9.2 million Clearview AI contract and a $30 million Palantir ImmigrationOS contract. ICE plans to extend the same facial recognition tool to more than 1,000 local police agencies.
