Haverhill, Massachusetts' City Council voted on Tuesday to prohibit data centers in every city zoning district, adding data centers to the ordinance as a defined use listed as prohibited everywhere, the Eagle-Tribune reports. Members also pulled plans for a 20-acre Battery Energy Storage Systems zoning district. Mayor Melinda E. Barrett urged an outright ban, saying the city would "not have the grid capacity to handle that" for the foreseeable future.

The vote follows Holyoke, which became the first Massachusetts city to enact a citywide zoning ban in June on a 9-4 vote, and comes after Gov. Maura Healey's Sept. 8 executive order requiring local approval before large data centers seek state permits.
