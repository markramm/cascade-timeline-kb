Illinois House Bill 5024 became **Public Act 104-0786**, effective **August 7, 2026**. The Act adds Section 11-5-12 to the Illinois Municipal Code, and its operative rule is: a detention center facility "may not be located, constructed, or operated within **1,500 feet** of the property boundaries of any school." The same sentence lists day care centers, cemeteries, public parks, forest preserves, public housing, private residences and places of worship. The Act states: "This Act takes effect upon becoming law."

The Act defines "Federal immigration authority" to include ICE, CBP and DHS, and any entity under contract with such an agency to perform immigration enforcement activities. The House passed the bill **072-035-002** and the Senate **039-017-000**. The Act's text contains no clause addressing facilities that already exist.
