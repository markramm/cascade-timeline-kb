Federal Judge Kathleen Cardone granted a preliminary injunction Friday to halt construction on the border wall in the Big Bend sector, including the national and state parks, Inside Climate News reports. She said the plaintiffs are likely to succeed on the merits in at least one of their claims and to suffer irreparable harm if construction continues. The ruling covers a sector that spans more than 500 miles across West Texas. The federal waivers had allowed construction to proceed without following the National Environmental Policy Act, the Archaeological and Historic Preservation Act and many other laws; the injunction stays their effect.

The Big Bend Sentinel reports the suit argues the five contracted border wall projects in the region are unconstitutional, and that infrastructure there totals over $7.5 billion in government contracts (walls, roads, fences, lights and sensors, not wall alone). So far, six lawsuits have been filed over the Big Bend wall. The docket was not retrieved; the order was read from the bench and the written order was not located.
