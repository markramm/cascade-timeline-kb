The 2020 Call to the Convention, adopted August 25, 2018, reads: "only pledged delegates will be permitted to vote", "pledged and automatic delegates to the Convention" and "automatic delegates will also be permitted to vote on the first ballot." The 2024 Call was adopted by the DNC on September 10, 2022.

Method: both files were converted with pdftotext and the first-ballot sentences compared; they match apart from a page break.

## What the text says

- The release condition is measured against pledged and automatic delegates together, not against pledged delegates alone. A candidate whose certified pledged total reaches a majority of that combined count lets the automatic delegates vote on the first ballot.
- If the contest "all pledged and automatic delegates will be permitted to vote for a presidential candidate on all subsequent ballots" once it moves beyond the first ballot.
- Elsewhere in the Call: "automatic delegates retain their ability to vote according to their own preferences" on every vote other than that first ballot.
- A footnote to the first-ballot sentence assigns the count to the DNC Secretary at the end of delegate selection and excludes alternates. That footnote is paraphrased here, not quoted.
