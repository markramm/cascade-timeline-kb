Lianyong Wei, 51, a Chinese national, died at approximately 7:30 a.m. Chamorro Standard Time on August 23, 2026, at the Commonwealth Health Care Corporation Emergency Room in Saipan. He had been arrested on August 21 by the CNMI Department of Public Safety on assault-related charges and taken into ICE custody on August 22. A Department of Corrections officer found him unresponsive during morning checks at about 7 a.m., roughly twenty-four hours after ICE assumed custody. ICE states his cause of death remains under investigation.

Two structural features distinguish this case. The first is where custody actually sat: Wei was held by the **CNMI Department of Corrections**, not in an ICE-run facility — the intergovernmental-agreement arrangement this timeline tracks across the detention system, in which the federal agency holds the legal custody and a local corrections department holds the person. In a remote U.S. territory, that arrangement carries the thinnest oversight and press access of any configuration in the network.

The second is disclosure. Northern Mariana Islands authorities announced the death on August 24. **ICE did not issue its own release until September 1** — nine days after the death, and eight days after local authorities had already made it public. ICE's release states as general policy that "all people in ICE custody receive medical, dental and mental health intake screenings within 12 hours of arriving at each detention facility," but does not say whether Wei received one in the roughly twenty-four hours he was in custody. That gap between a policy statement and a case-specific fact is the operative silence here.

The government of China has publicly pressed the United States for answers. Wei is at least the fifth Chinese national to die in ICE or Border Patrol custody since March 2025.

**A note on counting.** Advocacy reporting placed Wei as the "57th death since Trump's return," and independent trackers and ICE's own published figures have diverged sharply this year — a gap this corpus has documented before. This entry does not adopt any running total. The verified facts are the dated ones: arrest August 21, custody August 22, death August 23, ICE disclosure September 1.
