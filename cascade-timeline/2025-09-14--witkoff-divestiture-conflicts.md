Steve Witkoff's OGE financial disclosure form reports $120,000,000 in "Proceeds from the sale of an interest in the company as part of divestiture planning" from The Witkoff Group LLC, a real estate management company. The form calls it the sale of an interest, not of his whole stake. The disclosure became public in mid-September 2025, and the trade outlet TRD reported that Witkoff "disclosed assets worth at least $350 million, likely well short of his true net worth." Those assets include stakes in SpaceX, Cisco Systems, Reddit, Uber Technologies, hedge funds, airplanes, motorboats, and digital assets. World Liberty Financial, the cryptocurrency company he co-founded with members of the Trump family, was not divested: his 2026 annual report still lists "World Liberty Financial (cryptocurrency)" under WC Digital Fi LLC. Senate Democrats led by Adam Schiff demanded answers about Witkoff's failure to divest these Trump-linked crypto assets while serving as a key diplomatic envoy. The divestiture is partial.

Witkoff files as non-career executive branch personnel ("Non-Career"), not as a volunteer outside the disclosure system. The initial posting carried no agency signature, and Senators Murphy and Warren wrote that "no agency ethics officials have signed the form"; a revised posting was signed by the "Certifying Official" on 12/12/2025. Whether he is a special government employee is not stated on the form and is not established here.

## Corrections (2026-11-16)

- The earlier text said he sold his whole stake in the Witkoff Group. His disclosure form reports the proceeds as coming from the sale of an interest in the company as part of divestiture planning. The title and body now say so.
- The earlier title called the sale a stake sale; the title now names the sale of an interest and the World Liberty Financial listing, which the 2026 annual report still shows.
- Added the December 2025 certified disclosure form and the 2026 annual report as Tier 1 sources. Added his filing status (non-career, files a disclosure form) and that special-government-employee status is unproven.
