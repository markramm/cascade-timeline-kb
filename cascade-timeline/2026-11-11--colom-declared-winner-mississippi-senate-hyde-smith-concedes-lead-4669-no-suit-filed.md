# AP declares Colom the winner of the Mississippi Senate race; Hyde-Smith concedes

The Associated Press declared Democrat Scott Colom the winner Wednesday evening, and Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith conceded in a video the same evening. MS NOW reported Colom leading by 4,669 votes (unofficial). Other tallies in same-day coverage differ, so any margin must carry its source and time.
