Bloomberg reported, as summarized by Unchained, that World Liberty Financial quietly sold an additional 5.9 billion WLFI tokens to private accredited investors, in deals worth hundreds of millions of dollars, on top of the more than $550 million the project had raised from earlier token offerings. The project declined to say who received the money from the additional sales.

Who is entitled to what is set out in the project's own Gold Paper. DT Marks DEFI LLC, an entity affiliated with Donald Trump and certain family members, received a right to receive 75% of the net protocol revenues after agreed operating expenses and the initial treasury reserve. AMG and WC Digital Fi LLC, an affiliate of Steve Witkoff and certain of his family members, have the right to receive 25% of net protocol revenue. The Gold Paper's definition: “Net protocol revenues include revenues to WLF from any source, including without limitation platform use fees, token sale proceeds.” A right to a share of revenue is not a receipt of it, and no source read here says which entity received the proceeds of the 5.9 billion tokens.

Separately, World Liberty deposited 5 billion WLFI into Dolomite, a lending protocol, and borrowed roughly $75 million in stablecoins.
