Brennan Center for Justice analyst Eileen O'Connor reported that at least 10 states, home to over 37 million registered voters, had given the Justice Department their full voter lists. The 37 million is Brennan's figure for registered voters in those states, not a count of records; the article does not name all ten and gives no as-of date beyond its own publication.

The same piece describes a confidential memorandum of understanding that the department asked states to agree to. Under it the department would analyze each state's rolls and send a list of voters that states must remove within 45 days, which Brennan says may violate the National Voter Registration Act. Texas and Alaska have signed; Tennessee and South Dakota refused. Colorado, one of the states that refused to hand over its file, made the proposed agreement public.
