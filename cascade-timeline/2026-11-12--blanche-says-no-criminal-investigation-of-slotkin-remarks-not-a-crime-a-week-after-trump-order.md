On Thursday Attorney General Todd Blanche told SiriusXM's Dan Abrams Show that he had not threatened Democratic lawmakers with a criminal investigation. Newsmax reported him saying: "I have not threatened any member of Congress with a criminal investigation." Of Sen. Elissa Slotkin (D-Mich.), whom Trump had singled out, Blanche said: "What the senator said in and of itself is absolutely not a crime." Newsmax's reporter wrote that he emphasized no criminal investigation of Slotkin was underway; that line is the reporter's paraphrase, not a Blanche quotation.

What Slotkin said: at an Oct. 13 University of Chicago event, according to Fox News, which cites a video clip posted on X by The Chicago Thinker, she said: "I want to look at these signature cases and not just focus on the president, but on the people who enabled him." She went on to say she wanted young people coming out of school to think a Trump administration job "might be buying myself a ton of problems that I don’t want." Trump's accusation, as Axios reported it, was that she threatened young people who hold Republican views ([[2026-11-07--trump-orders-doj-to-investigate-democrats-who-pledged-oversight-blanche-wont-rule-out-prosecuting-lawmakers]]).
