Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy and FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford announced on December 4 that Peraton would be the prime integrator of the air traffic control overhaul.

GAO describes the award as a contract with a ceiling of $1.5 billion. A ceiling is the most the contract allows the FAA to order, not money spent.

Peraton is owned by Veritas Capital, according to Reuters. Parsons Corporation confirmed to Reuters that it bid for the work with IBM.

At a House Transportation and Infrastructure hearing, Representative Hank Johnson of Georgia questioned Bedford about the choice of Peraton. Bedford told the committee that career FAA staff recommended Peraton to him. He said Trump demanded a $200 million reduction in the fee cost.

Reuters later reported Bedford's account as a $200 million discount off the initial proposed contract price.
