GEO Group confirmed a signed 5-year contract giving ICE exclusive access to the shuttered Rivers Correctional Institution in Winton, Hertford County, North Carolina — one of the largest ICE detention facilities planned on the East Coast, at 1,400 beds. The contract is projected to generate roughly $80 million in annual revenue for GEO Group. Governor Josh Stein has said he "does not want it in our state," but the contract closes the facility's fight-stage from resisting to signed. Rivers Correctional previously operated as a Bureau of Prisons facility until its 2021 closure amid officer-violence and contraband investigations.

The signing follows the plan's announcement ([[2026-07-24--ice-plans-1400-bed-rivers-correctional-hertford-nc]]) and the community mobilization that followed within days ([[2026-07-22--hertford-county-nc-community-mobilizes-against-rivers-correctional]]) — community rallies did not stop the contract from closing. Rivers Correctional's own record of prior officer violence as a BOP facility, now handed to the same contractor implicated in the Fort Bliss guard-betting-on-deaths findings ([[2026-07-15--hrw-aclu-fort-bliss-guards-bet-on-detainee-deaths-84-page-report]]), repeats the pattern this corpus has documented elsewhere: a state governor's public opposition carries no legal weight against a federal detention contract signed directly with a private operator.
