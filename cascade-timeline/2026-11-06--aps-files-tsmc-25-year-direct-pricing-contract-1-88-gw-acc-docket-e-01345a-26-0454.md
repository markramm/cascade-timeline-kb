Arizona Public Service (APS) filed an application with the Arizona Corporation Commission, signed on the 6th day of November 2026, asking the Commission to approve a "direct pricing contract" with TSMC Arizona Corporation. It is docket E-01345A-26-0454.

The application says the contract governs electric service that APS will provide over a 25-year term. It says the contract contemplates ramping to approximately 1.88 GW for TSMC's semiconductor fabrication and advanced packaging facilities. According to the application, the resources serving that load include the planned 1,200 MW Desert Sun Power Plant, a gas combined-cycle plant near Gila Bend.

APS says TSMC pays fixed capacity charges that do not vary with consumption, and that collateral gives three years of coverage for all fixed contract capacity payments. The contract is dated October 16, 2026. A public copy is redacted, and the application says access to the unredacted contract can only be provided under a protective agreement.

APS also asks the Commission to find that Desert Sun represents a "genuine, unanticipated opportunity" under the Commission's procurement rule, or alternatively to waive competitive procurement. It asks for accounting orders on TSMC's prepayments and a balancing account for the contract.

On 11/17/2026 the State of Arizona asked the administrative law judge in APS's pending rate case (E-01345A-25-0105) to take official notice of the TSMC docket. The State's motion refers to 1.9 GW of electric service, together with substantial associated accounting and regulatory approvals. The motion quotes APS's earlier brief as saying there were no "customer proposals that can yet be provided for Commission evaluation". The motion says APS does not oppose it, "but Staff does."
