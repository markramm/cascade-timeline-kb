The AWS Public Sector blog published the winners of the 2021 AWS Global Public Sector Partner Awards ahead of a virtual launch on June 30, 2021. Palantir won best COVID-19 solution with its Foundry platform.

An AWS Partner Network blog post, written by Palantir and AWS staff, says Foundry deployed on AWS uses many services under the hood, such as Amazon Simple Storage Service (Amazon S3), Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (Amazon EC2) and AWS Key Management Service. It says Palantir's software development teams have worked closely with AWS on tighter integrations, with a focus on Amazon SageMaker. AWS Marketplace today lists the Palantir Platform as sold by Palantir Technologies and deployed on AWS; the listing says Palantir Platform is accessible via private pricing only.

Our reading, not a finding of these pages: a cloud vendor and an analytics vendor selling into the same public-sector customers is a concentration question for oversight and privacy. These pages are vendor and trade pages and do not address that question.
