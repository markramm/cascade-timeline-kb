On Monday, Oct. 19, 2026 (arrest date), federal law enforcement "throughout California today arrested 11 foreign nationals," according to the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Central District of California; the Justice Department's companion release puts them "among 28 total charged" in criminal complaints. The releases call this "Operation Protect the Vote," a coordinated effort among all four California-based U.S. Attorney's Offices. They say all "28 foreign nationals charged are lawful permanent residents" (green-card holders). The charges, the release says, include "making false statements in a naturalization application," with some defendants also charged with "fraudulent voter registration," felonies carrying a statutory maximum of five years. Eastern District defendants are charged with "voting by an alien in a federal election, a misdemeanor" (one-year maximum); Northern District defendants with "attempted procurement of naturalization contrary to law" among other counts; Southern District defendants with "false claim to citizenship to vote" among other counts. The release alleges that "in many cases, the defendants illegally voted in recent U.S. elections"; by Democracy Docket's count, the release accuses 23 of them of casting ballots in elections dating back to 2020. The release adds that the complaints "merely contain allegations" and that the defendants are presumed innocent. This entry does not name the defendants.

Acting Deputy Attorney General Trent McCotter said in the release that each of the 28 "defendants charged today appears on the list of more than 20,000 likely noncitizen registrants we provided to California election officials last week" and that "California refused to look at that list." The release says the federal government provided the list to California on October 13. Democracy Docket, reporting that the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Central District of California said in a press release that 11 were arrested and 28 charged, sets the 28 against California's 23 million registered voters.
