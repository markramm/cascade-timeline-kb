Marble Freedom Trust chairman Leonard Leo is the trustee of a trust that received Tripp Lite stock from Barre Seid, in what The New York Times first reported and ProPublica called the largest known donation to a political advocacy group in U.S. history, worth $1.6 billion.

The trust was created in April 2020, and Seid then transferred his 100% ownership stake in Tripp Lite to it. Eaton Corporation acquired Tripp Lite for $1.65 billion. ProPublica reports the structure let Seid avoid as much as $400 million in taxes, an estimate. The trust's tax return for the Fiscal Year Ending April 2021 lists contributions of $1,603,791,362.
