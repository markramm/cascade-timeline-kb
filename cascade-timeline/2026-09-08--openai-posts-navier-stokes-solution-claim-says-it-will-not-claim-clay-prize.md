OpenAI posted on September 8, 2026 that it is sharing a solution to the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of the Millennium Prize Problems. OpenAI says the fluid in its result has a smooth force applied to it. OpenAI says it does not intend to claim the Millennium Prize for this result.

OpenAI says the group that produced the result involved on the order of 10,000 concurrent agents. OpenAI says the agents arrived at their resolution about 88 hours after the first agents were launched.

The Clay Mathematics Institute published a Navier-Stokes announcement after the OpenAI post. Clay says it contemplates the announcement that the Navier-Stokes problem has apparently been settled. Clay says the process is deliberately unhurried, and that it will provide updates.
