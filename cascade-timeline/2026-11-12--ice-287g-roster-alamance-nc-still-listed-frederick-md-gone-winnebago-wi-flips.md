The ICE 287(g) page gives a snapshot time of November 12, 2026 7:48 pm. The participating-agencies spreadsheet linked from it has one Alamance County row: the Sheriff's Office, Warrant Service Officer model. It has no Task Force Model or Jail Enforcement row for Alamance. The earlier entry on this race is linked in related.

The spreadsheet has no Maryland rows. The only Frederick County Sheriff's Office on the list is Virginia's, a Task Force Model agreement and a different office. Jenkins's defeat therefore does not end a live agreement; the state ban did.

Winnebago County, Wisconsin, is listed as a Warrant Service Officer partner. The incumbent retired and Democrat Jason Rippl, who campaigned on ending the agreement, won the open seat, a flip rather than an incumbent's defeat. Sauk County and Broome County, N.Y., kept their Republican sheriffs.

Long takes office Dec. 7, so a termination or a roster change can be looked for after that date. Frederick and Winnebago canvass and start dates are open.
