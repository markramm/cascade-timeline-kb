President Trump departed Washington Monday for a multiday Asia swing that includes a meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping. The meeting marks the first among the three leaders and is expected to center on decreasing the proliferation of nuclear weapons, the White House said. CBS News reports Trump is scheduled to meet Putin and Xi in Shenzhen in a summit on Thursday.

An unnamed White House official told NPR: "We do not expect to get to any kind of agreement," calling the trilateral an "entry point" for the three countries to talk about reducing nuclear weapons stock. Trump said Monday they would discuss denuclearization, as well as Iran and Ukraine, although an official said over the weekend that Ukraine was not expected to be discussed. Elon Musk is expected to join the president on the trip but will not be part of the meeting with Putin and Xi. An analyst, Tong, expects the tariff agreement to be renewed past its January expiration.
