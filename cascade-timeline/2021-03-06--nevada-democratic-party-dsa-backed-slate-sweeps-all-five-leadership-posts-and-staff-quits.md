On March 6, a coalition of progressive candidates backed by the local Democratic Socialists of America chapter took the leadership of the Nevada Democratic Party, sweeping "all five" party leadership positions (The Intercept, 2021). In the certified results, Judith Whitmer, the former Clark County Democratic chair, beat Tick Segerblom for chair, 244 to Segerblom’s 214. The party's executive director, Alana Mounce, then emailed that she was resigning; per The Intercept, "So was every other employee" (and the consultants). Whitmer's opponents say she planned to fire them; per The Intercept, "Whitmer denies that claim."

## Two accounts, side by side

Segerblom: "the Bernie team won." Whitmer: the old guard did not expect that "we got better and better at organizing." Both are the participants' own characterizations.
