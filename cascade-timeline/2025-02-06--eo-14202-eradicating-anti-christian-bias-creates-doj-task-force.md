The White House page for the order is dated February 6, 2025. The Justice Department later identified it as Executive Order 14202.

The order says: "There is hereby established within the Department of Justice the Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias." It adds: "The Attorney General shall serve as Chair of the Task Force."

It sets a reporting schedule: "a report within 120 days from the date of this order regarding the Task Force's initial work." It also sets an end date: "The Task Force shall terminate 2 years from the date of this order unless extended by the President."
