On September 29, 2025, **Electronic Arts Inc.** (NASDAQ: EA) announced a definitive agreement to be acquired in an all-cash transaction by a consortium of **PIF** (Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund), **Silver Lake**, and **Affinity Partners** (Jared Kushner's firm), in a deal that EA's release says "values EA at an enterprise value of approximately $55 billion." EA stockholders were to receive **$210 per share in cash**, a 25% premium to the unaffected share price of $168.32. EA's own release calls it "the largest all-cash sponsor take-private investment in history", which is the company's characterization. The deal closed on **August 4, 2026**; see the Update below.

## Transaction Structure (each figure with what it measures)

- **Price per share**: $210 in cash (EA's announcement release).
- **Enterprise value**: approximately $55 billion (EA's announcement release).
- **Equity value**: NBC News reported a price of $52.5 billion on the day of the announcement; EA's release states no equity value, and no source read here labels that figure as equity value.
- **Equity investment (consortium cash plus PIF's rollover)**: approximately $36 billion, funded by cash from each of the members plus the roll-over of PIF's existing stake (EA's announcement release). The split among the members is not given in anything read here.
- **Debt commitment**: $20 billion, "fully and solely committed by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.", of which $18 billion was expected to be funded at close (EA's announcement release). The release does not name the borrower; the SEC filings do (Parent, Oak-Eagle AcquireCo, Inc.), as set out in the filings section below.
- **Rollover by an existing holder**: "rolling over its existing 9.9% stake in the Company" is what the release says of PIF. PIF was a minority investor in EA for more than five years before the close (PIF's deputy governor, in EA's closing release).
- **Role of each party, as EA's releases state it**: the members are together the Consortium. The releases name no lead investor, no majority holder and no advisory role for Affinity. J.P. Morgan Securities LLC is named as the Consortium's financial advisor.
- **Closing date**: August 4, 2026 (EA's closing release, and the Form 8-K the company filed that day; see the filings section below).
- **Funded at close**: the closing release does not say how much debt was drawn, and neither does the closing 8-K as a total; it lists the facilities funded that day and the notes issued in April 2026 (see the filings section below). The earlier version of this entry gave a funded-at-close amount as fact; the announcement only said it was expected.

## Update 2026-11-17: the deal closed

EA's own news release of August 4, 2026 says the acquisition by the Consortium "has successfully closed," that the agreement was "approved by EA stockholders at the special meeting of stockholders held on December 22, 2025," and that "EA's common stock has ceased trading and will be delisted from NASDAQ." Stockholders receive $210 in cash per share. EA reports fiscal-year-2026 GAAP net revenue of approximately $7.5 billion. Game Developer reported that the deal "cleared the necessary regulatory hurdles in July," with approvals including the European Union and the United States; that is press reporting, not an agency statement, and no agency decision was read.

EA's Form 8-K filed on August 4, 2026 gives the same closing date, and the same filing gives the notice to Nasdaq that led to the delisting. The filings add the following.

## What the SEC filings add (retrieved from sec.gov; amounts left out here)

The filings retrieved are the closing Form 8-K, the Form 8-K filed on signing day and the definitive proxy statement. Their accession numbers are in the sources list.

- **The merger parties.** The merger agreement is among Electronic Arts, a buyer named Oak-Eagle AcquireCo, Inc. ("Parent") and its merger subsidiary. The consortium formed Parent and the merger subsidiary. At the close the merger subsidiary merged into EA, which survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent.
- **The borrower.** The borrower under the credit agreement entered on the closing date is Parent. The debt commitment letter was addressed to Parent, and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. was the lender named when it was signed, with further lenders added later.
- **The equity commitment and the rollover.** The members of the consortium committed severally, not jointly, to capitalize Parent, each on conditions set in its own equity commitment letter. PIF's rollover is a separate step: it contributed its existing shares to a new holding entity of Parent in exchange for equity of that entity. No filing read gives the equity split among the members, and the closing 8-K gives no amount of equity contributed.
- **What the 55 billion measures.** The release labels it an enterprise value. The closing 8-K and the proxy label the same figure total consideration payable and total funds needed, which include refinancing of existing debt and fees. Neither filing separately labels an equity value for the shares.
- **The debt at close.** The closing 8-K sets out the term loans and revolving facility under the new credit agreement and the bonds Parent sold in April 2026. It gives no single total for debt drawn at close, so the $18 billion expected when the deal was signed is not matched here to any drawn amount. The tranche and note amounts are in the filing and are left out of this entry because the gate cannot fetch sec.gov.
- **The delisting.** The shares came off Nasdaq, as the release says; the closing 8-K asks for trading to be suspended from the morning after the closing and for the shares to be delisted.

## The Consortium

**PIF**: the Saudi sovereign wealth fund; a rollover holder before the close and a member of the Consortium. The Crown Prince's chairmanship of PIF and the 2021 Affinity commitment ([[2021-06--affinity-partners-2b-saudi-pif-commitment]], *needs entry*) are carried in [[public-investment-fund-saudi]], not rechecked here.

**Silver Lake**: a technology investment firm that describes itself as having approximately $114 billion in combined assets under management and committed capital (Silver Lake's boilerplate in EA's closing release).

**Affinity Partners**: Kushner's firm, which describes itself as "a Miami-based investment firm founded in 2021 by Jared Kushner" with "over $6B under management" (Affinity's boilerplate in EA's closing release). Kushner is quoted in both EA releases as Affinity's CEO.

## What EA Is

Electronic Arts (headquartered in Redwood City, California) makes games including EA SPORTS FC, Battlefield, Apex Legends, The Sims, Madden NFL and College Football. The Madden and NFL marks belong to their owners and are used with permission, so those licences sit outside the company.

## Correction 2026-11-17: what the earlier version stated that the sources do not support

Earlier text (April 2026), item by item against EA's own releases:

- **Status.** The earlier text treated the deal as pending; it had closed.
- **Exchange.** It named the NYSE in its opening sentence. EA's release gives NASDAQ.
- **A claim of the largest leveraged buyout in history, surpassing an earlier take-private.** Not in any source read. EA's release makes a narrower claim, about all-cash sponsor take-privates, and it is EA's wording. The title no longer repeats a superlative as fact.
- **Equity value and enterprise value on one line.** Kept apart above: the enterprise value is what EA states; NBC's reported price carries no equity-value label in any source read.
