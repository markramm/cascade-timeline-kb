**What the filings show.** Building the Future, a super PAC, made independent-expenditure disclosures supporting Darline Graham in the 2026 South Carolina Senate race. The cumulative totals across the successive disclosures were $407,500, $1,296,413.89, $2,192,802.78, $2,470,605.56 and $2,510,605.56. The payee on each was Prusik Partners LLC of Henderson, Nevada.

**Who funds it is not shown.** The committee's one periodic filing, for the third quarter, lists $0 in every money field, so no donor to the committee is itemized in the filings read. This is a snapshot of those filings; a committee's receipts can appear on a later filing, and it is not a finding that the committee missed a deadline.

**Context, not a finding of connection.** The pre-general filings of Leading the Future and American Mission name the same treasurer, Britney Gonzalez, as Building the Future, with Henderson, Nevada addresses. A shared treasurer is not by itself evidence of common control or common funding.
