The **International Institute for Democracy and Electoral Assistance** (International IDEA) published its annual *Global State of Democracy* report on **September 15, 2026**, finding the United States at its lowest democratic performance in the fifty years the organization has tracked democracies. The U.S. declined on nearly half of IDEA's thirty indicators, with **judicial independence, credible elections, freedom of expression and press freedom** at or near their lowest recorded levels. The report finds the American decline is exerting destabilizing effects on democratic performance worldwide.

**This is the second major index to register the same shift in 2026.** V-Dem downgraded the United States out of the liberal-democracy category in March ([[2026-03-18--v-dem-downgrades-us-no-longer-liberal-democracy]]), and international democracy organizations flagged a U.S. crisis as early as February 2025 ([[2025-02-28--international-democracy-groups-us-crisis]]). Two independent measurement projects, different methodologies, converging direction. The value of a fifty-year series is that it prices the current moment against the whole record rather than against the prior administration.

**What this entry does not claim.** No sub-scores appear in the sources read — the "nearly half of thirty indicators" figure and the four named declining areas are as specific as the cited reporting gets, and the entry deliberately goes no further.
