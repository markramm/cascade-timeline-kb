- April 30, 2026 (signature date, per the enrolled act): the Homeland Security and Further Additional Continuing Appropriations Act, 2026 was approved. It treats "the period which began on or about February 14, 2026, during which there occurred a lapse in appropriations" as covered by the earlier continuing resolution, so the funding lapse ran from about February 14 through April 30, 2026. The House concurred in the Senate amendment by voice vote on the signing day; the Senate had passed it with an amendment by voice vote in March (bill status record).
- The act's text sets the final-bill amounts for ICE and for CBP Border Security Operations at $0 ("shall all be $0").
- A search of the enrolled text for mask, warrant, judicial, use of force, racial and sensitive location returned no hits. The one enforcement-conduct provision found is an additional $20,000,000 for body-worn cameras for agents and officers performing enforcement activities, with a spend plan due to the appropriations committees within 30 days. A separate provision keeps DHS from blocking a Member of Congress from entering a detention facility for oversight; whether it is new this year or carried over was not checked.
