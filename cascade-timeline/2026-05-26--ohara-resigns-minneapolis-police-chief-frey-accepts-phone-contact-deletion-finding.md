Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey accepted the resignation of Police Chief Brian O'Hara on May 26, 2026, after an outside investigation. The report "could not substantiate the allegations of sexual relationships with city employees," but found O'Hara likely "knowingly and intentionally" deleted a contact card for a witness from his city-issued phone. Frey: "Trust is not secondary to the job, it is the job."

Frey had announced on May 7, 2026 that he was nominating O'Hara for a second term. He named Bill Peterson interim chief on June 2, 2026.

**Context.** The MPR story rests the resignation on the conduct finding and does not tie it to the federal surge. Related: [[2026-01-24--ohara-bca-blocked-pretti-scene-contaminated]].
