Sen. Tim Sheehy (R-MT), who sits on the Armed Services Committee, gave The Daily Wire a statement that was published in a Daily Wire article that ran on Wednesday. The article does not say when Sheehy gave it, so the date on this entry is the article's publication date. Sheehy said: "I fully support labeling the Muslim Brotherhood as a foreign terrorist organization."

In the next paragraph, Sheehy added: "Abdul El-Sayed’s ties to this group raise serious national security questions. As senators, we have access to some of our nation’s most sensitive, classified information, and I have serious concerns about the security risks posed by anyone associated with such a group serving in the Senate." Read in sequence, "this group" is the Muslim Brotherhood, the organization named in the sentence directly before it.

The Daily Wire's article ran alongside Rogers's own post on the same Wednesday ([[2026-10-21--rogers-posts-el-sayed-radical-ideology-threat-to-national-security-and-repeats-risk-line-in-final-debate]]). The article also states that El-Sayed denied family ties to the Brotherhood, which the Daily Wire disputes; his own words are in [[2026-10-17--caro-town-hall-questioner-tommy-identified-as-former-hawley-operations-director-thomas-teepell]].
