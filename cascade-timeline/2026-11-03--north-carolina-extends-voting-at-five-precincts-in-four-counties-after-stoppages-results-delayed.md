On Tuesday, Nov. 3, North Carolina election officials extended voting at five precincts across four counties after what they described as "stoppages in voting during the day," WBTV reported. The latest extension ran until 8:15 p.m., in Columbus County.

"It was not immediately clear what the stoppages were or what caused them."
