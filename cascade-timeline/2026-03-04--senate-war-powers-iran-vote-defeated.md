On March 4, 2026, the U.S. Senate voted 47 to 53 on a motion to discharge S.J.Res. 104, a joint resolution to direct the removal of U.S. armed forces from hostilities within or against Iran that have not been authorized by Congress. The Senate's record gives the result as "Motion to Discharge Rejected", so the resolution never came to a vote on passage. This was Senate roll call vote 46.

Only GOP Sen. Rand Paul of Kentucky supported it, and Democratic Sen. John Fetterman of Pennsylvania voted against, per CBS News.

Kaine, Schumer and Schiff put out a release on March 03 2026 saying the Senate would vote the next day.

## Correction (2026-11-17)

The earlier title overstated the result as a defeat of the resolution itself; the title now names the motion. The earlier text also carried details about cosponsors, a floor speech, a vote held open and the House, which no source we read carries, and they are cut. The entry dated March 3 covers this same vote and is folded into this one, its id kept in previous_ids. Votes 58 and 69, on March 18, 2026 and March 24, 2026, are separate votes.
