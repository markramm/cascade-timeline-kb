CNN, in text carried by KRIS, reported that new tax filings show the Donald J. Trump Presidential Library Foundation had amassed $57.6 million by the end of 2025. The filings, provided to CNN by the foundation, do not identify the donors or say how much of the money came from legal settlements with media and tech companies. The foundation reported spending $349,000 in its first seven months, and it estimated it will bring in nearly $1 billion by the end of 2027.
