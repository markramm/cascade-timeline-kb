ICE says its Homeland Security Investigations agents arrested Osmal Sanchez Loy, a Cuban national, for unlawful voting on Oct. 23. ICE also says the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania has accepted the case for prosecution.

The court record charges a person named Osmal Omar Loy in a one-count information filed Oct 21, 2026; the docket labels the case a misdemeanor. The information says he knowingly voted in the 2024 election on or about November 5, 2024, in Philadelphia. The docket entry reads "INFORMATION UNSEALED" on Oct. 23, and an initial appearance was held on 10/23/2026. He pleaded not guilty to all counts, was released on bail of $10,000 O/R, and a jury trial is set for 12/7/2026.
