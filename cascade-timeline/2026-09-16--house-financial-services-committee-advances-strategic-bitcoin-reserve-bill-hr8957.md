The House Financial Services Committee voted **28-21** on **September 16, 2026** to advance **H.R. 8957, the American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026** — legislation that would give statutory footing to the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve first created by Trump's March 6, 2025 executive order. The bill, introduced **May 21, 2026** by Rep. Nick Begich (R-AK) with Rep. Jared Golden (D-ME) as a co-sponsor, would require Treasury to custody government-held Bitcoin in a dedicated facility, mandate a minimum **20-year holding period** during which the Bitcoin cannot be sold, swapped, auctioned, or otherwise disposed of, and direct proceeds from non-Bitcoin digital assets toward either further Bitcoin purchases or debt reduction. Rep. Bryan Steil (R-WI) called it "a smart financial strategy that will increase our reserve strength while reducing our deficit"; Rep. Bill Foster (D-IL) was a stated skeptic, citing Bitcoin's volatility.

**Distinct from the "big announcement."** This is a *legislative codification* track — separate from, and not a resolution of, the executive-branch "big announcement" on the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve that White House digital-asset adviser Patrick Witt began teasing in April 2026 (PCADA executive director). No reporting found around this committee vote ties it to Witt or to any PCADA statement; the bill (introduced in May, voted out of committee in September) proceeded on its own committee calendar. It still requires a full House floor vote, Senate passage of a companion or reconciled bill, and a presidential signature — a companion has not passed the Senate.

**Timing note, not established causation.** The markup landed one day after the CLARITY Act's Senate cloture vote failed 49-50 ([[2026-09-15--senate-cloture-fails-clarity-act-crypto-market-structure-dies]]). Crypto-trade coverage frames the sequence as the reserve bill "advancing just days after" the CLARITY Act failure; no primary source or committee statement in this pass claims the two are procedurally linked, and the reserve bill's introduction predates the CLARITY Act vote by four months.

**Sourcing note.** The bill text (introduced May 21, 2026, Rep. Begich with Rep. Golden) and its place on the committee's September 16 markup agenda are confirmed from Congress.gov and the committee's own release. The **28-21 tally** is as reported by The Block and BeInCrypto; it has not yet been checked against the committee's recorded-vote sheet, which had not been posted when this entry was written.
