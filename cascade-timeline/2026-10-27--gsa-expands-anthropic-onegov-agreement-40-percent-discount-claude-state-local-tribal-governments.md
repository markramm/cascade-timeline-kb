The U.S. General Services Administration announced on October 27, 2026 that it has expanded its OneGov agreement with Anthropic, providing government buyers with a 40% discount on token-based usage across the Claude models available to the government, including FedRAMP-authorized environments. GSA says agencies pay only for what they use, with no minimum orders, and that the agreement extends to state, local, and tribal governments as well as federal buyers.

GSA's release also says OneGov has generated more than $1.8 billion in savings for the federal government since its inception, including approximately $1.6 billion through its super intelligence agreements.
