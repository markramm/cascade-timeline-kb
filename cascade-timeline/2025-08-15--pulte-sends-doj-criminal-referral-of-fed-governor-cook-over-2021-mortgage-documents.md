FHFA Director William Pulte's referral letter to Attorney General Pamela Bondi and Special Attorney Edward Martin is dated August 15, 2025.

The letter says: "it appears an individual, Ms. Lisa DeNell Cook, has falsified bank documents and property records to acquire more favorable loan terms, potentially committing mortgage fraud under the criminal statute." It points to two 2021 transactions, a loan on June 18, 2021 and a condominium purchase on July 2, 2021. It is a referral letter. The entry holds no court finding on it.

The Associated Press report of Oct 14, 2026, carried by PBS NewsHour, reads: "Trump has accused Cook of committing mortgage fraud in 2021, before she joined the board, a claim she has denied. No criminal charges have been filed."
