ICE agent Christian Castro walked out of the Cameron County jail near Brownsville, Texas on August 27, 2026, three months after his May 29 arrest, because Texas law caps a hold on the subject of an extradition order at 90 days — at which point the state must either transfer or release. Castro faces four counts of second-degree assault and one count of falsely reporting a crime in Hennepin County, Minnesota, for firing through the closed front door of a Minneapolis home during Operation Metro Surge and hitting Julio Cesar Sosa-Celis in the leg. Castro had told investigators that Sosa-Celis and another Venezuelan man attacked him with a broom and a snow shovel; assault charges filed against those two men were dropped after surveillance footage established that Castro's account was false. Governor Tim Walz filed the rendition paperwork; Governor Greg Abbott neither signed nor denied it, saying he does not trust Walz "on anything" and conditioning extradition on Walz first acknowledging responsibility for fraud in federal programs in Minnesota. Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison sued to compel Abbott to sign or to bar Sheriff Manuel Treviño from releasing Castro; U.S. District Judge Fernando Rodriguez Jr., a Trump appointee in the Southern District of Texas, held the claim unripe because Abbott had done neither and Castro was still in custody. The clock then ran out.

The structure here does the work that no explicit immunity grant would survive: a governor's inaction, a ripeness doctrine that requires harm before it will act, and a statutory 90-day ceiling combine so that a state criminal charge against a federal immigration officer expires without anyone having to deny it. Each component is individually defensible and the aggregate is a functioning impunity mechanism — which is what makes it durable. It follows the same jurisdictional pattern as Hennepin County Attorney Mary Moriarty's earlier charging of ICE agent Morgan ([[2026-04-16--moriarty-charges-ice-agent-morgan-assault-minnesota]]) and Bovino's Minnesota criminal investigation ([[2026-03-03--bovino-criminal-investigation-minnesota-ice]]), and sits inside the broader Minnesota federal-enforcement conflict ([[2026-08-13--operation-puppet-master-hsi-infiltrates-minnesota-anti-ice-community]], [[2026-08-05--dhs-contempt-ice-agent-personal-phones-la-sweeps]]). Castro was suspended without pay.
