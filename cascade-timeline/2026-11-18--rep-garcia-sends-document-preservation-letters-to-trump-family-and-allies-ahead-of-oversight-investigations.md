On Wednesday, the highest-ranking Democrat on the House Oversight Committee, Rep. Robert Garcia, who is poised to become the chair of the committee, sent document preservation letters to over a dozen of President Trump's allies and family members, laying the groundwork for investigations in January. NPR reports the targets fall into these categories: "Trump family corruption, corporate corruption, DHS misconduct, and the White House Epstein cover-up."

The letters follow White House Counsel Will Scharf's earlier letter warning that Democratic investigations "may cross the line into blatant criminality" ([[2026-11-09--white-house-counsel-scharf-letter-warns-garcia-oversight-subpoenas-may-cross-line-into-blatant-criminality]]). A White House spokeswoman responded: "If Democrats choose to pursue sham investigations and radical left policies, they are choosing to work against the American people."
