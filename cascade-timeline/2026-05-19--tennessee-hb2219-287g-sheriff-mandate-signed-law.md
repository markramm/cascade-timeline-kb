Gov. Bill Lee signed HB2219 on 05/19/2026. It became Public Chapter 1015. The amendment that rewrote the bill requires, by January 1, 2027, the sheriff of each county to enter into an agreement under an available federal 287(g) program. The Senate passed it with 24 ayes and 7 nays. The legislature's summary of the amendment says a violation may result in the state withholding all funds of the state allotted to the sheriff's office or to the local government for use by the sheriff's office. The kind of law is a mandate to sign agreements.

WTVF reported that 69 of Tennessee's 95 counties were enrolled in 287(g) at the time, and quoted Davidson County Sheriff Daron Hall: "It's all politics."

Other states enacted mandates of this kind earlier. Florida's law, approved June 17, 2022, requires each law enforcement agency that operates a county detention facility to enter into a written agreement with ICE by January 1, 2023. Texas SB 8, signed 06/20/2025 and effective January 1, 2026, says the sheriff of each county that operates a jail or contracts with a private vendor to operate one shall request and enter into an immigration law enforcement agreement. Tennessee's text speaks of the sheriff of each county, not only those who run jails, which is a difference in scope among these laws.
