Forbidden Stories reports that Shalev Hulio and Omri Lavie created NSO Group in 2010 after bringing on Niv Carmi, whom it describes as a former Mossad intelligence operative and security expert. By its account the company's name is drawn from the founders' names (Niv, Shalev and Omrie, or NSO, for short), and each had a role: Niv Carmi handled the tech and Hulio and Lavie the business.

Forbidden Stories describes Hulio and Lavie as friends from childhood and from later military training. The sources this entry carries do not state which unit any founder served in, so the entry does not. The founding year is the one Forbidden Stories gives, and the entry's date marks the year, not a day.

## Correction (2026-11-17)

Removed from the earlier version: the founders' military unit, one founder's departure, and the encyclopedia and trade-magazine sources. The founder's surname follows Forbidden Stories.
