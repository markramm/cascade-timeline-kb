The Palm Springs, California City Council approved a 45-day urgency moratorium on data centers on Aug. 26, 2026, barring the city from accepting, processing or approving data-center applications, at the request of Councilmember Grace Garner. City staff said data centers are "arguably" already prohibited under the zoning code, so the ordinance removes doubt while a citywide zoning update proceeds.

NBC Palm Springs reported that the council voted unanimously to extend the pause by 22 months and 15 days. See also [[synthesis-august-datacenter-moratorium-wave-2026-08-27]].
