Senate Republicans are pausing President Donald Trump's request to pass $350 billion in defense funding through a party-line bill to attempt to negotiate a deal with Democrats after facing midterm backlash over the Iran war, the Washington Post reports. Senate Majority Leader John Thune told reporters Wednesday that Republicans want to "explore all the possibilities for funding the military, one of which would be seeing whether or not there's an appetite on the Democrat side to do it". Sen. Rand Paul said: "I'm not for a partisan Republican bill to spend more money or borrow more money".

Republicans control the Senate 53-47, and the party's margin is set to shrink Dec. 15, when the term of Sen. Jon Husted expires. The $350 billion is part of Trump's broader $1.5 trillion defense budget request for fiscal year 2027. Arrington told The Washington Post: "I'm worried that they're going to pass something that just is a clean $350 billion in spending with no offsets".
