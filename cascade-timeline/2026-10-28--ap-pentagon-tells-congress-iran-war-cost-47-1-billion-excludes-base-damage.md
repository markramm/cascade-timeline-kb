AP reported that the Pentagon gave Congress a new estimate of $47.1 billion for the Iran war, a figure as of Oct. 16. AP says the estimate "does not include damage to U.S. bases in the Middle East." Replacing munitions accounts for about $29 billion.

The same report gives the Pentagon's official casualty tally as 21 service members killed and 865 wounded.
