Judge Milan D. Smith, Jr. wrote the Ninth Circuit's opinion in *Amazon.com Services, LLC v. Perplexity AI, Inc.*, No. 26-1444, dated August 4, 2026. The opinion states, "we vacate the district court’s preliminary injunction and remand for further proceedings." It also states that "it is a tool, not a person for statutory purposes." On the facts before it, the court wrote, it is the user who accesses Amazon’s computers, with the help of the Assistant.

This is a private-party case under the federal Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA). The caption lists Amazon.com Services, LLC as plaintiff-appellee and Perplexity AI, Inc. as defendant-appellant. The opinion does not address a government agency's use of AI agents, and the ruling is limited, in the court's own words, to "the facts before us."
