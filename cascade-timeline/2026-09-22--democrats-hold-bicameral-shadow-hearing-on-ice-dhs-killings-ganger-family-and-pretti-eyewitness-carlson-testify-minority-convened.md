On September 22, 2026, congressional Democrats held a forum on fatal shootings by immigration agents. The event was minority-convened: the Oversight Democrats' own page announced that "Oversight Dems will be holding another ICE hearing today", CBS Minnesota reported that Sen. Richard Blumenthal and Rep. Robert Garcia led it and that Republican colleagues invited did not attend, and Rep. Chellie Pingree's office called it a "bicameral shadow hearing". Rep. Ilhan Omar's office release says the witnesses joined the Committees as witnesses, naming the House Oversight Committee and the Senate Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations as hosts. The House Oversight majority's hearing calendar lists no hearing that day.

Witnesses included Donna and Brent Ganger, the mother and brother of Renee Good, and Stella Carlson, who filmed Alex Pretti's killing. Donna Ganger: "She should still be alive". Brent Ganger: "The time is absolutely now for accountability for the deep damage done by ICE and others". Carlson: "I watched Alex be taken to the ground with a swarm of masked agents on top of him", and in written testimony, "The truth does not become less true because someone with great power tells a louder lie".
