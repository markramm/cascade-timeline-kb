In a post on X (UTC timestamp on the entry date; the previous evening in U.S. time), Jake Lang wrote that "Christian Crusaders will descend upon the US Capitol in Washington DC to PROTEST the Certification of the RIGGED Midterms" (his post, read via X's syndication endpoint; quoted by Baptist News Global). Lang faced an 11-count indictment for the Capitol attack and was pardoned by Trump on Jan. 20, 2025, before trial.

The attached flyer, for a "Million Crusader Cross March," promises "Peaceful protest & Christian unity" and tells followers "Bring Your Cross." It sets the start for 11 a.m. on January 3, 2027; new congressional terms begin at noon on January 3, 2027. One pastor named on it posted that night: "I’m not speaking at this event. Never said I was. Please remove my name." Sewell, who co-led Lang's Dearborn march and sits on Mike Rogers's Senate campaign faith leadership team, said on Daystar's FlashPoint after the election: "We need another January 6."

**Not established.** Any permit application to the U.S. Capitol Police or National Park Service. Neither agency publishes a searchable register of demonstration permits.

Reporting by Wendy A. Lawrence (Glass Empires) led us here; the post itself and its timestamp were read at source.
