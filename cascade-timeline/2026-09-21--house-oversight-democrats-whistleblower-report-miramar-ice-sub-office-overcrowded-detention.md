Democrats on the House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform released a whistleblower report on Sept. 21 that prompted the committee to open an investigation. According to The Bulwark, the report found the Miramar, Florida ICE office, with a maximum capacity of 50 people, at one point held 342 people. ICE employees told lawmakers of brutal conditions that carried an "imminent risk of serious harm or death", per WLRN. The facility is intended as a check-in and processing center where people are held for less than 12 hours.

A federal class action filed in Miami the Friday after WLRN's report alleges DHS and ICE are using the office as a de facto civil detention center. The suit puts the maximum occupancy at 56 rather than 50 and alleges as many as 150 people held there at the time it was filed.
