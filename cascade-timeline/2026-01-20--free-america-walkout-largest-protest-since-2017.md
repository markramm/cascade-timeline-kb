The "Free America Walkout" took place on January 20, 2026, the anniversary of President Donald Trump's second inauguration. People walked out of school and work across the country, and the Women's March was one of the organizing partners.

The protests targeted ICE raids and National Guard deployments. Fox News reported that thousands of people flooded the streets, with demonstrators converging in Atlanta, New York City, Minneapolis and Washington, D.C. Fox described the Women's March as the group that mobilized millions of protesters against Trump's first administration in 2017. The protests followed the shooting death of Renee Nicole Good by an ICE agent in Minneapolis.

Neither TIME nor Fox News gave a national turnout count, and this entry does not state one.

## Correction (2026-11-17)

This entry has been edited: the earlier turnout, event-count, protest-count and ranking statements are removed (the old text is kept below in a comment), and the title and importance were revised. The Crowd Counting Consortium's researchers estimated that between 2 and 4.8 million people participated in the No Kings protests of June 14, 2025, and wrote that No Kings was probably the second-largest single day demonstration since January 2017. The earlier claim of the largest day of protest since the 2017 Women's March therefore does not hold. The entry's title and importance were revised to match.
