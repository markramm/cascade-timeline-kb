DOE posted a letter from Under Secretary for Science Darío Gil titled One Year In on November 12, 2026.

The letter says DOE mobilized its first cohort in under a year, with 297 multidisciplinary teams across 369 institutions.

The letter says $2.5 billion in SI tools and computing resources have been committed.

The letter says the Genesis Mission Consortium now comprises more than 70 partners.

The letter says the first call for proposals drew over 5,000 proposals.

The letter says federal agency partners have announced more than $5 billion in combined support.

The letter does not say what the $2.5 billion is made of or who committed it. See [[genesis-year-one-scorecard]].
