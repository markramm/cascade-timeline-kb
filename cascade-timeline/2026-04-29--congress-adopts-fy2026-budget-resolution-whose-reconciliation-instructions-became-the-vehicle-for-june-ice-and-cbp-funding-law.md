- April 29, 2026 (the date the enrolled resolution was agreed to, completing adoption): Congress adopted the concurrent resolution on the budget for fiscal year 2026. The Senate agreed to it with an amendment, 50 - 48; the House agreed 215 - 211 with one voting present.
- Its reconciliation title instructed the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs and Judiciary committees, and their House counterparts, each to report changes that increase the deficit by not more than $70,000,000,000 over fiscal years 2026 through 2035.
- The later reconciliation law, Public Law 119-98, was approved June 10, 2026; its own text opens "To provide for reconciliation pursuant to title II of S. Con. Res. 33."
