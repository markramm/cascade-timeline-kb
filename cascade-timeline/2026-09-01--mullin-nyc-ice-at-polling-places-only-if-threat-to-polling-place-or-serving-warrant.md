Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin spoke to reporters in New York City on Tuesday (statement date), and said: “The only reason why we would be at polling places is if there is a threat to that polling place or we're serving a warrant on someone that we have been actively tracking down,” This entry relies on Raw Story's text, which attributes the account to Politico.

The statement is a conditional that names circumstances and does not say any is planned.
