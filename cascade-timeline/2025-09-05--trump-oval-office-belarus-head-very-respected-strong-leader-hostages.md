Trump says in the Oval Office on September 5, 2025: "The head of Belarus, who's a very respected man—strong—strong person, strong leader—he released 16 hostages."

Later in the same remarks, Trump says of the leader of Belarus: "he's a very strong man."

Trump also says Belarus had "another 1,400 hostages."
