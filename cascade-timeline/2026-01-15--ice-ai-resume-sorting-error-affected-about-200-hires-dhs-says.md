During the 2025 hiring surge, ICE used an AI tool to categorize applicants' résumés. According to two law enforcement officials who spoke to [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/immigration/ice-error-meant-recruits-sent-field-offices-proper-training-sources-sa-rcna254054), it flagged anyone with the word "officer" on their résumé, including a "compliance officer" or people who said they aspired to be ICE officers.

A DHS spokesperson said the error "impacted about 200 hires who then reported to the Federal Law Enforcement Training Center for full training."
