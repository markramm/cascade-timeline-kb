Acting ICE Director David Venturella announced August 11, 2026 that the agency is "on track" to equip all field personnel with body cameras by the end of August, clarifying that footage will only be released when it would not compromise "investigations or privacy" — an undefined standard that leaves disclosure to agency discretion.

The rollout follows the same director's June 2026 decision, documented at [[2026-06-04--venturella-memo-eliminates-post-release-death-reporting-ice-accountability-erasure]], to eliminate post-release death reporting. A bodycam program without mandatory disclosure rules extends rather than closes the accountability gap: cameras generate footage, but the "appropriate" release standard gives ICE the same discretionary control over what becomes public that it exercised in narrowing death reporting.
