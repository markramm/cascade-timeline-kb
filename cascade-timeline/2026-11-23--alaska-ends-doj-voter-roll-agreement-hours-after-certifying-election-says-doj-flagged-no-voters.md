Alaska Lt. Gov. Nancy Dahlstrom announced on Monday that the state is terminating its agreement with the U.S. Department of Justice to share uncensored voter information, effective immediately. The announcement came hours after the Alaska Division of Elections certified the election results.

According to the state's statement, the DOJ did not flag anyone to be removed. The statement said no voters were removed from Alaska's voter rolls as a result of the agreement. The division said it had directed the DOJ to destroy Alaska's confidential voter information.

The agreement's memorandum of understanding stated that the division would remove voters from the rolls within 45 days of a request from the DOJ if it deemed them ineligible. The lawsuit challenging the agreement, brought by the League of Women Voters, Alaska Black Caucus and the Alaska Public Interest Research Group, is still open in Anchorage Superior Court.
