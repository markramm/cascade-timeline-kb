Daisy Thomas was elected chair of the Utah Democratic Party at the organization convention on Saturday, June 17, 2017, according to KUER. The KUER article does not mention Bernie Sanders, Our Revolution or any alignment for her.

## Alignment, as attributed

An Associated Press story of June 24, 2019, carried by Utah Public Radio, says: "She supported Bernie Sanders in the last presidential election." That is AP's statement about her 2016 presidential preference. It is not a statement that she led a Sanders slate. A post said to be from Our Revolution, reposted on Reddit, describes her as an Our Revolution member; the repost refused the fetch tool (HTTP 403) and was not read.

## What happened

At the party's organizing convention in Park City, Jeff Merchant defeated Thomas in June 2019, per AP. Merchant won "more than 76% of votes." AP says he pledged to lift the party from "financial and relational turmoil." AP gives no specifics of either.
