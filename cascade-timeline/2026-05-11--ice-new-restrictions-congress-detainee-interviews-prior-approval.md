An ICE policy memo signed by the acting ICE director, handed to two California members of Congress on their Monday visit to Otay Mesa Detention Center, requires members to name each detainee at least two business days before a visit and to provide a signed consent form from each detainee, per the Spokesman-Review. The memo calls congressional visits disruptive and resource-intensive.

Rep. Mike Levin (D-Calif.) called the policy "a deliberate effort to make sure we don't hear from people in ICE custody"; that is his characterization, not ICE's. DHS and ICE had not responded to a request for comment when the story ran.
