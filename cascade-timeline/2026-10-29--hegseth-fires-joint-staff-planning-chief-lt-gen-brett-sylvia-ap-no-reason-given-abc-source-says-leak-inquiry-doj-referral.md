Army Lt. Gen. Brett Sylvia was removed as head of strategy, plans and policy on the Joint Staff. The Associated Press reported on Oct. 29, 2026 that a defense official said Wednesday that Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth had fired him.

According to the AP, he is the second general ousted from that post under Hegseth. According to the AP, his predecessor, Lt. Gen. Joseph McGee, was forced to retire in October 2025.

According to the AP, the defense official said no reason has been given for the firing. ABC News reported that a source said Sylvia was removed for a lack of confidence. ABC News also reported that a senior Pentagon official said Hegseth fired him for "severe misconduct" after an inquiry into alleged leaks of classified information to the press, and that an investigation had been referred to the Justice Department.

The two accounts of the reason differ. Neither names a document.

The AP counts the firing as the latest among nearly two dozen generals, admirals and other military leaders who have been fired, forced to retire or otherwise departed under Hegseth's tenure.
