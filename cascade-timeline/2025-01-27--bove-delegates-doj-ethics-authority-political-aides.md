The senators' letter to Attorney General Pam Bondi and Acting Deputy Attorney General Emil Bove objects to a decision to grant "decision-making authority regarding sensitive ethics and personnel issues" to political appointees. The letter says the changes were "reportedly outlined in a January 27 memo from Acting Deputy Attorney General Emil Bove" and asks for the memo, which it dates January 27, 2025. The letter describes the memo; the memo itself was not read for this entry.

## What the letter says the memo did

- It reassigned responsibilities the senators describe as long held by a senior career official to Bove's chief of staff, who "graduated from law school in 2021," and to a former criminal defense attorney on President Trump's defense team. The letter says neither had worked at the Department before.
- It gave them final decision-making authority over "adverse personnel actions and bar referral matters," and reportedly over "nominee financial disclosures" and other ethics matters.
- The letter also asks for records on the removal of Associate Deputy Attorney General Bradley Weinsheimer from his position with authority over ethics and employee discipline. The letter names him; this entry takes the removal as the senators' description.

## The senators' reading

The senators wrote that "Mr. Bove's directive eliminated a critical safeguard against corruption within the Department." That is their characterization.
