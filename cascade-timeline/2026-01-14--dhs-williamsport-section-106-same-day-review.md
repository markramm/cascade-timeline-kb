## Correction (2026-10-26)

The earlier title and text of this entry (a same-day open-and-close of the review) are withdrawn; see the corrections record in the frontmatter. The DHS letter (Exhibit A to the State of Maryland's complaint) is dated January 12, 2026, has the subject line "Initiation of Consultation and Finding of No Historic Properties Affected", and asks for comments "within 30 calendar days of the date of receipt of this letter". The Maryland Historical Trust concurred on January 29 and asked DHS for more information on February 10, 2026 (see [[2026-02-10--maryland-historical-trust-asks-dhs-for-more-information-wright-road-relocation]]). The filename keeps "same-day-review" so existing links resolve; the framing is withdrawn. The primary-source entry is [[2026-01-12--dhs-ice-section-106-letter-to-maryland-historical-trust-williamsport]].

## Opening paragraph

On January 14, 2026, a letter addressed to the Historic District Commission, Washington County Planning and Zoning Department was received from the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, per the county. It concerned an undertaking subject to Section 106 of the National Historic Preservation Act (NHPA). The DHS letter to the Maryland Historical Trust is dated January 12, 2026 and proposes a finding of no historic properties affected. The State's complaint alleges that on January 16, 2026 ICE executed the deed to the Williamsport warehouse, without awaiting any response from the Trust (see [[2026-01-16--dhs-purchases-fundrise-williamsport-md-warehouse]]).

## What Happened / Key Facts

Date kinds: letter dated January 12; received (county) January 14; deed executed January 16 and recorded January 22, 2026 (both per the State's complaint); Trust concurrence January 29.

The letter's 30-day comment period runs from receipt, so it extended past the deed. The Trust's February 10, 2026 letter says it would be able to provide "informed and updated findings regarding the complete project's effects on historic properties" once DHS supplied more information. The complaint says, "Upon information and belief, DHS has not responded to the Maryland Historical Trust's request for more information."

## Why This Event Matters

The checkable point is sequence: consultation initiated and a no-effect finding proposed January 12, deed executed January 16, Trust concurrence January 29, after the deed.
