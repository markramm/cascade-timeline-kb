At an executive session on August 13, 2024 (vote date), the Federal Election Commission decided by a vote of 6-0 to dismiss MUR 8125, a complaint about DCCC payments to Elias Law Group from the party's recounts-and-legal-proceedings account.

**The complaint.** Filed by the Committee to Defeat the President and Ted Harvey, it contended that "no general election recounts were pending" when the payments labeled "RECOUNT LEGAL SVCS" were made.

**The record.** The FEC general counsel's report counted 126 such disbursements from October 19, 2021 to July 15, 2022, totaling $5,542,969.36, of which $5,177,460.62 went to Elias Law Group. The DCCC answered that FEC guidance has it label every payment from that account as a recount payment "whether the expense is for a recount or for an election contest" or another legal proceeding. The Commission dismissed the allegations against the DCCC and the firm.

**How to read party filings after this.** Contributions to this account run to 300% of the national-party limit, and its payments are reported as other disbursements on Line 29, outside the FEC's bulk operating-expenditure file. The word recount on a national party's legal payment identifies the paying account; it is not evidence that a recount took place.
