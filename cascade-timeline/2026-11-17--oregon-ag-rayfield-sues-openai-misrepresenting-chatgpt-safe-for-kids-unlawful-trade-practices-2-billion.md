Oregon's Department of Justice sued OpenAI on Tuesday, accusing it of violating the state's consumer protection laws by misrepresenting ChatGPT as safe for kids. The complaint calls for OpenAI to institute stronger safeguards and compensate Oregonians with at least $2 billion in damages. Attorney General Dan Rayfield said there was no specific incident in Oregon that led his office to file suit. OPB reports that eight U.S. states sued OpenAI in October.
