On March 4, 2026, the Rev. Mariah Tollgaard, senior pastor of Hamline Church United Methodist in St. Paul, testified before the U.S. House Oversight Committee alongside Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz and Attorney General Keith Ellison. UM News said she was the only non-elected official speaking.

Her opening statement described herself as "an ordinary Minnesotan who has witnessed, up close, the fear and harm that Operation Metro Surge has inflicted on our state." She told the committee "No nation can build a true future on the terror of its own people."

She was one of the faith leaders arrested at the Minneapolis-St. Paul airport in the January clergy protest (see [[2026-01-22--march-clergy-westminster-minneapolis-airport-arrests]]). UM News reports she described that arrest as handled respectfully by local law enforcement.

Scope: her public testimony and role only.
