On 2025-10-01 the Trump administration sent nine universities — Brown, Dartmouth, MIT, University of Arizona, University of Pennsylvania, USC, UT Austin, University of Virginia, and Vanderbilt (per Wikipedia, sourced to Washington Post/NYT/Cavalier Daily reporting) — a document titled the "Compact for Academic Excellence in Higher Education," offering funding preference in exchange for commitments on admissions, campus speech, and other policy areas. **Princeton was not among the nine invited universities.** Princeton President Christopher Eisgruber nonetheless publicly criticized the Compact, in a LinkedIn post dated 2025-10-10 and a CNN "Global Public Square" interview with Fareed Zakaria the week of 2025-10-12 (Daily Princetonian article reporting these, published 2025-10-20). Verbatim quotes, per the Daily Princetonian's report of Eisgruber's LinkedIn post: "a dangerous step in the wrong direction"; "It should be withdrawn, and we should work together to find better ways to strengthen the research enterprise on which America so vitally depends." Per the same report of his CNN remarks: "[The compact] is an example of using federal funds to try to control what it is universities are doing or teaching"; "It's going to erode the quality of the research and it's going to erode academic freedom."

**This is not a letter** — it is a LinkedIn post plus a broadcast interview, both individual (not joint/coalition) statements from a university not party to the document being criticized. The direct-party rejections came from Dartmouth (Beilock), Brown (Paxson), MIT, Penn, USC, UVA, and Arizona (Garimella) — a different set of presidents than Garber/Eisgruber. AAC&U issued a separate **organizational** statement on the Compact on 2025-10-03 (not individually signed by university presidents) and a follow-on joint statement "Higher Education's Compact with America: Shared Principles for the Common Good" on 2025-10-17, co-issued with Phi Beta Kappa Society and signed by 70+ college/university presidents — but Garber and Eisgruber's names do not appear among the signers found in that document's endorsement list as fetched.
