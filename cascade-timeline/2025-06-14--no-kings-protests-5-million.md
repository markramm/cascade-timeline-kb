People demonstrate during a "No Kings" protest Saturday, June 14, 2025, in Nashville, Tenn. Organizers estimated that more than 5 million people participated in more than 2,000 planned protests, NPR reported. Protests were held in over 2,100 cities and towns, Democracy Now reported.

The Crowd Counting Consortium has been collecting data on protest events and participation. Its estimate: between 2 and 4.8 million people participated in over 2,150 actions. The authors could not confirm figures at 18 percent of events, and No Kings saw events in over three times as many locations as the 2017 Women's March.

Truthout described the protests as inspired by the 3.5 percent principle.

## Correction (2026-11-17)

The title attributes the organizers' count to organizers and the other count to the Consortium. The 3.5 percent line, the size rankings and the Washington Post link were removed, and the NPR link was replaced.
