On Monday, U.S. District Judge Mustafa Kasubhai ruled that ICE officers have continued to make arrests in Oregon without warrants and failed to properly document them, "in violation of his February order," OPB reported. The government had told Innovation Law Lab, which brought the class action, that "only 13 arrests" lacked a proper warrant; Kasubhai wrote: "The Court finds that ICE officers conducted significantly more than 13 warrantless arrests in Oregon between February 2026 and August 2026."

He found that an ICE deportation officer, identified as J.G., "lied in an official record to facilitate the impression he was conducting a lawful warrant-based arrest," and wrote that the misconduct "calls into question the veracity of all I-213 forms completed by ICE officers in Oregon." Of boilerplate language in the reports, he wrote: "This is sloppy work." The order requires the government to "provide all arrest documents to Innovation Law Lab" and ICE to produce all "body-worn camera footage for arrests conducted on or after Sept. 1, 2026." The written order itself was not read for this entry; the account is OPB's. Earlier testimony in Kasubhai's court is at [[2025-12-02--ice-agent-testimony-elite-quota-kasubhai-oregon]].
