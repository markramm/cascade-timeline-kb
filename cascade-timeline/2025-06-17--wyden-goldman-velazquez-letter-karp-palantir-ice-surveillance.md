## Opening

On June 17, 2025, Sen. Ron Wyden (D-Ore.), the Senate Finance Committee's ranking member, and Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-N.Y.) sent a letter to Palantir Technologies CEO Alex Karp, joined by eight other Democratic members of Congress: Sens. Elizabeth Warren, Jeffrey Merkley and Edward Markey, and Reps. James McGovern, Sara Jacobs, Rashida Tlaib, Summer Lee and Rep. Paul Tonko. The letter demanded information about reports that Palantir is helping the IRS build a government-wide, searchable "mega-database" of taxpayer data, and it raised Palantir's work for ICE.

## What the letter says

The letter's lead concern is the IRS. It cites press reports that Palantir employees have been installed at the agency to help create a "single, searchable database" of taxpayer records, argues that sharing such a database across government would violate the Internal Revenue Code (sections 6103 and 7213A) and the Privacy Act of 1974, and says Palantir and its employees can face liability. ICE is a secondary paragraph: it quotes press reports that Palantir's ICE work includes "producing leads for law enforcement to find people to deport." The letter also cites a $795 million Defense Department award to Palantir.

It asks for a litigation hold on records, and answers by July 10, 2025, to seven questions: all current federal contracts with value, agency, product and services; whether Palantir sought or received assurances against liability; whether it has paid or committed to pay executives' legal costs and fines; what administration requests it declined; whether it knows and has advised on Privacy Act requirements; whether it has a "red line" that would end its government services; and how many employees have quit since January 20, 2025 over the work.

## What the letter does not say

The letter does not name ELITE, ImmigrationOS, Medicaid or the CMS-ICE data-sharing agreement, passport or license-plate data, the 2024 PCLOB recommendations, the Anti-Deficiency Act, or Clark Minor, and it sets no 30-day deadline, and Reps. Goldman and Velazquez did not sign it. An earlier version of this entry attributed those to it.

## Correction (2026-11-16)

This entry was rechecked against the letter on the Senate Finance Committee site and the committee's press release. Corrected:

1. **Signatories.** The entry named Wyden, Goldman and Velazquez with "approximately 30 lawmakers." The letter is signed by Wyden, Ocasio-Cortez and eight other members, ten in all; Reps. Goldman and Velazquez are not signatories. The Goldman-Wyden-Velazquez letter with 30 lawmakers is a different, later letter, sent in April 2026 to DHS and ICE rather than to Palantir: [[2026-04-14--goldman-wyden-30-lawmakers-demand-palantir-elite-immigrationos-oversight]]. The two had been merged. The title is corrected; the filename keeps its old slug so existing links resolve.
2. **Subject.** The entry described a letter about ELITE, ImmigrationOS and Medicaid data. The letter is about the IRS mega-database, with ICE work in one paragraph. The five-point list of asks (ICE contracts, IRS/SSA/passport/license-plate integration, Medicaid and ELITE, Privacy Act/PCLOB/Anti-Deficiency compliance, HHS personnel, SEC disclosure) was not in the letter and is replaced with the letter's seven questions.
3. **Deadline.** Not 30 days: July 10, 2025.
4. **Karp's non-response, and publication in full.** Neither is in the letter or the press release page we read. Palantir's answer, if any, is unverified (see gaps); the claim is removed as a finding.
5. **Inferences stated as documented, removed:** that the letter "mapped" the ELITE, Medicaid and ICE architecture, that it "predicts" the January 2026 EFF exposure, and that it was the first major congressional oversight of Palantir and ICE. The June 2025 letter predates any ELITE reporting we hold, and the ELITE and Medicaid material in this record comes from later sources. Related entries that cite this letter as a Karp or ELITE letter ([[2026-08-10--lawfare-documents-three-dhs-ai-systems-immigrationos-hurricane-score-asylum-analytics]]) need the same correction.
