U.S. District Court Chief Judge Patrick Schiltz, a George W. Bush appointee, wrote in a Wednesday order that an appendix "identifies 96 court orders that ICE has violated in 74 cases" in Minnesota since the start of January 2026. He wrote that ICE "has likely violated more court orders in January 2026 than some federal agencies have violated in their entire existence," and that the list was incomplete: "This list is confined to orders issued since January 1, 2026, and the list was hurriedly compiled by extraordinarily busy judges. Undoubtedly, mistakes were made, and orders that should have appeared on this list were omitted." The cases were all habeas cases challenging the legality of a person's detention.

The Fox Minneapolis station described judges losing patience with an agency that quickly moves detainees to detention centers out of state but fails to return them to Minnesota by court-ordered deadlines, and reported that the U.S. Attorney's office had revealed it was taking ICE four days to return a detainee to Minnesota.

The sequence, per CNBC: in an order on Monday, Schiltz demanded that Acting ICE Director Todd Lyons appear on Friday to explain why federal officials refused to hold a court-ordered bond hearing for a detained immigrant, Juan Hugo Tobay Robles. The immigrant was released from custody in Texas on Tuesday, a day after Schiltz scheduled the hearing. In his order on Wednesday the judge wrote, "As promised, the Court will cancel the hearing, and Lyons will not be required to appear."

Schiltz wrote: "ICE is not a law unto itself," and "ICE has every right to challenge the orders of this Court, but, like any litigant, ICE must follow those orders unless and until they are overturned or vacated."

DHS Assistant Secretary Tricia McLaughlin, in a statement to the Fox Minneapolis station, said: "Despite the diatribe from this activist judge, the only order involved with this case issued yesterday was that Acting Director Lyons was no longer ordered to appear to testify in court." The statement ends: "We will not be deterred by activists either in the streets or on the bench."
