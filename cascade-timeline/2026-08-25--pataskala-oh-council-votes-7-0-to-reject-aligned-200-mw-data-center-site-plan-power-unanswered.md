The Pataskala, Ohio City Council voted 7-0 on Tuesday night to reject a site plan for Aligned Data Centers' 200-megawatt campus, accepting the Planning and Zoning Commission's earlier 5-2 recommendation to deny. Council members said the developer could not give specific information about power, which would be decided by the Ohio Power Siting Board only after city approval; Councilman Chad Wagy said electricity was "because it's in limbo with AEP and the power siting board."

Voters still face a November referendum on whether to allow data centers that use over 25 megawatts. See also [[synthesis-august-datacenter-moratorium-wave-2026-08-27]].
