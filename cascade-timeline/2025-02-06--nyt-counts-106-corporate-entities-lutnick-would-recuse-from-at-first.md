The New York Times counted 106 different corporate entities in matters involving which Howard Lutnick, then the nominee for Commerce Secretary, would have to recuse himself, at least initially, if confirmed, as Salon relayed it on February 6, 2025. Salon, citing the Times, called it the largest number of recusal requirements imposed on a member of Trump's cabinet.

## Correction (2026-11-16)

An earlier version of this entry carried the wrong unit, the wrong comparison, an earlier date and news links that do not match real articles. The count is of corporate entities, not conflicts. It is the Times count as Salon relays it, and the figure is absent from Lutnick's ethics agreement and his financial disclosure. The comparison is to Trump's cabinet, not to every cabinet or to watchdog groups' tracking. The date now follows the Salon page. The net worth and the control of his former firm were dropped because nothing read here carries them. The Times article itself was not reachable and was not read.
