Paul Dans departed as Project 2025 director on July 30, 2024, amid Trump campaign pressure to distance from the Heritage Foundation blueprint. The departure followed campaign concerns about the project becoming a political liability, though Russell Vought later claimed Trump privately supports the work. In September 2024, Heritage Foundation revealed Dans was actually fired due to 'dismissive and unprofessional' workplace behavior, particularly at the Republican National Convention. Dans had led the policy development phase of Project 2025, which was nearly complete by July 2024.

## Update (2024-07-30, reporting date): Correction: a duplicate entry with a conflicting date is merged here

A second entry for this departure carried a start-of-month date that its own text contradicted, and it is merged here; its old id redirects through previous_ids. Its headline claim about Kevin Roberts is not carried over. The AP report carried by PBS says the Project 2025 director had stepped down, as a Heritage spokesperson confirmed. It quotes Roberts only on the project having "completed exactly what it set out to do", and it carries a statement from campaign advisers Susie Wiles and Chris LaCivita.
