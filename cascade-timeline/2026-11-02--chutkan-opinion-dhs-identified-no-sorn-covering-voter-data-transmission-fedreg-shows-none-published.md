In the memorandum opinion granting the plaintiffs' motion, Judge Tanya Chutkan wrote that "Defendants do not contend that a SORN has been published or otherwise applies to its data collection." The plaintiffs alleged that DHS had not published any new or revised system-of-records notice covering the collection or transmission of the voter data, and the opinion says "Defendants have identified none." The government's opposition had called a notice irrelevant: "if a SORN were relevant here, and it is not." The court reasoned that a routine use must be announced in a notice, and that the government's failure to identify a notice covering transmission to states and localities for election administration forecloses reliance on the routine-use exception. This is a likelihood-of-success finding at the preliminary stage, not a final ruling.
