Meta's greenhouse gas emissions rose 45% last year to 11.8 million metric tons, according to its 2026 Sustainability Report, released Thursday, and the company said in the same report that it is "re-evaluating" its goal of net-zero emissions across its operations and supply chain by 2030, Scripps News reported. The report says Meta is also reconsidering its interim milestones and near-term targets. Meta's electricity use rose 35% in 2025, but most of the increase in emissions came from Meta's supply chain, the equipment and construction behind the new data centers. The report says Meta may turn to natural gas plants when utilities cannot deliver power fast enough, and its water consumption in highly water-stressed regions rose 24%.

The rivals' numbers frame it: Scripps reports Microsoft at 25%, Google at 18% and Amazon at 16%, and none has said its climate deadline is under review. Meta set the target in 2020, before the generative AI boom. The report says several of Meta's gas projects are already underway, and their full impact is not reflected in last year's numbers.
