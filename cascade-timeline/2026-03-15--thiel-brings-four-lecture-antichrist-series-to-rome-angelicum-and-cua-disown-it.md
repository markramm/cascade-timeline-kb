Peter Thiel's Antichrist lectures reached Rome on Sunday. The Associated Press described a four-lecture series on the Antichrist, and the Daily Beast reported that Thiel began a run of religious meetings close to the Vatican on Sunday.

The Angelicum, the Dominican university in Rome, said on its website: "We would like to clarify that this event is not organized by the University, will not take place at the Angelicum, and is not part of any of our institutional initiatives." A Catholic University of America spokesperson told AP: "The Catholic University of America is not sponsoring or hosting an event featuring Peter Thiel this month in Rome."

An announcement AP saw named the Vincenzo Gioberti Cultural Association, and the Cluny Institute at the Catholic University of America in Washington, as organizers. Open reported 165 invitati a Palazzo Taverna per il creatore di PayPal e Palantir.
