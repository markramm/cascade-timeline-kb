title: Warren Presses Commerce Nominee Lutnick on Tether Ties; Release Says Cantor Fitzgerald Reportedly Holds 5 Percent Stake
---
On January 28, 2025, Sen. Elizabeth Warren wrote to Howard Lutnick, President Trump's nominee for Commerce Secretary, ahead of his confirmation hearing, about his ties to Tether. Her release says Cantor Fitzgerald, the firm he led, "reportedly holds a 5 percent stake" in Tether. In the letter she wrote that Tether's use "has been the subject of over 150 investigations across four continents, including here with the Department of Justice and the Department of the Treasury." At the hearing Lutnick testified that Cantor has "a convertible bond but not a direct equity stake," according to CoinDesk. Sludge reported on February 4, 2025 that he "declined to say whether he would recuse himself from the president’s Working Group on Digital Asset Markets."

What his own filings bind is narrower than the earlier title. CBS reported from his ethics filing that he "agreed to divest his holdings in a range of business interests," including Cantor Fitzgerald, BGC Group and Newmark Group. His ethics agreement was signed 01/21/2025. The Senate confirmed him on February 18, 2025 (Roll Call Vote 57), and he was sworn in on February 21, 2025 ("Vance then performed the swearing-in."). We found no Tether holding listed in his ethics agreement or his nominee financial disclosure.

The $600 million figure is Cantor's reported convertible-bond price for the reported stake. It is not Lutnick's own holding. See [[2025-02-20--crypto-appointees-193-million]] and [[2025-01-20--digital-asset-working-group]] for the surrounding record.

## Corrections (2026-11-16)

- The title and body previously said Lutnick maintains a $600 million stake in Tether. The reconciliation against his OGE filings found no Tether holding of his; the $600 million is Cantor Fitzgerald's reported convertible-bond price for a reported stake of about 5 percent. The 5 percent is attributed to Warren's release (Cantor, "reportedly"); Lutnick testified that Cantor holds a convertible bond, not direct equity.
- The source labeled ProPublica linked to Warren's press release, which gives no dollar figure; it is now labeled as her release. The source labeled Reuters is CoinDesk, dated 2025-01-29, not 2025-01-22.
- "Despite DOJ investigation" is removed as the entry's own claim. The only support found is Warren's letter, as quoted by CoinDesk, which says Tether's use has been the subject of investigations including with the Department of Justice and the Department of the Treasury; it is now attributed to her.
- The entry was dated 2025-01-20 (inauguration day). It records the Warren letter and nomination-stage reporting, so it is redated to 2025-01-28 and renamed from 2025-01-20--howard-lutnick-600-million-tether-conflict. The old text implied confirmation on January 20; he was confirmed on February 18, 2025 and sworn in on February 21, 2025.
