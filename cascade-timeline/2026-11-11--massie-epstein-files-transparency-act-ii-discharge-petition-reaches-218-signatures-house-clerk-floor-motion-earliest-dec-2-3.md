On Wednesday, the Epstein Files Transparency Act II discharge petition reached 218 signatures, according to ABC News. ABC reported that all 214 Democrats and four Republicans signed. The four Republicans were Thomas Massie, Nancy Mace, Ralph Norman and Brian Fitzpatrick.

Massie wrote on X that "this morning we reached the required 218 signatures." The House Clerk lists a Massie petition filed on August 31st, 2026, on H.Res. 1430. The Clerk's page does not name the Epstein bill. It names the underlying bill as H.R. 185.

The bill would let state attorneys general, victims and members of Congress sue the attorney general for unlawfully withholding or redacting Epstein records, according to ABC. The earlier House vote on the 2025 law was forced by a Massie discharge petition, according to ABC.

Under House Rule XV, clause 2(c), a motion to discharge that has been on the calendar at least seven legislative days becomes privileged at a time the Speaker designates, within two legislative days after a signer announces an intention to offer it. ABC reported that any floor action would come after the Thanksgiving recess, potentially as early as Dec. 2 or 3.
