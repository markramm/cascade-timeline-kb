Republican Senator Thom Tillis of North Carolina issued a scathing public rebuke of White House Deputy Chief of Staff Stephen Miller following Miller's declaration that Greenland should "obviously" be part of the United States.

Tillis told CNN's Jake Tapper on The Lead that Miller "needs to get into a lane where he knows what he's talking about or get out of this job." On the Senate floor he said "the amateurs who said it was a good idea should lose their jobs." It was rare public GOP criticism of a senior administration official.

The rebuke came as Miller's Greenland statements sparked international alarm, with Denmark's Prime Minister warning that a U.S. attack on Greenland—a NATO ally—could end the alliance. Tillis's criticism highlighted how Miller's aggressive rhetoric was damaging U.S. relationships with key allies while exposing divisions within the Republican Party over the administration's increasingly erratic foreign policy.
