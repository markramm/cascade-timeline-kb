Javier Ramirez (2:26-cv-05633), Andrea Velez (2:26-cv-05634), Joel Acuña (5:26-cv-02834), Juan Jose Gutierrez (2:26-cv-05646), Cary Lopez (2:26-cv-05628) and Angel Piña (5:26-cv-02843) each filed a complaint against the United States in the Central District of California; every docket stamps the complaint 05/26/2026. Luis Allen Carrillo is the attorney of record on the original complaints. These are separate actions, not a consolidated case.

What the docket pages show since filing:

- **Related-case notice.** Plaintiffs filed notices of related cases on 07/02/2026; the one in the Ramirez docket lists the other cases.
- **Two dismissed without prejudice by the plaintiffs.** Velez and Gutierrez each filed a notice of voluntary dismissal on 07/31/2026 stating the dismissal is without prejudice, which leaves each free to refile.
- **Acuña moved.** The docket lists entries titled "Transfer Case" and "Transferring Case" in early September. The text of those entries is not on the page, so the destination is not established here.
- **Amended complaints.** Ramirez, Lopez and Piña each have a First Amended Complaint stamped 09/29/2026. Piña's drew a clerk's notice of deficiencies on 09/30/2026 about how the defendants are named in the caption versus the body.
