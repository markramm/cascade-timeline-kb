The V-Dem Institute at the University of Gothenburg released its Democracy Report 2026 on March 17th, according to Verfassungsblog; the institute's own press release carries the same date, and CNN and La Voce di New York covered it the next day. The report's cover title is "Unraveling The Democratic Era?".

Its finding on the United States is that the country "loses its long-term status as a liberal democracy." The scale runs from 0 to 1, and the report puts the United States at 0.79 in 2023 and 0.57 in 2025. Its words: "Within one year, the USA's LDI score has declined by 24%". The same passage has the world rank dropping from 20th to 51st place out of 179 nations. The institute's press release says the United States is in a "much faster deterioration process than any other democracy in modern times." In the report's regime counts, electoral democracies "jumps in 2025 to 19% (1.6 billion) when the USA joins this category."

For the figures: a footnote puts the 2024 change at a 0.04-decline and the 2024 to 2025 drop at 0.18, which is why the one-year figure is not the change between the 2023 and 2025 scores.

On speech and elections the report states that "Electoral components of democracy remain stable." and that Freedom of Expression is at its "lowest level since the end of WWII." It calls the scale and speed of autocratization under the Trump administration "unprecedented in modern times."

On the comparison with other cases, the report sets the U.S. decline against five other autocratizers and writes that the decline "under Donald Trump took Hungary's Viktor Orbán over four years". Its line on Hungary is "Hungary is an electoral autocracy since 2018."
