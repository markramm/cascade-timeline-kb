> **Correction, 2026-08-27 — DUPLICATE, MERGED.** This entry covers the same June 21, 2026 offload announcement as the canonical [[2026-06-21--ice-offloads-seven-of-eleven-warehouses-detention-reengineering]], which now carries the merged, labeled writeup (including this entry's Philadelphia Inquirer source). **"$336M-plus" is a subset-of-sites acquisition figure**, not the program total — it is closer to (though does not exactly match) the combined purchase price of the specific Pennsylvania/New Jersey sites named in the June 19 Inquirer reporting this figure derives from (Tremont $119.5M + Upper Bern $87.4M + Roxbury $129.3M ≈ $336.2M), not all seven sites ultimately offloaded. The corpus-verified total acquisition cost across **all 11** WEXMAC-TITUS warehouses is **$1.074 billion** (see [[wexmac-titus-warehouse-census]]). This entry's distinct analytical content (the "$45B mandate holds" framing, the 152-facilities context, and its related-events links below) is preserved here rather than discarded, but the canonical entry should be cited for the primary writeup and for the two dollar figures' scope.

On **June 21, 2026**, reporting confirmed that DHS under new Secretary **Markwayne Mullin** is offloading **seven warehouses** purchased under predecessor **Kristi Noem** — including properties in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and elsewhere. The Philadelphia Inquirer's June 19 reporting on the Pennsylvania/New Jersey sites specifically cited a combined acquisition cost of **$336M-plus** for those sites — not the full seven-site or program-wide total — many above appraised value. The agency plans to sell or transfer them to other federal agencies while *continuing* warehouse acquisitions in Texas, Arizona, and Maryland. The reversal exposes how the Noem-era detention expansion was built on overpriced, operationally unusable sites.

It is a procurement-waste reversal, **not** a retreat from mass detention overall: the Big Beautiful Bill's $45B detention mandate remains in force, and ICE has already opened 152 new facilities nationwide. A property transfer to another federal agency (e.g. Army Corps or CBP) is not a cancellation. This continues the Mullin-era walk-back of the WEXMAC-TITUS warehouse buildout Mullin paused on taking office (2026-03-24), and lands amid the municipal lawsuits challenging individual conversions (Salt Lake City/County, Romulus MI, Roxbury NJ).
