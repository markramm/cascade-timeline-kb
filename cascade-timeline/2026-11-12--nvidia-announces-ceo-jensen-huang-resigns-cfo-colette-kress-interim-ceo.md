Nvidia announced that founder and CEO Jensen Huang "has resigned as president, chief executive, and a member of the board," ending a 33-year run leading the company. CFO Colette Kress takes over on an interim basis while the board searches for a permanent successor.

The resignation followed the board's statement that it was "starting an independent investigation into his conduct" ([[2026-11-08--nvidia-board-opens-investigation-after-reports-ceo-huang-targeted-by-chinese-intelligence-affair]]).
