The Robert and Ethel Kennedy Human Rights Center and allied groups sued DHS and ICE in D.C. federal court alleging systematic violation of a 2021 ICE directive banning detention of pregnant, postpartum and nursing women. FOIA data obtained by the Women's Refugee Commission shows detained pregnant women surged 1,320%, rising from nine to 128; since 2025 ICE held more than 557 pregnant women for an average of 43 days, with one woman detained for 219 days.
