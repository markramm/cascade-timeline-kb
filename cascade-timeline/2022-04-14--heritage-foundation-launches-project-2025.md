The Heritage Foundation announced the 2025 Presidential Transition Project, later called Project 2025, on April 14, 2022, and said Paul Dans, a former chief of staff at the Office of Personnel Management, would direct it. Heritage published the ninth edition of its Mandate for Leadership a year later, on April 21, 2023. Chapter authors named in Heritage's chapter list include Christopher Miller (Defense) and Gene Hamilton (Justice).

## Correction (2026-11-16)

This entry absorbs a separate April 2023 entry that duplicated the launch; that entry's old id redirects here (`previous_ids`). It listed Stephen Miller as an author of the Mandate. Heritage's chapter list and the archived Mandate PDF do not support that. A text search of the archived PDF finds Christopher Miller, the Defense chapter's author, and no Stephen Miller. The old entry also named Mark Meadows among its actors; neither Heritage's chapter list nor the PDF's contributor pages name him, so he is removed.

What the reporting does tie Stephen Miller to is narrower. Media Matters reports that Miller founded America First Legal, whose general counsel wrote the Justice chapter, that the group was listed on the project's advisory board, and that Miller appeared in a promotional video for the Presidential Administration Academy. Miller told ABC News "I have zero involvement with Project 2025." The record shows the founder of an advisory-board organization and a participant in a recruitment video, not a chapter author.

The old entries also carried figures that none of their listed sources state: a dollar amount for the project, counts of former Trump staffers and of participating organizations, and a "playbook". They are removed. Related: [[2023-02-01--project-2025-presidential-administration-academy-launch]], [[2023-03-01--project-2025-presidential-personnel-database-launch]].
