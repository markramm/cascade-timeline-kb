The Department of Energy announced agreements with 24 organizations on December 18, 2025. The agreements are collaboration agreements for the Genesis Mission.

According to DOE, the signers have signed memorandums of understanding. DOE says each signer either answered a request for information or has active projects with DOE and the National Laboratories.

DOE says any products produced for the Genesis Mission will be architecture-agnostic.

The Register describes the agreements as not contracts to provide services as such. Its account is that each is a memorandum of understanding.

CoreWeave said in its own release that it plans to make its AI cloud platform available to support advanced scientific workloads. The Register reports that OpenAI described its agreement as a path to discuss follow-on agreements as projects take shape.
