Senator Lindsey Graham (R-SC), 71, died on the evening of Saturday, the date this entry carries, after a brief and sudden illness, his office said. CBS reports an emergency call that evening to a residence belonging to the senator, mentioning a dispatch for cardiac arrest, and that Graham had returned from Ukraine, where he had been the day before. His spokesperson said Sunday afternoon that the preliminary finding of the D.C. Medical Examiner was aortic dissection due to arteriosclerotic cardiovascular disease. Graham had recently won the Republican primary in his bid for a fifth term.

On Monday, Governor Henry McMaster appointed Graham's sister, Darline Graham, to finish the term, which runs until January 3, 2027. Trump said he had recommended her to McMaster. CBS's earlier version of its story described the appointment as still pending and gave her surname as Nordone; CBS later updated it after Graham's office clarified that she does not use her husband's last name. As of Ballotpedia's post on the vacancy, the Senate stood at 52 Republicans to 45, with one vacancy, King and Sanders being independents who caucus with Democrats.

Because Graham was already the Republican nominee for the November general election, South Carolina scheduled a special Republican candidate filing period of July 21 through July 28, a special primary on August 11 and a runoff, if needed, on August 25. In the August 11 primary Darline Graham received 33% to Representative Ralph Norman's 24%, sending both to the August 25 runoff, according to Ballotpedia's report ahead of it. The runoff result is not recorded in this entry.
