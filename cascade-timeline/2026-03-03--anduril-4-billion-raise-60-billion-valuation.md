Reports published on March 3 and 4, 2026 said Anduril Industries was raising a multibillion-dollar round that could double its valuation to about $60 billion. The Wall Street Journal was the source cited. On May 13, 2026 the company announced a Series H of $5 billion at a $61 billion valuation. TechCrunch reported on May 13 that "The funding round that was rumored to be in process in March has officially closed." Reading the March reports and the May announcement as one round is TechCrunch's, and the amounts differ because the March figures were reports of a round in progress.

## What the sources say

Tech Funding News, citing the Wall Street Journal, reported that the round was "being co-led by Thrive Capital and Andreessen Horowitz, with existing investors Lux Capital and Founders Fund also expected to participate", and that "the funding round has not yet been finalised, and the final terms could still change". A Yahoo Finance piece of March 9, 2026 said Andreessen Horowitz had "announced talks to raise an additional $4 billion for the company alongside Thrive Capital". The $4 billion figure is that piece's, not a closed amount.

Gunderson Dettmer, which represented Anduril, reported on May 13, 2026 that the company's "$5 billion Series H funding round" was "led by Thrive Capital and Andreessen Horowitz" and that "The round raises Anduril's valuation to $61 billion". Anduril's chief executive Brian Schimpf is quoted there on the purpose: "When we founded Anduril in 2017, defense was not a category that attracted significant venture investment".
