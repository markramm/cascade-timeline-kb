NBC4 (publication date Oct. 9, 2026) reported that three National Guard members stand near what News4 verified is the election ballot drop box outside the GW-Foggy Bottom Metro station. Task & Purpose reports the photos emerged on Thursday. The D.C. Board of Elections asked the Guard to impose limitations on the placement of National Guard service members near drop boxes and other voting locations in the District, and a Joint Task Force-DC statement said JTF-DC service members are directed not to monitor or secure polling locations.
