Just Security's tracker, dated October 30, 2026, lists 60 cases of noncompliance with court orders, 112 of courts' distrust of government information and 145 of arbitrary and capricious action, since Jan. 20, 2025. It adds at least 740 cases from the habeas immigration wave. Those are the tracker's three categories and counts.
