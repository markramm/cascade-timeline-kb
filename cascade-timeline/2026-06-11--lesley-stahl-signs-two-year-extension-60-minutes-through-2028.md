**Resolves the open question, since the June 3, 2026 capture-complete entry, of whether Stahl would depart.** On or before **June 11, 2026**, Lesley Stahl signed a **two-year contract extension with 60 Minutes, running through 2028**. She is 84 (turning 85 during the upcoming season) and has been with the broadcast since 1991. Fellow correspondents **Bill Whitaker and Jon Wertheim** also announced they would remain, leaving Stahl, Whitaker, and Wertheim as the only correspondents left on staff following the Black Thursday mass firing (Vega, Alfonsi) and the subsequent firings/departures of Pelley and Cooper.

Stahl described the period publicly: "It's just been obviously the hardest chapter of my career" (Deadline). She has no announced plans to retire.

Separately, Variety reported Paramount Skydance CEO **David Ellison** telling Stahl directly that 60 Minutes would retain editorial independence — a claim not independently corroborated beyond the Variety headline (full article paywalled; only the headline claim confirmed).

**Why this does not change the broader assessment:** the 2026-06-03 entry already described 60 Minutes' capture as "structurally complete" independent of Stahl's individual decision — that assessment rests on the loss of Pelley, Cooper, Vega, Alfonsi, and the installation of Weiss/Bilton, not on Stahl's presence. Her extension is a factual update (she is, in fact, staying) and does not reopen that question.

**Note on contemporaneous commentary:** Barrett Media's June 15 follow-up piece characterizes the extension as "a band-aid, not a fix" — i.e. contemporaneous commentary treated her staying as compatible with, not a refutation of, the broader capture-complete assessment.
