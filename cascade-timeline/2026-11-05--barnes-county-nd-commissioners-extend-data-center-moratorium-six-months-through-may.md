Barnes County, North Dakota, commissioners voted unanimously on Thursday to approve a six-month moratorium on commercial data centers, KFGO and NewsDakota report. The move pushes back the county's initial freeze, which was scheduled to expire in mid-November, through next May. County Emergency Manager Jessica Jenrich said the extra time lets staff work on the ordinance.

The report ties local scrutiny to residents' concerns about developers using non-disclosure agreements, which opponents said tied up agricultural land before neighbors could weigh in. It names no developer.
