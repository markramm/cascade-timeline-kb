HHS's Administration for Children and Families published a Privacy Act notice on 09/28/2026. The notice modifies System No. 09-80-0321, ORR Unaccompanied Children Bureau Administrative Program Records. The notice is effective October 28, 2026.

The system covers records about unaccompanied children, their intended sponsors, and members of the intended sponsors' households.

ORR "is revising three routine uses in this system of records". The three are the routine use for disclosure to DHS, the one for disclosure for law enforcement, child welfare investigation and state licensing, and the one for disclosure to the Department of State.

For the DHS routine use, the notice says: "ORR has removed the general procedural requirement for an express written advisory while maintaining the substantive prohibition against sharing information for immigration enforcement purposes."

For the law-enforcement routine use, the notice says it was revised "to remove the procedural requirements that ORR determine each disclosure is in the best interest of the child."

The notice states that the revisions keep "continuing to prohibit information sharing for immigration enforcement purposes." HHS describes the purpose of the changes as streamlining procedure.

The notice changes the wording of the human-trafficking purpose for sharing with HSI, the DHS investigations arm that sits inside ICE. The new wording limits that purpose to child safety and trafficking.

Public comments were due October 28, 2026.
