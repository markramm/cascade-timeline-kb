MAGA Inc. moved **$200,000,000** to a second super PAC, No Going Back PAC Inc., in six transfers between Sept 1 and Oct 13, 2026. No Going Back's own reports itemize each one as a committee contribution received from MAGA Inc.

- **Sept 1 and Sept 14:** $50,000,000 each (receipt dates). Both sit on No Going Back's October monthly report (covering Sept 1-30), which FEC received Oct 20.
- **Sept 24 and Sept 28:** $25,000,000 each (receipt dates), same report.
- **Oct 5 and Oct 13:** $25,000,000 each (receipt dates), on the pre-general report (covering Oct 1-14), also received by FEC Oct 20.

The dates above are the receipt dates the committee reported, not the dates the reports were filed; both reports reached FEC on Oct 20, after most of the money had already been spent. Over the same weeks No Going Back's independent expenditures against Abdul El-Sayed in Michigan began on Sept 10 (see the linked entry on its spending). This entry records the transfers only; it does not trace the donors behind MAGA Inc.'s contributions.
