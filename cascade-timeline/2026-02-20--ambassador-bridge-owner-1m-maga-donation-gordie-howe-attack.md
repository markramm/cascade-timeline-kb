The billionaire owner of the Ambassador Bridge between Detroit and Windsor donated $1 million to the MAGA Inc. super PAC shortly before lobbying against the competing publicly-funded Gordie Howe International Bridge. Trump publicly attacked the rival bridge hours after a meeting between the owner and Commerce Secretary. The sequence—donation, meeting, presidential intervention on behalf of donor—illustrates the pay-to-play dynamics operating at the highest levels of government.

The Gordie Howe bridge was built with Canadian and U.S. public funds to reduce reliance on the privately-owned monopoly crossing. The Ambassador Bridge, owned by the Moroun family, has long been one of the most profitable private infrastructure assets in North America, generating enormous toll revenue from its position as the busiest international border crossing on the continent. Any competition from a publicly-owned alternative directly threatens this monopoly profit stream.

The episode demonstrates how large political donations can translate directly into presidential action against competitors, using the power of the federal government to protect private monopoly interests. The timeline of donation, lobbying meeting, and public presidential attack leaves little ambiguity about the transactional nature of the relationship between political contributions and government action.

## Manuel vs. Matthew Moroun — Source Discrepancy Resolved (2026-08-10 research pass)

This entry's actors field and the original NYT source name **Manuel Moroun**; the CBS News source headline names **Matthew Moroun**. This is a father/son succession issue, not a reporting error. **Manuel J. "Matty" Moroun** (1927-2020) acquired control of the Ambassador Bridge in 1979 and built the Moroun family's CenTra/Detroit International Bridge Company empire; he died July 12, 2020. His son **Matthew T. Moroun** became the controlling owner of the bridge and CenTra following his father's death and has run the company for nearly six years by the time of this donation. FEC Schedule A records confirm the $1,000,000 non-contribution receipt to MAGA Inc. was filed January 16, 2026 under "Matthew Moroun," employer Centra Inc./Central Transport LLC, occupation Executive/Chairman — **35 days before** this February 20, 2026 public attack, consistent with the Michigan Advance headline framing. The Commerce Secretary in the NYT/CBS reporting is named as **Howard Lutnick**. See [[detroit-international-bridge-company]] for the full multi-cycle (2016-2026) FEC political-spending history and the family succession structure, and [[2026-07-27--gordie-howe-bridge-opens-toll-revenue-settlement]] for the bridge's subsequent opening and toll-revenue settlement.
