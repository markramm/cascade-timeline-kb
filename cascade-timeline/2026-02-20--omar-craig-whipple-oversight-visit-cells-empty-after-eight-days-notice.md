On Friday, Reps. Ilhan Omar and Angie Craig toured the ICE holding area at the Whipple Federal Building, having given eight days advance notice. Their later letter says that on arrival they were told five detainees remained in the building and were refused when they asked to give them privacy release forms. Omar told reporters that as they were taken towards the cell, "someone said, there is not a single person left, and they took us into an empty cell." Federal officials told them fewer than 500 ICE agents remained in Minnesota, down from the approximately 3,000 officers deployed under Operation Metro Surge.

In their later letter to DHS, the members wrote that officials had "used the time between our notice and our visit to formulate a plan to remove detainees from the premises or obscure their presence."
