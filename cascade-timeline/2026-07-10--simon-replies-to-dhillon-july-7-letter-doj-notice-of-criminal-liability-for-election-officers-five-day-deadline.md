The Minnesota Secretary of State's July 10, 2026 release says Simon received a letter from Dhillon on July 7 and that his office issued a formal reply on July 10. The letter, which the release links, is a notice that election officers face criminal exposure: it says an election officer who knowingly retains noncitizens on the state's voter list "could be subject to criminal liability," and asks for a response within five days. Simon's statement calls it "an unnecessary distraction" from running elections and says "we abide by them all."

This is the state-level view of the July 7 notice the timeline carries as [[2026-07-07--doj-threatens-criminal-prosecution-election-officials-50-states]]. The state's reply letter, linked from the release, was not retrievable for this pass.
