Date: Federal Register publication, October 22, 2026, a Treasury Departmental Offices notice. The comment deadline is December 21, 2026.

**What it asks.** Treasury asks for comment on an "Extension without change of a currently approved collection" for the Fraud.gov Tip Intake and Referral Form, estimating 80,000 responses a year. The description repeats the earlier text: "An artificial intelligence tool will process the initial entry and populate it into standardized fields, along with suggested routing recommendations". No model, vendor, host or retention term is named.

**The clock.** OIRA's terms of clearance for the emergency approval of the form say: "Prior to the expiration of this package, the Department shall undertake standard notice and comment procedures". The reginfo page shows the expiration date as 11/30/2026. The comment deadline in this notice, December 21, 2026, falls after that expiration date.
