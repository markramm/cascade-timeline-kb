A deed transferring the former Big Lots warehouse on Rausch Creek Road, Tremont Township, Pennsylvania, to the U.S. Department of Homeland Security went into effect Jan. 29, 2026, according to WVIA, which read the deed: it says the deed was agreed upon Jan. 15. The deed is between BIGTRPA001 LLC and the Department of Homeland Security, and BIGTRPA001 is a subsidiary of Blue Owl Real Estate Net Lease Property Fund. The deed lists the purchase price at $119,515,000. Local residents feared the 1.3-million-square-foot warehouse would become a processing center for detained immigrants. Pennsylvania's Department of Environmental Protection wrote in March that DHS has announced in documents made public that it plans to use facilities like the warehouse to incarcerate as many as 7,500 people.

The county's parcel record lists a total market value of $59,687,400, and the price DHS paid is roughly double that figure. On 06/12/2020 BIGTRPA001 LLC bought the property for $129,500,000, more than DHS paid. WVIA reported in January that the warehouse closed last year amid Big Lots' bankruptcy.

Blue Owl Capital Inc. is a defendant in a securities class action, docketed as 1:25-cv-10047 in the Southern District of New York; the case alleges Blue Owl and senior executives misled investors about redemption pressure within its business development companies, the firm's liquidity, and the likelihood of curbing redemptions.
