The opinion of Chief Judge Eric C. Tostrud of the District of Minnesota is dated November 2, 2026. The court wrote that the motion to enforce judgment and the amended petition will be denied. The petitioner is identified by initials on the caption.

The court had granted the original habeas petition on September 21, 2026. According to the opinion, that order rested on the finding that Respondents had not shown that Manuel's initial arrest complied with 8 U.S.C. § 1357(a)(2)'s warrantless arrest requirements.

On September 22, 2026, ICE processed his release inside the Whipple Federal Building. The opinion says the release paperwork stated Manuel was "released from ICE custody with no conditions." Manuel states that four ICE officers stopped him before he exited the facility and placed him under arrest again.

Manuel argued that the re-arrest violated the release order. The court wrote that there does appear to be a factual dispute as to whether he had reached an unsecured area in the Whipple Building. The court held the dispute did not change the outcome. It reasoned that Manuel was given release paperwork without conditions, that his belongings were returned, and that he did not assert he was restrained when re-arrested. The court wrote that Manuel provides no legal authority that would make another release the effective remedy even if the re-arrest occurred in a secured area.
