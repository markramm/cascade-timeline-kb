In a complaint filed Friday on behalf of a voter, the ACLU of Georgia, the Legal Defense Fund and the Southern Poverty Law Center said that more than 1,800 voters in Gwinnett County still have not received their absentee ballots. A Gwinnett County voter sued on behalf of himself and 1,819 other voters who applied for absentee ballots on or after Oct. 6. Nearly 10,000 voters across three of Georgia's most populous counties have experienced absentee ballot delays, the civil rights organizations said in the lawsuit.

A similar lawsuit against Cobb County attributed the delays to a New York-based print company called Fort Orange Press, which is also contracted to manage Gwinnett and DeKalb counties' mail ballots. Cobb County has since agreed to overnight ballots to the affected voters. Fort Orange Press is also the vendor for several other counties in Colorado, Pennsylvania and New York that have complained about voters not receiving ballots, Democracy Docket reports. Fort Orange Press has not yet responded to Democracy Docket's request for comment.
