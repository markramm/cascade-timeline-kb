Several thousand people gathered at the **Lincoln Memorial on August 28, 2026** for "March on
Washington 2026: Defend the Vote," held on the 63rd anniversary of the 1963 March on Washington and
at the same site as King's "I Have a Dream" speech. The march was **convened by the National Action
Network and the Drum Major Institute** and co-chaired by **Rev. Al Sharpton, Martin Luther King III,
and Arndrea Waters King**, organizing around voting rights, economic dignity, and affordability.
Attendance included union members, students, and members of Black Greek-letter organizations
traveling from across the country.

Organizers stated the march was called **in direct response to the Supreme Court's April 29 ruling
in *Louisiana v. Callais*** ([[2026-04-29--scotus-callais-guts-section-2-vra-reworks-gingles-standard]]),
the 6-3 decision striking down a Louisiana congressional map drawn to comply with Section 2 of the
Voting Rights Act. That ruling set off the Southern redistricting wave eliminating majority-minority
districts before the midterms ([[2026-06-12--vra-gutted-southern-states-race-to-redraw-maps-eliminating-majority-black-districts]]),
and it landed in the same window as the mail-voting order taking effect
([[2026-08-28--scotus-lifts-talwani-block-mail-voting-order-usps-rule-effective-nc-first-to-comply]]) —
the rally and the rule's effective date fall on the same day.

The entry is recorded for the mobilization record: the anniversary date and the Lincoln Memorial
site were chosen to place the VRA's dismantling against the moment the Act was won, and the
organizing infrastructure named here is the one that will carry any 2026 franchise fight.
