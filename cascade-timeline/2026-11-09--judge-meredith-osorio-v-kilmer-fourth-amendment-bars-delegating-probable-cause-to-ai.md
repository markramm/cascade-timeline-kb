On November 9, 2026, a federal judge in the Eastern District of Kentucky handed down the ruling in Osorio v. Kilmer, per Eyes on ICE. It quotes U.S. District Judge S. Chad Meredith: "The Fourth Amendment to the United States Constitution does not allow a detective to delegate to AI the task of assessing probable cause." The quotation is as printed by Eyes on ICE; the ruling itself was not read for this entry, and the case concerns a detective, not ICE.

Eyes on ICE draws the connection to immigration enforcement: it reports that federal agents had already run Mobile Fortify scans more than 100,000 times on American streets by early 2026. That link is the outlet's framing, not a holding of the court. See the related entries on ICE's facial-recognition apps.
