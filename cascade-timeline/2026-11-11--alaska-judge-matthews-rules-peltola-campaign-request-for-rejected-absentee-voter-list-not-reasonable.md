Superior Court Judge Thomas Matthews ruled Wednesday, Nov. 11, that the Peltola campaign's request for a list of absentee voters whose ballots were rejected is "not reasonable," according to the Associated Press. Attorneys for Peltola's campaign sued state elections officials the previous Friday, seeking the list and the reasons for the rejections.

Matthews wrote that the division is not required to disclose the records because they "cannot reasonably be produced at this juncture, nor redacted of confidential information," according to the AP. He also wrote, "The timing of the Campaign's request could simply not be worse."

Attorneys for the state said the information requested would violate voter privacy, according to the AP. The AP's article does not mention an appeal.
