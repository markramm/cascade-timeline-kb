Heritage Foundation president Jim DeMint and founder Edwin Feulner wrote in July 2014 of Richard Scaife's "steadfast support since 1973," and named him one of Heritage's three original financial backers. InfluenceWatch dates his giving to Heritage from 1973 and puts the total through 1998 at $23 million. Wikipedia's Heritage article has Scaife following Coors's initial seed money "a year later," which is how an earlier version of this entry came to use 1974.

The 1976 figure is a different kind of datum. In 1976, Scaife Foundation support was 42 percent of Heritage's $1 million budget. That measures how large his giving had become, not when it began.

## Correction (2026-11-16)

This entry was dated 1974, with major funding beginning that year. Heritage's own statement dates his support from 1973, and Wikipedia's "a year later" is the only source we read that points to 1974. The date is now 1973 at year precision, since no source gives the day or the size of an initial gift, and the file is renamed. Washington Post quotations in the old text were cut because the Post piece did not load for us. The 1976 figure in the spokes is a budget share, not a start date; see [[1976-01-01--scaife-funding-heritage-foundation-major-expansion]].
