ICE awarded a **$17.3 million contract on August 27, 2026** to **GardaWorld Federal Services LLC**
for renovation of an ICE-owned warehouse at **542 Southeast Loop 410** on San Antonio's East Side,
in Bexar County, Texas. The contract language describes the work as procuring "the renovation of
existing, ICE-owned permanent structure in San Antonio, TX to serve as a processing and detention
facility" — the acquire-then-convert sequence rather than a lease.

The award makes San Antonio one of the first Texas sites with a formally awarded conversion
contract under the **Detention Reengineering Initiative** ([[2026-06-23--ice-detention-reengineering-initiative-100k-warehouse-network]]),
the program targeting a network of ICE-owned warehouse facilities ahead of the fiscal-year
deadline. GardaWorld is the same contractor holding the $313M operating contract at the Surprise,
Arizona warehouse ([[2026-03-06--ice-awards-gardaworld-313m-surprise-arizona-contract]]) — the
recurrence of a single vendor across geographically separate warehouse conversions is a feature of
the program's procurement structure, not a coincidence of bidding.

Bexar County has prior history in this program: a NEPA challenge already paused a warehouse
conversion in the county ([[2026-04-15--hurson-maryland-nepa-precedent-bexar-tx-warehouse-pause]]),
and San Antonio passed an ordinance restricting detention facilities
([[2026-04-17--san-antonio-restricts-detention-facilities-ordinance]]). Federal ownership of the
structure is what routes around both — the pattern documented at Surprise, where a municipal
detention ban was nullified by federal preemption.

Contractor identity and the specific address were confirmed from the KSAT reporting; the ledger
capture that surfaced this event had flagged both as unverified.
