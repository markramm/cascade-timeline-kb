Ken Paxton beat incumbent Sen. John Cornyn in the Texas Republican Senate runoff on May 26, 2026, according to a race call by the Associated Press (NPR), a week after President Trump endorsed Paxton. NPR reported that Republicans spent $100 million on the primary. Paxton faces Democratic nominee James Talarico in November. Trump's stated reason, on Truth Social per CNBC, was that Cornyn "was not supportive of me when times were tough"; he called Paxton a fighter and wrote, "Our Country needs Fighters, and also Loyalty to the Cause of Greatness".

**The cases NPR and CNBC allude to, each at its stage.**

- *Impeachment.* The Texas House impeached Paxton in 2023; the Texas Senate acquitted him of 16 articles of impeachment on a Saturday, reported in a Texas Tribune story published September 16, 2023. That is an acquittal, not a conviction.
- *Securities fraud.* Indicted in 2015, he agreed in a deal reported in March 2024 to pay nearly $300,000 in restitution under a pretrial agreement in which special prosecutors would drop three felony counts. FOX 26 reported on June 18, 2025 that the case was dropped after he completed the program, per his attorney, who posted a signed motion; the court's own record was not read. No conviction.
- *Whistleblower case.* A civil case, not a criminal charge, brought by four former aides. On April 4, 2025 a Travis County judge awarded $6.6 million to four former senior aides who said they were improperly fired after reporting Paxton to the FBI; the judgment recorded that Paxton's office did not dispute any issue of fact. This was a judgment, not a settlement. The Texas Tribune reported that Paxton dropped his appeal on a Wednesday in July 2025 and that the Legislature must still appropriate the funds; whether it has is not in the sources read.
