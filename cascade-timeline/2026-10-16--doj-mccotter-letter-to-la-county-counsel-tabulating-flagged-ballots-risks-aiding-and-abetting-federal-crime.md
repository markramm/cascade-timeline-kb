The letter is dated October 16, 2026 and is signed by Trent McCotter, Acting Deputy Attorney General, to Peter M. Bollinger of the Los Angeles County Counsel's Office. It answers the county's October 15 letter. It says: "If persons knowingly tabulate ballots cast by individuals identified by federal agencies as noncitizens, they risk becoming aiders and abettors to a federal crime."

The letter says the Department hand delivered a dataset on October 13 identifying more than 20,000 individuals. It says the investigative list includes individuals residing outside Los Angeles County, and that the Department requested only temporary ballot segregation pending citizenship verification.
