Georgia's Public Service Commission goes to a 3-2 Democratic majority after the November election. The new members take their oaths on Jan. 1, 2027 and inherit a data-center program that Georgia Power's own May 2026 filing puts at 12,400 MW committed across 31 large-load customers, with a pipeline of 76,200 MW and every project name redacted.

## The "Affordability Docket" is a proposal, not a docket

The lead's phrase traces to Commissioner Peter Hubbard, who proposed it and has described it as a series of hearings on high bills and ways to save customers money (Fortune, Politico/Yahoo and Canary Media all attribute it to him). One local report says only that commissioners plan to open it once the new majority takes office, and that it would weigh what households can afford and how data-center infrastructure costs are allocated. No docket number appears in anything reviewed, and it cannot open before the new members are seated.

## Dockets the new majority inherits that cite data-center load

- **Docket 56002, Georgia Power's 2025 IRP**, approved by stipulation in December 2025 for just under 10GW of new generation (GPB). Georgia Power files its Large Load Economic Development Reports in this docket and in Docket 55378, the 2023 IRP Update. The Q1 2026 report is the primary disclosure; it also notes 3,200 MW of commitments added in April that it does not count.
- **The April 23, 2026 request** to procure 2,000 to 6,000 megawatts of new dispatchable capacity through a 2032-2033 All-Source RFP, with selected projects returning for certification in mid-2027. Canary Media quotes Hubbard calling it an opportunity to engage.
- **The Real Time Pricing fuel-credit investigation**, which grew out of this spring's fuel-cost case. Staff found large power users could raise fuel costs for others by up to 11% per month by 2028, and findings are due Dec. 31 (GovTech).

Already decided, so not pending: the December 2025 buildout, and the August OpenAI data-center contract, approved by the three Republican commissioners over Hubbard and Alicia Johnson.

## Who the customers are

The filings do not name them. The one customer named in the coverage reviewed is OpenAI, whose contract Fortune reports was entirely redacted.

## Open

PSC docket numbers for the April RFP request and the RTP investigation (neither confirmed); whether Georgia Power's next quarterly report names any customer.
