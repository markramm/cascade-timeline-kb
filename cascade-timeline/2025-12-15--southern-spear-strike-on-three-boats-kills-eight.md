On December 15, 2025 the US military struck three alleged drug-smuggling boats in the eastern Pacific Ocean, killing eight people, Southern Command announced (PBS NewsHour timeline). The strikes are part of the campaign the administration runs under the name Operation Southern Spear against vessels it says carry drugs.

## Counts of the dead, by whose count and as of when

Each figure below is a named party's, and the two counts differ in what they count and when.

- Human Rights Watch, in a report published before the December 15 strikes, counted at least 23 lethal military strikes on vessels in the Caribbean Sea and nearby parts of the Pacific Ocean since mid-September 2025, and 87 people killed, and said there were only two known survivors. HRW calls the strikes unlawful extrajudicial executions. That characterization is HRW's; it is not a court's finding.
- NBC News reported days after the strikes that the administration's campaign had resulted in 28 boat strikes that had killed more than 100 people.
- PBS NewsHour reports that before the January 2026 raid on Venezuela there had been 35 known strikes since early September that killed at least 115 people, according to announcements from the administration. This is a tally of the administration's own announcements, compiled after the year's end.

The administration describes the people killed as "narco-terrorists", in HRW's rendering of its claim. The sources read do not name the dead.
