Donald Trump started his Middle East tour, arriving in Riyadh, and then travelled to Qatar and the United Arab Emirates. Rest of World reported that the UAE pledged $1.4 trillion over the next decade, Saudi Arabia committed $600 billion over four years, and Qatar added $200 billion. These are announced pledges, not money received; Middle East Eye noted that, like other pledges announced by Trump, "it's unclear if it will materialise."

On chips, Rest of World reported that Nvidia partnered with Humain, shipping 18,000 Blackwell chips, that AMD secured a $10 billion collaboration with Humain, and that Trump reversed his predecessor’s so-called AI diffusion rule.
