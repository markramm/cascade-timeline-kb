In an October 28 letter, reviewed by LOOKOUT and The Intercept, Marana Finance Director and License Inspector Yiannis Kalaitzidis asked MTC Senior Vice President Mary Calvin for a written explanation of what the town official called an "apparent inconsistency" between state records and the facility's 2023 Prison Rape Elimination Act, or PREA, audit: state records reference three allegations of sexual misconduct at the prison in 2022 and 2023, but MTC's report for the Marana prison recorded none. MTC ran a former state prison in Marana, Arizona, and plans to reopen it as an ICE detention camp.

The request is part of the town's review of MTC's October 1 application for a business license to "Operate Marana Detention Facility." The Intercept reports a concealed allegation would be grounds for denial of a business license according to the town's code. The assistant town manager said: "The application remains under review, and no final determination has been made at this time." MTC did not respond to multiple requests for comment.
