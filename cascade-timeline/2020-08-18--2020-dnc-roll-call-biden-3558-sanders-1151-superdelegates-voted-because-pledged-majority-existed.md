ABC News reported of the virtual roll call: "All pledged and superdelegates were able to vote on the first ballot since Biden secured a majority of pledged delegates in the primary." Biden finished with 3,558 delegates against Sanders' 1,151, and the "magic number" was 2,374.

## What the rule did

The first-ballot restriction adopted in 2018 releases when a candidate holds a pledged-delegate majority. In 2020 that condition was met, so the restriction barred no one.
