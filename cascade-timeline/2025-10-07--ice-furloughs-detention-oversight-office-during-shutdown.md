During the fall 2025 government shutdown, ICE furloughed its Office of Detention Oversight (ODO), the office that inspects ICE detention facilities. Detention Watch Network objected on October 7, 2025, despite all other ICE operations and detention continuing, and put the number held at nearly 60,000; the figure is the advocacy group's, not ICE's.

A letter from Sen. Mark Warner and House colleagues to the DHS Secretary says DHS has furloughed the entire ODO, and calls the decision a clear attempt to sabotage oversight into the conditions of ICE facilities. That reading is theirs. The sources we read do not say how long the furlough lasted.

This was a furlough, not a closure, and ODO is a different office from the Office of the Immigration Detention Ombudsman.
