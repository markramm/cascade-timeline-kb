## Opening

On the morning of **August 20, 2026** — one week after the Minnesota criminal discovery unsealed
Operation Puppet Master — the church plaintiffs in *New England Synod, ELCA v. DHS* filed a
**Motion for Order to Show Cause, Motion to Expedite, and Notice of Defendants' Non-Compliance**
(ECF No. 117, D. Mass. 4:25-cv-40102).

Attached as **Exhibit 1**: an HSI Report of Investigation from **Project Whipple Shield**, case
number `MS02PR26MS0004`, ROI `MS02PR26MS0004-193`, approved **June 4, 2026** — reported by
**SA Katherine Wespetal**, approved by **SSA Hakan Catalan**, the same pair who appear across the
Minnesota exhibit set.

**This is a second, independent litigation surface for the same surveillance program.** Until now
every public Whipple Shield document came from the Minnesota criminal docket. This one is a civil
RFRA case in Massachusetts, filed by the churches themselves.

## What the ROI documents

**May 28, 2026 — a "Left Jab" training in the downstairs gymnasium of University Baptist Church.**

- UCA-5022 arrived at approximately 7:05 p.m. and "was greeted by several people."
- The report is explicit about its own limits: "The following is a summary, may not be in
  chronological order, and is not verbatim. **Reference audio and video recordings** for
  additional details and context."
- The agent recorded attendees introducing themselves, and identified a Signal group.
- **"All recordings will be maintained as a part of this case file. The investigation continues."**

**The wire fell on the floor.** In the report's own words, as an "Investigative note": "Due to the
physicality of the event, UCA-5022's **concealed body-worn audio recorder** erroneously fell to
the floor of the gymnasium near the end of the training." The agent left, realized the device was
gone, and returned to retrieve it. The ROI records that she was absent for approximately three
minutes and is "last heard on the body-worn recorder's timestamp at approximately 1:59:58."

An undercover federal agent wearing a concealed recorder dropped it on the floor of a church
gymnasium, and wrote up the gap in coverage as an investigative note.

## Why It Matters

**The churches are now alleging DHS violated the injunction.** Judge Saylor granted a preliminary
injunction on 2026-02-13 barring warrantless immigration enforcement inside plaintiff churches,
finding plaintiffs likely to succeed on their RFRA claim. This motion is styled as a notice of
**non-compliance** — and the exhibit is the government's own report placing an undercover agent
inside a plaintiff church on May 28, more than three months after the injunction issued.

Note the plaintiff list has grown well beyond the original five ELCA synods: it now includes
Delaware-Maryland, Metro D.C., Metropolitan Chicago, Metropolitan New York, **Minneapolis Area**,
Northwest Washington, Southeastern, Sierra Pacific, Southwest California, Southwestern Texas and
Upstate New York Synods, plus Alliance of Baptists, American Baptist Churches USA, D.C. Baptist
Convention, Metropolitan Community Churches, Southern New England Conference UCC, and three Quaker
bodies. Responses are due **September 3, 2026**.

**It confirms the recordings exist.** The Ann Arbor ROI said the agent attended "equipped with
recording devices"; this one says plainly that audio and video recordings are maintained in the
case file and directs readers to them. That is a discoverable, subpoenable body of material.

**It sharpens the evidentiary-weight point.** Like the Ann Arbor report, this ROI states on its
face that it "is a summary... and is not verbatim." Unlike Ann Arbor, it says recordings exist to
check it against. See [[2026-04-08--hsi-undercover-operation-zion-lutheran-church-ann-arbor]].
