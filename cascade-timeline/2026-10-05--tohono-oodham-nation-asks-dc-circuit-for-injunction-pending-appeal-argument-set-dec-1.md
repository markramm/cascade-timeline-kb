The Tohono O'odham Nation appealed Judge Leon's denial of its preliminary injunction to the D.C. Circuit. The docket carries the appeal as No. 26-5306.

According to the notice of appeal, the Nation appeals "from the Order of this Court entered in the above-captioned matter on August 14, 2026 (Dkt. 46)".

The Nation filed an emergency motion for an injunction pending appeal in the D.C. Circuit on Oct. 5, 2026.

According to the motion, "the Secretary intends to begin construction as early as next Monday, October 12". The Nation asked for relief by that date.

The motion argues that "the Secretary's contractors cannot restrict their work to within sixty feet of the border and hence will be in trespass regardless of whether the Roosevelt Reservation attached to the Nation's lands". That is the Nation's argument, not a court finding.

Judge Leon's separate order on the same relief says "plaintiff's motion for an injunction pending appeal is DENIED".

The D.C. Circuit then scheduled oral argument. Its order says "this case be scheduled for oral argument on December 1, 2026, at 9:30 A.M."
