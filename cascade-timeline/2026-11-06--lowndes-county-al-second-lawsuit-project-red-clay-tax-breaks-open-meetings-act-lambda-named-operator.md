Alabama Reporter reports that Protect Lowndes County and the Alabama Rivers Alliance are the second group of plaintiffs to sue the Lowndes County Commission over the tax breaks. They allege the commission violated Alabama's Open Meetings Act and Open Records Act in approving more than $2.7 billion in tax abatements for Project Red Clay; the Southern Environmental Law Center and the Natural Resources Defense Council represent the plaintiffs, who seek to invalidate the tax breaks. Commissioners voted 3-2 to approve them. The suit alleges the commission led residents to believe the meeting would take place October 20, then approved the abatements a week earlier.

Lambda Inc. announced it will operate the proposed center. The campus is proposed at 1,500 megawatts, and the project has requested 100,000 gallons of water per day.
