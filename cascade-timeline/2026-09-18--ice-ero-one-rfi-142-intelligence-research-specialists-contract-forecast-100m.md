Intelligence Community News reported that DHS posted a request for information on September 18 for ERO Overwatch and National Security Enforcement (ERO ONE) support services, a market-research notice for approximately 142 contract Intelligence Research Specialists and two project managers supporting ERO headquarters and field locations. The contemplated services include "network and relationship analysis." The RFI is market research, not a solicitation or award.

Eyes on ICE reports that the requirement is a contract valued at more than $100 million with solicitation scheduled for December 10, 2026. The DHS forecast record and the SAM.gov notice were not read.
