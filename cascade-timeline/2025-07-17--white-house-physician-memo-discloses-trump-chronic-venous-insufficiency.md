On July 17, 2025, the White House released a memo from the President's physician, Navy Capt. Sean P. Barbabella, addressed to Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt, saying bilateral venous Doppler ultrasounds had found chronic venous insufficiency (CVI), which the memo calls "a benign and common condition, particularly in individuals over the age of 70." The memo reports no deep vein thrombosis or arterial disease, normal laboratory results and echocardiogram, attributes recent bruising on the back of the President's hand to handshaking and aspirin, and concludes: "President Trump remains in excellent health." Leavitt read it at that day's briefing. This is the date the diagnosis entered the record, well before the later Walter Reed physical whose results were released at the end of May 2026.
