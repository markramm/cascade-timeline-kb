Federal prosecutors announced on Friday, October 23, 2026 separate charges against nine Pennsylvania residents for voting while not citizens, with the charges spanning both federal districts in the state. Votebeat reports two of the nine were both charged with two counts of voting by an alien, in 2022 and 2024; the others were all charged with one count for 2024, except one charged with one count for 2022. The maximum possible sentence is one year and a fine for all other defendants, while one faces up to two years. Votebeat reports the department lists those whose status it gave as lawful permanent residents.

The record read bears one statute: the Middle District information for the 2022 voter cites 18 U.S.C. § 611, and so does an affidavit of probable cause sworn by a Special Agent with Homeland Security Investigations in a second Middle District case. That affidavit rests on records from the Office of Principal Legal Advisor to the Department of Homeland Security and from the Snyder County Board of Elections. Its jurat reads the 15th day of October, 2026, and its text refers to October 23, 2026; the entry does not resolve that mismatch. Homeland Security Investigations is also among the sources the Philadelphia station's report names for the announcement. Votebeat finds there is no evidence the cases are related, and DHS has not yet shared the list of potential noncitizen voters, so no filing read connects these charges to that list.
