Syria's Ministry of Energy, the Syrian counterparty, signed memoranda of understanding with each of UCC Holding Group, Urbacon Trading & Contracting Company, Power International, Cengiz Energy, and Kalyon Energy. Energy Minister Mohammad al-Bashir gave the investment value as $7 billion, for gas and solar power plants.

UCC's release says construction is expected to begin following final agreements and financial close, under the corresponding power purchase agreements.
