The United States filed a Statement of Interest in X.AI LLC v. Ellison, before Judge Donovan M. Frank in the District of Minnesota. The plaintiff, xAI, challenges a Minnesota law, House File 1606, on computer-generated sexual imagery. The defendant is Minnesota Attorney General Keith Ellison.

The brief is signed by Associate Attorney General Stanley E. Woodward Jr. It states that the United States submits this Statement of Interest pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 517.

The statement argues that House File 1606 sweeps more broadly than federal law, implicating the First Amendment.

The brief quotes an executive order stating that it is the policy of the United States to sustain and enhance the United States' global AI dominance through a minimally burdensome national policy framework for AI.
