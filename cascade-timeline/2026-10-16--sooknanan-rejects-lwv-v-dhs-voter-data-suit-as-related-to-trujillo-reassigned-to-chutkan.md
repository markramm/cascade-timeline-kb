The day after the League of Women Voters, EPIC and LULAC filed their voter-data suit against DHS (D.D.C. 1:26-cv-03602), the plaintiffs had marked it related to Trujillo v. DHS (1:26-cv-03072), which sat with Judge Sparkle Sooknanan. On the order's date, Oct. 16, 2026, Sooknanan ruled by minute order that the cases are not related under Local Civil Rule 40.5 and directed random reassignment. The docket shows random reassignment to Judge Tanya Chutkan the same day.

The court's stated reason is that the new case challenges a DHS policy of collecting, maintaining and using voter records, while Trujillo challenges federal action directed at California. The docket carries no preliminary-injunction motion in the new case as of the Oct. 19, 2026 RECAP read (entries 1 to 10 plus unnumbered orders; the only Oct. 19 entry is a pro hac vice order), and no government filing. RECAP's last-modified date for the Trujillo docket is Oct. 19; this docket's copy may lag the court's own.
