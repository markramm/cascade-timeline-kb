ICE says Homeland Security Investigations Tucson took a Canadian lawful permanent resident into custody on Oct. 28 for illegally voting as an alien. According to the [ICE release](https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/maple-leaf-meet-ballot-thief-ice-hsi-tucson-arrests-canadian-voter-fraud), the person had registered to vote in the state of Wisconsin, falsely claiming U.S. citizenship, and voted in the Nov. 8, 2022, and Nov. 5, 2024, general elections. A federal misdemeanor arrest warrant was issued on Oct. 23, 2026 out of the Western District of Wisconsin.
