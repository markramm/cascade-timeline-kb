ProPublica reported that members of Georgia's State Election Board and much of its professional staff were coordinating with an activist group led by Cleta Mitchell. Vice chair Janelle King and her predecessor Janice Johnston have both attended meetings of EIN or VoterGA. Board chair John Fervier said the Election Integrity Network has had an "undue influence" on the board; King acknowledged speaking with EIN and VoterGA activists but said they did not influence her decisions. VoterGA's Garland Favorito called the idea a "patently false conspiracy theory". Fervier has called a special meeting, in part to discuss personnel matters.

ProPublica reports that the group has been able to enlist the board's help in getting counties to remove voters from their rolls based on ELLY information, a tool voting rights groups say often erroneously flags legitimate voters; the board's investigative staff pressured Chatham County to purge more than 200 registered voters. Governor-elect Keisha Lance Bottoms' platform pledged to eliminate the current board and replace it with a more independent structure.
