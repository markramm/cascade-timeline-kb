Aaron Reitz, U.S. attorney for the Southern District of Texas, posted a preservation letter to Sen. Elizabeth Warren, D-Mass., on X on Sunday, Nov. 15. His post said Warren "is on the warpath to wield her committee gavel like a tomahawk, seeking the scalps of those who associate 'too closely' with President Trump." The letter, as Fox News quotes it, says "You appear eager to wield your future gavel like a tomahawk, hunting for the scalps of President Trump’s private associates," and gives formal notice of an obligation to preserve documents relevant to Warren's alleged intent to "weaponize congressional investigations and deprive citizens of their core constitutional rights in violation of 18 U.S.C. §§ 241 and 242." It sets a 6 p.m. ET deadline on Nov. 23 for Warren to confirm in writing that she received the notice and preserved the records. Fox News reports the letter does not establish that Warren violated federal law.

NBC reports the letter references an appearance in which Warren said tech CEOs, Wall Street executives and giant corporations who have broken the law "better get ready for some investigations — serious ones." Fox says the letter cited Warren's Nov. 8 "Face the Nation" appearance. The letter also references a Nov. 11 letter from Warren, Sen. Richard Blumenthal and Rep. Robert Garcia asking Meredith O’Rourke, a National Park Foundation board member and top fundraiser for Trump, to retain documents on funding for the White House ballroom and other projects, according to NBC. NBC says the letter also contains numerous references to Native Americans, such as "scalps," "war cries" and "tomahawk," which it calls a dig at Warren over her past claims of Native American descent. NBC reports Trump reposted the letter on Truth Social the same day.

Fox quotes Reitz telling Warren that Sens. Blumenthal and Slotkin were "each already recipients of preservation demands from my office" ([[2026-11-11--reitz-letter-tells-blumenthal-to-preserve-documents-citing-18-usc-241-242-over-ballroom-donor-inquiry]], [[2026-11-12--reitz-letter-tells-slotkin-to-preserve-documents-over-go-hard-on-accountability-comments]]). The letter itself is an image attached to Reitz's post and was not read here; its wording above is as Fox News, NBC and The Hill quote it. Warren had not responded to NBC's request for comment when it published.
