MPR News reported on April 30, 2026 that Police Chief Brian O'Hara signed an agreement with the FBI seeking reimbursement for overtime expenses for 63 Minneapolis police officers who participated in a federal task force. The agreement authorized payment of $30,000 to the city and was finalized days after Alex Pretti was shot and killed by federal agents. O'Hara said the timing "occurred through the normal course of business."
