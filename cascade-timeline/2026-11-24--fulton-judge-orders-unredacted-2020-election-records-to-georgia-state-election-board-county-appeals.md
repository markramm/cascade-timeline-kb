A Fulton County judge ordered the county's Board of Registration and Elections to turn over a complete, unredacted copy of its 2020 election records to Georgia's State Election Board, the Georgia Recorder reported. In her Tuesday order, Judge Kelly Lee Ellerbe ordered the documents delivered to the board on a flash drive by 5 p.m. Wednesday.

The records included all ballot images from the 2020 election in Fulton County and the county's 2020 voter rolls. The Georgia Recorder reported that FBI agents seized them during a raid on the Fulton County elections warehouse. Attorneys for Fulton County have filed an appeal in the case, meaning a hearing will likely be held Wednesday.

According to the Georgia Recorder, no criminal charges have come from the FBI's investigation of Fulton's 2020 election records. The Georgia Recorder also reported that previous investigations into the 2020 election found no merit to claims of widespread voting fraud or intentional misconduct by Fulton election workers and leaders.
