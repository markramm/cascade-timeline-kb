WRDW/WAGT reports that Georgia governor-elect Keisha Lance Bottoms has said she will sign an executive order imposing a temporary moratorium on new data center approvals, a promise she made on the campaign trail. She also wants to comb through state and local tax incentive deals. The outlet notes her promised moratorium can't unwind projects already in the pipeline; existing projects like Google and QTS will move ahead under the deals already in place. The order had not been signed at the time of reporting.

The pledge follows the election in which the Public Service Commission flipped: [[2026-11-03--georgia-psc-flips-democratic-3-2-inherits-data-center-large-load-dockets]].
