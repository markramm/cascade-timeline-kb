On July 23, 2026, Reps. Jay Obernolte (R-CA) and Lori Trahan (D-MA) introduced the FRONTIER Act, a bipartisan House bill on frontier AI. The sponsors' release says the bill "establishes tiered requirements based on the size of a frontier AI developer, including model cards, risk-management frameworks, independent audits, incident reporting, and ongoing assessments."

This is a statutory route to oversight of frontier developers through Congress. The executive actions on frontier AI in the same period (the pre-release access framework and the Commerce export-control directive to Anthropic) were taken without legislation. The sources here do not say whether the bill responds to either.
