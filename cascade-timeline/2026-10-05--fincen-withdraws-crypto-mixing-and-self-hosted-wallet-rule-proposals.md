FinCEN posted notices to the Federal Register's public inspection site on Monday withdrawing crypto rule proposals, The Block reports. One withdraws its October 2023 finding that international crypto mixing is a primary money laundering concern, citing concerns the rule could have "a chilling effect on legitimate activity". The other ends a December 2020 proposal that would have had banks and money services businesses keep records on customers' transactions with self-hosted wallets above $3,000 and report those topping $10,000, per Decrypt.

Because neither proposal was finalized, the withdrawals do not change financial institutions' existing obligations. The Federal Register text was not retrieved.
