On September 22, 2025, the Supreme Court granted the government's application (No. 25A264) to stay the July 17, 2025 order of the district court in Slaughter's suit over her removal from the Federal Trade Commission. The Court also treated the application as a petition for a writ of certiorari before judgment and granted it (No. 25-332). Justice Kagan dissented, joined by Justices Sotomayor and Jackson. The order is an interim-docket stay, not a decision on the merits: it overturned no precedent. It directed the parties to brief and argue whether the removal protections for FTC members violate the separation of powers and, if so, whether *Humphrey's Executor v. United States* (1935) should be overruled.

The path to that order ran through the Chief Justice. The application was filed September 4, 2025, and on September 8 the Chief Justice entered an administrative stay of the same district-court order pending the Court's action.

The merits decision came on June 29, 2026 ([[2026-06-29--scotus-overturns-humphreys-executor-presidential-firing-power]]). The same day's order is also covered as a grant of review at [[2025-09-22--scotus-agrees-reconsider-humphreys-executor-agency-firings]].

## Correction (2026-11-16)

This entry merges an earlier entry that dated the stay to the opening days of September 2025 (its id is in `previous_ids`). Both entries covered the same order. Its vote count and its overturning of precedent are not in the order, which is a stay and a grant of review with Justices Kagan, Sotomayor and Jackson dissenting. *Humphrey's Executor* was left for the merits and overruled in the 2026 decision linked above. The order stayed the district court's order; no appellate ruling was overturned. Headline wording was removed from the title.
