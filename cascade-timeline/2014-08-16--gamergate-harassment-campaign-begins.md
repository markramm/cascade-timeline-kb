Gamergate began on August 16th, 2014, when an online post by a game developer's ex-boyfriend set off a personal attack on her. Mother Jones described it in October 2014 as a revenge post about a breakup. It reported that the attacks on the developer, which included the circulation of nude photos and death and rape threats, quickly flooded Reddit and 4chan. Threads about Gamergate began on 4chan and were banned there for breaking its policy on distributing personal information, and the conversation largely moved to 8chan.

The Guardian wrote in 2016 that the campaign, ostensibly founded to protest ethical failures in games journalism, clearly thrived on hate, and that Gamergate effectively began because a man wanted to punish his ex girlfriend. Mother Jones reported that Anita Sarkeesian said she had been “subjected to the worst harassment I’ve ever faced”. Mother Jones reported that Brianna Wu received a series of graphic death threats and said she had to flee her home as a result.

The Guardian reported that Gamergate's backers used 4chan and then 8chan to plan their attacks. It also wrote that Steve Bannon, as a founder member and former executive chair of Breitbart News, had a hand in creating Milo Yiannopoulos, who built his fame by supporting Gamergate, and judged the similarities between Gamergate and the alt-right to be in no way a coincidence. BuzzFeed News reported that Yiannopoulos, then Breitbart's tech editor, convinced Breitbart's management in summer 2015 to give him his own section after spending a year gathering momentum through GamerGate.

The FBI investigated the threats. Digital Trends reported in 2017, on the release of the FBI's file, that of the people the FBI tracked down who admitted responsibility for the death threats, at least two were let off with little more than a warning. Wikipedia reports that no arrests have been made nor charges filed, with parts of the investigation closed in September 2015 for lack of leads. Wu said of the file: "All this report does for me is show how little the FBI cared about the investigation," on the report's release.
