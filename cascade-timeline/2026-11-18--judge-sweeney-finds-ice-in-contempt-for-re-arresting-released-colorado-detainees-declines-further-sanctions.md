U.S. District Judge Charlotte Sweeney said on Wednesday that ICE "made a mockery of court orders" in Colorado, and the court found government officials in contempt for instantly rearresting a man the judge had ordered released, NBC News reported. The judge wrote that ICE's new "policy" of immediately re-detaining petitioners after release "made a mockery of court orders mandating petitioners' immediate release." She took no further action because, she said, the officials had "cured the contempt by ceasing the practice at issue," as an official swore in court.

CPR News reported that at least 18 people in Colorado had been held in detention longer than they should have been or had been rearrested within minutes of their judge-ordered release in the past six weeks. The finding follows the earlier contempt inquiry and field-office-director hearing in the same court (see related entries); the Minnesota and Nevada entries document earlier instances of the same practice elsewhere.
