New York Governor Kathy Hochul confronted ICE and contractor Akima Infrastructure Protection LLC after state regulators challenged an unpermitted soft-sided tent structure already built at the Buffalo Federal Detention Facility in Batavia, NY, designed to house 100 women. The NY Department of Environmental Conservation and Department of Health sent formal letters to ICE and Akima stating they received no prior information or outreach about the project. Hochul demanded permits and answers on health conditions, saying "There's no reason for ICE to expand any facility in New York State, full stop." Federal records show Akima holds an indefinite-delivery contract for Batavia detention with a ceiling of roughly $545.6 million through 2029; on September 29, 2026, ICE obligated $7.18 million for the soft-sided tent on a sole-source basis. The tent was constructed without state environmental or public health review.
