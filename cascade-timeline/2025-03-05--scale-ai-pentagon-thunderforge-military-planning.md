The Defense Innovation Unit announced on March 5, 2025 that it had awarded Scale AI a prototype contract for Thunderforge, an initiative to bring AI into military operational and theater-level planning. DIU's release names campaign development, theater-wide resource allocation and strategic assessment as the planning activities at Indo-Pacific Command and European Command. The release gives no contract value.

DIU names the partners in one sentence: Thunderforge "will also include Anduril's Lattice software platform and state of the art LLMs enabled by Microsoft." Scale's own announcement, published the same day, says Anduril "is integrating Scale AI's LLM capability into its Lattice advanced modeling and simulation infrastructure to enhance mission planning," and that Microsoft "is providing state of the art LLM technology to enable a leading edge, multimodal solution." Scale describes the workflows as "always under human oversight."

Palantir does not appear in either document.

Earlier text of this entry tied Scale's data work to Palantir's analytics and Anduril's weapons systems, and placed Scale in a Thiel investment network. Those lines are withdrawn.
