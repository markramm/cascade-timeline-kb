The Supreme Court docket for the Rollins stay application records that the stay application, on which Justice Jackson had earlier entered an administrative stay, was referred to the full Court. The order: "The administrative stay entered on November 7, 2025, is hereby extended until 11:59 p.m. (EST) on November 13, 2025." The docket adds: "Justice Jackson would deny the request for extension of the administrative stay and would deny the application." The docket order names no other Justices and records no vote count.
