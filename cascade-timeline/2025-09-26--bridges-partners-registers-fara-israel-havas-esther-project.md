**Filing date (registration): 2025-09-26.** Bridges Partners LLC (Delaware, organized 2025-06-03; owners Uri Steinberg and Yair Levi, 50% each) registered for "Government of Israel through Havas Media Group Germany." Exhibit A names the Ministry of Foreign Affairs as the agency represented, "conducted through Havas." Agreement date on Exhibit B: 2025-06-16.

**What the filed Havas order shows (Exhibit B attachment, dated 2025-06-16).** Order 93702/0, "(INT) Influencer Campaign (USD)", product "Ministry of Foreign Affairs", campaign "Esther Project," six monthly lines June to November 2025: $60,000 + $140,000 + $200,000 + $250,000 + $200,000 + $50,000 = $900,000 (sum computed from the filed lines). Notes on the lines describe influencer payments, "Post volume: approx. 25-30" a month, and influencers activated in waves of 5-6, 3-4, 3-4, and 3-4. The $900,000 is a booked order, not a payment record.

**Money as filed.** Receipt (payment date 2025-07-03): $199,985, "advance for campaign expenses," from the foreign principal. Pre-registration disbursements $97,054 (to 2025-09-16); a 2026-05-01 amendment adds $6,638 to Pinkston Group Inc (2025-09-22). Supplemental for 2025-10-01 to 2026-03-31 (filed 2026-04-30): no receipts; disbursements $197,960 across 24 rows (largest payee D3 Entertainment, $75,000 "segmented audience marketing"; Rolop LLC and an individual project manager, "project management and media outreach"; Candor Marketing, Pinkston Group, Matt Brown Group, Wholehearted Love LLC). The filing shows no payments to named creators.

**What the registrant says it did.** The supplemental states Bridges "had no role in developing, creating, promoting, or disseminating any content," and checks no informational materials. Verdict: documented that a $900,000 influencer order exists in the filing; not established who the influencers were or who paid them, since the filings show Havas, not Bridges, as the booking party. No short-form registrant is a creator.
