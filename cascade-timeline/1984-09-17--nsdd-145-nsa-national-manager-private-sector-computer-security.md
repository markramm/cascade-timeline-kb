- A directive dated September 17, 1984 designates the NSA Director as National Manager for Telecommunications and Automated Information Systems Security, responsible to the Secretary of Defense as Executive Agent.
- The National Manager is to "act as the government focal point for cryptography, telecommunications systems security, and automated information systems security."
- The directive says the government "shall encourage, advise, and, where appropriate, assist the private sector" to identify systems that handle "sensitive non-government information, the loss of which could adversely affect the national security."
- It authorizes technical security material and assistance to go to "private institutions (including government contractors)."
- The directive's stated subject is protecting systems from foreign exploitation; it does not authorize collection from them.
