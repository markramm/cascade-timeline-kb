## Opening

**The Intercept reported on 2026-08-27 that new evidence contradicts a DHS "fact check" which had
asserted a woman in ICE custody received "FULL" prenatal care before suffering a stillbirth.** Per
the reporting, the evidence points to a **lack** of prenatal care, and the administration had
publicly characterized her account as false.

## Why This Event Matters — and the structural finding is the second-order one

**The first-order fact is medical neglect in custody.** The corpus documents that pattern already.

**The second-order fact is what makes this entry worth writing: DHS issued a public "fact check"
against a detained person's account of her own care, and the evidence now points the other way.**
That is an accountability-mechanism finding — **the agency used a messaging instrument normally
aimed at press claims against the account of someone in its own custody**, who had no comparable
platform and no access to her own records.

**A "fact check" from a party to the events is not a fact check; it is a party's account.** Report
it as DHS's characterization and say who was in a position to check it.
