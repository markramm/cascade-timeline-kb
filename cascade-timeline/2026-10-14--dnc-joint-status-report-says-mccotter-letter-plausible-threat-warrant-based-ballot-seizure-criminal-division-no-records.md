Democracy Docket reports that the DNC's side of the joint status report in its FOIA suit over ballot-seizure plans called the acting Deputy Attorney General's California letter "a plausible threat that DOJ will attempt to engage in warrant-based ballot seizure," and that the Criminal Division's final response letter said "no responsive records." That is the DNC's inference as relayed by Democracy Docket; DOJ has not said it plans any seizure. I could not read the report itself, which is not on RECAP.

## Update (2026-10-30, filing date): Second joint status report filed

In the second joint status report (filed October 30, 2026), the Justice Department said the Criminal Division and the Civil Rights Division had each issued a final response and "concluded it does not possess records responsive to the request"; the FBI is still searching and "anticipates being able to provide an update within the next 30 days." The DNC's section says the Civil Rights Division's result deepens its concern that the search was not reasonable, and that it has asked to meet and confer. The next report is due on or before November 13, 2026.
