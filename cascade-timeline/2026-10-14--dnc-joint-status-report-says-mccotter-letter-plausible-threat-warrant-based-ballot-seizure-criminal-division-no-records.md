On Wednesday, October 14, 2026 (docket entry date), the parties filed a joint status report in the DNC's FOIA suit over DOJ plans to seize ballots or voting equipment (D.D.C. 1:26-cv-02928). Democracy Docket reports that the DNC's side of the report calls the Oct. 13 letter from Acting Deputy Attorney General R. Trent McCotter "a plausible threat that DOJ will attempt to engage in warrant-based ballot seizure," and that the Criminal Division's final response letter in early October said its search found "no responsive records." That is the DNC's inference and its account of DOJ's letter; DOJ has not said it plans any seizure. The court's Oct. 16 minute order confirms the report asked it to order the Criminal Division and the Civil Rights Division to disclose their search parameters and to order the FBI to finish its review, tells the plaintiffs a court order requires a motion, and sets the next report for October 30.

I did not read Dkt. 16 or its exhibit (not in RECAP). The quote and the "no responsive records" statement come from Democracy Docket's account, so they are secondary; the filing date and the relief requested come from the docket.
