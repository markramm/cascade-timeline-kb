The PJM and Duke orders authorize dispatch of specified units and, as a last resort before or during an Energy Emergency Alert 3, operation of backup generation; Duke order 202-26-34 authorizes operation "notwithstanding air quality or other permit limitations." These are weather-driven and are a different instrument from the retention orders: they do not defer a retirement.

## Counts and what this does and does not show

From the page: numbers 202-26-26 through 202-26-50 are 25 order numbers in about four months (the May log recorded 202-26-01 to 202-26-25 as of May 21). Roughly 8 of the long-duration retention lines above are coal or gas units kept online past planned retirement (Culley, Schahfer, Centralia, Craig, Campbell, Eddystone, Wagner, Stanton); the rest of the stream is short-term emergency dispatch. No order here is a lapse: every plant's order is replaced two to three days before or at expiry.

**Next scheduled expiries (document triggers):** Campbell Nov 14, 2026 (renewal expected ~Nov 12); Eddystone Nov 20; Wagner 4 Nov 17; Stanton Nov 30; PREPA Nov 7; Guam Nov 4; Centralia Dec 11; Schahfer and Culley Dec 18; Craig Dec 25.
