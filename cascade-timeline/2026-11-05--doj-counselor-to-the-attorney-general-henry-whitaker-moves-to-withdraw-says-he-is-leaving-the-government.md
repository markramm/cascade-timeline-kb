In a motion to withdraw as counsel filed on November 5, 2026, in the consolidated appeals in United States v. Letitia A. James in the Fourth Circuit, Counselor to the Attorney General Henry Whitaker wrote: "Mr. Whitaker is imminently leaving the government for another opportunity." The motion says the United States will continue to be represented in these cases by R. Trent McCotter.
