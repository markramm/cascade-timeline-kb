Hennepin County Attorney Mary Moriarty's office announced state charges against ICE agent **Christian Castro** on **5/18/2026** (release date) over the **Jan. 14** shooting of **Julio Sosa-Celis** in north Minneapolis: **four counts of second-degree assault and one count of falsely reporting a crime**, case number **27-CR-26-12537**. This is the state charge, a different occurrence from the federal false-statement indictment unsealed in September.

The county's allegation, in its own words: Castro "discharged his weapon through the front door of a home knowing there were people who had just run inside." It also says he "was not hit with any shovels, brooms, or weapons of any kind at any time during this incident," contrary to the January 15 DHS press release, which, the county says, "remains on the DHS website." Prosecutors allege Castro fired "knowing it was occupied by multiple individuals," and Fox Twin Cities reports the complaint alleges he "provided false accounts of the confrontation to fellow ICE agents, medical staff and the FBI." These are allegations; Castro has not been convicted.

The county frames this as the "second federal agent to be criminally charged" for Operation Metro Surge conduct, after [[2026-04-16--moriarty-charges-ice-agent-morgan-assault-minnesota]]. On removal, the county says "should a judge grant removal, it will still be prosecuted by HCAO attorneys, under Minnesota Statute," and that a conviction would be "ineligible for presidential pardon."
