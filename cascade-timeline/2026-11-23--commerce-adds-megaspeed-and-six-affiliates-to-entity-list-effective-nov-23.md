The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security added 7 entities linked to Megaspeed to the Entity List. The rule is effective November 23, 2026. The Federal Register published it on 11/25/2026 as 91 FR 76148.

The rule names Megaspeed (Thailand) Co., Ltd. among the entities. The rule says the additions are made because of the "significant risk of these entities being or becoming involved in the diversion of advanced computing commodities to unauthorized end uses or end users." The entities are added with a license requirement, and the rule says licenses will be reviewed under a "presumption of denial."
