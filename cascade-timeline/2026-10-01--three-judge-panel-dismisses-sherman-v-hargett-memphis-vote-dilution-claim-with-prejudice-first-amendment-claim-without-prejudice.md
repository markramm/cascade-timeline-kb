The federal panel in *Sherman v. Hargett* (M.D. Tenn.; Judges Nalbandian, Campbell and Stivers) granted the defendants' motion to dismiss in a per curiam order and opinion. The plaintiffs are Black Memphians challenging the May 2026 congressional map that splits Memphis and Shelby County across districts.

- **Vote dilution count**: dismissed with prejudice for failure to state a claim. The panel held the complaint did not plausibly allege discriminatory purpose, applied the presumption of legislative good faith, and treated the sponsors' goal of an all-Republican delegation as the "obvious alternative explanation."
- **First Amendment count**: dismissed without prejudice for want of jurisdiction, as a nonjusticiable political question under *Rucho*.
- **Continuity**: the order dismisses for "essentially the same reasons" as the panel's earlier denial of a preliminary injunction.
