On November 24, 2026, DOE published a fact sheet marking the first anniversary of the Genesis Mission.

The fact sheet says the mission launched 297 research projects across 369 institutions.

The fact sheet says the mission expanded from 26 to 33 National Science and Technology Challenges, with participation from 20 federal agencies.

The fact sheet says that by July 2026, 20 federal agencies had joined the mission.

Executive Order 14363, in its Evaluation and Reporting section, directs the Secretary to submit a report to the President, through the APST and the Director of the Office of Management and Budget, within a year of the order.

Context: the fact sheet is DOE's own account of year one, published as a press document.
