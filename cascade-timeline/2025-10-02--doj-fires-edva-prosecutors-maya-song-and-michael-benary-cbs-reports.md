CBS News reported, citing two sources familiar with the matter, that the Justice Department fired two of the highest-level prosecutors in the Eastern District of Virginia: Maya Song, a longtime high-ranking official in the office, who CBS said was fired last week, and Michael Ben'Ary, the national security prosecutor, who was also terminated. CBS's story is updated October 2, 2025. See also [[2025-10-01--doj-fires-prosecutor-benary-false-social-media]].

**Reason.** A Justice Department spokesperson said the agency "does not discuss personnel matters". No reason is given by the department in the source read, and CBS does not say either prosecutor resisted charging Comey or Letitia James. CBS separately reported that staff at the office had circulated a memo arguing the charges should not be brought; the memo is not tied in the source to Song or Ben'Ary.

## Correction (2026-11-17)

Earlier text: titled and dated as a purge of two prosecutors who resisted political prosecutions, with one removed after activists flagged his résumé and another for resisting pressure to charge Comey and James.
