Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced a new Office of Religious Affairs in a State of the Force address at Marine Corps Base Quantico on Sept. 30, 2026, per the AP-based report syndicated by a Fox station. Hegseth said the office will facilitate offering "first-class religious support" and will report directly to him.

The Pentagon said the office will not "compel religious participation, favor one faith, or diminish the rights of personnel of any faith or lack thereof." The report says a Pentagon memo describes the director coordinating spiritual resources, including "faith-based homeschool support for military families." No director had been named.

The Pentagon memo itself was not located; this entry rests on press coverage.
