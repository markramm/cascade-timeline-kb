An affidavit filed 10/07/26 in a criminal complaint in the Southern District of Alabama says the Alabama Secretary of State with the U.S. Department of Homeland Security "identified numerous non-U.S. citizens registered to vote in Alabama". The affidavit does not say which DHS tool produced the list.

In the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, an affidavit in support of an arrest warrant by a Special Agent with Homeland Security Investigations assigned to the HSI Philadelphia Field Office is for a violation of 18 U.S.C. § 611 (voting by an alien) in connection with the November 5, 2024, election for federal office. The affidavit says the person has been charged by the United States Attorney in an Information with this offense.

The defendants are private individuals and are not named here.
