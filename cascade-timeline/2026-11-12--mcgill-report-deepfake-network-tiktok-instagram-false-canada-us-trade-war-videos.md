A report from McGill University's Centre for Media, Technology and Democracy says a network uses deepfakes of real influencers to spread false information about the Canada-U.S. trade war, The Canadian Press reported on Nov. 12, 2026. The network includes nearly 300 TikTok and Instagram accounts and has posted more than 3,201 videos that have been seen 22.1 million times in the past three months.

One video falsely says Prime Minister Mark Carney responded to a non-existent ultimatum from U.S. President Donald Trump by cancelling $8 billion in U.S. defence contracts. The story was shared in 161 videos on 129 accounts and played 5.1 million times, the report says. TikTok has only labelled four per cent of such videos as AI-generated, and only 6.3 per cent of commenters said the video is inauthentic or propaganda. The network is "almost certainly" run outside of North America, the report says. A TikTok spokesperson told AFP it had permanently banned the accounts mentioned in the report, along with several others.
