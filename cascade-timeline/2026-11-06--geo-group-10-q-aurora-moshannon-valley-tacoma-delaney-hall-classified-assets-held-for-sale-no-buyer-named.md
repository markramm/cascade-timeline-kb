GEO Group's quarterly report states that in October, 2026 its Aurora ICE Processing Center, Moshannon Valley Processing Center, Northwest ICE Processing Center and Delaney Hall Facility met the criteria to be classified as assets held for sale, with net carrying values of about $ 69 million, $ 54 million, $ 60 million and $ 36 million. The passages read name no buyer for them.

GEO's October press release on its Adelanto sale to DHS said the company was in an active process to sell other facilities to ICE, and that there was "no definitive agreement in place" for any further transaction. The Adelanto sale is at [[2026-10-05--geo-group-completes-950m-sale-of-three-adelanto-ice-facilities-to-dhs-keeps-operating-them]]; GEO's Tacoma contract award is at [[2026-10-28--ice-awards-geo-group-1-5-billion-10-year-contract-tacoma-northwest-ice-processing-center-single-offer]].
