On November 9, 2026, the Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security published a final rule in the Federal Register extending the suspension of its September 2025 interim final rule. The new final rule further stays the Affiliates Rule until January 10, 2027. The rule extended Entity List and Military End User List restrictions to non-U.S. entities owned 50% or more by listed parties. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and other administration officials have indicated that the extension is tied to the "Busan agreement" on trade issues between the U.S. and China, which was also extended to January 10, 2027. The rule does not alter the substance of the Affiliates Rule, and merely extends the suspension.

The sourcing is a law-firm summary (Skadden) of the Federal Register notice, not the notice itself. The extension keeps affiliate-owned firms outside Entity List reach while the truce holds, relevant to the pending question of Commerce's handling of chip-smuggling intermediaries such as Megaspeed.
