The independent-expenditure report of Fighting for Michigan PAC stamped 07/09/2026 (filing date) lists POLITICLIP LLC as payee and $25,000 for Digital Advertising.

The purpose says digital advertising and nothing about creators. Politiclip's own site describes "a private network of 10,000+ KYC-verified American creators". That is the vendor's marketing. The filing does not say what the PAC bought, and neither it nor the site names a creator or a campaign.
