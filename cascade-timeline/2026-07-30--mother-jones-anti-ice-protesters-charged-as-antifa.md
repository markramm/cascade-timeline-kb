A Mother Jones investigation published July 30, 2026 documents how anti-ICE protesters are being swept into the Trump administration's antifa prosecution campaign, facing federal charges that carry enhanced terrorism penalties. The reporting traces the machinery now being applied to immigration protesters back to three decades of FBI surveillance of animal-rights and environmental activists: long-running informant networks, broad conspiracy theories of liability, and enhancement statutes that let prosecutors charge people at the periphery of a protest as co-conspirators in acts committed by others.

The pattern extends the domestic-terrorism framework the timeline has tracked since AG Bondi's December 2025 memo directing the FBI to treat immigration-enforcement opposition as a domestic-terrorism indicator ([[2025-12-04--bondi-fbi-domestic-terrorism-memo]]), operationalized locally via NSPM-7 fusion-center bulletins and FBI informant recruitment ($200K offer to a Texas activist) reported in July ([[2026-07-07--nspm7-antifa-fusion-centers-fbi-informant-offer-texas]]). It sits alongside the Prairieland case — the first federal antifa terrorism prosecution, ending in 30-100 year sentences for eight ICE-protest defendants ([[2026-06-23--prairieland-defendants-sentenced-30-100-years-antifa-terrorism]]) — and The Intercept's July 3 documentation of DOJ's broader pattern of charging protesters as terrorists ([[2026-07-03--intercept-doj-charges-protesters-terrorism-communist-boogeyman-playbook]]). Mother Jones's contribution is the surveillance-infrastructure lineage: the same apparatus built against environmental and animal-rights activism over 30 years has been redirected, largely intact, at the immigration-protest movement.
