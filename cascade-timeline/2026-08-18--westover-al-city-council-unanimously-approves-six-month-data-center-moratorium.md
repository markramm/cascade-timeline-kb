The Westover, Alabama City Council unanimously approved a six-month data-center moratorium on Tuesday night, Aug. 18, 2026, per a Birmingham television report. The ordinance had been tabled at the council's previous meeting. Mayor Bobby Pardue said the pause was about "giving us time to make changes that we need to make."
