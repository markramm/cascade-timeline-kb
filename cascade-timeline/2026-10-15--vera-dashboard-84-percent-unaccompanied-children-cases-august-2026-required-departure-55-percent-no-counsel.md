The Vera Institute of Justice updated its Immigration Court Legal Representation Dashboard to add representation data for children, and its release reports that an estimated 84 percent of unaccompanied children's cases completed in August 2026 resulted in outcomes that required their departure, against 54 percent in August 2025 and 33 percent in August 2024. Of unaccompanied children's cases completed so far in 2026, Vera reports 55 percent never had representation in immigration court. Vera estimates 123,320 unaccompanied children are currently facing deportation in immigration court, and counts at least 702,220 children of all categories in that position as of August 2026. Vera's Shayna Kessler called the administration's treatment of these children "legally suspect and morally unconscionable." The figures are Vera's estimates from immigration-court records, not government-published counts.

The release is the organization's own account of a dashboard built because the data are, in its words, not readily available in government records.
