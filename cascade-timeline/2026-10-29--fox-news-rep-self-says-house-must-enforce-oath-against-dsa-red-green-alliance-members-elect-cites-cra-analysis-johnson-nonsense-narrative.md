Fox News published the report on Oct. 29, 2026.

Rep. Keith Self (R-Texas) told Fox News Digital: "You cannot swear an oath to support and defend the Constitution while actively working to destroy it."

Self also said: "As DSA candidates and the Red-Green Alliance gain ground, we must enforce the Constitution as written or risk allowing people committed to replacing it to take seats of power under false pretenses."

The article quotes an analysis by the Center for Renewing America: "The objecting member should place the documentary record before the House, and the House should debate whether that evidence establishes that the member-elect remains committed to destroying the constitutional order,"

The article says Speaker Mike Johnson (R-La.) told reporters at an Iowa event that month: "We are going to follow the law."

The article says Rep. Tim Burchett (R-Tenn.) told the New York Post earlier this month that he would use the maneuver to try to block Darializa Avila Chevalier from joining Congress next year.
