- A General Accounting Office letter dated July 17, 1992 answered a request from Rep. Edward Markey, chairman of the House Energy and Commerce telecommunications subcommittee, who "asked us to evaluate" the technological alternatives available to the FBI for wiretapping voice and data communications.
- GAO wrote that, "since 1986, the FBI has become increasingly aware of the potential loss of wiretapping capability" because of new technologies.
- On how the FBI had dealt with carriers, GAO wrote that these security officers "were the designated company contacts" for the FBI's wiretapping needs, meaning the telephone companies' security officers, according to the FBI and industry officials.
