On December 4, 2025, Attorney General Pam Bondi issued a memorandum implementing the President's national security memorandum on domestic terrorism. Its tax sentence reads: "In addition, federal law enforcement and federal prosecutors should consider any applicable tax crimes in cases in which extremist groups are suspected of defrauding the Internal Revenue Service." The next sentence says the Justice Department, as it receives referrals for violations of tax obligations, "should investigate and, where appropriate, prosecute those responsible." The memo is the Justice Department's. It does not revoke a tax exemption, and the sentence does not mention donors.

Other provisions that coverage runs together with it are separate. Within 14 days, all federal law enforcement agencies are to review their files and holdings for "Antifa and Antifa-related intelligence" and deliver the material to the FBI. Separately, the FBI is to investigate matters from the past five years that involved potential acts of domestic terrorism. Neither provision is about taxes, and the 14-day review is of agencies' files, not a five-year retroactive review. The FBI is also to disseminate an intelligence bulletin on antifa-aligned groups within 60 days. The memo's footnote says: "No investigation may be opened based solely on activities protected by the First Amendment."

The entry holds no tax investigation or prosecution brought under the memo.
