Republican candidate Greg Cunningham, running for New Mexico's 2nd Congressional District, told Migrant Insider on August 7, 2026 that Renee Good, 37, and Alex Pretti, 37 — two U.S. citizens shot dead by federal immigration agents in Minneapolis in January 2026 during Operation Metro Surge — were acceptable losses: "They got to go, so they are collateral damage to a system that put ICE in the position where they have to go locate this person." Cunningham, citing a claimed background in surveillance operations, blamed anti-ICE protesters for the shootings and called for "public training" to accept enforcement casualties.

The killings of Good and Pretti are documented at [[2026-01-07--ice-agent-kills-legal-observer-minneapolis-operation-metro-surge]], part of the Operation Metro Surge deployment announced at [[2025-12-04--operation-metro-surge-announced-minneapolis]] that produced over 1,300 documented "collateral" arrests. Cunningham's statement is notable as the most explicit normalization by a major-party candidate to date of civilian deaths during ICE operations as an acceptable operational cost — using the same "collateral" framing ICE itself applies to bystander arrests, now extended by a candidate to justify bystander deaths.
