A Bloomberg investigation published July 22, 2026 documents a revolving-door sequence around the GENIUS Act, the first federal stablecoin law. Before taking office as Commerce Secretary, Howard Lutnick worked to kill earlier stablecoin legislation that was unfavorable to Tether, the stablecoin issuer in which he holds a roughly $600 million stake ([[2025-01-20--howard-lutnick-600-million-tether-conflict]]). During GENIUS Act drafting, White House crypto aide Bo Hines personally defended provisions preferred by Tether. Within one month of the Act's signing, Tether hired Hines — later promoting him to CEO of its new U.S. stablecoin, USAT — completing the loop from regulatory advocacy to direct employment by the company that benefited.

This closes the loop on a pattern the timeline has been tracking piecemeal since Tether's early 2025 political rehabilitation ([[2025-04-29--tether-was-accused-of-fraud-now-it-s-a-crypto-darling-in-was]]), which already named both Hines and Lutnick as vectors of Tether's rising Washington influence, and Lutnick's undisclosed-scale financial exposure to the company he was positioned to regulate ([[2025-01-20--howard-lutnick-600-million-tether-conflict]]), further extended by the UCC filing revealing Tether as collateral agent for the Lutnick family trust ([[2025-10-06--tether-loan-lutnick-children-ucc-filing]]). It also sits alongside the broader GENIUS Act implementation record — the OCC's industry-preferred reserve-standard rulemaking ([[2026-06-24--genius-act-occ-stablecoin-charters-crypto-industry-reserve-standards]]) and the July 18 deadline opening the stablecoin market to banks ([[2026-07-12--genius-act-july18-deadline-banks-enter-stablecoin-market]]) — as documentation that the law Trump signed hailing a "new frontier" ([[2025-07-18--trump-signs-landmark-genius-act-hailing-exciting-new-frontie]]) was shaped at the drafting stage by the people who personally profited from its passage, a textbook case of the personnel-capture mechanism found throughout the crypto-regulatory apparatus.
