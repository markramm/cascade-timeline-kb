Federal court filings in the District of Minnesota, first reported by the New York Times and analyzed across independent outlets in mid-August 2026, revealed "Operation Puppet Master" — a Homeland Security Investigations surveillance program opened four days after federal immigration agents shot and killed Alex Pretti, a nurse and protester, in Minneapolis. Undercover HSI agents posing as protesters and activists surveilled community meetings at churches, parks, libraries, union halls, schools, and restaurants. Agents secretly recorded political discussions, entered encrypted Signal chats, collected license plate numbers, and compiled dossiers containing names, photographs, home addresses, and employment and family information. Targets included labor unions, community groups, the Sunrise Movement, and a bicycle repair collective. The Intercept reported that at least one undercover agent attempted to entice Minneapolis activists toward more confrontational tactics.

The predicate matters as much as the surveillance. Emptywheel's reporting traced the investigative chain: Attorney General Pam Bondi's high-profile "trophy arrests" of anti-ICE activists — announced with staged photos that defense attorneys argued violated a sealing order DOJ itself had requested — supplied the conspiracy predicate for the Minnesota 15 indictment and, through it, for the broader infiltration. Prosecution operated as a surveillance-expansion wedge: charge a handful publicly, then use the resulting investigative authorities to map an entire political community. Many of the underlying cases have since collapsed. This is the same machinery documented when anti-ICE protesters were charged as antifa (2026-07-30--mother-jones-anti-ice-protesters-charged-as-antifa), when Prairieland defendants drew 30-to-100-year terrorism sentences (2026-06-23--prairieland-defendants-sentenced-30-100-years-antifa-terrorism), and when a federal terrorism probe was opened against Minnesota protesters banging pots and pans outside hotels housing ICE agents. Minnesota was already a designated enforcement theater — Operation Metro Surge, the Bovino criminal investigation (2026-03-03--bovino-criminal-investigation-minnesota-ice), and Bondi's linkage of voter-roll demands to ICE enforcement (2026-01-26--bondi-voter-rolls-request-minnesota-ice-enforcement) all preceded it. The state killing of a protester did not trigger an investigation of the killing; it triggered an investigation of the people who objected.
