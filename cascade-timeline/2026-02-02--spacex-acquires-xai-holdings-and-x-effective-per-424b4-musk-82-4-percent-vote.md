SpaceX's final IPO prospectus states that SpaceX acquired X.AI Holdings Corp., the parent of xAI, effective February 2, 2026. The prospectus calls this the "xAI Merger". It also states that X Holdings Corp., which owns X, was acquired by xAI effective March 28, 2025.

**Accounting treatment.** The prospectus recasts SpaceX's financial statements for all periods to include X.AI Holdings and X Holdings, and gives as the reason that the transactions were between entities under common control.

**Control after the IPO.** At the initial public offering price of $135 per share, the prospectus says Musk, as founder, chief executive and chairman, will hold approximately 82.4% of the voting power.
