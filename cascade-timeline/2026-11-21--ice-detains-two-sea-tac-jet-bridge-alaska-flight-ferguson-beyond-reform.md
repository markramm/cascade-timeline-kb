Masked ICE officers detained two people on a jet bridge at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport on Saturday as travelers boarded an Alaska Airlines flight bound for Los Angeles, causing what the airline described as a distressing scene for passengers and employees. ICE said in a statement that its officers conducted a targeted enforcement operation and that the man it targeted had overstayed his visa; it said the woman also detained, who was with him, had also overstayed her visa and could be charged with assaulting a federal officer. Alaska Airlines said it had received no advance notice. Washington Governor Bob Ferguson said the video was horrific and called ICE beyond reform, adding that he was looking into how masked agents accessed the jet bridge and whether their face coverings violated a state law he signed in March.
