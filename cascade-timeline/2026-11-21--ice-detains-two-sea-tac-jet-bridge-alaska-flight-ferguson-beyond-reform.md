Masked ICE officers detained two people on a jet bridge at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport on Saturday as travelers boarded an Alaska Airlines flight bound for Los Angeles, causing what the airline described as a distressing scene for passengers and employees. ICE said in a statement that its officers conducted a targeted enforcement operation and that the man it targeted had overstayed his visa; it said the woman also detained, who was with him, had also overstayed her visa and could be charged with assaulting a federal officer. Alaska Airlines said it had received no advance notice. Washington Governor Bob Ferguson said the video was horrific and called ICE beyond reform, adding that he was looking into how masked agents accessed the jet bridge and whether their face coverings violated a state law he signed in March. The Port of Seattle, which runs the airport, said the enforcement was conducted without the Port's participation or knowledge and that it was in conversation with DHS on whether any of these protocols were breached; KUOW reported that agents took the two out the side door of a jet bridge, and Port Commissioner Ryan Calkins said, "We are preparing a comprehensive review of the incident." KOMO News reported that the two are a father and his adult daughter, and that their attorney says both have pending asylum.

## Update (2026-11-23, public meeting date): Port of Seattle opens review of the jet-bridge arrests; commission asks Congress to stop ICE airport enforcement

At a Port Commission meeting on Monday, Port of Seattle officials said they are preparing a "comprehensive review of the incident" and will examine whether federal agents violated airport safety protocols. Under current agreements, agents at the airport have agreed not to wear face coverings and to identify themselves; video of the arrests shows agents in surgical masks and plain clothes without any badge or uniform identifying them as ICE.

The Port Commission said in a statement that airport immigration enforcement "is new, disruptive, and needs to stop" and called on the state's federal delegation to stop ICE from making such arrests at U.S. airports. Commissioner Hamdi Mohamed proposed deactivating any Port-issued badges ICE holds. Commissioner Ryan Calkins, speaking as a citizen, said: "I don’t believe this particular institution can be reformed."
