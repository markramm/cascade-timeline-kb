Executive Order 11773 was signed on March 21, 1974, according to the National Archives disposition table.

The order is titled "Revoking the authority of the Department of Agriculture to inspect income tax returns".

The table lists it as revoking EO 11697 of January 17, 1973, and EO 11709 of March 27, 1973.

Context: the National Archives 1973 table gives EO 11697 and EO 11709 the same title, "Inspection by Department of Agriculture of income tax returns made under the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 of persons having farm operations". We read the tables and the Federal Register record pages only; the texts of these orders were not read.
