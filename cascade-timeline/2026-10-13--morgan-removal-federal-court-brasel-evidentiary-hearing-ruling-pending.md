# Morgan's State Assault Case Is in Federal Court

The KB's earlier entry records Hennepin County's assault charge against ICE officer Gregory Donnell Morgan Jr. and stops there. The federal docket for State of Minnesota v. Morgan (Judge Nancy E. Brasel, District of Minnesota) lists the case as pending in federal court after removal.

The United States filed a statement of interest "in support of the removal of this criminal prosecution to federal court." After a multi-day evidentiary hearing, the courtroom minutes of the hearing's last day read "Post hearing briefs due: Three weeks after the transcripts are filed." The transcripts were docketed late in October, so briefing runs into mid-November. The docket's last RECAP entry is one attorney's withdrawal, with no reason given. No ruling on removal or immunity appears, and no trial date exists in either court.

RECAP mirrors only what has been pulled from PACER, so later filings may exist unmirrored.
