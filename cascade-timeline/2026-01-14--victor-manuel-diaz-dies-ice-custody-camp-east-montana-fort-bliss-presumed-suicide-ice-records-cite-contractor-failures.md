Victor Manuel Diaz, 36, a Nicaraguan man, died in ICE custody on Jan. 14, 2026 at Camp East Montana at Fort Bliss in El Paso, Texas. ICE's own detainee-death notification says he died of a presumed suicide and that the official cause remained under investigation. It says ICE officers encountered him in Minneapolis, Minnesota, that he entered ICE custody on Jan. 6, and that he was pronounced deceased at 4:09 p.m. Mother Jones reports that ICE's January announcement said he was found with "a ligature around his neck."

Records obtained by Mother Jones's Samantha Michaels through a Freedom of Information Act request show what ICE wrote to its contractor, Acquisition Logistics, afterward. By Mother Jones's account, the agency cited his death as one of several reasons for ending the company's roughly $1.3 billion contract. ICE's detention standards, as Mother Jones describes them, call for at-risk detainees to be watched at irregular intervals no more than 15 minutes apart; ICE wrote that he was held in a holding room with less frequent monitoring "despite risk indicators." The same notice faulted the company because the medical holding rooms lacked "vision panel doors" it had promised to install, and called that failure a "material contributing factor" in the inability to prevent or timely intervene in the suicide. These are ICE's findings as quoted by one outlet from records not published in full here. The official cause of death, per ICE's notification, remained under investigation.

Mother Jones places the death beside the other one ICE announced at the camp in January: the El Paso medical examiner later determined that death was a homicide ([[2026-01-21--lunas-campos-el-paso-medical-examiner-rules-homicide-ice-camp-east-montana]]).
