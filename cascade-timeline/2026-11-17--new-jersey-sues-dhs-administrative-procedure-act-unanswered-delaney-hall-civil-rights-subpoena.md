New Jersey Attorney General Jennifer Davenport sued the Department of Homeland Security on Tuesday, saying DHS officials have failed for months to respond to a subpoena seeking information about the treatment of detainees at Delaney Hall in Newark. The suit asks a federal judge to declare Homeland Security in violation of the Administrative Procedure Act for unreasonable delays and to set a deadline for a response. The New Jersey Monitor reports that Geo Group has said it cannot respond to the subpoena without authorization from Homeland Security; the lawsuit says Geo Group does not need permission. The state says its investigators already found problems with the facility's medical intake and screening. DHS's statement did not address the state's subpoena.

This is a new suit against the federal agency, after the state's civil rights subpoenas ([[2026-08-07--nj-ag-davenport-civil-rights-subpoenas-delaney-hall-geo-group]]) and its earlier suit against the operator over inspector access ([[2026-06-02--nj-sues-geo-group-delaney-hall-health-inspector-access]]).
