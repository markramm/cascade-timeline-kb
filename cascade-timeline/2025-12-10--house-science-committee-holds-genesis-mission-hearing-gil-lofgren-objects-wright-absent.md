The House Science, Space, and Technology Committee held a full-committee hearing titled The Genesis Mission: Prioritizing American Science and Technology Leadership. Ranking Member Zoe Lofgren's prepared statement is headed December 10th, 2025.

Lofgren said: "However, I am duty-bound to raise my strong objection to the notion of even holding such a hearing before Secretary Wright appears before this Committee". Energy Secretary Chris Wright did not testify at this hearing; Under Secretary for Science Darío Gil did.

Lofgren also said: "This Congress, we haven’t heard from a single agency head or a single Inspector General".

According to FedScoop, lawmakers asked questions about the dangers of unbounded agentic capabilities and adversaries reverse-engineering Genesis Mission AI models. FedScoop quotes Gil in reply: "We’re going to do a tremendous amount to protect what we’re creating".

Hearing detail and the privacy exchange are in [[genesis-oversight-record]]; Gil's remark that access to AI models will be restricted is in [[genesis-mission-va-mvp-data-at-ornl]].
