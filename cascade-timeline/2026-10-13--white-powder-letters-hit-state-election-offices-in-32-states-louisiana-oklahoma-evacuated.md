Date shown is the first documented receipt (the Louisiana letter opened Oct 13, 2026), not the mailing date, which no source gives. The FBI and U.S. Postal Inspection Service say they are investigating "a series of suspicious mailings" to Secretary of State and related election offices and are working to identify the sender or senders. WIRED's tally (Oct 19) lists 32 states that confirmed receipt, but that is a count of states targeted, not of letters opened: the Postal Service intercepted most before delivery, and WIRED names five states where letters were not intercepted. Louisiana, Oklahoma and Arizona evacuations are confirmed by their own officials' statements as reported; Louisiana's was a partial evacuation of the elections section per WAFB.

Tests reported so far found nothing harmful (Louisiana, Arizona, Wisconsin, Mississippi). Envelopes bore a "DSA" return address; DSA denies involvement, says it has not occupied that office address since 2013, and unnamed authorities quoted by Fox 9 said there is no evidence linking it. No arrest or charge had been reported as of Oct 19. Nebraska's secretary of state said this was the second such envelope to his office, the first having come in the September 2024 wave.
