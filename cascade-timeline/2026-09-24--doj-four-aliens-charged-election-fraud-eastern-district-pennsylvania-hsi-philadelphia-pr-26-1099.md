U.S. Attorney David Metcalf announced election-related charges against four people in the Eastern District of Pennsylvania. Metcalf said, "Anyone, including illegal aliens, who votes illegally in our elections will be prosecuted for corrupting our democracy." The release states, "These cases were investigated by Homeland Security Investigations." 

## Update (2026-10-23, announcement date (DOJ news release, as reported)): Nine more Pennsylvania residents charged; state total reaches 20 since September

The Justice Department charged nine more Pennsylvania residents with voting as a noncitizen, Votebeat reported on Oct. 23, 2026, "according to a Friday news release." The charges "have not yet been proven," and Votebeat found "no evidence the cases are related." Most of those charged are "lawful permanent residents of the U.S., according to the department." The new cases "bring the total number of individuals charged with noncitizen-voting-related crimes in Pennsylvania to 20 since September."

The count sits beside DHS's July claim of 250,000 potential noncitizens registered in four states, 14,576 of them in Pennsylvania; Votebeat reports DHS "has not yet shared the list of potential noncitizen voters with Pennsylvania officials." This record names no defendants; they are private people charged, not convicted.
