Homeland Security Investigations — the investigative arm of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement — issued a solicitation seeking a private contractor to supply "publicly available" voter registration files and voter history files, described in the procurement language as supporting HSI "fraud detection and data segmentation activities." 404 Media, which first reported the records on August 26, 2026, obtained the procurement documents; Courthouse News Service and Democracy Docket reported the same solicitation the same day. The vehicle is a commercial acquisition: rather than demanding files from secretaries of state, HSI is asking a vendor to assemble and hand over the data as a delivered product.

The "all 50 states" figure that ran in headlines is a **required contractor capability, not the scope of the purchase**. The solicitation asks bidders to demonstrate they can obtain voter data from all fifty states and the territories; it says separately that the list of jurisdictions ICE actually wants files from is likely to be limited at first. That distinction matters for reading the document honestly — this is a procurement establishing a pipeline with room to grow, not a completed nationwide acquisition, and describing it as ICE "buying voter records from all fifty states" overstates what the records show.

The structurally useful point is that this runs **parallel to but separate from** the DOJ voter-roll campaign already documented in this timeline, and takes a different route to the same data. DOJ has spent a year on the compulsion route: demand letters to 44 states ([[2025-05-01--doj-demand-letters-44-states-voter-data]]), a push for a centralized federal database ([[2025-09-09--doj-demands-voter-data-centralized-database]]), confidential MOUs covering 37 million voters ([[2026-02-17--doj-secret-voter-file-agreements-37-million-voters]]), and a litigation record that reached 0-23 by mid-August ([[2026-08-17--minnesota-judge-dismisses-doj-voter-roll-suit-fishing-expedition-0-23]]) even as twelve states complied anyway ([[2026-08-06--aclu-doj-voter-database-12-states-complied-5-suits-dismissed]]). A different agency buying the same category of records from a vendor needs no state's cooperation, no MOU, and no court's permission — the commercial-purchase route sidesteps every point where the compulsion route has been losing. It is the same mechanism already documented in ICE's other data acquisitions: the Penlink PLX no-bid contract for real-time interception ([[2026-04-29--dhs-penlink-plx-no-bid-contract-warrantless-realtime-interception]]), the LexisNexis and Palantir bulk facial-recognition contract ([[2026-08-10--ice-lexisnexis-palantir-bulk-facial-recognition-contract]]), and the geolocation purchases that drew a Maryland attorney general complaint against Penlink and Thomson Reuters ([[2026-08-20--maryland-ag-complaint-penlink-thomson-reuters-geolocation-ice]]). What is new is the category of record: voter files, sought by the agency that conducts immigration investigations, at a moment when Bondi's DOJ had already framed Minnesota's rolls as an ICE-enforcement resource ([[2026-01-26--bondi-voter-rolls-request-minnesota-ice-enforcement]]).

**Update, 2026-08-28**: primary-source confirmation from SAM.gov establishes this is a **Request for Information** (notice `HSIvoter26`, live as of 2026-08-28, no award, none possible yet — the notice states outright it "does not commit the Government to contract for any supply or service"), not a solicitation. The draft statement of work names no specific jurisdictions at all — the "limited initial list" will be set later, in writing, at the government's discretion. It also contains a previously unreported mechanism: where state law restricts commercial resale of voter files but exempts law enforcement, DHS will issue "Government Interest" / "Need-to-Know" letters making the winning contractor its "designated acquisition agent," clearing the state-law hurdle while keeping the deal structured as a commercial contract. No legal authority or statute is cited anywhere in the RFI or draft SOW. Full primary-document detail: [[hsi-voter-records-solicitation-jurisdiction-scope-2026-08-28]].
