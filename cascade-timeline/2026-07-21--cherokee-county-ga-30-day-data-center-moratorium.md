The Cherokee County, Georgia Board of Commissioners unanimously approved a 30-day moratorium on new data-center development on July 21, 2026, with a public hearing scheduled for August 18 to determine whether to extend it. Cherokee County sits in the outer Atlanta metro, under the same hyperscale-corridor siting pressure driving buildout across Georgia's Douglas, Newton, Spalding, and Coweta counties — a state that has become a national data-center hotspot, and where DeKalb County had already extended its own moratorium into 2027 ([[2026-07-07--dekalb-county-ga-extends-data-center-moratorium-march-2027]]).
