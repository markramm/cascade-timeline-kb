Daily Wire co-founder Jeremy Boreing announced on X on March 22, 2024 that the company and Candace Owens had ended their relationship. Owens confirmed the departure the same day. Deadline reports that she said she would be bringing her show to her personal YouTube page after a brief hiatus, and NBC News reports that she wrote that many announcements would follow.

NBC News reports that it is unclear exactly what led to the end of the partnership, and that The Daily Wire did not immediately respond to a request for comment. Deadline reports that Owens had publicly clashed with Ben Shapiro, a Daily Wire co-founder, over the Israel-Hamas war, that the Anti-Defamation League had criticised Owens that week, and that she answered the criticism in a post of her own.
