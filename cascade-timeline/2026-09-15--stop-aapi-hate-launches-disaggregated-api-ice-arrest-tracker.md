**Stop AAPI Hate** launched an **A/PI ICE Tracker** on **September 15, 2026**, disaggregating ICE arrest, detention, and deportation data by state, country of origin, age, and gender. Its New York finding: average monthly ICE arrests of AAPI immigrants rose to **103 per month from an average of 24** under the prior administration — a **more than fourfold** increase — across data running October 2022 through July 2026. Arrests of **Chinese immigrants in New York** rose more than tenfold, to **34 monthly from about three**. Nationally, arrests of Asian immigrants rose **436%** against a **219%** increase overall, with California (25.2%), Texas (12.6%), and New York (9.4%) holding the largest shares. Arrests of Asian immigrants hit their highest recorded level in **July 2026**.

The tracker is a measurement instrument, and the structurally interesting number is the ratio, not the level: 436% against 219% overall means Asian immigrants were arrested at roughly twice the rate of the enforcement surge as a whole. That is a targeting signal visible only because someone built the disaggregation — ICE's own published figures do not break out this population, which is the recurring shape of this beat. An earlier **Documented NY** analysis in April 2026 put the New York increase at 600% on a different window and denominator; the two figures are not in conflict so much as measuring different spans, and the discrepancy is a reason to cite the window whenever the number is used. The corpus has tracked the New York enforcement surge alongside the collapse of the legal-services funding that would have answered it ([[2026-06-25--asian-immigrant-ice-arrests-nyc-legal-services-funding]]).

*Sourcing note: the "over 400%" framing widely attached to this tracker comes from downstream reporting (Documented NY, AsAmNews), not from the Stop AAPI Hate tracker pages themselves, which were checked and do not contain that figure. This entry carries the underlying monthly counts (24 → 103) that the percentage is derived from, and the national 436%/219% comparison as reported. Cite the counts, not the rounded percentage, and name the window.*
