Voice of San Diego and Times of San Diego reported that the San Diego City Council voted unanimously on Monday, Nov. 16, to rescind the city's use policy for its network of 500 automated license plate readers. A related motion to suspend the city's smart streetlights passed 5-2. A San Diego Police Department spokesperson said during the meeting that the cameras will go dark within 24 hours and all data will be deleted. The physical cameras will remain for now: the mayor must first cancel the city's contract with the vendor, Ubicquia, before they can be removed. Voice of San Diego reported that the decision makes San Diego the largest city in America to shut off its ALPR network, which runs on software from Flock.
