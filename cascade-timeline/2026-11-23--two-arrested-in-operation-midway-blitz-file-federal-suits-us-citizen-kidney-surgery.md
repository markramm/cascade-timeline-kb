A man and a woman arrested during Operation Midway Blitz filed separate lawsuits against the federal government on Monday in the Northern District of Illinois. She is a U.S. citizen whose complaint says agents dragged her from her car, handcuffed her, knelt on her body near a surgical incision and detained her despite her pleas that she was recovering from kidney surgery. She was one of five U.S. citizens who spoke at a congressional public forum last December to counter DHS claims that the department does not arrest U.S. citizens. He was later flown out of the country; his attorney, Robert Held, said his departure was legally classified as voluntary. DHS referred back to earlier statements on each arrest.

The suits join the accountability record for the Chicago-area operation; see [[2026-03-02--cbp-opr-compelled-declaration-bovino-ellis-credibility-findings]] and [[2026-02-11--marimar-martinez-bodycam-texts-released-contradict-cbp-account]].
