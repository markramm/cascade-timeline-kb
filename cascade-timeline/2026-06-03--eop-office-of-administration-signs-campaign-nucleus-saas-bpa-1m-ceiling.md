On 2026-06-03 the Executive Office of the President's Office of Administration signed blanket purchase agreement 11316026A0003EOP with Campaign Nucleus LLC of Medina, Ohio, for a "software as a service platform" (USAspending description: "CAMPAIGN NUCLEUS SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SAAS) PLATFORM"). The BPA is a single-award, commercial-item agreement with a $1,000,000 ceiling and nothing obligated on the agreement itself. Performance runs 2026-07-01 to 2031-06-30.

Two orders sit under it. An "event planning software" order for $66,000 (11316026F0015WHO) was signed 2026-06-10 and runs the full five years. A $33,000 "subscription" order (11316026F0008OVP) was signed 2026-07-08 and runs 2026-07-22 to 2027-07-21. Together that is $99,000 obligated of the $1,000,000 ceiling as of the USAspending record read on 2026-10-16.

Campaign Nucleus is the same Parscale-linked vendor that political committees pay for fundraising software. TIME (2026-07-14) describes it as one of the firms in a network Parscale oversees. This entry records only the procurement: who signed, for how much, and when.

**Dates, by kind.** BPA signed 2026-06-03. Order 1 signed 2026-06-10. Order 2 signed 2026-07-08. Performance starts 2026-07-01 (BPA, order 1) and 2026-07-22 (order 2).
