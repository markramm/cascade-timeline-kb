Paul Martin, the inspector general of the U.S. Agency for International Development, was fired on Tuesday, Feb. 11, 2025. NBC News reported that he "was fired Tuesday, a day after his office released a report". Voice of America, citing officials who spoke without authorization, said the report warned that the dismantling of USAID had made it all but impossible to monitor $8.2 billion in unspent humanitarian funds.

Martin was notified by email. According to NBC, the email from Deputy Director of Presidential Personnel Trent Morse said "your position as Inspector General of the United States Agency for International Development is terminated, effective immediately". Voice of America reported: "The White House gave no reason for the firing of Inspector General Paul Martin".

This is a separate removal from the earlier mass firing of inspectors general: see [[2025-01-24--trump-fires-18-inspectors-general-purge]].

## Correction (2026-11-17)

An earlier version of this entry was filed under a date in late January and called Martin the eighteenth inspector general removed. Both NBC and Voice of America date the firing to Tuesday, Feb. 11, 2025, so the entry was renamed to that date and the old id is kept under `previous_ids`. Neither source gives an ordinal. The earlier text also said Martin was fired after publishing a critical report and was part of a pattern of removing inspectors general who issued unfavorable reports; the cited sources give a report and then a firing the next day, and no reason. The link between them is our reading.
