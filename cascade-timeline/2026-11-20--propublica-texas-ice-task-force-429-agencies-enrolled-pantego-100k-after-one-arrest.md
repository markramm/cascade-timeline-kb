A ProPublica/KXAN/Texas Tribune investigation documents how the federal ICE task force program funnels money to local police departments with few strings attached. As of mid-September, 429 law enforcement agencies in Texas had enrolled; one — Pantego — received $100,000 after a single immigration arrest. At least 241 task force model agreements were signed in Texas in 2026 alone. Smith County received nearly $2.2 million in task force funding from January 2025 through June 2026. Texas also runs a $20 million state grant program for sheriff's offices; more than $6.5 million had been awarded to 78 agencies by late September.
