At a rally in Dubuque, Iowa, on Sunday, Nov. 1, President Trump said, in the Roll Call Factba.se transcript of the speech, "groups of Somalians going into Ohio and they're trying to play with the elections," and of Somali immigrants in Minnesota, "they vote exactly the way they're told to vote." On the Alaska Senate race he said "This is voter fraud at the highest level," said the Democratic candidate Mary Peltola "should be removed from the ballot and prosecuted," and added "This behavior in our elections cannot be allowed to continue." The transcript is a machine transcription of the video, so the wording is as transcribed. This entry records what was said and does not test it.
