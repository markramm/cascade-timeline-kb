Advocacy group Do Something Southern Indiana appeared at the July 31, 2026 Clark County Commissioners meeting demanding review of the county jail's ICE detention agreement, which has generated nearly $3 million across 783 detainees since April 2025. Residents raised concerns about liability exposure and lack of cost transparency; officials confirmed they have not yet deployed any of the accumulated funds, citing uncertainty about the agreement's future.

Clark County is a case study in IGSA revenue becoming a political liability rather than a straightforward budget win: the contract exists and the money is accumulating, but community organizing is now pressuring commissioners before the funds are spent — unlike jurisdictions documented at [[2026-06-23--crow-wing-county-mn-residents-demand-igsa-termination]] where organizing began after money was already committed. Clark County sits in the Louisville, KY metro area; whether the county deploys the $3M will determine how difficult a future political exit from the agreement becomes.
