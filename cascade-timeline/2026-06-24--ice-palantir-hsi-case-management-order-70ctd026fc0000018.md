USAspending lists order 70CTD026FC0000018 to Palantir Technologies Inc. as signed on 06/24/2026 (month/day/year). Its performance period runs from 06/26/2026 to 06/25/2027. Total obligation on the award is $45,848,616.8.

The description reads "THIS ORDER PROVIDES HOMELAND SECURITY INVESTIGATIONS SYSTEM MODERNIZATION FOR THE INVESTIGATIVE CASE MANAGEMENT AND INVESTIGATIVE ANALYTICS ON THE CASE MANAGEMENT AND ANALYTICS PLATFORM IN SUPPORT OF THE U.S. IMMIGRATIONS CUSTOMS ENFORCEMENT MISSION." The competition fields read "FULL AND OPEN COMPETITION" under a multiple-award fair-opportunity procedure.
