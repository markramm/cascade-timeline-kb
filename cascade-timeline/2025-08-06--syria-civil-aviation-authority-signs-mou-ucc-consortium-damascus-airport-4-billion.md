Syria signed a memorandum of understanding with a consortium led by UCC Holding to redevelop and expand Damascus International Airport. The Syrian counterparty was the Syrian Civil Aviation Authority. SANA lists the airport among the investment projects for which memorandums of understanding were signed that day, and quotes Talal al-Hilali, chairman of the Syrian Investment Authority, giving an investment value of $4 billion.
