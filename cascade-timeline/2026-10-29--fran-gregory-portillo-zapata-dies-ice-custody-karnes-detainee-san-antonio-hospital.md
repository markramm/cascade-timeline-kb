Fran Gregory Portillo-Zapata, 53, a Venezuelan national, died Oct. 29, 2026, at North Central Baptist Hospital in San Antonio, Texas, while detained at the Karnes County Immigration Processing Center. ICE's detainee death notification, says he was admitted to the hospital on Sept. 30, that he "suffered from severe sepsis, acute pancreatitis, and end-stage kidney disease," and that the official cause of death remains pending further medical examination.

ICE says its San Antonio office conducted an enforcement action on Aug. 30, 2026, and that it notified his next of kin and the Venezuelan Embassy.

**Not established here:** the cause and manner of death, which ICE says is pending, and the care he received between his arrest and his hospital admission. ICE's notification is the only source read for this entry.
