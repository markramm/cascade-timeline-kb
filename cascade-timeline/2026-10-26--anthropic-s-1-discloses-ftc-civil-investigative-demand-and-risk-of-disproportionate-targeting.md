Anthropic, PBC filed a public registration statement with the SEC on the date of this entry (EDGAR filing date). Its risk factors include passages that bear on the FTC probe.

One, in the AI-safety risk factor, says the FTC issued a civil investigative demand to Anthropic in October 2026 seeking information on its consumer protection, data protection and privacy practices and the safety and security of its products. It adds that any investigation or enforcement action could require changes to how it designs, tests or deploys its models.

Another, in a separate political-environment risk factor, says Anthropic "may continue to be subject to heightened scrutiny or the risk of being disproportionately or selectively targeted by particular governmental bodies, even where similarly situated companies are not". The filing does not tie this passage to the FTC.

Correction to a secondary summary: the capture ticket relayed Axios as quoting the filing as saying Anthropic would likely continue to be the subject of disproportionate government scrutiny. That wording does not appear in the filing (full text searched); the closest language is the passage above.
