The Madison County, Arkansas Quorum Court approved a three-year data-center moratorium on Aug. 17, running through Aug. 31, 2029, per KATV citing its content partner. The Arkansas Advocate reported that the measure passed unanimously and that the county has no known projects in development.

The Advocate counts it as the second-longest of the state's county moratoria; only Independence County's runs longer. See also [[synthesis-august-datacenter-moratorium-wave-2026-08-27]].
